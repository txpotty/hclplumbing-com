    What a Toilet Replacement Covers in Yonkers, NY

    High-Efficiency Toilet Installation in Yonkers, NY

    Toilets made before 1994 often use 3.5 gallons a flush, and some from the 1970s use five or more. Current WaterSense models are rated at 1.28 gallons per flush or less, and the better ones still clear the bowl in one try because the trapway and flush valve are larger than they used to be. Savings show up fast. Some water providers offer a rebate, so ask before you buy.

    One-Piece Toilet Replacement in Yonkers, NY

    One-piece toilets have no tank-to-bowl joint, so there are fewer leak points and fewer crevices to clean. They cost more and weigh more. A one-piece toilet usually takes two people to carry upstairs, and we set it without dragging it across your tile so the floor stays unscratched and the wax ring lands centered on the flange.

    Standard Toilet Replacement in Yonkers

    Two-piece toilets are what most houses have, and after twenty or thirty years the tank bolts corrode, the porcelain gets crazed and the flush never quite clears the bowl. That is when we swap them. The old unit comes off, the flange gets scraped clean and checked for cracks, then set the new one on a fresh wax ring with new closet bolts.

    Toilet Rough-In Adjustments in Yonkers, NY

    Rough-in is the distance from the finished wall to the center of the drain, usually 12 inches, sometimes 10 or 14 in older homes. Measure to the closet bolts, not the baseboard. If the new toilet's rough-in does not match, it either sits too far out or will not fit. An offset flange can correct an inch or two without moving the drain.

    Cracked Bowl or Tank Replacement in Yonkers

    Sometimes only the tank is cracked, and if the model is still in production, a matching tank costs less than a whole new toilet. First we find the model number inside the tank. If the bowl is the cracked part, the whole toilet gets replaced, since the bowl carries the seal to the floor and cannot be patched.

    Commercial Flush Valve Upgrades in Yonkers

    Manual flush valves on older commercial toilets leak at the handle and stick open, and swapping to a sensor valve during a replacement improves hygiene without changing the rough-in. Battery and hardwired models are both available. Work can happen after hours, and we check that the new fixtures meet accessibility requirements.

    What a Standard Swap Includes in Yonkers

    The swap itself runs about two hours: water off, tank drained, old toilet out, flange scraped and inspected, new toilet set on a fresh seal, and a braided stainless supply line connected. Then the leveling. The base gets caulked, with a small gap left at the back so any future leak shows up instead of hiding under the floor.

    Cracked Toilet Replacement in Yonkers

    Porcelain cracks below the waterline are not safely repairable, and epoxy fixes on a tank under constant water pressure rarely last more than a few months. You should replace it. We pull the cracked toilet and check the floor for water damage before setting the new one, since a slow crack leak often soaks the subfloor first.
