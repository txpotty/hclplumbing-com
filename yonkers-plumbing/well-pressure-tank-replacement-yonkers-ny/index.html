    Tank Tee, Gauge and Relief Valve Replacement in Yonkers

    The tank tee is the brass manifold that connects tank, switch, gauge, drain and relief valve. Old ones leak at threads. The relief valve should open before the system reaches a dangerous pressure, and a corroded valve may not open when needed. Whole set, one visit.

    Well-Rite Pressure Tank Installation in Yonkers, NY

    Pressure Tank Sizing and Upsizing in Yonkers

    Drawdown is the usable water between cut-out and cut-in. A 20-gallon tank might give you five or six gallons. We divide pump flow by drawdown to see how many starts per hour the pump will face, and target a minimum one-minute run time. That often points to a bigger tank.

    Galvanized Steel Pressure Tank Upgrades in Yonkers

    Old galvanized tanks have no bladder at all. Air and water share the same space. Over time the water absorbs the air, the tank waterlogs, and someone has to drain and recharge it every few months. A sealed bladder tank ends that chore.

    Constant pressure systems use a variable speed drive that adjusts pump speed to demand. Pressure stays steady even with two showers running. These systems can run on a smaller tank, but they cost more up front and need a compatible pump and drive. We explain the trade-off.
