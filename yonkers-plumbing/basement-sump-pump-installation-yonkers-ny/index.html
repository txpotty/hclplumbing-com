    Plumber licensing is handled locally rather than statewide, so requirements vary from one city or county to the next. HCL Plumbing is familiar with local permitting requirements in Yonkers and can help you obtain the necessary permits so your installation is compliant.

    We recommend having your sump pump inspected and maintained once a year, ideally before the rainy season. Regular maintenance includes checking the float switch, cleaning debris out of the pit, testing the check valve and making sure the discharge line is clear. This helps prevent unexpected failures and extends the life of your pump.

    Discharge should go to your yard, a dry well or an approved drainage area at least 10 feet from the foundation. Connecting a sump pump to the sanitary sewer is generally not allowed by local codes.
