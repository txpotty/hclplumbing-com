    Understanding Hydrojetting vs. Snaking in Yonkers, NY

    While snaking can punch a hole through a clog, hydrojetting in Yonkers, NY provides a complete pipe cleaning. Snaking is effective for simple, localized clogs, but it often leaves behind residual buildup that can lead to recurring problems. Hydrojetting, by contrast, scours the entire interior of the pipe, removing debris, grease, and scale, offering a more lasting solution to your drainage issues.

    Hydrojetting vs Snaking Cost Comparison in Yonkers, NY

    Snaking a main line typically costs $200 to $450, while residential hydrojetting usually runs $400 to $900 including camera inspection. Commercial lines and long runs can cost $800 to $2,000 or more. Jetting costs more up front, but it often prevents repeat calls that add up over a year. We help you decide which method suits your line and budget.

    Sewer Camera Inspection Before Hydrojetting in Yonkers

    Before performing any hydrojet plumbing in Yonkers, HCL Plumbing typically conducts a thorough sewer camera inspection. This allows us to accurately diagnose the type, location, and severity of the blockage, as well as assess the condition of your pipes. Knowing if there is existing damage like cracks or collapses helps us determine the right pressure and nozzle, ensuring the hydrojetting process is safe and effective, especially for lines under older slabs and foundations.

    Kitchen Grease Line Jetting in Yonkers

    Kitchen drains collect grease that cools and hardens along the pipe wall, especially in long horizontal runs under floors and slabs. Snaking clears a path, but the grease soon builds back. Jetting with a rotating nozzle peels that layer off and flushes it downstream. Homeowners who cook often and restaurants benefit most from regular kitchen line jetting.
