    Storm-Ready Sump Pump Installation in Yonkers, NY

    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Yonkers

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. Sizing comes from two measurements we take on site, the pit diameter and the vertical lift to the discharge. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. We usually pair that with a 1-1/2 inch discharge line.

    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Yonkers

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. Because everything comes assembled on a single riser, they fit into an 18-inch pit that would be too tight for two separate pumps and two check valves. That saves some labor. They suit homes that want backup protection but do not want to enlarge the pit.

    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Yonkers, NY

    A dead pump during a storm means the water is already climbing. Our trucks carry 1/3 and 1/2 HP submersibles, check valves and fittings, so in most homes we can pull the old pump and set a new one in one visit. Figure on two hours for a typical swap. We test it with a full pit before leaving.

    Sump Pump Replacement in Yonkers, NY

    Floats stick and motors seize when a pump sits idle for months between storms. We pull the old pump and inspect the pit. Undersized pumps get upsized. Testing is done with a full pit.

    Commercial Sump Pump Solutions in Yonkers

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and loading dock drains need pumps rated for more volume. For those jobs we install duplex systems where two pumps alternate, with a control panel and a high water alarm that can tie into the building monitoring. Downtime costs money. We plan the install around your business hours.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Yonkers

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. They run quieter and move more water. Lids close flat over them. We seal the cord grommet and the lid on every install.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Yonkers, NY

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the pit, so the motor never sits in water. You can reach the motor and the switch without fishing anything out of the pit, which helps in a basement where the pit is shallow or tight. Expect more motor noise than a submersible. We set the float rod and the discharge so the pump cycles cleanly.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Yonkers, NY

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. A smart charger keeps the battery topped off and sounds an alarm if the battery weakens, the primary fails or the water rises past the backup float. Expect a battery swap every four to five years. Finished basements get the most out of them.
