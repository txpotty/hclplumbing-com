    Frequently Asked Questions About Rooter Services in Yonkers

    Rooting a sink, tub or toilet typically runs $125 to $300. Main sewer rooting through a cleanout usually runs $200 to $500. Adding a jetting pass brings the total to about $450 to $1,000, depending on line length and how much grease is in it. Nights and weekends run higher.

    Most single-drain clogs take 1 to 3 hours from arrival to flow test. Main lines with heavy roots, or jobs that need a jetting pass, can stretch to half a day or more. Access is the wild card. With no usable cleanout, we pull a toilet to get in, and that adds about an hour.

    Chemical drain openers rarely clear a real clog and can eat at old pipe. Old cast iron and the thin brass traps found in Yonkers's older homes suffer the worst of it. They also put our techs at risk. Let us know if you used a chemical, because that water splashes when the trap comes off.

    Slow drains, gurgling pipes and sewer smell are the early ones. If the tub fills when the toilet flushes, the main is blocked. Do not wait on that one. More than one fixture draining slow at the same time usually means the main, not a branch.

    Keep grease, coffee grounds and wipes out of drains. Hair strainers in tubs and showers help. After using the disposal, run hot water for a bit and scrape food into the trash first to keep the kitchen line clearer. Root-prone lines need cutting every year or two.

    No permit is required for routine rooting. A new cleanout or a pipe repair may need one. We pull permits for any repair work we do.

    Our trucks carry Spartan and RIDGID machines, plus jetters sized for residential and commercial lines. Both brands hold up to daily use. We keep cables and cutter heads in several sizes on board, which lets the tech match the tool to your pipe instead of forcing one setup on every drain.
