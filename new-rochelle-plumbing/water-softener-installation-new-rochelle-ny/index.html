    Residential Water Softener Installation in New Rochelle

    Hard water costs homeowners money in shorter appliance life and extra cleaning. We test the water, ask about daily use and choose a capacity that lets the system regenerate roughly once a week. Weekly is the sweet spot. Every detail is handled on one visit.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in New Rochelle, NY

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. HCL Plumbing sets them up with the right resin and brine tanks. We program regeneration by metered water use so the system only recharges when the resin is actually spent, which saves a lot of salt. Most effective option.

    Water Softener Installation for New Construction in New Rochelle, NY

    Building a home in New Rochelle, NY? New construction is the cheapest time to add a softener loop, because the walls are open and the main can be routed past the garage or utility room without cutting finished drywall. Builders call us in early. Hose bibs can stay on hard water.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in New Rochelle

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. Our RO installs go under the sink with their own faucet, and we can tie in the fridge or ice maker too. Pairs well with a softener. Filters get changed on schedule.

    Ecodyne Water Softener Installation in New Rochelle

    Ecodyne softeners are sold at many home stores, and we install them for homeowners who already bought one. Install follows the manufacturer manual for bypass, drain and overflow, then we set hardness and regeneration. We install store-bought units. We set it up for you.

    Culligan Water Softener Installation in New Rochelle

    Culligan systems are common in homes around here, and we install and connect them. Each install follows the brand's specs for drain, bypass and power, then we program it to your water test. We handle all of it. A loop saves time.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in New Rochelle

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. Instead of removing hardness, they convert it into a form that resists sticking, which reduces scale without salt or brine waste. Little upkeep is needed. They work best on moderate water.
