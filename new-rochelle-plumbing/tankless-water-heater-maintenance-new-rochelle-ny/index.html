    Navien Condensing Tune-Ups in New Rochelle

    Navien NPE units are condensing heaters with a second heat exchanger that pulls extra heat out of the exhaust before it leaves the house. The secondary exchanger produces acidic water. We clear the condensate trap and check the neutralizer media. A-series models with built-in recirculation also get a pump and buffer tank check.

    Yearly Tankless Inspection in New Rochelle

    A yearly inspection costs less than one service call for a dead heater. Gas connections get a leak test, and we work the pressure relief valve. We pull the air filter and check the igniter. Anything that is wearing out goes on a written list with a plain price, and you decide what gets done now and what can wait until next year.

    Rinnai Air Filter and Flame Rod Care in New Rochelle

    Rinnai cabinets are tight. On these units we pull the air intake filter, wash it and check the flame rod for carbon buildup. Recirculating Rinnai models also have a pump setting worth checking, since a wrong schedule wastes gas every hour the pump runs for no reason.

    Rinnai Tankless Service in New Rochelle

    Rinnai units tell you a lot through the controller. A code 11 is a failed ignition, while code 12 is a flame that lit and then dropped out. We clean the Rinnai air intake filter and look over the flame rod for soot or cracks. The LC codes are scale warnings, and they usually clear once the exchanger has been flushed and the unit is reset.

    Annual Tankless Checkup in New Rochelle, NY

    The annual checkup is a top-to-bottom pass on the heater. We read gas pressure at the inlet with a manometer while the burner is at full fire and compare it to the rating plate. We check the flue for blockages. You get a written report with the readings, so next year's visit has something to compare against.

    Noritz Tankless Tune-Ups in New Rochelle, NY

    Noritz builds a solid heater, but solid does not mean maintenance-free. A Noritz tune-up starts at the exhaust, where we look for soot and blocked terminations. Then we check the fan and the combustion air path. Last, we check the temperature setting and run a few fixtures at once, which shows whether the unit holds temperature between back-to-back showers.

    Commercial Tankless Maintenance in New Rochelle, NY

    Commercial kitchens and salons burn through hot water all day, so their tankless units scale up much faster than a house unit. Gas pressure gets checked with every burner lit, not at idle. Inlet screens and air filters get pulled and cleaned. Downtime is the real cost. We can book it before you open or after you close.

    Condensate and Neutralizer Service in New Rochelle

    Condensing tankless heaters drip acidic condensate, often around pH 3 to 4, and it needs to pass through a neutralizer before it reaches a drain. We clean the trap and look at the neutralizer media. We replace spent media. An outdoor run of condensate line gets checked for support and protection.
