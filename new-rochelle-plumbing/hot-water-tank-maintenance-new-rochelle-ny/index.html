    Gas and Electric Tank Checks in New Rochelle, NY

    Gas and electric tanks share the same steel shell, but the parts that wear out are different. Gas units get a burner, thermocouple and vent check.     Burner and Pilot Service in New Rochelle, NY

    On a gas tank, a dirty burner throws a lazy yellow flame and leaves soot on the flue baffle. The burner comes out and gets brushed and vacuumed. A tired thermocouple drops the pilot, so we test it too. The goal is a steady blue flame.

    Emergency Tank Troubleshooting in New Rochelle

    No hot water at all is usually a pilot, a gas valve, a tripped high-limit or a dead element. Those are fixable. Leaks from under the jacket mean the tank itself has failed.

    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in New Rochelle, NY

    The anode rod is a sacrificial metal rod that corrodes so the steel tank does not. Water hardness in New York varies a lot, with many surface water supplies running soft and groundwater in some areas running hard. We loosen the hex head with a breaker bar and an impact gun, pull the rod and look at how much core wire is showing. Six inches of bare wire means replace it.

    Gas Venting Tune-Up in New Rochelle, NY

    Atmospheric gas tanks vent by draft alone, so the vent connector needs upward slope of at least a quarter inch per foot and tight joints with three screws each. We check draft at the hood. Lint on the flame arrestor of an FVIR tank can starve the burner and kill the pilot. We vacuum it clean.
