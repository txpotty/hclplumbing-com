    What a Plumbing Inspection Covers in New Rochelle, NY

    Sewer Camera Inspection in New Rochelle, NY

    Many older homes have clay, cast iron or Orangeburg sewer lines that can crack, sag or fill with roots. We run a high-resolution camera from a cleanout to the city connection or septic tank. The video shows offsets, bellies, roots and breaks along with their depth and location, and you keep a copy of the recording for your records or for a contractor.

    Pipe Material and Water Quality Review in New Rochelle, NY

    Pipe material says a lot about what to expect from a house. Our plumber identifies the supply pipe, whether copper, CPVC, PEX, galvanized, poly or Kitec, and explains how each usually fails. Certain types need a plan. Poly and Kitec fail suddenly and galvanized chokes off with rust, so we flag them with replacement options.

    Gas Connection Safety Check in New Rochelle, NY

    During a full inspection, we look at the visible gas piping and the connectors to each appliance. Old brass connectors, missing shutoffs or drip legs and rust all get noted, and we sweep joints with a gas detector. Any leak is reported on the spot. Most of our gas findings are in older homes, often at original appliance connectors.

    Leak Detection and Assessment in New Rochelle, NY

    Hidden leaks waste water and quietly damage floors and walls. Start at the meter. With all fixtures off, we watch the meter's leak indicator, then use acoustic listening gear and a thermal camera to trace the leak behind walls, under floors or in the slab. Early detection limits the damage. Frost reaches deep into the ground here, so water service lines must be buried well below the surface to avoid freezing.

    Fixture, Drain and Shutoff Valve Check in New Rochelle, NY

    We run every faucet, shower and toilet and look for slow drains, leaks and weak flow. Shutoffs get tested too. Stops and the main valve tend to seize with age, and gurgling drains can mean a vent problem, both of which are simple fixes once found.

    Preventive Maintenance Checks in New Rochelle, NY

    A maintenance check is a lighter version of a full inspection for owners who want to stay ahead of problems. We flush the water heater, exercise the main shutoff and angle stops, check pressure and look for slow drains and weeping fittings. It takes about an hour. Yearly or every-other-year checks stretch heater life and keep valves from seizing.
