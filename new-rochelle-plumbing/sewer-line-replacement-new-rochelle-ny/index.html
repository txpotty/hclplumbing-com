    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in New Rochelle

    Cast iron drains in older homes corrode from the inside, particularly along the bottom of the pipe. Over time this leaves cracks, holes and a rough surface that catches waste. Replacing the cast iron sewer outside the home is often the first step. We can also evaluate under-slab cast iron and discuss lining or rerouting options. New PVC or HDPE will not rust or scale the same way.

    Partial Sewer Line Repair in New Rochelle, NY

    Not every damaged sewer needs full replacement. If the camera shows a single break or one root-damaged joint, a spot repair may fix the problem. We locate the exact spot and depth, excavate a small area and replace that section. This costs far less than replacing the whole lateral. We only recommend it when the rest of the line is in good condition.

    Sewer Pipe Lining as an Alternative in New Rochelle

    Cured-in-place pipe lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. After cleaning, a resin-soaked liner is installed and cured in place. This works when the host pipe still holds its shape without major sags or collapse. Lining can be a good option under slabs or finished areas. We tell you plainly if your line is a candidate.

    Sewer Line Inspection and Diagnostics in New Rochelle

    Before any replacement, a thorough video inspection is crucial. We use high-resolution cameras to travel through your sewer lines, identifying the exact location and nature of the problem, whether it is cracks, root intrusion, or collapsed sections. This precision lets us recommend the most appropriate and cost-effective replacement plan. That way we address the root cause of your sewer issues in New Rochelle.

    Permitting and Code Compliance in New Rochelle, NY

    Sewer Line Material Upgrades in New Rochelle

    Many older homes in New Rochelle may have sewer lines made from outdated materials like clay or cast iron, which are prone to cracking, corrosion, and root invasion. We offer upgrades to newer, more durable materials such as PVC or HDPE (high-density polyethylene). These materials resist corrosion, roots, and shifting soil, giving your sewer system a much longer lifespan.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in New Rochelle, NY

    Traditional excavation is sometimes the best option, especially when the old line has a severe belly or the slope must be corrected. We dig along the line, remove the old pipe and set new PVC on compacted bedding at the proper grade. Rocky or hard-packed soil can slow digging, and we account for that in the estimate. Each joint is glued and checked before backfill. The inspector sees the open trench before we cover it.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Replacement in New Rochelle, NY

    To minimize disruption, trenchless sewer line replacement replaces pipes without extensive digging. We offer pipe bursting, where a new pipe is pulled through the old one, fracturing the old pipe outward. This is ideal when protecting landscaping or driveways is a priority. The method works well for many homes in New Rochelle and can often be completed faster than traditional methods, using high-density polyethylene (HDPE) pipe known for its long life.
