    Whole-Home Electric Tankless Installation in New Rochelle

    Whole-home electric units like the Stiebel Eltron Tempra and Rheem RTEX can feed several fixtures at once when sized right.     Electric Tankless Installation Cost in New Rochelle, NY

    Converting from Tank to Electric Tankless in New Rochelle, NY

    Converting a tank to electric tankless frees floor space and ends standby loss. We remove the old tank, mount the new unit on the wall, reroute the water lines and add isolation valves, while the electrician runs the new circuits to the unit. Most finish in a day. It looks tidier.

    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in New Rochelle

    Hard water builds scale on tankless heating elements, which cuts efficiency and can trigger error codes. Water hardness in New York varies a lot, with many surface water supplies running soft and groundwater in some areas running mineral-heavy. A softener or scale filter helps, and the service valves we install make yearly descaling simple. Flushing takes an hour.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Sizing and Selection in New Rochelle

    Sizing comes down to two numbers, flow rate and temperature rise. If your groundwater comes in at 50 degrees and you want 120 at the shower, that is a 70 degree rise, and a 27 kW unit delivers about 2.6 gallons per minute at that rise. Summer water helps.
