    Old urinals come off harder than they went on. Rusted bolts and seized valves often have to be cut, so we protect the tile while working. New gaskets always go in. The flange gets cleaned to bare metal.

    Rough-In Plumbing for Urinals in New Rochelle, NY

    Rough-in decides whether the finish goes smoothly. We set the carrier, run a 2 inch waste line to the outlet height on the spec sheet, stub the 3/4 inch supply where the flush valve will land, and tie the vent in before anyone hangs drywall. The rough gets inspected before cover. Then the walls close.

    Residential Urinal Plumbing in New Rochelle, NY

    Home urinals use the same rules as commercial ones. Expect a 2 inch drain, a built-in trap, a vent and a supply sized to the flush valve. Permit rules apply too. We check that first.

    Waterless Urinal Installation in New Rochelle

    Waterless urinals save the most water and need the most discipline. They use a cartridge or a layer of sealing liquid that lets urine pass while blocking sewer gas, and the cartridge has to be swapped on schedule or the restroom starts to smell. Your custodial team needs training. We show them how.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Urinal Installation in New Rochelle

    Most urinal installs run $400 to $1,500. A waterless or flush valve unit set on an existing carrier with good lines sits near the low end, while a commercial job that needs new drain, vent or supply work lands closer to the top. The price is set before work starts.

    Two to four hours, usually. Removing the old fixture, cleaning the outlet flange, setting the new urinal with a fresh gasket and connecting the flush valve all fit in one visit when the carrier and lines are in good shape. Rusted bolts add time.

    A carrier is a steel frame inside the wall. It bolts to the floor slab and holds the urinal by its hanger bolts, so the fixture's weight and anyone leaning on it never rests on drywall or on the drain pipe connection. Most commercial jobs use one.

    New urinals almost always need one. New fixtures need permits and inspections, but like-for-like swaps often do not. We pull permits when needed.

    The common ones, yes. Sloan and Zurn rebuild kits, outlet gaskets, hanger bolts, vacuum breakers and control stop parts ride on every truck, so most flush valve repairs finish on the first visit. China gets ordered per job.
