    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in New Rochelle

    While both sump pumps and sewage ejector pumps remove water, their applications are distinct. HCL Plumbing provides clear guidance and installation for both in New Rochelle. A sump pump handles groundwater and storm runoff, usually at a basement's lowest point to prevent flooding. A sewage ejector pump, however, is designed for wastewater containing solids from toilets, sinks and showers in below-grade plumbing. We make sure you get the correct pump for your drainage needs, preventing costly mistakes.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Basin Installation in New Rochelle, NY

    The basin is a key component of any sewage ejector pump system. HCL Plumbing provides professional sewage ejector pump basin installation in New Rochelle, NY, making sure it is properly sealed, vented and sized for your pump. An incorrectly installed or undersized basin can lead to frequent pump cycling, premature wear and even overflows. We use durable, corrosion-resistant basins and make sure all connections are watertight and gas-tight to prevent odors and health hazards in your home or business.
