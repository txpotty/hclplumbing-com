    Urgent Softener Problems in New Rochelle

    Not every softener failure is urgent. A unit leaking from the valve body or the brine tank overflowing onto the floor is, and we move those calls up the schedule. Turn the bypass handle and wait for us.

    Brine Tank Replacement in New Rochelle

    Salt bridges, crusty layers of salt that form over a hollow, stop the brine from making contact with the water and the softener quits softening. We break them up and clean out the mush at the bottom. Cracked tanks and stuck safety floats get replaced. Use pellet salt.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in New Rochelle

    Salt-based softeners pull calcium and magnesium out of the water by trading them for sodium on a bed of resin beads, which is the only method that actually makes water soft. We replace timer units with metered valves that regenerate on gallons used. Salt use usually drops. We set the brine refill to your hardness so the system is not dumping salt down the drain every few days.

    Metered Softener Replacement in New Rochelle

    Timer softeners regenerate every few days whether you used 50 gallons or 500. A metered valve, Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1, tracks gallons and regenerates when the resin actually needs it. Most people notice less salt in a few weeks.

    Upsizing a Whole-Home Softener in New Rochelle

    Undersized softeners are common in homes that added a bathroom or a couple of teenagers since the original system went in. What worked for three people can run out of capacity in a few days with five. We size for the house you have today.

    Emergency Softener Replacement in New Rochelle, NY

    A softener that sticks in regeneration can run water to the drain for hours, and a cracked bypass or tank can flood a garage floor. First step, turn the bypass handle. Most stuck or leaking systems get replaced in a day or two, sooner if the right size is in stock.

    Salt-Based Softener Swaps in New Rochelle, NY

    Swapping a salt-based softener in New York homes means disconnecting the old resin and brine tanks, cutting in new fittings if the loop is corroded, and setting the new valve. Every joint gets checked for leaks under pressure. The drain line gets an air gap.

    Softener Failure Diagnosis in New Rochelle, NY

    A clogged injector, a dead motor or a wrong setting can make a good softener look finished. We check those first and test hardness before and after the unit, so you know whether it is softening at all. Discontinued valves usually mean replacement.
