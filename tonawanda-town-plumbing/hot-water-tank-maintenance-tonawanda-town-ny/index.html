    Anode Upgrades for Odor in Tonawanda Town, NY

    A rotten egg smell from the hot side only is almost always sulfate-reducing bacteria reacting with a magnesium anode. Swapping the rod usually fixes it.     Relief Valve and Expansion Tank Testing in Tonawanda Town
