    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Tonawanda Town, NY

    One leak on a newer copper or PEX line is a spot repair. Two or more leaks on galvanized is a replacement. Age matters too, and a line past about 40 years is usually near the end no matter the material. Spot repairs often run a few hundred dollars while a full replacement costs more and resets the clock for decades. We show you the pipe we exposed.

    Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Tonawanda Town

    A slab leak is a supply pipe leaking under the concrete floor of the house. Newer homes and remodels around Tonawanda Town typically use copper or PEX supply lines with PVC drains, which are easier to repair. Signs include a warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water and a meter that moves with everything off. We pinpoint the leak with acoustic gear and thermal imaging. From there you have three choices: break the slab at the spot, reroute the line through the walls or attic, or abandon the old pipe under the floor and run new PEX above it. We price each one for you.

    Commercial Water Line Repair in Tonawanda Town, NY

    Galvanized Pipe Water Line Repair in Tonawanda Town, NY

    Galvanized steel service lines rust from the inside out. By the time one leaks, the rest of the pipe is close behind. We can clamp or replace a short section to get you through, and sometimes that buys a few good years. More often we recommend replacing the whole run from the meter to the house with copper or PEX. That fixes the pressure too.

    PEX and Copper Water Line Repair in Tonawanda Town

    Copper and PEX fail in different places. Copper tends to pinhole where a rock rubs it or where the water chemistry eats the wall from inside. With PEX, the fitting is the weak point most of the time. We cut out the bad section of copper and sweat or press in a new length, and for PEX we use the matching crimp or expansion tool so the joint is made the way the manufacturer intends.
