    Scheduled Jetting Maintenance in White Plains

    Properties with chronic drain issues benefit from scheduled jetting. Commercial kitchens often jet quarterly. Homes with root-prone sewers usually jet every year or two, and we keep a camera record from each visit so you can see whether the roots are coming back slower, faster or the same.

    Tree Root Removal with Hydrojetting in White Plains

    Roots find their way into sewer lines through cracked joints, and older clay pipe has a joint every few feet. A root-cutting nozzle spins through the mass. After cutting, the jet flushes the root pieces down to the main, and the camera shows which joints let the roots in so you know where a future repair or liner would go.

    Sewer Camera Inspection Before Hydrojetting in White Plains

    Jetting a broken pipe can make the break worse. The camera always goes in first. The video shows the pipe material, the condition of each joint, and any cracks, offsets or sags, which tells us the nozzle and pressure to use or whether we should not jet that line at all.

    Hydrojetting for Shops and Offices in White Plains, NY

    Retail and office buildings have long horizontal drain runs that collect buildup. Low-slope lines clog more. After-hours jetting keeps equipment out of hallways while tenants are working.

    Emergency Hydrojet Services in White Plains, NY

    A full sewer backup needs a truck today. First, we open the line. Usually a cable gets the water moving, and then we jet the line to remove what caused the backup, whether that is a grease plug, a root mass or a buildup of wipes caught on a rough joint.

    Grease Trap Line Cleaning in White Plains, NY

    Restaurant kitchen lines send warm grease downstream where it cools and sticks. By the time it reaches the interceptor, a lot of it has already coated the pipe. Short jetting passes keep loosened grease from plugging the line downstream. Quarterly works for most.

    Hard Water Scale Removal in White Plains, NY

    Scale builds slowly inside drain pipe, narrowing it a little more each year. Private wells serve a large share of rural homes, and they can carry iron, sulfur or hardness that stains fixtures and clogs filters. Scouring nozzles remove the scale, and badly scaled cast iron may get a descaling pass too. You can see the change on video.

    We run professional jetters from manufacturers like Spartan Tool and RIDGID. Cart units handle home lines. Trailer jetters with larger water tanks and higher flow handle sewer mains and commercial lines, and we pair them with RIDGID SeeSnake cameras for the before and after inspection.

    We do not recommend it. Jetters can injure people. Professional jetters run at pressures that can cut skin, and without a camera you cannot tell whether the pipe is sound enough to jet, so a DIY attempt can injure you or turn a clog into a broken pipe.

    We use cart and trailer jetters from manufacturers like Spartan and RIDGID. Nozzles vary by job. Penetrating, spinning and root-cutting nozzles ride on every truck, along with a locating camera.
