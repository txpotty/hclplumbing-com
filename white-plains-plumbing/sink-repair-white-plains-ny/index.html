    Sink Trap and P-Trap Repair in White Plains, NY

    The P-trap under your sink is crucial for preventing sewer gases from entering your home and catching items that fall down the drain. If it's leaking, corroded, or improperly installed, it can cause significant problems. We provide professional repair and replacement of P-traps, ensuring a proper seal and function for your sink plumbing in White Plains, NY.

    Leaking P-Trap Repair in White Plains

    Most puddles under a bathroom sink start at the P-trap. Slip-joint washers flatten over time, plastic traps crack, and old chrome traps rust through from the inside. We replace the trap with a correctly sized 1-1/4 inch assembly, align the tailpiece and wall arm, and run water to confirm a dry seal. This is one of the fastest and least expensive fixes we do.

    Slow Drain and Clog Clearing in White Plains

    Bathroom sinks clog with hair, soap scum and toothpaste mixed with mineral scale. We pull the stopper, clean the overflow channel, and clear the trap and branch line with a hand auger instead of harsh chemicals. If the clog sits past the wall, we check whether a shared line with the tub or shower is the real problem. You end up with a sink that drains at full speed.

    Overflow and Drain Body Leak Repair in White Plains, NY

    Water that appears only when the sink is full often comes from the overflow gasket or the drain flange putty. We pull the drain, clean the old putty or sealant, and reset the flange with fresh plumber's putty or silicone based on the basin material. Then we fill the bowl past the overflow and check for drips. This kind of leak is easy to miss and easy to fix once found.

    Garbage Disposal Repair in White Plains

    If your garbage disposal is jammed, humming but not grinding, or leaking, it needs professional attention. Our team in White Plains can troubleshoot and repair issues with all types of disposals, from brands like InSinkErator and Waste King. We can free jams, replace worn impellers, or fix electrical connections to get your disposal working safely again.

    Pop-Up Drain and Stopper Repair in White Plains, NY

    When the stopper will not close, will not open or lets water slowly escape, the pop-up linkage is usually to blame. The pivot rod nut can leak, the clevis strap can slip, and hair wraps around the stopper body. We clean or replace the assembly and adjust it so the lift rod works freely. If the drain body itself is corroded, we replace it with a matching finish.

    Sink Repair vs Replacement Costs in White Plains, NY

    A trap, stopper or supply line repair usually runs a fraction of the cost of a new sink. Replacement starts to make sense when the basin is cracked, the finish is badly etched, or the drain body is corroded into the bowl. We give you both prices when it is close so you can decide.
