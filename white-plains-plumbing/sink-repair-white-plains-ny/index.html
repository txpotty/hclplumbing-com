    Common Sink Repairs in White Plains, NY

    Office and Restaurant Sink Repair in White Plains

    Office breakroom sinks get abused in ways a home sink never sees. Coffee grounds and paper towels go down all day, and nobody checks the trap until water is on the carpet. We clear it and add a strainer. For restaurants, we keep three-bowl sinks running between the lunch and dinner rush.

    P-Trap Repair and Replacement in White Plains, NY

    The trap holds a plug of water that keeps sewer gas out of the room. Code calls for a trap seal between 2 and 4 inches, and a trap that leaks, sags or was cut down by a past owner can lose that seal and let the smell in. We replace bad traps with a matched kit. Chrome traps that have rusted thin get swapped for PVC or brass.

    Leaking P-Trap Fix in White Plains

    Check the slip nuts first. Overtightened plastic slip nuts crack, and the beveled washers inside them flatten until each joint weeps. The whole trap gets replaced as a kit. A 1-1/4 inch kit for bath sinks, 1-1/2 inch for kitchens.

    Hair Clog Clearing in White Plains

    Hair is the bathroom sink's main problem. We pull the stopper, strip the hair mat off it, then run a small hand auger through the trap and into the wall arm instead of pouring in drain cleaner. We flush the overflow channel while we are there. That slot smells when it fills with slime.

    Recurring Sink Clog Repair in White Plains, NY

    Monthly clogs have a cause past the trap. We run a camera or a longer cable into the wall arm and look for a sagging line or buildup where the sink ties into the tub drain. Sometimes the vent is blocked. A sink that gurgles as it drains is the giveaway.

    Garbage Disposal Repair in White Plains

    A disposal that hums but will not spin is usually jammed. We free the flywheel from underneath with a 1/4 inch hex key, press the red reset button, and check for a bent impeller or a broken grinding plate. Leaks are a different story. A cracked housing means a new disposal, usually an InSinkErator or a Waste King.

    Pop-Up Stopper Repair in White Plains, NY

    Stoppers that will not hold water have a linkage problem. Either the clevis slipped on the lift rod or hair has wrapped the stopper so it will not seat. Cleaning fixes half of them. The other half get a new pop-up drain in a matching finish.

    Supply Line and Angle Stop Replacement in White Plains, NY

    Braided supply lines have a rubber core that ages. After five to ten years the core can split without warning, and old multi-turn stops often seize open. We swap both in one visit. Quarter-turn stops close in one motion.
