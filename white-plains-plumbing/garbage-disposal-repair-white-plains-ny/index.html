    Electrical Issues and Motor Repair in White Plains

    A disposal that hums but will not grind is getting power but is stuck, while one that is silent has no power at all. They are different problems. For a silent unit we test the breaker, reset and switch, and for a humming one we check the flywheel and motor before deciding if it is worth repairing.

    Leak Detection and Repair in White Plains, NY

    Disposal leaks come from four places: the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube and the housing itself. We dry everything, run water with a little food coloring and watch to see which joint shows color first, since water runs down the outside of the unit and hides the real source. Three of the four are fixable. When the housing leaks, the unit has to go.

    Noisy Disposal Repair in White Plains

    Grinding, rattling or a hum that sounds rough usually mean trouble inside the unit. A loose mount will rattle. We check the mount, then look for debris, then run the motor empty, since a whine with an empty chamber means the bearings are going.

    Slow Draining Disposal Repair in White Plains, NY

    Fine grinding with slow draining usually means the discharge tube or the trap arm is packed. Rice and coffee grounds are the usual culprits. We pull the trap, clear the tube and the arm with the right size cable, and check that the arm has at least a quarter inch of fall per foot toward the wall.

    InSinkErator Disposal Repair in White Plains, NY

    The InSinkErator lineup runs from Badger units to the Evolution series, and we repair both. Badgers are simple single-stage units, while Evolution models have multi-stage grinding and, on some models, a separate stage that can jam on its own. Common parts are on board. A failed motor means a new unit.

    Emergency Disposal and Sink Backup Repair in White Plains

    When a disposal fails, water can back into the sink and the dishwasher. If water is rising or reaching the floor, shut off the disposal and the dishwasher and call us, since running either one just adds more water to the cabinet. We move quickly on these.

    Humming Disposal Repair in White Plains

    Humming with no spin almost always means a jam. Bones, pits, glass and spoons are the usual suspects. With the power off, we free the flywheel using the hex socket underneath, pull the object, and test the motor and reset with water running.
