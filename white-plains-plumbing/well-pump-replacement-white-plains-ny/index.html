    What We Replace on Well Systems in White Plains, NY

    Well Pump Brands We Install in White Plains, NY

    Goulds, Grundfos, Franklin Electric and Sta-Rite cover nearly every well pump we install. We pick by the well, not the logo. Parts for Goulds and Franklin are easy to get, which matters when a control box dies on a weekend.

    Drop Pipe and Wire Replacement in White Plains

    Galvanized drop pipe from the 1970s and 1980s rusts from the inside, and a pinhole at 150 feet will bleed pressure every time the pump shuts off. We replace it with schedule 120 PVC or poly pipe, depending on the depth and the pump weight. The wire gets checked too. Nicked jackets and old tape splices go.

    Solar Well Pump Systems in White Plains, NY

    Solar well pumps make sense for livestock tanks and cabins where running power from the meter would cost more than the pump itself. A DC pump and a controller run off a few panels during daylight. You store water, not electricity. We size panels to your daily gallons.

    Franklin Electric Well Pump Replacement in White Plains

    Franklin Electric builds a large share of the submersible motors sold in the US, including ones sitting under other brands' pump ends. Parts and control boxes are easy to find. We match the motor and the wire gauge to the depth of your well.

    Submersible Well Pump Replacement in White Plains

    A submersible pump hangs on drop pipe inside a 4-inch or 6-inch casing, often 100 to 400 feet down, so pulling one is a two-person job with a pump hoist. Most fail from sand chewing up the impellers or a lightning surge that cooks the windings. We megger the motor before we pull it. That tells us whether the problem is down the hole or up top.

    Pump and Pressure Tank Matching in White Plains

    Pump and tank work as a pair. An undersized or waterlogged tank forces dozens of extra starts a day, and each start puts heat into the motor windings. Every pump job includes a tank check.

    Sandy Wells and Submersible Pumps in White Plains

    Sand kills submersibles early. Grit in a well chews up impellers fast, so we look for it on the old pump and may set the new one higher or add a sand separator. Pump setting depth matters.

    Agricultural and Ranch Well Pumps in White Plains

    Irrigation and stock wells often run 3 to 10 HP pumps, sometimes on three-phase power with a motor starter and overload protection at the panel. We replace those pumps and the starters that protect them. Timing matters on a ranch.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Well Pump Replacement in White Plains

    Most well pump replacements in White Plains, NY land between $1,500 and $5,000. Depth and horsepower drive most of the price, along with any new tank or wire the job needs. You get it in writing first.

    Expect 1 to 5 years on the pump. We back the installation with a labor warranty that covers our splices and pipe joints. We go over the terms before you choose.

    Ten to twenty years is a normal life for a well-sized pump. Sandy water and short cycling can cut that to under five years. Replace it when it fails.

    Our usual picks are Franklin and Grundfos for submersibles, Goulds for jet pumps, and Zoeller for some shallow well work. We can match an existing brand. Budget matters too.

    Location is the difference. Jet pumps stay on the surface and suck water up from shallow wells, and submersibles sit in the well and push water up from far deeper. Submersibles are more efficient at depth.

    Shallow well pumps, usually jet pumps, sit above ground and pull water by suction from about 25 feet or less. Submersibles go down in the water and push it to the surface. Physics sets that 25-foot limit.

    Permit rules vary by county. We follow the local rules for well work.

    No water at the tap is the obvious one. Weak or surging pressure, a pump that short cycles, air spitting from faucets or sudden cloudy water also point to a pump or tank problem. We can tell you which in one visit.
