    Drop-In Kitchen Sink Installation in White Plains

    Sitting on the counter by their rim, drop-in sinks are a natural fit for tile, laminate and butcher block counters. It is often the quickest swap. After checking the cutout, we seal the rim and draw the clips down evenly, and in older homes and rentals it is often a two-hour job.

    Composite Sink Installation in White Plains

    Granite and quartz composite sinks are tough on top but can chip at the drain opening if a strainer is overtightened. Hand tight comes first. Most composite brands want silicone rather than putty under the strainer because putty can stain them, so we go by the maker's instructions.

    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in White Plains

    An undermount depends on its clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. We mount the bowl, seal the rim with silicone and brace it while the adhesive sets, which usually takes overnight before the sink can hold a full load of water. Strainers and traps go in after.

    Farmhouse Sink Plumbing in White Plains, NY

    Fireclay and cast iron farmhouse sinks can weigh well over 100 pounds empty, before a single drop of water goes in. A support frame goes into the cabinet and the sink is leveled before the countertop goes on. A deep bowl means a lower trap. We plan the drain for that depth from the start.

    Undermount Sinks on Stone in White Plains, NY

    Stone shops vary on how they mount undermounts. Some epoxy threaded studs into the stone, others use a rail system, and we work with whichever one your countertop shop prefers. Either way, the bowl gets braced. We do not connect the trap until the adhesive has cured.

    Commercial Kitchen Sink Installation in White Plains, NY
