    Emergency Water Line Break Repair in White Plains, NY

    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in White Plains, NY

    Many homes built before the 1970s still have galvanized steel service lines that rust from the inside. Homeowners around White Plains with hard water often add a softener to protect water heaters and keep scale out of faucets. Hard water speeds up scale buildup, which restricts flow and causes rust-colored water. Patching galvanized pipe seldom holds up because the surrounding pipe is just as corroded. We usually recommend replacing the full run from meter to house with copper or PEX, which restores pressure and stops repeat leaks.

    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in White Plains, NY

    A single leak on a newer copper or PEX line is usually a simple spot repair. Repeat leaks, galvanized pipe or a line older than about 40 years usually justify full replacement. Spot repairs often run a few hundred dollars, while a full replacement costs more but resets the clock for decades. We show you the condition of the pipe we expose so you can make the call with actual information.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in White Plains

    If your service line runs under a driveway, pool deck or mature landscaping, trenchless replacement can save a lot of restoration cost. We dig small pits at each end and pull new HDPE or PEX pipe through using a pneumatic boring tool or pipe pulling equipment. The process usually takes one day. You keep your pavers and trees, and the new line carries a lengthy manufacturer warranty.

    PEX Water Line Repair in White Plains, NY

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping is a flexible, durable, and corrosion-resistant option increasingly used in homes across White Plains, NY. While PEX is less prone to leaks than older materials, damage can still occur from improper installation, rodent chewing, or impact. Our team is fully experienced in PEX repair, using specialized crimping tools and fittings to make solid, leak-proof connections. We ensure your PEX system continues to perform well.

    Trenchless Water Line Repair in White Plains

    For some water line repairs, traditional trenching can be avoided.
