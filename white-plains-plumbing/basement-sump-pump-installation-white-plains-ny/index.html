    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in White Plains

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the water. That keeps it dry and easy to reach, which is why they suit narrow pits under 14 inches across, and why they often outlast cheap submersibles in White Plains homes. Expect more noise.

    Commercial and Multi-Unit Sump Installations in White Plains

    Duplex systems with two pumps and an alternating panel are standard for parking garages and multi-unit buildings with below-grade levels in White Plains. Each cycle, the panel switches which pump leads, so wear stays even. A high water alarm alerts building staff. We coordinate the wiring with your electrician.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Installation in White Plains

    Rigid PVC carries the water from the pump out through the rim joist and at least 10 feet from the house. A spring check valve stops the column from falling back into the pit, which cuts motor run time and the loud thunk you hear after every cycle. Shutting off and draining outdoor spigots before winter is a must for homes around White Plains, along with keeping the heat on when traveling. We fit a freeze-resistant outlet.

    New Sump Pit Excavation in White Plains

    No pit yet? We find the low spot where water collects, saw cut the slab, dig roughly 24 to 30 inches deep, and set a liner surrounded by washed gravel so water can enter while fine soil stays out. New concrete goes back flush and level.

    Sump Pump Installation Cost Comparison in White Plains, NY

    Pricing starts with whether you already have a pit. A pump swap in an existing basin costs the least, while a new pit adds saw cutting, digging and a concrete patch, and a battery backup adds the second pump and battery on top of that. Each line is itemized.

    Sump Pump Alarms and Smart Monitoring in White Plains, NY

    High water alarms cost little and buy you time. The basic version sounds off when water climbs above the float, while a Wi-Fi monitor sends a text if the level rises or the power drops, which homeowners who travel tend to like. We test it in front of you.

    Sump Pit Installation in White Plains, NY

    Pit size controls how often the pump starts. Too small a pit means the pump short cycles in heavy flow, so we usually install an 18 by 22 inch or 18 by 30 inch basin with a solid bottom and perforated sides wrapped in filter fabric. Fewer starts mean a longer life.

A second pump goes into the same pit on its own float switch, wired to a sealed AGM battery on a smart charger that tests itself and beeps when the battery gets weak.
