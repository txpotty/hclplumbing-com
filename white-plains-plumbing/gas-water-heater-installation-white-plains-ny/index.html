    Traditional Tank Gas Water Heater Installation in White Plains

    Tank gas heaters are still the workhorse in most garages and utility rooms, usually 40 or 50 gallons. Each install gets a new gas connector and drip leg, a vent connector pitched upward and a relief line piped where code allows. We strap it if code calls for it. Your household sets the size.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Installation in White Plains, NY

    Gas tankless units fire at up to 199,000 BTU, far more than a 40,000 BTU tank burner. That load usually calls for a 3/4 inch gas line or larger and a dedicated vent in PVC or stainless steel, depending on whether the unit is condensing. They never run out. Homeowners around White Plains with hard water often add a softener to protect water heaters and keep mineral buildup out of faucets. A scale filter may be worth adding.

    Direct Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in White Plains

    Direct vent heaters pull combustion air from outside and send exhaust out through a sealed, concentric vent. Because the combustion is sealed, they work well in small closets where room air is limited. Most run without electricity. The vent goes through a wall with proper clearances.

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in White Plains

    Atmospheric vent heaters rely on hot exhaust rising through a metal flue, with no fan. They are common in older homes and usually the least expensive gas option, but they only work if the vent has the right size, slope and clearance to combustibles. Old vents get checked. We replace bad sections.
