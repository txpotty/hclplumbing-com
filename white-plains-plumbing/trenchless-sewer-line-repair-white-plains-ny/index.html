    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in White Plains

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method used for full sewer line replacement. It involves pulling a new pipe through the old one while breaking apart the existing pipe. This creates a seamless, durable replacement, often using high-density polyethylene pipe, which is very resistant to corrosion and root intrusion, common problems in older White Plains homes. This method is well suited when the existing pipe is severely damaged or collapsed.

    Causes of Sewer Line Damage in White Plains, NY

    Sewer lines in White Plains, NY can suffer damage from several factors, including shifting soils that cause pipes to crack or misalign. Frost reaches deep into the ground here, so water service lines must be buried well below the surface to avoid freeze damage. Tree root intrusion is another major cause, as roots seek out moisture in pipes and grow, causing blockages and structural damage. Age and corrosion, especially in older cast iron or clay pipes, also add to deterioration. Our trenchless solutions address these common issues effectively.

    Cost Comparison of Trenchless vs Traditional in White Plains, NY

    While the upfront cost of trenchless repair might sometimes seem higher than traditional excavation, it often becomes more cost-effective when you count the savings on landscaping, driveway or foundation repair. For many White Plains, NY homeowners, avoiding extensive property restoration makes trenchless the more economical long-term choice.

    Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Repair in White Plains

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg fiber pipe or vitrified clay sewer laterals. Orangeburg flattens and blisters over time, while clay cracks and separates at joints. Pipe bursting is often the best option for Orangeburg, and lining can work well for clay that still holds its shape. We determine the material on camera before recommending a method.

    Pipe Bursting in White Plains, NY

    Pipe bursting replaces a badly damaged line by pulling a bursting head through the old pipe. The head breaks the old pipe outward while new HDPE pipe follows behind it. Bursting is ideal for collapsed or severely offset lines that cannot be lined. It usually requires an entry pit and an exit pit.

    Sectional Point Repair in White Plains

    When only one section of a line is damaged, a point repair can fix it without lining the whole length. We position a short resin liner at the damaged spot using the camera and inflate it until it cures. Point repairs are a cost-effective fix for a single crack, hole or root entry. They are common on otherwise healthy lines.
