    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in White Plains

    A bursting head is a steel cone, slightly wider than the old pipe, pulled through the line by a hydraulic unit or a winch cable. As it moves, it splits the old clay or cast iron and shoves the pieces into the surrounding soil while a string of new HDPE pipe follows right behind it. Fused joints, no gaps. We use it when the old line is too broken or flattened to hold a liner.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Upgrades in White Plains

    Bursting can upsize the line. We can pull in 6-inch HDPE behind a head that bursts an old 4-inch clay pipe, which helps on lines that clog because they were undersized from the start or have been added onto with extra bathrooms. Each end only needs a pit around three by four feet. No trench in between.

    Commercial Trenchless Sewer Lining in White Plains, NY

    Office and retail buildings often have a cast iron building drain under the slab that has rotted along the bottom. Lining it avoids cutting the floor. Work gets scheduled around tenant hours, nights included. A typical commercial lining job runs one to three days, depending on footage and how many branch connections need to be reinstated with a cutter.

    Causes of Sewer Line Failure in White Plains, NY

    Shifting soil is one of the main reasons sewer joints separate, crack or drop out of grade over the years. Basements are standard in most homes, and many of them depend on sump pumps to handle groundwater during wet seasons. Roots are the other big one, since a hairline gap at a clay joint is enough for a feeder root to get in and then swell into a mat that catches paper. Age does the rest. Cast iron scales and thins, and clay bells crack under load.

    Perma-Liner Trenchless Solutions in White Plains

    Perma-Liner makes inversion drums and epoxy systems for lining 2- to 6-inch drain lines, which covers most of what we see in houses. The setup is compact enough to fit in a pickup bed and roll through a side gate. Compact gear matters on tight lots. Resin gets mixed on site and timed to the length of the run, so it does not start curing before the liner is in place.

    Root Intrusion Fixes in White Plains, NY

    Roots enter at the joints, where clay bells or cast iron hubs have a small gap or a failed gasket. Once inside, they branch into a mat that fills the pipe. We cut them out with a mechanical root cutter or a hydro jet, then line the pipe so those joints are sealed for good and the line stops needing a cleaning every few months. The roots cannot get back in.

    Cast Iron Sewer Line Lining in White Plains, NY

    Cast iron rusts from the inside out, and the bottom of the pipe goes first. That is called channel rot. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or carbide cutters spinning on a flexible shaft, which brings the wall back to bare metal so a liner can bond to it. Under a slab, that beats jackhammering.

    Sectional Point Repairs in White Plains

    A point repair is a short liner, often 3 to 5 feet long, set over one bad spot. We push it in on a packer, guided by the camera footage, and the packer inflates to press the resin-soaked sleeve against the pipe wall until it cures. One crack, one patch. Sound lines with one bad joint are the right candidates.
