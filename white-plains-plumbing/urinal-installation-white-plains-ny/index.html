    Drainage and Venting for Urinals in White Plains, NY

    Urinal drains clog with scale, not paper. Urine leaves a hard mineral buildup inside the trap and the drain line, especially on waterless units and low-flow fixtures that send less water through, so we slope the line properly and add a cleanout where we can. A proper vent keeps odor out. We tie in within code distance.

    Urinal Brands We Install in White Plains, NY

    Brand choice is mostly about parts support. American Standard, Kohler, Zurn and Sloan all make reliable urinals, and we steer customers toward models whose flush valves and cartridges are stocked at local supply houses so a repair never waits on shipping. Each of those brands is routine for us. Spec sheets come first.

    Waterless Cartridge Urinals in White Plains

    Each waterless brand uses its own cartridge. Falcon, Waterless Co, Kohler and Zurn units all trap odor differently, and the cartridge or sealing liquid has to be replaced after a set number of uses, which in a busy restroom can mean every few months. We set up a schedule with you. Spare cartridges should stay on site.

    Residential Urinal Plumbing in White Plains, NY

    Home urinals use the same rules as commercial ones. The drain is usually 2 inch, the trap is built into the china, the fixture needs a vent within code distance, and the flush valve needs either a 3/4 inch supply or a lower-flow residential valve on 1/2 inch. Your city may want a permit. We check that first.

    Flushometer Urinal Installation in White Plains

    Manual flushometers are still the most common setup. We install Sloan Royal or Zurn AquaFlush valves on a 3/4 inch supply, adjust the control stop for proper flow, and set the vacuum breaker the right distance above the bowl so it protects the drinking water. Handle side follows the layout. ADA units need the handle within reach.

    Waterless Urinal Upgrades in White Plains

    Switching to waterless is mostly a fixture swap. We remove the flush valve, cap the supply and set the waterless bowl on the same carrier. Many jurisdictions want a capped supply kept for later. That allows a future change back.
