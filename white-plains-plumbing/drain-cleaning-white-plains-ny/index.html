    Laundry and Floor Drain Cleaning in White Plains, NY

    Washers push lint and softener residue into the standpipe. That gunk builds until water spills onto the floor.     Hydro Jetting for Drains in White Plains

    Jetting does what a cable cannot. A jetter sprays the full circumference of the pipe at high pressure. Cabling opens a path, but jetting strips the buildup off the walls. It works best on a kitchen or main line that keeps clogging every few months no matter how often it gets cabled.

    Office and Retail Drains in White Plains, NY

    Restroom batteries, mop sinks and floor sinks all clog in their own way. Paper towels are the usual suspect. Our crew handles floor sinks, mop sinks and restroom lines outside of business hours when that works better for you, and you get a written report for your maintenance file.

    Tub and Shower Drain Cleaning in White Plains, NY

    Showers clog slowly, then all at once. Hair builds a mat under the strainer. For tubs, the cable goes in through the overflow plate rather than the drain, which protects the tub surface, and we clear the trap and the branch beyond it, then run the shower for a few minutes to confirm it drains at full speed with no gurgle.

    Bathroom Sink, Tub and Shower Drains in White Plains, NY

    Toothpaste and soap scum glue hair into a stringy plug. Pop-up stoppers catch it first. After the stopper comes out and the trap is clean, a quarter inch cable goes down the branch, and if the stopper keeps catching hair we can adjust it or swap it for one with a simpler design. Usually under an hour.

    Jetting Versus Cabling in White Plains, NY

    Cabling and jetting solve different problems. A cable cuts through a blockage. A jetter scrubs the pipe wall clean and flushes the debris downstream to the city main or septic tank, so we cable first on a hard root mass and jet afterward when the line has years of buildup on it.
