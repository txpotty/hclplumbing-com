    Undermount Bowl Setting on Stone in White Plains

    Bathroom sinks are lighter but fussier. Faucet holes must match the faucet, single hole, 4 inch centerset or 8 inch widespread, and the overflow must suit the drain. The spec sheet gets read first. Mismatches get caught before the counter is cut.

    Sink Installation Cost Factors in White Plains, NY

    Labor is the main cost driver. Moving the drain or adding a second bowl adds hours, and a straight drop-in swap is the cheapest. We quote labor apart from the sink. You can buy it or we can.

    Public restroom lavatories take a beating. Wall-hung or countertop lavatories go on carriers with sensor faucets and mixing valves where tempered water is required. Vandal-resistant parts are available. Spec sheets go to your general contractor.

    Pedestal Sink Installation in White Plains, NY

    Pedestal sinks hide almost nothing. The supply stops and the trap sit right behind the column, so we center the drain stub, use chrome or polished brass trap parts, and set the stops tight to the wall where the pedestal can hide them. The wall needs solid blocking. The basin hangs from it, not the pedestal.

    Vessel Sink Installation in White Plains, NY

    Vessel sinks sit on top of the counter. That raises the rim several inches, so we either pick a taller vessel faucet or mount a wall faucet, and we use a vessel drain that matches the bowl's overflow or lack of one. No overflow means no slotted drain. Getting that wrong leaves water under the bowl.
