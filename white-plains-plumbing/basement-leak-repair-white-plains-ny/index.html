    How We Repair Basement Leaks in White Plains, NY

    Clogged Drain Tile Repair in White Plains

    Footing drains clog with silt and roots over the years, and once they stop flowing the water table at the wall rises. We camera the tile from the sump inlet to see where it is blocked. A jetter usually opens it. If the old clay tile has collapsed, it gets replaced with new perforated pipe.

    Drain and Sewer Pipe Leak Repair in White Plains, NY

    Cast iron drain stacks in older homes rust through from the inside over decades. A wet spot at the base of the stack or a crack along the hub is common, and we replace the damaged section with PVC or new cast iron joined with no-hub couplings. Plastic joints can fail too. A separated PVC joint usually means the pipe was not fully seated or not supported.

    Interior Drainage Systems in White Plains

    Where digging outside is not practical, an interior drain system does the same job from inside. We cut the slab along the perimeter, lay perforated pipe in washed stone, and slope it to a new or existing sump pit before patching the floor with fresh concrete. The wall gets a dimpled membrane. Water then runs behind it into the channel.

    Exterior Waterproofing Solutions in White Plains, NY

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing, cleaning the wall, and applying a membrane with a drainage board over it. It fixes the problem from the outside, at the cost of a torn-up yard. Plan on several days. Our crew handles the footing drain and its outlet, and a waterproofing partner applies the membrane.

    New Sump Pump Installation in White Plains

    Installing a new sump means breaking out a section of slab, setting a lined pit, and running the discharge out through the rim joist. Most of our installs get a cast iron pump, a check valve and 1-1/2 inch discharge. Discharge cannot go into the sewer in most places. It usually ends outside, well away from the foundation, at a spot your local rules allow.

    Sump Pump Installation & Repair in White Plains

    A sump pump lives in a pit cut below floor level and kicks on when a float rises. Most failures come from a stuck float switch, a burned-out motor, or a check valve that lets the discharge water fall back into the pit and short-cycles the pump. We test yours with a bucket. Add a battery backup if storms knock out power.

    Water Damage Assessment in White Plains, NY

    Before any repair, we figure out where the water came from and how far it spread. Moisture meters on drywall and baseboards show the wet area, and a thermal camera picks up cold spots where water is wicking behind the finish. Guessing costs money. You get photos and readings for your insurance file.

    Commercial Basement Waterproofing in White Plains

    Commercial basements often hold stock, files or equipment rooms that cannot sit in water. Elevator pits, floor drains and sumps all get checked together because one weak link floods the floor. Duplex pumps help. Alarms on the pit tell staff before water reaches the floor.
