    Why Polybutylene Fails in Hempstead

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. Over the years, the pipe and its acetal fittings get brittle from the inside, then crack along the inner wall until one splits under normal pressure. Homeowners around Hempstead with hard water often add a softener to protect water heaters and keep mineral buildup out of faucets. Hot water speeds it up. Most of the failures we see are on hot lines and at fittings close to the heater.
