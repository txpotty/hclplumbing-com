    Repiping and Pipe Replacement Options in Hempstead, NY

    Full Repipes for Older Homes in Hempstead

    Homes from the 1950s and 1960s often still have original galvanized pipe. That pipe is worn out. A full repipe brings new PEX or copper to every fixture, and fixes pressure and water color at once. Worth it.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Hempstead, NY

    Galvanized steel was common before the 1960s. Inside, rust builds once the coating fails. Over decades the opening shrinks, pressure drops and the water turns brown after sitting, and the threads where pipes join are often the first to leak. Replacing it is the only fix.

    Slab Leak Pipe Replacement in Hempstead

    Rerouting is often better than digging. Breaking a slab is messy and costly. We run new pipe through the attic or walls to bypass the leaking line under the concrete, which leaves the floors alone and prevents the same line from failing again. Fast too.

    PEX Pipe Installation in Hempstead

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster. Scale and rust do not affect it. Water hardness in New York varies a lot, with many surface water supplies running soft and groundwater in some areas running mineral-heavy. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings or PEX-b with crimp rings depending on the job, choosing a layout that fits the house. Fewer joints, fewer leaks.

    Slab Leak Pipe Repair and Replacement in Hempstead

    Water under a slab shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a sound of running water, or a meter that spins with everything off. We locate the leak first. Then you have a choice between breaking the slab to fix that spot or rerouting the line through walls or the attic, and rerouting often costs less and avoids future slab leaks on that line. We explain both.

    Burst Pipe Repair and Replacement in Hempstead, NY

    Frozen pipes are a real winter risk across New York, especially in exterior walls, crawlspaces, unheated garages and vacant homes. Frozen pipes split when water inside expands. Exposed pipe in crawlspaces, garages and outside walls is most at risk, and when it bursts we replace the damaged section with PEX or copper and add insulation or reroute it away from the cold. Then we test it.

    Drain Pipe Replacement in Hempstead, NY

    Drain lines fail too. Old cast iron rusts through. We replace cracked cast iron, failing ABS or old galvanized drains with PVC, and making sure vents and fall are correct. Camera first.
