    Copper Pinhole Leak Repair in Hempstead

    Pinhole leaks are tiny holes that form when the inner wall of copper pipe erodes or pits. They often appear in thin Type M pipe and on hot water lines where velocity and temperature are higher. We cut out the affected section and replace it with new Type L copper or a PEX transition. While the wall is open, we inspect nearby pipe for discoloration or thinning.

    Slab Leak Detection and Repair in Hempstead

    Copper pipes often run under concrete slabs in Hempstead homes. A slab leak can be particularly challenging to detect, and shifting soil can put extra stress on those pipes. Much of New York sits on glacial till, rock and clay, which makes excavation for sewer and water lines slower and more involved. We use acoustic and thermal imaging technology to locate these hidden leaks without invasive demolition. Once found, we offer direct access repair or re-routing options to fix the damaged copper section, minimizing disruption to your home.

    Solder Joint Leak Repair in Hempstead, NY

    Older solder joints can fail from poor original workmanship, thermal expansion or vibration. A leaking joint usually shows a white or green crust around the fitting. We clean, reheat and resolder the joint or replace the fitting entirely if it is corroded. The result is a clean, fully sweated connection that holds pressure.

    Corrosion-Related Leak Repair in Hempstead

    Corrosion is a natural enemy of copper pipes and is made worse by certain water chemistries. Homeowners around Hempstead with hard water often add a softener to protect water heaters and keep scale out of faucets. This can lead to thinning pipe walls and eventual leaks. Our team assesses the extent of corrosion, which may show up as blue-green stains on pipes or fixtures. For localized corrosion, we perform spot repairs, but if corrosion is widespread, we may recommend re-piping sections to prevent recurring issues. We make sure all new connections are properly sealed and supported.

    Commercial Copper Pipe Repair in Hempstead

    Many commercial buildings use larger diameter copper for domestic water supply. Leaks in these systems can affect multiple tenants and require careful shutdown planning. We repair commercial copper lines with press fittings to reduce downtime and avoid hot work permits where possible. We coordinate schedules with building management.

    Underground Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Hempstead

    Leaking underground copper pipes can lead to serious water loss and foundation issues, especially after heavy rainfall. We use trenchless repair methods where possible to fix or replace damaged underground copper lines, reducing the need for major excavation.     Hot Water Copper Line Repair in Hempstead, NY

    Hot water lines see more corrosion than cold lines because heat speeds up chemical reactions. Recirculation pumps that run constantly can also wear copper from the inside. We repair leaking hot lines and can adjust or add timers to your recirculation system. That reduces wear and lowers energy costs.
