    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Hempstead, NY

    PEX-A, such as Uponor, uses an expansion tool to stretch the pipe and a ring over the fitting, which then shrinks back into a secure seal. The fittings have a larger inner diameter, so flow restriction is lower. PEX-A is also more flexible and can recover from kinks with a heat gun. It is our preferred choice for many full repipes.

    Whole-Home PEX Repiping in Hempstead

    If your home has old, corroded or leaking pipes made of galvanized steel or polybutylene, a whole-home PEX repipe is an excellent solution.     Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Hempstead, NY

    Gray polybutylene pipe was common in homes built from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s and is known for sudden failures. Chlorine in municipal water breaks down the plastic and its fittings over time. Many insurers now ask about polybutylene when writing a policy. Replacing it with PEX removes that risk and can make your home easier to sell and insure.

    Frequently Asked Questions About PEX Pipe Installation in Hempstead

    The cost of PEX pipe installation in Hempstead, NY varies with the scope of the project, such as a full repipe or a small repair. In general, homeowners can expect to pay between $3,000 and $10,000 for a whole-home repipe, while smaller projects might range from $500 to $2,000. We provide detailed, clear estimates before starting any work.

    Small repairs are possible for handy homeowners, but a full repipe requires proper sizing, support spacing and code compliance. Mistakes behind walls can cause hidden leaks and rejected inspections.

    We install high-quality PEX piping from reputable manufacturers such as Uponor (PEX-A), Viega (PEX-B) and Zurn (PEX-B). We also work with other trusted brands like SharkBite for fittings, so the best materials are used for your installation.

    We install Uponor PEX-A along with Viega, Zurn and SharkBite PEX-B systems. We recommend the system that fits your project and budget.

    PEX resists scale and corrosion, flexes with temperature changes and has far fewer joints. Copper is very durable but prone to pinhole leaks in some water conditions and costs more to install.

    PEX piping is designed to be highly durable and has an estimated lifespan of 50 years or more. Its resistance to corrosion, scaling and freeze damage contributes to its long-term reliability, making it a sound investment for your Hempstead home.

    Most whole-home PEX repipes take two to four days. Larger homes or difficult attic access can take a bit longer.

    PEX offers several advantages over copper, including lower material and installation costs, greater flexibility and resistance to corrosion and freezing. Unlike copper, PEX does not form pinhole leaks over time due to water chemistry, making it a more durable choice for many Hempstead homes. It also cuts down on water hammer noise.
