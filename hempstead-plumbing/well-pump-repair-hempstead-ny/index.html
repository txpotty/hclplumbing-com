    Pressure Switch Replacement in Hempstead

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Most switches fail from pitted contacts, a plugged sensing tube or insects in the housing. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank, typically 40 and 60 psi. One of the cheaper well repairs.

    Submersible Pump Pulling and Repair in Hempstead

    Sand wears impellers down over time, and a pump that once filled the tank in a minute starts taking three. Other common failures are the motor, the splice and the check valve. Before pulling anything, we test the motor and wire from the wellhead. New pumps match your well's yield.

    Pressure Tank Repair in Hempstead, NY

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes the pump short cycle, turning on and off every few seconds. That rapid cycling burns out pumps early. We check the air charge, inspect the bladder and recharge or replace the tank, then confirm the pump runs at least a minute per cycle. Steadier pressure follows.

    Control Box and Surge Repair in Hempstead, NY

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and a relay. Spring snowmelt and heavy rain can raise water tables quickly in New York, sending water into basements and testing sump pumps. Surges from lightning are the usual cause of control box failures. After testing the capacitor, relay and overload, we replace the bad part and suggest a surge arrestor if the box lacks one. An arrestor costs little.

    Low Water Pressure Diagnosis in Hempstead

    Weak pressure on a well is not always the pump. A clogged sediment filter, a pressure switch set too low or a leak in the yard line can all cause it. In some cases the pump is fine and the well just cannot keep up with demand. We test each one.

    Franklin Electric Well Pump Service in Hempstead, NY

    Franklin Electric motors and control boxes are in a lot of local wells. We work on Franklin motors, QD control boxes and SubDrive units regularly. Common Franklin parts are on the truck. Replacing a failed motor usually costs less than a rewind once shipping is counted.
