    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Hempstead, NY

    For low-hazard applications, such as lawn irrigation systems in Hempstead, NY, a pressure vacuum breaker, or PVB, is a common and cost-effective backflow prevention device. PVBs protect against back-siphonage by letting air into the system when pressure drops. Our certified plumbers expertly install PVBs above the highest point of downstream piping, as code requires, to maintain effective protection. We also make sure of proper clearance and drainage.

    Reduced Pressure Zone Assembly Installation in Hempstead, NY

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of protection and are required for high hazard connections like commercial kitchens, medical facilities, chemical injection and car washes. They include two independent check valves with a relief valve that dumps water if either check fails. Because the relief valve can discharge, we plan for drainage and install the assembly above grade where it can be tested. We carry proven brands like Watts, Febco, Wilkins and Apollo.

    Irrigation Backflow Preventer Installation in Hempstead

    Landscape irrigation is the most common reason homeowners need a backflow device. Fertilizer, herbicides and stagnant water in sprinkler and drip lines can be siphoned into your drinking water if pressure drops. We install pressure vacuum breakers or double check assemblies based on local requirements and the design of your system. Above-ground installs are set at least 12 inches above the highest sprinkler head when a PVB is used.

    Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA) Installation in Hempstead

    The double check valve assembly, or DCVA, is a widely used backflow preventer for low-hazard applications where the consequences of backflow are not severe. In Hempstead, DCVAs are frequently installed on fire sprinkler systems or irrigation lines. Our certified plumbers carefully install these devices, making sure both check valves operate correctly and are properly sealed to prevent backpressure or back-siphonage into the main water supply.

    Most manufacturers offer one to five year warranties depending on the model. We also warranty our installation labor and will review the coverage with you.

    Backflow is when water flows the wrong way through a pipe and carries contaminants into the clean water supply. It can happen from back siphonage during a pressure drop or back pressure from pumps and boilers.

    In Hempstead, most backflow preventers must be tested once a year by a certified backflow tester to confirm they are working correctly and providing adequate protection. This is a separate service from installation but is essential for ongoing compliance and safety.

    Backflow preventers are installed at cross-connection points where non-potable water could enter the potable water supply.
