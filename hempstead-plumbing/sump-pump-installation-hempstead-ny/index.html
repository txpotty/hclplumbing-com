    Exterior Drainage and Yard Sump Pumps in Hempstead

    Sunken patios, below-grade driveways and deep window wells collect water with nowhere to go. For those areas we set an exterior basin with a submersible pump and run the discharge to a safe point, often a pop-up emitter in the yard. Slopes toward the house are the usual cause. The outlet sits well clear of the foundation and the property line.

    Commercial Sump Pump Solutions in Hempstead

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and loading dock drains need pumps rated for more volume. We install duplex systems with alternating pumps. Downtime costs money. Work is scheduled around your operating hours.

    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Hempstead

    A high water alarm is a float switch that sounds when the pit rises past normal. Wi-Fi models send a text too. Landlords like them. They can go on a new pump or one that is already working.

    Combination Sump Pump System Installation in Hempstead, NY

    Two pumps on one riser cover both kinds of failure, a dead motor and a dead outlet. The primary handles daily water. People with carpet downstairs buy these. They are worth it anywhere a few inches of water would mean new drywall.

    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Hempstead

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. Because everything comes assembled on a single riser, they fit into an 18-inch pit that would be too tight for two separate pumps and two check valves. That saves some labor. They suit homes that want backup protection but do not want to enlarge the pit.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Hempstead

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. They run quieter and move more water. The pit lid sits flush. Each install gets a sealed lid and a grommet around the cord to keep out radon and odor.

    Crawl Space Sump Pump Installation in Hempstead, NY

    Crawl spaces with standing water breed mold and rot sill plates within a few seasons. A small sump basin at the low point fixes that. Access is often tight. We route the discharge out through the rim joist and away from the foundation.

    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Hempstead

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. Sizing comes from two measurements we take on site, the pit diameter and the vertical lift to the discharge. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. We usually pair that with a 1-1/2 inch discharge line.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Installation in Hempstead

    Sump pump installation in Hempstead, NY runs about $800 to $2,500 for most homes. The pump type and whether we dig a new pit move the price most. Backups add to it. We quote it in writing after a site visit.

    Plenty of cities require a plumbing permit for new sump pump installs or major replacements. HCL Plumbing handles the permit in Hempstead, NY so the work passes inspection.

    Odd noises, rust, constant cycling or a pump that fails to start are the common signs. Any pump past seven years is worth a closer look, even if it seems fine, because the switch is usually the first part to go. We can test it.

    A submersible sits in the pit under water, so it runs quieter and takes no floor space. A pedestal pump keeps the motor on a column above the pit. Both work. We help you pick based on your basement.

    Have it cleaned and inspected once a year, ideally before the rainy season. We check the float, clean the pit and test the discharge. It catches problems early.
