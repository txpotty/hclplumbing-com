    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Hempstead, NY

    Interior walls waterproofing focuses on managing water that has already penetrated the foundation walls. This typically involves installing an interior drainage system, often called a French drain, along the perimeter of the basement floor. Water is collected and channeled to a sump pump, which then sends it safely away from the foundation. While not preventing water from entering the wall, it stops it from pooling inside your living space. This solution is less disruptive than exterior excavation and is suitable where exterior access is limited.

    Sump Pump Installation and Repair in Hempstead, NY

    Sump pumps are a critical part of many interior waterproofing systems, particularly in homes that see heavy rainfall. We install high-capacity sump pumps designed to handle large volumes of water, keeping your basement dry even during flash floods. Our services include primary sump pump installation, battery backup sump pump systems for power outages, and routine maintenance or repair of existing units. We make sure the discharge line is routed properly so water does not circulate back toward the foundation.

    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Hempstead, NY

    Block and stem walls often show efflorescence, which is the white mineral crust left when water passes through masonry. Irrigation and sprinklers aimed at the house are common culprits. We adjust or reroute irrigation, clean the wall and apply a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating. Proper grading and drip line placement help keep the wall dry.

    Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in Hempstead

    Newer homes and remodels around Hempstead typically use copper or PEX supply lines with PVC drains, which are easier to repair. Wherever a home has a basement or below-grade walls, heavy rain and snowmelt can push water through foundation walls and cracks. We seal cracks with injection products, apply interior or exterior waterproofing and install drainage where needed. A sump pump may be added if groundwater builds up.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Hempstead

    Many homes in Hempstead have crawl spaces, which are highly susceptible to moisture issues. Installing a heavy-duty vapor barrier on crawl space walls and floors is a key waterproofing strategy. This thick polyethylene sheeting stops ground moisture from evaporating into the crawl space, protecting subflooring and joists and improving indoor air quality. It's an affordable way to control humidity and prevent wood rot.

    Plumbing Leak Behind Walls in Hempstead

The damaged section of pipe or valve is replaced, and wet drywall is cut out and dried. Once the cavity is dry, we restore the wall and seal any wet-area walls.
