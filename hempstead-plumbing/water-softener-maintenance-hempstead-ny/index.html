    Control Valve and Timer Diagnostics in Hempstead, NY

    The control valve runs every stage of regeneration, from backwash through brine draw to the final refill. We advance it manually through each cycle and watch for a motor that stalls, a drive gear that skips, a piston that drags or a drain that never shuts off. Worn pistons turn up a lot.

    Salt Bridge Removal and Brine Tank Cleaning in Hempstead, NY

    Humidity and cheap salt cause most salt bridges. The top layer fuses into a crust with an empty pocket below it. The softener keeps regenerating on schedule, but it draws plain water instead of brine, which leaves the resin without a recharge and hard water flows to every tap.

    Salt-Free Conditioner Service in Hempstead

    Salt-free conditioners, usually template assisted crystallization units, do not remove hardness. A hardness test still reads high afterward. They change the minerals into tiny crystals that are less likely to stick. The media needs replacing every three to five years, and the sediment prefilter ahead of it needs changing every few months, which is the part most owners skip.

    Resin Cleaning and Regeneration Tuning in Hempstead, NY

    Iron is the most common resin fouler we see. It coats the beads orange and blocks the exchange sites. We feed a resin cleaner through the brine well, run a regeneration and retest, and if the capacity comes back we shorten the time between regenerations so the iron does not build back up.

    Yearly Water Softener Tune-Ups in Hempstead

    A tune-up starts with two hardness readings and ends with a full manual regeneration that we watch from start to finish. In between, we check the salt level, the brine well and the float. Figure on an hour for a typical unit.

    Resin Bed Checks and Regeneration Settings in Hempstead

    Resin does the actual softening. The beads swap sodium for calcium and magnesium, and once they are loaded up they need brine to recharge. We compare the softened hardness against the gallons used since the last regeneration to tell whether the bed is still at full strength.

    Commercial Softener Service in Hempstead

    Commercial units work harder. Restaurants and laundromats can burn through a softener's capacity in a single day, so twin-tank systems swap tanks, keeping one in service while the other regenerates. We set a service schedule around your hours so the water never goes hard during a rush.

    Control Valve Diagnostics and Repair in Hempstead

    Leaks at the valve body, water running to drain around the clock, a motor that hums without moving or a display stuck on one cycle usually point to worn seals and spacers. On a Fleck 5600 the fix is a seal kit and a new piston, often done in under an hour without pulling the valve off the tank. It costs a fraction of a new control head.
