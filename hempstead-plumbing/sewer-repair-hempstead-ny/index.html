    What Sewer Repair Covers in Hempstead, NY

    Sewer Line Backups and Blockages in Hempstead

    Water coming up in the tub when the washer drains is a classic main line symptom. The clog is past the branches. We clear it from the nearest cleanout, then run the camera to see what caused it, since a grease clog and a root mass need very different fixes. Older New York homes with clay lines often show roots right at the joints. We record what we find.

    After-Hours Sewer Repair in Hempstead

    Main lines like to fail on holidays. Our phones stay on. An after-hours call gets a tech with a cable machine and a camera, which is enough to restore flow in most cases and to find out whether the trouble is a blockage or a structural break. If it is structural, we schedule the dig for the earliest safe window. We avoid digging blind at night near gas and electric lines.

    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Hempstead

    Clay and cast iron crack from settling soil, tree roots and vehicles parked over the line. The crack lets soil in and sewage out. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded stainless couplings rated for the transition between materials, then test the repair with water and video before closing the hole. Most of these are one-day jobs.

    Commercial Sewer Repair in Hempstead

    Multifamily buildings and offices put several units' worth of flow through one lateral. Small defects turn into big backups. We repair larger diameter lines, add two-way cleanouts so future maintenance is easy, and give managers recorded footage for owners, insurers and their own files. We can work outside business hours.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Hempstead, NY

    Trenchless repair means we fix the pipe without opening a trench along its full length. Two access holes, sometimes one. Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE line through the old one and splits the old pipe outward, while cured-in-place lining pushes a resin-soaked felt tube into the old pipe and hardens it into a new wall. Either way, the driveway and the trees stay put. We tell you which one your pipe can take.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Hempstead, NY

    Cutting roots treats the symptom. The joint is the real problem. After clearing the line, we look at each rooted joint on camera and decide between a short sectional liner, a full liner or cutting in new pipe, depending on how many joints are affected and how far apart they sit. One or two joints is a small job. A line rooted end to end is a lining or replacement job.

    Sewer Vent Pipe Repair in Hempstead, NY

    A plugged vent makes drains gurgle and traps run dry. Then the sewer smell shows up. Bird nests, leaves and ice can block a roof vent, and older galvanized or cast iron vent stacks crack inside the walls where nobody sees them, which is why we look at the vent whenever the lateral checks out fine. We clear it from the roof or replace the damaged section.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Hempstead, NY

    Relining builds a new pipe inside the old one. The old pipe gets jetted clean before anything else. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy is pulled or inverted into place, inflated against the old wall and left to cure, so every crack and leaking joint is covered by one jointless pipe. The finished pipe loses a little diameter. On a four-inch residential line, that rarely matters.
