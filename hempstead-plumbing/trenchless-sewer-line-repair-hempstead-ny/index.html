    Causes of Sewer Line Damage in Hempstead, NY

    Shifting soil is one of the main reasons sewer joints pull apart or crack. Basements are standard in most homes, and many of them depend on sump pumps to manage groundwater during wet seasons. Roots are the other big one, since a hairline gap at a clay joint is enough for a feeder root to get in and then swell into a mat that catches paper. Age does the rest. Cast iron scales and thins, and clay bells crack under the weight of soil and traffic.

    Cast Iron Sewer Line Lining in Hempstead, NY

    Cast iron rusts from the inside out, and the bottom of the pipe goes first. That is called channel rot. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or carbide cutters spinning on a flexible shaft, which brings the wall back to bare metal so a liner can bond to it. Under a slab, that beats jackhammering.
