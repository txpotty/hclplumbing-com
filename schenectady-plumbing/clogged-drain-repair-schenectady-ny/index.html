    Outdoor Drain & Storm Drain Clearing in Schenectady, NY

    Outdoor drains, including those for patios, driveways, and storm drains, are crucial for preventing water damage. Nor'easters and tropical storm remnants can overwhelm municipal sewers, so backwater valves are a smart addition for basement homes. These drains can become clogged with leaves, dirt, and debris. We provide thorough cleaning and repair for outdoor drainage systems to ensure proper water runoff and protect your property's foundation in Schenectady.

    Laundry and Utility Drain Clogs in Schenectady, NY

    Washing machines discharge a lot of water quickly, and lint, fabric fibers and detergent residue build up in the standpipe and branch line. A clogged laundry drain often overflows at the standpipe mid-cycle. We clear the line with a cable and recommend a lint trap for the discharge hose. In some homes the laundry line ties into the kitchen line, so we inspect both.

    Tub and Shower Drain Clogs in Schenectady

    Hair and soap scum are the most common causes of slow tubs and showers. We remove the strainer or overflow plate and pull the clog out with a drum auger designed for small diameter drains. If the drain is still slow, we check the trap under the slab or in the access panel. We take care not to scratch acrylic, fiberglass or porcelain finishes.

    Kitchen Sink Drain Clogs in Schenectady

    Kitchen drains clog from cooking grease, coffee grounds and starchy food that stick to the pipe walls and harden. Garbage disposals can push food further down the line where it collects in horizontal runs. We remove the trap, run a cable through the trap arm and branch line and clear the buildup back to the main stack. For heavy grease, we may follow with a small jetter to wash the walls clear.

    Toilet Clog Repair in Schenectady

    Stubborn toilet clogs often require more than a plunger. Our plumbers use specialized closet augers to clear blockages without scratching the porcelain. If a deeper issue is suspected, such as a foreign object or a main sewer line problem, we'll perform a camera inspection to find the exact cause and offer a precise repair in Schenectady.

    Main Sewer Line Clog Clearing in Schenectady, NY

    A clogged main sewer line can affect all drains in your home and is a serious issue. Causes often include tree root intrusion, pipe collapse, or significant grease buildup, common in older homes. We use advanced camera inspections to locate the blockage and hydro-jetting to thoroughly clear it, restoring full drainage to your entire property.

    Hydro-Jetting Services in Schenectady, NY

    For severe or recurring clogs, hydro-jetting is an effective solution. This method uses high-pressure water streams to blast away buildup, grease, scale, and even tree roots from your pipes. It's a complete cleaning process that leaves pipes scoured, reducing the likelihood of future blockages and extending pipe life, particularly in homes with hard water deposits.
