    Steam Shower Plumbing in Schenectady

    Steam showers need four things: a generator, a water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit.     Custom Shower System Installation in Schenectady, NY

    Multi-head showers depend on valve capacity. Layout comes first. The water heater gets checked for capacity too.
