    Regeneration Programming and Hardness Testing in Schenectady, NY

    Plenty of softeners still run factory settings. Either the installer left the defaults in, or the family got bigger and the settings stayed put. We enter your raw hardness and household size, then set a metered valve to regenerate by gallons used instead of every few days on a timer.

    Breaking Up Salt Bridges in Schenectady

    To check for a bridge, we push a broom handle straight down through the salt. A hard stop, then a drop, means a bridge. We break the crust, pull out the mushy salt underneath with a wet-dry vac, then check the brine well, safety float, brine line and grid plate before refilling to about half full.

    Resin Rebedding in Schenectady

    Chlorine and chloramine in treated water slowly swell and soften resin beads, and iron or sediment can coat them until capacity drops off. A resin cleaner is the first thing we try. If the bed is spent, we drain the tank, pull the old resin and refill it with fresh high-capacity resin, which runs far less than a new softener.

    Salt-Free Conditioner Service in Schenectady

    Salt-free conditioners, usually template assisted crystallization units, do not remove hardness. A hardness test still reads high afterward. They turn the hardness into microscopic crystals that mostly pass through without sticking. Plan on new media every three to five years, and the sediment prefilter ahead of it needs changing every few months, which is the part most owners skip.

    We test with a titration kit, counting drops until the sample changes color, because test strips are too rough for setting a valve. Raw water gets tested from an unsoftened hose bib or with the valve on bypass. Softened water comes from the kitchen tap.
