    Reduced Pressure Zone Assembly Installation in Schenectady, NY

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of protection and are required for high hazard connections like commercial kitchens, medical facilities, chemical injection and car washes. They include two independent check valves with a relief valve that dumps water if either check fails. Because the relief valve can discharge, we plan for drainage and install the assembly above grade where it can be tested. We carry proven brands like Watts, Febco, Wilkins and Apollo.

    Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA) Installation in Schenectady

    The double check valve assembly, or DCVA, is a common backflow preventer for low-hazard applications where the consequences of backflow are not severe. In Schenectady, DCVAs are often installed on fire sprinkler systems or irrigation lines. Our certified plumbers carefully install these devices, making sure both check valves operate correctly and are properly sealed to prevent backpressure or back-siphonage into the main water supply.

    Backflow Testing and Certification in Schenectady

    Most water providers require testable backflow assemblies to be certified at installation and then annually. Our certified testers check each valve's opening point, relief valve operation and differential pressure. If a device fails, we can repair or rebuild it right away with genuine parts. We submit passing results so you avoid compliance letters or service interruption.

    Pool and Spa Fill Backflow Protection in Schenectady, NY

    An auto-fill line or hose connection on a pool or spa creates a straight path for chlorinated water to reach the supply. We install the correct device on pool fill lines and check that hose bibbs have vacuum breakers. This simple step protects your household water and keeps you in line with local code. We can add the work when a pool is being built or remodeled.

    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Schenectady, NY

    For low-hazard applications, such as lawn irrigation systems in Schenectady, NY, a pressure vacuum breaker, or PVB, is a common and cost-effective backflow prevention device. PVBs protect against back-siphonage by letting air into the system when pressure drops. Our certified plumbers carefully install PVBs above the highest point of downstream piping, as code requires, to maintain effective protection. We also make sure of proper clearance and drainage.

    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Schenectady

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of backflow protection and are commonly required for high-hazard applications in Schenectady. These devices feature two independent check valves and a relief valve that opens to discharge water if pressure differentials indicate a backflow condition. HCL Plumbing specializes in precise RPZ installation, with proper sizing, placement and testing to meet all regulatory requirements for residential and commercial properties. We work with top brands like Watts and Febco.

    Backflow Prevention for Irrigation Systems in Schenectady, NY

    Irrigation systems in Schenectady, NY are a common source of backflow, as stagnant water and lawn chemicals can be drawn back into the potable water supply. Installing a dedicated backflow preventer for your sprinkler system is often a local code requirement. We are experienced in selecting and installing the correct device, such as a pressure vacuum breaker or atmospheric vacuum breaker, to protect your home's main water line from contaminants coming from your landscape irrigation.
