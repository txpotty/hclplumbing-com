    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Schenectady, NY

    CIPP stands for cured-in-place pipe. The crew saturates a felt tube with two-part epoxy on a roller table at the truck, invert it into the cleaned line with air pressure, and hold it against the old pipe wall until the resin hardens. Cure time runs a few hours. The finished liner seals cracks and leaking joints in the original pipe.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Schenectady, NY

    Roots enter at the joints, where clay bells or cast iron hubs have a small gap or a failed gasket. Once inside, they branch into a mat that fills the pipe. We cut them out with a mechanical root cutter or a hydro jet, then line the pipe so those joints are sealed for good and the line stops needing a cleaning every few months. The roots cannot get back in.

    Sectional Point Repairs in Schenectady

    A point repair is a short liner, often 3 to 5 feet long, set over one bad spot. It rides in on an inflatable packer, guided by the camera footage, and the packer inflates to press the resin-soaked sleeve against the pipe wall until it cures. One crack, one patch. Sound lines with one bad joint are the right candidates.

    Perma-Liner and NuFlow Lining Systems in Schenectady

    Perma-Liner and NuFlow both make lining systems we use, each with its own resin chemistry and installation equipment. Both systems are made to meet the ASTM standards written for cured-in-place pipe. The brand matters less than the prep, because a liner bonded to a dirty, scaly pipe wall will not hold the way the manufacturer rates it. We clean to bare pipe first.

    Cast Iron Sewer Line Repair in Schenectady, NY

    Cast iron rusts from the inside out, and the bottom of the pipe goes first. Plumbers call that channel rot, a groove that eats through the bottom. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or carbide cutters spinning on a flexible shaft, which brings the wall back to bare metal so a liner can bond to it. Under a slab, that beats jackhammering.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in Schenectady
