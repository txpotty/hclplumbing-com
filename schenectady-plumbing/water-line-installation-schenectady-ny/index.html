    PEX Water Line Installation in Schenectady

    PEX, or cross-linked polyethylene, is a popular choice for its flexibility, durability and resistance to corrosion and freezing. Wide swings between summer heat and winter cold cause pipes and fittings to expand and contract, which gradually loosens joints. HCL Plumbing professionally installs PEX water lines for both new construction and replacements in Schenectady. PEX offers excellent flow rates and is less prone to bursting than traditional rigid pipe, providing a long-lasting, cost-effective solution for your home's plumbing.
