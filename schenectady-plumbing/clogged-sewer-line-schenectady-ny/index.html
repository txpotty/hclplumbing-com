    Residential Clogged Sewer Lines in Schenectady

    Hydro-Jetting Services in Schenectady, NY

    Jetting sends water at thousands of psi through a nozzle that sprays backward and drives itself down the pipe. It scrubs the whole wall.     Main Line vs. Branch Line Clogs in Schenectady, NY

    One slow drain is a branch clog. More than one usually means the main. Water rising in the shower when you flush is a strong sign the clog is past the point where those two lines join, so the pattern of slow drains tells us where to go in. The right entry point saves time.
