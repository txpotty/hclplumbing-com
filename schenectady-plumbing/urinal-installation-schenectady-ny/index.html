    Urinal Installation Cost Factors in Schenectady, NY

    Carrier work is the biggest cost swing. A replacement on an existing carrier with good supply and drain lines costs far less than a new install that needs a carrier, a new 2 inch drain and a 3/4 inch supply run through finished walls. Touchless valves cost more than manual. We itemize each piece.

    Flush Valve Urinal Installation in Schenectady, NY

    Flush valve urinals are the commercial standard. A flushometer from Sloan or Zurn delivers a measured volume from a 3/4 inch supply with no tank, and it recovers instantly for the next user, which matters in a stadium or school restroom. We set the volume to the bowl. Wrong settings stain bowls.

    Residential Urinal Plumbing in Schenectady, NY

    Home urinals use the same rules as commercial ones. The drain is usually 2 inch, the trap is built into the china, the fixture needs a vent within code distance, and the flush valve needs either a 3/4 inch supply or a lower-flow residential valve on 1/2 inch. Permit rules apply too. We check that first.

    Drainage and Venting for Urinals in Schenectady, NY

    Urinal drains clog with scale, not paper. Low-flow and waterless units leave mineral buildup in the line, so proper slope and a cleanout matter. Venting stops gurgle and odor. We tie in within code distance.

    ADA-Compliant Urinal Installation in Schenectady

    At least one urinal in a public restroom with more than one usually has to be accessible. That means a rim no higher than 17 inches above the floor, an elongated bowl projecting at least 13-1/2 inches from the wall, and a 30 by 48 inch clear floor space in front. The flush handle has to be easy to reach. We mark it all during rough-in.

    Urinal Replacement and Upgrades in Schenectady

    Cracked or stained urinals are worth replacing. Cracked china leaks eventually and heavy scale rarely comes clean, so a new bowl on the old carrier is usually best. We check the carrier bolts first. Rusted bolts get replaced.
