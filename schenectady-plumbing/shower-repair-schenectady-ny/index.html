    Hot Water Pressure Issues in Schenectady, NY

    Shower Temperature Problem Repair in Schenectady

    If your shower turns cold when someone flushes, the pressure-balance spool may be stuck.     Faulty Shower Valve Repair in Schenectady

    Problems with your shower's water temperature or pressure often point to a malfunctioning mixing valve or cartridge. These components control the hot and cold water flow.     Showerhead and Arm Repair in Schenectady

    Dripping showerheads, leaking arm connections and broken handheld hoses are frequent problems. We clean or replace showerheads, reseal shower arms and install new handheld hoses and holders.     Leaking Shower Valve Repair in Schenectady

    A shower that drips after it is turned off usually has a worn cartridge or seals. Hard water scale scratches cartridge surfaces and breaks down O-rings faster than soft water does. We replace the cartridge with a genuine part and lubricate the valve with silicone grease. This stops the drip and makes the handle turn smoothly again.

    Shower Diverter Repair in Schenectady, NY

    A diverter sends water from the tub spout to the showerhead. When it wears out, water keeps running from the spout and shower pressure drops. We replace diverter spouts, three-handle diverter stems and multi-function diverter valves. This restores full flow to the showerhead.
