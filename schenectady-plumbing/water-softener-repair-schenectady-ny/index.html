    Softener Repair vs Replacement Cost in Schenectady, NY

    Most softener repairs cost $150 to $450, while a resin replacement often runs $400 to $900 depending on tank size. A new quality softener installed typically costs $1,500 to $3,500.     Resin Bed Cleaning and Replacement in Schenectady

    Fouled resin is the usual reason a softener regenerates on schedule but still sends hard water to every faucet in the house. Iron and sediment coat the beads so they cannot grab hardness. Depending on the test, the resin gets cleaned or replaced. New resin restores full capacity.

    Softener Not Regenerating in Schenectady

    Hard water coming back usually means the softener has stopped regenerating. The usual causes are a dead timer motor, a bad board, a stuck meter or settings wiped by an outage. We test each part. After the repair, the valve is programmed for your household size and hardness so you have soft water by the next cycle.

    Brine Tank and Salt Bridge Repair in Schenectady, NY

    A crust of salt across the brine tank stops regeneration, and hard water shows up at every tap within a day or two. We break up and remove the bridge and clean the tank. Cracked brine tanks on older units get replaced. Fill the tank halfway.
