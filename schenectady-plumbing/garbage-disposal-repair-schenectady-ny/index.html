    What Goes Wrong on a Disposal, and How We Fix It in Schenectady, NY

    Emergency Disposal and Sink Backup Repair in Schenectady

    When a disposal fails, water can back into the sink and the dishwasher. With water rising or on the floor, turn off the disposal and dishwasher and call, because running them only adds water to the cabinet. We move quickly on these.

    Disposal Reset and Electrical Repair in Schenectady

    If a disposal is completely dead, the thermal overload may have tripped or the switch may be bad. The reset, breaker, wall switch and wiring all get tested, along with air switches and the magnetic lids on batch-feed units. Most are simple fixes.

    Noisy Disposal Repair in Schenectady

    Grinding, rattling or a hum that sounds rough usually mean trouble inside the unit. A loose mount will rattle. We check the mount first, then the chamber for debris, then listen to the motor with the chamber empty, because a motor that whines with nothing in it has bad bearings.

    Jammed Disposal Repair in Schenectady

    A jam usually comes from one hard object, like a peach pit, a chicken bone or a twist tie wrapped around the impeller. We free the flywheel from below with the hex key and pull the object with pliers, never a hand, then run it with water to make sure nothing else is stuck. The reset button may need a press. Repeat jams point us to the trap arm.

    Electrical Issues and Motor Repair in Schenectady

    A disposal that hums but will not grind has power but cannot turn, while one that is silent has no power at all. The two need different fixes. For a silent unit we test the breaker, reset and switch, and for a humming one we check the flywheel and motor before deciding if it is worth repairing.

    After-Hours Disposal Service in Schenectady

    A dead disposal during dinner prep is a real problem, especially if the sink is full of water. Do not try drain chemicals. Drain chemicals can eat at the seals and splash on whoever opens the trap, so leave the water where it is and give us a call.

    Leaking Disposal Fix in Schenectady, NY

    A drip at the top usually means the sink flange putty has dried out. We drop the unit, pull the flange, clean off the old putty and reset it with fresh putty and new gaskets, then hang the disposal back up. Most top leaks stop there. A leak on the side is usually the discharge gasket or a loose hose clamp.

    Jam Clearing and Motor Repair in Schenectady

    Bones, fruit pits and bottle caps wedge between the impeller lugs and the grind ring, and the motor stalls. We shut off power, insert a 1/4 inch hex key into the socket on the bottom of the unit and work it back and forth until the flywheel turns freely. Then the object comes out with pliers. A motor that stalled for a long time may have overheated, so we test it under load before calling the job done.
