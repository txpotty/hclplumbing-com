    Submersible Well Pump Repair in Schenectady

    Submersible pumps are common in residential wells, designed to operate submerged within the well casing. When these pumps fail, symptoms usually include no water, low water pressure, or strange noises from the well. Our technicians are skilled at diagnosing issues like motor burnout, damaged impellers, or faulty check valves that can affect submersible pump performance. We use specialized equipment for safe and efficient pump extraction and reinstallation, ensuring the integrity of your well system.

    Control Box and Electrical Repair in Schenectady, NY

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and relay. Coastal areas face storm surge and flooding, which can destroy water heaters and boilers installed at basement level. Lightning and power surges often damage these components.     Variable speed systems like the Grundfos SQE and Franklin SubDrive keep pressure steady by adjusting motor speed. When they fault out, the drive usually shows a code that guides diagnosis.
