    Copper Repair vs Repipe Cost in Schenectady, NY

    A single copper leak repair is affordable and fast. Patterns matter. If you have had two or more pinhole leaks in a year, the copper is likely thinning throughout the system, so repiping often beats another round of spot fixes on total cost. You get both numbers.

    Hot Water Copper Line Repair in Schenectady, NY

    Hot water lines corrode faster than cold lines. Temperature drives it. Recirculation pumps that run around the clock push water through the copper at high velocity and wear it from the inside, so we repair leaking hot lines and can add a timer or adjust the pump to cut down on wear. Less run time, less wear.

    Copper Pipe Joint Leak Repair in Schenectady, NY

    Joints leak from poor original soldering, thermal expansion or a house that settles. Every joint gets tested.
