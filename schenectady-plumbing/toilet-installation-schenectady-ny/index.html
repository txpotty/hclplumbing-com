    Standard Toilet Installation in Schenectady

    A standard floor-mount toilet is a two-hour job when the flange is sound. We shut off the stop, drain the tank and pull the old bowl. Once the old wax is scraped off, the flange gets checked for cracks, new stainless bolts go in, and the new bowl is lowered straight onto a fresh seal. The old one leaves with us.

    Two-Piece Toilet Installation in Schenectady

    Two-piece toilets come with the tank and bowl separate, joined by a rubber gasket and two or three bolts. The tank goes on before the bowl is set. Even tightening, alternating sides, matters more than force, since uneven pressure either cracks the china or leaves one bolt weeping. It should not wobble.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Installation in Schenectady, NY

    Wall-hung toilets need a wall at least 2 by 6 deep, or a furred-out chase built in front of an existing 2 by 4 wall. The floor underneath stays open. Cleaning gets easier, and the bowl height can be set anywhere within the carrier range, useful for a tall family. Service happens through the flush plate.

    Wall-Hung Carrier Systems in Schenectady

    In-wall carriers from Geberit and Grohe each mount a little differently, and the instructions matter. The frame height chart from the maker gets followed to the letter. A frame set wrong puts the bowl too low or too high, and once tile is up there is no adjusting it. Measure twice here.

    Smart Toilet Installation in Schenectady, NY

    Smart toilets with heated seats and built-in bidets need two things the old toilet did not: power and a dedicated water connection. Most need a GFCI outlet close enough for the cord. We run the water side and mount the unit, and if a new outlet is needed we bring in a licensed electrician rather than splicing anything ourselves. Then we test every button.

    Rough-In Toilet Installation in Schenectady, NY

    Rough-in is the plumbing that goes in before walls and floors close up. On a toilet, that is a vented 3 inch drain and a cold water stub.
