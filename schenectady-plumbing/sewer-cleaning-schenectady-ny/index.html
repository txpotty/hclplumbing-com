    Cast Iron Sewer Cleaning in Schenectady, NY

    Cast iron rots from the inside. New York has some of the oldest housing in the country, and many homes still have galvanized steel supply pipes and cast iron drains. The bottom of the pipe wears first. Aggressive cutters can break through thin spots, so we use descaling chains and careful jetting made for older iron, which restores flow and gives a clear view of the remaining pipe wall on camera.

    Scheduled Sewer Cleaning in Schenectady, NY

    History predicts the next backup. We set a cleaning interval. A line that has backed up before can be cleaned on a set schedule, often every year or two, so it never stops again without warning.

    Jetting a Sewer Line in Schenectady, NY

    Grease and scale are jetting work. Cables leave them behind. A jetter nozzle with rear-facing jets pulls itself down the line and blasts the buildup off the pipe wall, restoring close to the full diameter of the pipe and slowing the next clog.

    Residential Sewer Cleaning in Schenectady

    Household sewers clog slowly. It takes months of buildup to stop a main. Debris catches at a rough joint or a sag, and every load of laundry adds to it until the line closes, and we clear it with a sectional cable or a small jetter sized for the pipe.

    Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in Schenectady

    Jetting cleans the whole pipe wall. A cable only makes a path. Water at 3,000 to 4,000 PSI strips grease, scale and fine roots off the inside of the line and flushes it all toward the street, which leaves the pipe close to its original diameter.

    Root Masses in Schenectady Sewer Laterals

    Mature trees and old clay laterals do not mix. Roots find the joints. Our root cutting heads attach to the sectional cable and saw through the mass in passes, and a camera run afterward shows how much growth is left and where the roots are coming in.

    Video Inspection Before Cleaning in Schenectady, NY

    Some lines need a look first. Old pipe especially. Seeing the pipe first lets us pick the cheapest method that will work, and avoid damaging a line that is already failing.
