    Annual Sump Pump Check-ups in Saratoga Springs

    Regular annual maintenance is crucial for the longevity and reliability of your sump pump. Our technicians perform a thorough inspection, checking the motor, impeller, float switch and discharge lines for any signs of wear or blockage.     Check Valve and Discharge Line Service in Saratoga Springs, NY

    The check valve stops pumped water from falling back into the pit when the pump shuts off. A failed valve makes the pump run twice as often and wears out the motor. We test and replace check valves and inspect the discharge pipe for cracks and loose joints. We also make sure water exits away from the house so it does not soak into the soil around the slab or foundation.

    Emergency Sump Pump Diagnostics in Saratoga Springs, NY

    If your sump pump is acting up, making odd noises or not turning on, our emergency diagnostic service can quickly identify the problem.     Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Saratoga Springs, NY

    The float switch is the most frequent reason a sump pump fails to start. Tethered floats can tangle on the pump body, and vertical floats can stick from mineral crust or debris. We clean or replace the switch and set the on and off levels so the pump does not short cycle. Electronic sensor switches are an option for tight pits where floats get hung up.

    Battery Backup System Testing in Saratoga Springs, NY

    A power outage during a heavy storm can leave your primary sump pump useless. Our maintenance service includes complete testing of your battery backup sump pump system. We check the battery charge, verify the charger's function and ensure the backup pump activates when the main power is interrupted. This dual protection is vital for homes in Saratoga Springs, NY where power interruptions can coincide with severe weather.

    Crawlspace and Window Well Pumps in Saratoga Springs, NY

    Not every sump sits in a basement. Many homes with crawlspaces or deep window wells use small pumps to clear runoff.
