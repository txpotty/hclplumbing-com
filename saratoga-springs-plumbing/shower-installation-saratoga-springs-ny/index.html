    Custom Shower System Installation in Saratoga Springs, NY

    Multi-head showers depend on valve capacity.     Curbless Shower Installation in Saratoga Springs

    Curbless showers need the floor lowered in the shower area. A wood floor gets dropped joists or a recessed pan, while a slab gets cut and lowered so the floor flows straight in. A linear drain often works best. Slope and waterproofing get planned before demo.

    Walk-In Shower Installation in Saratoga Springs

    Walk-in showers depend on floor slope. Without a curb to hold water back, the floor has to pitch a quarter inch per foot toward the drain, and the waterproofing has to run well past the shower opening. We set that slope. We add blocking for grab bars while the studs are bare.
