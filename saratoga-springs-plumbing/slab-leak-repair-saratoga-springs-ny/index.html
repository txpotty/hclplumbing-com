    Slab Leak Pipe Rerouting in Saratoga Springs

    For more complex or recurring slab leaks, or when the original piping is badly deteriorated, pipe rerouting is an effective long-term solution. This method involves abandoning the leaky pipe under the slab and installing new plumbing lines through walls or the attic, bypassing the foundation entirely. This removes future slab leak risks from that particular line and is often preferred for older homes in Saratoga Springs with aging copper or galvanized pipes.

    PEX Repiping for Slab Leaks in Saratoga Springs, NY

    If multiple slab leaks indicate a systemic issue with your home's original plumbing, a full PEX repipe might be the most cost-effective and durable solution. PEX tubing is flexible, corrosion-resistant, and less prone to leaks caused by foundation shifts, making it a good choice for many homes. We can replace all the water supply lines in your Saratoga Springs home, providing a brand-new, leak-free plumbing system that will last for decades.
