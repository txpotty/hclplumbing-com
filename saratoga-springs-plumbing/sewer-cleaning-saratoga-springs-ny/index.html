    Cast Iron Sewer Cleaning in Saratoga Springs, NY

    Many older homes have cast iron drains under the slab or floor. Newer homes and remodels around Saratoga Springs typically use copper or PEX supply lines with PVC drains, which are easier to repair. Over time the inside of cast iron corrodes, scales and develops a channel along the bottom. Aggressive cutters can break thin spots, so we use descaling chains and careful jetting made for older iron. This restores flow and gives a clear view on camera of the remaining wall.     Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Lines in Saratoga Springs

    Older properties may still have vitrified clay or even Orangeburg fiber pipe running to the street. Clay joints are open invitations for roots, and Orangeburg can flatten under soil weight. We clean these lines gently and use the camera to map joints and soft spots. If the pipe is failing, we will tell you plainly and show the footage. Cleaning keeps you running while you budget for a replacement.
