    PEX Versus Copper Repipe Pricing in Saratoga Springs, NY

    PEX repipes usually cost less than copper because the material is cheaper and installation goes faster. Copper costs more. For a typical house, copper often runs 20 to 40 percent above PEX, though some buyers and homeowners like the look and feel of metal pipe and are willing to pay for it. Our quotes for either include permit, fittings, patching and cleanup.

    Drain Line Repiping in Saratoga Springs

    Drain pipe wears out too, especially old cast iron that cracks and scales up inside. Repeat clogs, sewer smells and several slow drains at once often mean the drain pipe itself is failing. We camera the lines first. Failing sections are replaced with PVC or ABS sloped and vented to code, which drains faster and resists buildup much better than the pipe it replaces.

    Commercial Repiping in Saratoga Springs, NY

    Commercial buildings in Saratoga Springs, NY often have larger pipe, risers that serve several floors and tenants who cannot lose water for a full day. We phase commercial repipes by floor, suite or riser so most of the building stays in service while we work. Night work is possible. Materials are usually Type L copper or commercial PEX, sized to the fixture count and installed to commercial code.

    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Saratoga Springs

    Kitec, an orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe with brass fittings, and gray polybutylene are both known for failures. Many homes built from the 1980s through the early 2000s have one or the other. The pipe at the water heater usually tells us which one you have, and we replace the full system so the home is easier to insure and to sell.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Saratoga Springs, NY

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside, and over decades the inside diameter closes down to a fraction of what it was. Pressure drops slowly. Brown water after the house sits overnight, weak flow at upstairs showers and leaks at threaded joints are the usual signs that galvanized is near the end. We replace it with PEX or copper. Owners usually notice stronger flow the first day.

    Urgent Repipes After Repeated Failures in Saratoga Springs, NY

    A widespread pipe failure sometimes forces a repipe sooner than you planned. We make the house safe and usable first, with temporary repairs that get you water, then schedule the full repipe as soon as the crew and permit allow. We move fast. Most emergency repipes still finish within the normal two to four day window once work starts.

    Slab Leak Repiping in Saratoga Springs

    A slab leak shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a running meter or a wet patch of carpet with no visible cause. Frost reaches deep into the ground here, so water service lines must be buried well below the surface to avoid freeze damage. Instead of breaking concrete to patch one pipe, we reroute new supply lines overhead through the attic and down interior walls, then abandon the old lines in the slab. No jackhammer needed. It ends this leak and takes the rest of the slab plumbing out of the picture.

    A repipe needs a permit, specialized tools, code knowledge and a passing pressure test. It takes skill and time.     A documented repipe removes a known risk that buyers and inspectors look for. It helps at sale time. Replacing polybutylene, Kitec or old galvanized makes a home easier to insure and easier to sell, and a permit record plus a transferable warranty gives the next owner proof the work was done.
