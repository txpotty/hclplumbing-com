    Cast iron sewer pipe lasts a long time, though not indefinitely. Over decades it scales up inside and the bottom wears through. We replace it with PVC, and where cast iron remains under the house we use a shielded no-hub coupling at the transition. Clean seal.

    Code sets the minimum slope, the cleanout spacing and the pipe type. Inspectors check all three in an open trench.     Traditional Trench Sewer Replacement in Saratoga Springs

    An open trench gives full access to the pipe from the house to the main. A small excavator does most of it, with hand digging near other lines.
