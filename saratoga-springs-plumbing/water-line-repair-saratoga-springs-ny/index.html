    Water Line Repairs We Handle in Saratoga Springs, NY

    Main Water Line Repair in Saratoga Springs

    Your main line is the pipe from the meter to the house, usually 3/4 inch or 1 inch, buried anywhere from a foot to several feet down depending on frost depth. When it fails you tend to see wet ground, a spinning meter and weak pressure at every tap. We start by closing the house shutoff and watching the dial. A moving dial with the house valve closed means an underground leak. Then a ground microphone narrows the spot, so the crew opens one small pit rather than trenching the whole run.

    After-Hours Water Line Breaks in Saratoga Springs, NY

    Water lines tend to break on a Saturday night. We answer around the clock. HCL Plumbing sends a plumber who can shut off the curb stop, pump the hole and make a permanent repair on the spot, which saves you from a weekend on a temporary patch. If the break is on the utility side of the meter, we tell you right away and help you get the provider out. That happens more than you would think.

    PEX Water Line Repair in Saratoga Springs, NY

    PEX itself rarely fails underground, but the joints and the first few feet near the house take a beating. Rats and gophers sometimes chew it. A shovel or a fence post auger can nick it, and pipe left in the sun before it was buried can get brittle. We cut out the damaged section and install new pipe with crimp, clamp or expansion fittings, matched to the PEX brand already in the ground.

    Copper Water Line Repair in Saratoga Springs

    Buried copper is tough, but it is not forever. Pinholes show up where stones press on the pipe, and acidic soil or high water velocity can thin the wall from the inside. We usually cut out two feet. The new piece goes in with soldered or pressed couplings, and we check the exposed pipe on both sides for green crust or soft spots.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Saratoga Springs

    High static pressure shortens the life of the service line and every fixture behind it. Most codes cap it at 80 psi. We gauge the pressure at a hose bib, and if it reads high we replace the pressure reducing valve with a Watts or Zurn Wilkins unit. Old gate valves that will not close get replaced with full-port ball valves. That way you can actually shut the water off.

    Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Saratoga Springs

    Pipe bursting means splitting the old line in place while the new one follows behind it. Straight runs are ideal. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe outward, and a length of HDPE trails behind it in the same path. You end up with a fresh line and two small pits, and the lawn, sidewalk and driveway in between stay where they are, which is the whole reason people pick this method over a trench.

    Water Line Repair at the Meter in Saratoga Springs, NY

    The meter box is where a lot of leaks start. Fittings in the box loosen with ground movement and traffic. Before we touch anything, we figure out whether the leak is before or after the meter, because the water provider owns the meter and everything on the street side. If it is ours to fix, we fix it. That often means new fittings and a new customer shutoff valve on the same visit.

    Main Water Line Leak Repair in Saratoga Springs

The house shutoff gets closed, the dial gets watched, and continued movement tells us the leak is in the buried line. Ground microphones find the spot. A repair coupling or a new length of pipe goes in, and then we pressure test it.
