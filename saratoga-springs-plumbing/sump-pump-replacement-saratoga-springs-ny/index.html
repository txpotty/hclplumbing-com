    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Saratoga Springs, NY

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to access for maintenance and often more durable.     High-Capacity Sump Pump Upgrades in Saratoga Springs

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Saratoga Springs

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves without binding. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats work best in larger pits.

    Combination Sump Pump System Replacement in Saratoga Springs, NY

    For maximum protection, many homeowners in Saratoga Springs choose a combination sump pump system, which pairs a primary electric pump with a battery backup pump in one unit. This keeps the system running even if the power fails or the primary pump malfunctions. HCL Plumbing can replace your existing combination system or install a new one, evaluating your needs for groundwater management. We make sure the components are properly matched for strong performance and longevity.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in Saratoga Springs

    Basement bathrooms and laundry rooms below the sewer line need sewage ejector pumps instead of sump pumps. These pumps handle solids and must be installed in sealed, vented basins. We replace ejector pumps with models rated for two inch solids. We also check the basin seal, vent and discharge check valve. Proper installation prevents sewer gas odors.
