    Yes, we stand behind the quality of our work. HCL Plumbing offers a satisfaction guarantee and specific warranties on our drain cleaning, which we review with you before starting any work. Our goal is your peace of mind.

    We usually advise against chemical drain cleaners. While they might offer a temporary fix, they can corrode pipes, especially older ones, and are often ineffective against tough clogs like tree roots or heavy grease buildup. They can also pose health risks.

    Hydro jetting is recommended for tough, persistent clogs, especially those caused by heavy grease buildup, mineral deposits, or significant tree root intrusion. It provides a more complete cleaning than traditional snaking, scouring the entire pipe interior.

    To prevent future clogs, avoid pouring grease down drains, use drain screens to catch hair and food particles, flush only toilet paper, and consider periodic professional drain maintenance, especially if you have older pipes or large trees nearby.

    Common signs include slow drains, gurgling noises from pipes, foul odors coming from drains, standing water in sinks or showers, and water backing up into other fixtures. If you notice any of these, it is time to call a professional.
