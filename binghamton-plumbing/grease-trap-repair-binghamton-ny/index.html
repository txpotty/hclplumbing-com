    Dependable Grease Trap Repair Services in Binghamton, NY

    Damaged Baffle Repair in Binghamton

    The baffles inside a grease trap are critical for separating FOGs from wastewater. If these baffles are corroded, broken, or dislodged, the trap's efficiency drops sharply, allowing grease into the sewer system. Our service includes inspecting the condition of all baffles and repairing or replacing them as needed, often using durable, corrosion-resistant materials. Proper baffle function is key to compliance with wastewater regulations in Binghamton.

    Inlet and Outlet Tee Repair in Binghamton, NY

    Tees direct flow into and out of the trap at the correct depth. Missing or broken tees allow floating grease to escape into the sewer line. We replace damaged tees with durable PVC or other approved materials. We also check that they are set at the right depth for your unit. This is one of the most common and important grease trap repairs.

    Flow Control Device Repair in Binghamton

    Hydromechanical traps rely on a flow control fitting to keep water moving at the rated speed. If this fitting is missing or clogged, the trap can be overwhelmed and grease escapes. We inspect, clean or replace flow control devices during repair visits. We also check the air intake that allows the device to work properly. Correct flow control is necessary for a trap to pass inspection.

    Preventative Grease Trap Maintenance & Repair in Binghamton, NY

    While our focus is on repair, we also emphasize preventive maintenance to reduce the need for emergency services. After a repair, we can advise on sensible cleaning schedules, proper waste disposal practices, and regular inspections to keep your grease trap in top condition. This planned approach helps extend the lifespan of your system and prevent costly breakdowns, which is especially important for businesses in Binghamton, NY facing high usage demands.

    Baffle Repair and Replacement in Binghamton

    Baffles slow incoming water so grease can rise and solids can settle. When baffles crack, corrode or break loose, grease passes straight through the trap. We remove damaged baffles and install new ones made for your trap model. In concrete interceptors, we can repair or rebuild baffle walls. Restoring baffles brings back proper separation and helps you stay compliant.
