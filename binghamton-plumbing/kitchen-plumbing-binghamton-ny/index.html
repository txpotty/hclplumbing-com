    Burst Supply Lines in Binghamton, NY

    Supply lines with plastic coupling nuts split more often than braided stainless, especially after years of pressure swings.     Kitchen Drain Clearing in Binghamton, NY

    Water rising in the second bowl when the first drains tells us the clog is past the trap, in the branch line inside the wall.     Dishwasher and Ice Maker Lines in Binghamton

    A dishwasher needs a hot supply with its own shutoff, a drain to the disposal or a wye tailpiece, and an air gap on the sink deck or a high loop fastened under the counter, depending on local code.     Grease Trap Repair in Binghamton, NY

    Rusted inlet tees, cracked lids and missing baffles all let grease pass straight through a trap. We inspect each part during cleaning and replace what has failed, usually on the same visit.     Grease Trap Installation and Cleaning in Binghamton

    Grease traps are sized by flow, and a three-compartment sink typically calls for a trap rated at 20 gallons per minute or more. We set the trap level, install the flow control fitting and vent it so it does not siphon. Cleaning schedules depend on volume.     Grease Trap Cleaning in Binghamton

    A grease trap that smells sour is normally overdue for a pump-out.
