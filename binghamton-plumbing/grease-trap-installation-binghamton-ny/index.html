    Frequently Asked Questions About Grease Trap Installation in Binghamton

    The cost of grease trap installation in Binghamton, NY varies widely based on the type and size of the trap, whether it's an indoor or outdoor unit, and the complexity of the installation. For a small under-sink hydro-mechanical unit, expect to pay between $1,500 and $4,000. Larger outdoor gravity interceptors can range from $7,000 to $20,000 or more, including excavation and concrete work.

    We install all common types of grease traps, including gravity grease interceptors, hydro-mechanical grease traps (automatic grease removal units), and compact under-sink grease traps. Our team helps you choose the best system for your commercial kitchen's specific needs and local code requirements.

    The lifespan of a grease trap depends on its type, material, and how well it's maintained. Steel grease traps may last 10 to 15 years, while durable fiberglass or polyethylene units can last 20 to 30 years or even longer. Regular cleaning and proper usage are key to extending its lifespan.

    HCL Plumbing stands behind our work with a comprehensive warranty on all grease trap installations. The specific warranty period will be outlined in your service agreement and usually covers parts and labor for a designated timeframe. Manufacturer warranties also apply to the grease trap unit itself.

    Signs you might need a new grease trap include frequent drain clogs, slow drainage in multiple fixtures, persistent foul odors coming from drains, or if your current unit is old, corroded, or no longer meeting local code requirements. An inspection by a professional plumber can confirm the need for replacement.

    We calculate flow from every connected fixture, including sinks, floor sinks and dish machines. Hydromechanical units are typically rated by gallons per minute, while gravity interceptors are sized by volume and retention time.
