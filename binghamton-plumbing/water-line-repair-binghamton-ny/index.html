    Many policies do not cover the pipe itself but may cover resulting damage to your home. Some homeowners carry separate service line coverage through their utility or insurer. We provide photos and an itemized invoice to back up your claim.

    We use only high-quality, reputable brands for all our water line repairs and installations.     Common signs of a water line leak include an unexplained increase in your water bill, damp or soggy spots in your yard (even during dry weather), a clear drop in water pressure, discolored water, or the sound of running water when no fixtures are in use.     Common signs of a broken water line include surprisingly high water bills, a sudden drop in water pressure, wet spots in your yard or on pavement that don't dry, discolored water, or the sound of running water when no fixtures are in use. For homes on slab foundations, look for cracks in the foundation or warm spots on the floor.

    We typically install Type K copper, PEX rated for direct burial or HDPE for trenchless pulls. Each holds up well in most soils when properly bedded. We will recommend the right material based on your soil, line length and budget.
