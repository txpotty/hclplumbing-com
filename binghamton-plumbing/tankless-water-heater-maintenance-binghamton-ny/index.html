    Navien Tankless Heater Tune-Ups in Binghamton

    Navien tankless water heaters often feature condensing technology, making them highly efficient but also requiring specific maintenance. Our tune-ups include checking the condensate trap for blockages, cleaning the secondary heat exchanger and verifying the operation of the circulator pump. We also confirm a proper air-fuel mixture and calibrate sensors for peak efficiency in Binghamton homes.

    Commercial Tankless Rack Maintenance in Binghamton

    Restaurants, salons and multi-family buildings often run several tankless units linked together. Commercial racks see heavy use and scale faster than home units. We descale each heater, check cascade controls and balance the load across the system. Quarterly or semiannual service keeps the hot water flowing during busy hours.

    Inlet Filter and Air Intake Cleaning in Binghamton

    Every tankless unit has an inlet water screen that catches grit and scale flakes. Those screens can clog with sand and debris, especially after municipal line work. We pull and clean the screen and inspect the air intake filter, which can load up with dust and dryer lint. Clean filters keep the flow sensor reading accurately and the burner breathing properly.

    Error Code Diagnostics in Binghamton, NY

    Codes like Rinnai LC, Navien E003 or Noritz 90 tell us a lot about what is going wrong. We read the code history, test flame rods, flow sensors and igniters and check gas pressure at the inlet. Many codes trace back to scale or a dirty filter and clear after maintenance. When a part has failed we explain the repair and the cost before doing any work.

    Heat Exchanger Descaling in Binghamton

    The heat exchanger in a tankless heater is a coil of small tubes that water races through while the burner fires. Hard water drops calcium and magnesium on those hot surfaces, narrowing the passages over time. We connect a submersible pump and hoses to the service valves and circulate descaling solution for 45 to 60 minutes. Then we flush with fresh water to rinse the system completely. Restored flow means more stable temperatures and fewer shutdowns.

    Noritz Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Binghamton, NY

    Noritz tankless heaters are known for their solid build. Our descaling service for Noritz units focuses on their intricate heat exchangers, which can be prone to scale buildup from mineral-rich water. We use manufacturer-approved descaling agents and follow precise flushing procedures to avoid damage. We also inspect the fan motor and exhaust system for proper airflow, a common area for attention in Binghamton.

    Commercial Tankless System Checks in Binghamton, NY

    Commercial tankless water heaters face higher demand and require more frequent, rigorous maintenance. Our service for businesses in Binghamton includes detailed inspections of multiple units, checking for proper gas pressure, water flow rates and exhaust venting. We look for any signs of wear on components like igniters, sensors and heat exchangers, which is vital for businesses that rely on consistent hot water, such as restaurants or salons.

    Isolation Valve Kit Installation in Binghamton, NY

    Some tankless heaters were installed without service valves, which makes proper flushing very difficult. We install a set of isolation valves with purge ports on the hot and cold lines so future maintenance takes minutes. The kit also lets you shut off water to the heater without closing the main. It is a one-time upgrade that pays for itself in easier service.
