    Bypass Valve and Drain Line Repairs in Binghamton, NY

    A bypass valve that does not seat fully lets raw water blend into the softened supply, and the hardness test is usually the first clue. The bypass gets rebuilt or swapped out and check the drain line for kinks. Code requires an air gap at the drain.

    Control Valve Diagnostics and Repair in Binghamton

    Leaks at the valve body, water running to drain around the clock, a motor that hums without moving or a display stuck on one cycle tend to mean the seal and spacer stack is worn out.     Hardness Testing at the Tap in Binghamton

    We test with a titration kit, counting drops until the sample changes color, because test strips are too rough for setting a valve. We take the raw sample from an unsoftened hose bib or with the softener on bypass. The softened sample comes from a kitchen faucet.

    Control Valve and Timer Diagnostics in Binghamton, NY

    The control valve runs every stage of regeneration, from backwash through brine draw to the final refill. We advance it manually through each cycle and watch for a motor that stalls, a drive gear that skips, a piston that drags or a drain that never shuts off. Worn pistons turn up a lot.

    Control Valve and Injector Cleaning in Binghamton, NY

    Brine draw depends on a venturi injector, a tiny nozzle and throat that pulls brine out of the salt tank as water rushes past. It does not take much debris to block it.
