    Clogged Main Sewer Line Solutions in Utica, NY

    Several drains backing up at once is a main line problem. Every drain shares that one pipe. We clear it with a sectional cable or a jetter, then camera the line to see whether the cause was roots, debris or a structural problem like a broken joint or an offset section.

    Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in Utica

    Jetting cleans the whole pipe wall. Cabling just opens a hole. Water at 3,000 to 4,000 PSI strips grease, scale and fine roots off the inside of the line and flushes it all toward the street, which leaves the pipe close to its original diameter.

    Shop and Restaurant Sewers in Utica

    Busy buildings clog more often. Volume is the reason. Our commercial setup handles bigger pipe, and we schedule around your hours so customers never notice.

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Lines in Utica

    Older properties may still have clay or Orangeburg pipe. Each fails in its own way. Clay joints invite roots, and Orangeburg, a tar-paper pipe from the mid-1900s, flattens under the weight of the soil over time, so we clean these lines carefully and map the joints and soft spots on camera.

    Main Sewer Line Clearing in Utica

    The main carries everything out of the house. A blockage there stops every drain.     Preventative Sewer Maintenance in Utica
