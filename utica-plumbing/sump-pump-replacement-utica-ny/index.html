    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Utica

    Submersible sump pumps are popular in Utica for their quiet operation and ability to sit fully submerged in the sump pit.     Key signs include the pump not turning on, unusual noises such as grinding or rattling, running continuously, visible rust or corrosion, or an age over 7 to 10 years. Any of these could point to coming failure and a need for replacement.

    It is a good idea to test your sump pump at least every three months, and especially before and after rainy seasons. To test, simply pour a bucket of water into the sump pit. The pump should activate, drain the water and then shut off automatically. If it does not, it is time for a professional inspection.

    Manufacturer warranties typically range from 3 to 5 years. We also offer a labor warranty.

    Most sump pump replacements take one to two hours. Battery backups or new discharge lines take longer.

    A primary sump pump is your main defense against basement flooding, powered by your home's electricity. A backup sump pump, usually battery-powered or water-powered, is a secondary system that kicks on if the primary pump fails, loses power or cannot keep up with heavy water inflow. Many Utica homes benefit from a backup system due to storm risks.
