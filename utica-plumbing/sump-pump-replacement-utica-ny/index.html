    Pedestal Pumps for Shallow Pits in Utica, NY

    Older basements sometimes have a narrow, shallow pit poured right into the floor, and a pedestal pump is often the only thing that fits without breaking concrete. We swap the pump, replace the float rod and stops, and run new 1.5-inch PVC to the check valve. They usually cost less than a submersible.

    Sizing a Submersible Sump Pump in Utica

    Horsepower matters less than people think. For most houses a 1/3 HP pump is enough, provided the water only has to climb 10 to 15 feet to get out. Before we choose, we read the pump curve at your real discharge height. Oversize it and it short cycles.

    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Utica

    A submersible pump sits at the bottom of the pit, sealed in an oil-filled housing, and it runs quieter than anything else you can put down there. Out comes the old pump, out comes the muck at the bottom of the basin, and the new one goes in on a brick to stay clear of silt. The check valve gets replaced at the same time. A worn valve lets water fall back and makes the pump run twice as often.

    Elevator and Garage Pit Pumps in Utica

    Elevator pits and parking garage sumps often require oil-sensing controls that stop the pump from sending hydraulic fluid or motor oil into the storm drain. We replace those pumps and controls to meet code. Two alternating pumps on one controller mean one can fail without flooding the pit. Some insurance carriers ask for test records.

    Combination Sump Pump System Replacement in Utica, NY

    Combination units put a primary AC pump and a DC backup on one discharge manifold, so you get two pumps and two floats in a single assembly. They are handy when the basin is too small for two pumps side by side. Depending on what failed, we swap one pump or the entire unit.

    Zoeller Switch and Pump Repair in Utica

    Zoeller units with a piggyback float plug the pump cord into the switch cord, and if that piggyback plug fails the pump never gets power. We test that first. Some calls only need a new switch, while others need the whole pump. We tell you which before we sell you anything.

    Pedestal Pump Service in Utica, NY

    Louder, yes, but a pedestal motor sits high and dry, so in a narrow pit it often outlasts a submersible by several years. Narrow pits favor pedestals. Worn units get replaced, and we reset the float rod stops so the cycle is right. A submersible is worth a look when the pit is wide enough.

    Sump Basin and Pit Replacement in Utica, NY

    Old clay tile or 18-inch plastic basins crack and fill with silt, and a pump in a basin that small cycles every few minutes during a heavy rain. We break out the concrete, set a larger poly basin with a sealed lid, and pour the floor back. More volume in the pit means fewer starts per hour. Fewer starts, longer motor life.
