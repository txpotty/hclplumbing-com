    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydro Jetting in Utica

    Residential hydro jetting in Utica usually costs $350 to $800, depending on line length, access and how heavy the buildup is. Commercial jetting is often priced by the hour and may run higher.

    We use professional cart and trailer jetters along with rotating and root-cutting nozzles from well-known manufacturers. Color camera systems record the line before and after.

    It is safe for most pipes in fair condition when pressure is set correctly. We camera the line first and avoid jetting pipe that is broken or collapsed.

    Hydro jetting is highly effective against a wide range of stubborn clogs that traditional snaking cannot fully clear. This includes heavy grease buildup, mineral scale, sludge, soap scum, hair clogs, and even invasive tree roots that have penetrated your sewer lines. It is designed to scour the entire pipe surface.

    Repeat clogs in the same line, slow drains after snaking, sewer odors and known grease or root problems are solid reasons to consider jetting.

    A standard residential hydro jetting service in Utica typically takes between 1 and 3 hours. This includes the initial camera inspection, the jetting process itself, and a final inspection. More extensive commercial jobs or severe main line blockages might require more time.

    For most homes in Utica, hydro jetting every 1 to 3 years can be a good preventative measure, especially if you have recurring drain issues or older pipes. Businesses, particularly restaurants, may need quarterly or twice-yearly hydro jetting to manage grease buildup. We can recommend a schedule based on your needs.

    While hydro jetting fully removes existing tree roots from your sewer line, it does not prevent new roots from growing back. For a long-term solution to recurring root intrusion, we might recommend trenchless sewer repair or pipe replacement, which we can go over during our assessment.
