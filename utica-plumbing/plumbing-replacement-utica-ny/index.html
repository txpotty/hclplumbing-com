    Water Line Replacement in Utica, NY

    A leaking main water line can lead to significant water loss, high bills and foundation damage, which is a serious concern for homes on slab foundations. Our water line replacement services address issues like corroded pipes, leaks or low water pressure. We locate the leak, excavate the area if necessary and replace the damaged section or the entire line with durable PEX or copper piping. We make sure of a secure, leak-free connection from the meter to your home, bringing back reliable water flow.

    Sewer Line Replacement in Utica

    Persistent sewer backups, foul odors or lush spots in your yard can signal a failing sewer line. Much of New York sits on glacial till, rock and clay, which makes excavation for sewer and water lines slower and more involved. We offer complete sewer line replacement using trenchless technology whenever possible, minimizing disruption to your landscaping. For more severe damage, traditional excavation is performed with careful attention. We make sure new lines are properly graded, connected and compliant with local Utica plumbing codes, preventing future blockages and costly repairs.

    Water Heater Replacement in Utica

    When your water heater is leaking, not heating or providing too little hot water, it is often more cost-effective to replace it than to repair it, especially if it is over 10 years old. We install standard tank water heaters, sized properly for your home's needs and compliant with current energy efficiency standards. Our team handles the safe removal of your old unit and the careful installation of your new one, connecting gas or electric lines and confirming correct venting and pressure relief valve operation for performance and safety.

    Water Softener Replacement in Utica, NY

    Hard water leads to mineral buildup in pipes and appliances. Water hardness in New York varies a lot, with many surface water supplies running soft and groundwater in some areas running hard. If your old water softener is no longer properly removing minerals, or if it is over 15 years old, a replacement will protect your plumbing and improve water quality. We replace all types of water softeners, including salt-based and salt-free systems, sized for your household's water usage and installed correctly on your main water line to provide consistently soft water throughout your Utica home.

    Whole-Home Pipe Replacement in Utica

    Homes with multiple pinhole leaks, galvanized pipe or polybutylene may benefit from a complete repipe. We replace supply lines with PEX or copper, often rerouting through the attic to avoid future slab leaks. Most repipes take two to four days with water restored each evening when possible. Drywall openings are patched when the work is complete.
