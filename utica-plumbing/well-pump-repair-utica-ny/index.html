    Well Pump Brands We Service in Utica, NY

    Goulds, Grundfos, Franklin Electric and Myers make most of the pumps we pull in this area, and HCL Plumbing services all of them. We work on old standard pumps and newer variable speed drives alike. Common parts are stocked. Keeping a pump that still has life left saves you the cost of a new motor and the labor of setting it.

    Control Box and Surge Repair in Utica, NY

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and a relay. Nor'easters and tropical storm remnants can overwhelm municipal sewers, so backwater valves are a sensible addition for basement homes. Lightning and power surges damage those parts more than anything else. We test capacitors, relays and overload protection, replace what failed and recommend a surge arrestor at the box if one is missing. Arrestors are cheap.

    Well Pressure Tank Repair in Utica

    Bladder tanks hold a cushion of air that lets the pump rest between cycles. When the bladder fails, the tank fills with water and the pump starts and stops every time someone opens a faucet. The bladder, the switch and the tank fittings all get checked. Proper sizing adds years to a pump.

    Grundfos Well Pump Repair in Utica

    Grundfos SQ and SQE pumps have built-in electronics that protect the motor and report faults. Most Grundfos calls involve a sensor or a drive fault. HCL Plumbing reads the fault code, checks the wiring and tests the pressure sensor before replacing anything in a Utica, NY well. Electronics fail less often than people think.

    Franklin Electric Well Pump Service in Utica, NY

    Franklin Electric motors and control boxes are in a lot of local wells. We work on Franklin motors, QD control boxes and SubDrive units regularly. We carry the common Franklin parts. A motor that tests bad on a megohm meter usually gets replaced rather than rewound, since a new motor costs less than shipping and rebuilding the old one.

    Pressure Tank Problems in Utica

    Short cycling is the classic pressure tank symptom, with the pump clicking on and off every few seconds while a faucet runs. A waterlogged tank or a ruptured bladder causes most of it. We check the precharge with the tank drained. Set it 2 psi under cut-in, which means about 38 psi for a 40/60 switch.

    Deep Well Pump Repair in Utica

    Deep wells get submersible pumps. They push water up, run quietly and last, but motors, impellers and splices still wear out. We pull the pump with the right equipment, test it and replace only what failed. Heat-shrink splice kits and a torque arrestor protect the new work in your Utica, NY well.

    Control Box and Electrical Repair in Utica, NY

    Capacitors, relays and corroded terminals inside the control box cause many pump failures. A tripped breaker is often the first sign. Licensed technicians test these parts safely with the power locked out, replace what failed and tighten every connection in the box. Wiring meets New York code when the job is done.
