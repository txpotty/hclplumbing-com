    Outdoor Faucet and Hose Bib Replacement in Utica, NY

    A split hose bib often looks fine from outside. Lake-effect snow and deep cold snaps can keep temperatures below freezing for days, so insulating exposed lines pays off. Most splits sit inside the wall, either from trapped water or plain wear on a valve that has been cranked hard for years. We check the wall cavity.     Moen Faucet Replacement in Utica

    Moen's 1225 cartridge runs a huge share of single-handle Moen faucets. A stuck 1225 makes the handle stiff and the temperature wander, and after many years in place it can be cemented in by minerals. Old cartridges can crack the body coming out. At that point a new faucet is the fix.

    Utility Sink Faucet Replacement in Utica

    Utility faucets in garages and laundry rooms take hard use. People hang buckets on the spout and yank hoses off the threads, which loosens the mounting and cracks the supply connections over time. Brass bodies hold up best here. The new faucet is anchored tight to the wall or the tub.

    Commercial Kitchen Faucet Replacement in Utica

    Commercial kitchens are hard on faucets. Pre-rinse units and hand sink faucets get swapped for T&S or Chicago Faucets models, both of which stock parts for a long time. We work around your hours. Every install meets health and accessibility code.

    Outdoor Spigot Replacement in Utica, NY
