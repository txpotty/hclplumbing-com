    PEX Water Line Replacement in Utica

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster, which matters on a long run. Scale and corrosion are not a problem with it.     Burst Main Line Response in Utica, NY

    PEX Water Line Installation in Utica, NY

    PEX bends around corners, so a typical service run needs no fittings between the meter and the house. Lake-effect snow and deep cold snaps can keep temperatures below freezing for days, so insulating exposed lines pays off. A frozen PEX line often survives where copper would crack. We still bury it below the frost depth the local code sets, and keep it covered from sunlight at the meter box, since UV light breaks it down. Short exposure only.

    PEX Service Lines for Long Runs in Utica

    PEX comes in coils up to several hundred feet. That makes it the easy pick when the meter sits out at the road and the house is set far back, since a single coil reaches all the way without a splice. Fewer joints underground means fewer places a leak can start, and it is the joints that usually fail first.

    Traditional Water Line Excavation in Utica

    Sometimes the open trench wins. A collapsed line, a route with sharp bends, or solid rock can rule out trenchless. We cut the sod and set it aside, dig to depth with a mini excavator or by hand near other utilities, lay the new pipe on a sand bed, and backfill in layers with compaction so the trench does not sink after the first good rain. Sod goes back on top.

    Water Line Materials We Install in Utica, NY

    Our trucks carry Type K copper, PEX-a and HDPE. Each one fits a different job. Copper suits short runs and high pressure, PEX handles long runs with bends, and HDPE is the usual pick for pipe pulling and directional boring, where we need a tough, fused pipe that can be dragged a long way. All of it is listed for drinking water, carrying the NSF 61 listing.
