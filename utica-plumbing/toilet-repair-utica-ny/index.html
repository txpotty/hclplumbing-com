    Clogged Toilet Solutions in Utica, NY

    Paper towels, wipes, dental floss and toys cause most of the repeat clogs we clear, and none of them break down the way toilet paper does. The closet auger usually hooks them and pulls them out.     Flush Mechanism Repair in Utica, NY

    A weak flush usually means the tank is not filling to the fill line, the chain has too much slack, or the flapper is closing before the tank empties. We bring the water up to the line stamped on the overflow tube and adjust the chain to about half an inch of slack. Handles break too. A cracked handle arm or a stripped mounting nut takes about five minutes to replace.

    After-Hours Toilet Repair in Utica, NY

    Shutting off the angle stop buys you time, but an old multi-turn stop valve can crumble or leak at the stem when it has not been moved in fifteen years. Do not force it. If it will not close, the main shutoff for the house is the backup, and we can replace the stop with a quarter-turn valve once the water is off.

    Fill Valve Replacement in Utica

    Old ballcock valves with a brass arm and a big plastic float are loud and slow to refill, and they tend to stick open when scale builds up in the ports. We swap them for an adjustable fill valve from Fluidmaster or Korky and set the height so the critical level mark sits at least an inch above the overflow. It is a twenty-minute job. You will notice the difference the first night, because the tank stops hissing.

    Older Toilet Brands We Service in Utica, NY

    Older toilets from Eljer, Briggs, Crane and Universal Rundle still turn up in homes built before the 1990s, and their flush valves often take parts nobody stocks anymore.     Commercial Toilet Repair in Utica

    Office and retail restrooms take more flushes in a day than a house sees in a month, so the parts wear faster and fail harder.     Toilet Brands We Service in Utica

    Weak Flush and Clog Repair in Utica, NY

    Needing two flushes on a regular basis usually comes down to scale in the rim jets, a clogged siphon jet, a partial blockage in the trapway, or a flush valve that does not open all the way.
