    Choosing Drain Material in Utica, NY

    PVC is the default choice under most sinks. It will not rust and costs less, while thick brass is worth it where the trap shows, like under a pedestal. Some houses use black ABS instead. We match what you have, with a proper transition where they meet.

    Utility Sink Drain Replacement in Utica

    Laundry tubs see lint and paint water. Over time that wears the thin plastic drain and trap on a utility sink until it cracks, and lint from the washer standpipe often builds up in the trap so badly that it never drains at full speed again. We fit a 1-1/2 inch trap with a cleanout. A lint trap on the washer hose helps.

    Kitchen Sink Drain Replacement in Utica

    Kitchen drains start failing at the basket strainer. Old strainer putty dries out and the locknut rusts, so water runs down the tailpiece and looks like a trap leak. Strainer and trap get swapped as a pair. Disposal sinks get a new flange instead.

    Corroded Drain Replacement in Utica, NY

    Drain cleaners speed up corrosion. Chemicals left sitting in a metal trap eat through it, so many old traps we pull have pinholes at the bottom. Plastic traps resist that damage. We recommend it for most cabinets.

    Leaking P-Trap Replacement in Utica

    Traps leak at the joints first. Slip nut washers flatten and harden with age, and a trap opened often for clogs usually has stripped threads. Trap kits are cheap. Labor is most of the bill.

    Trap Arm and Wall Drain Repair in Utica, NY

    Leaks right at the wall point to the trap arm or stub. A cracked trap arm or a stub rusted through behind the escutcheon will drip down the inside of the cabinet back panel, where you only notice it when the wood swells or the cabinet smells musty. We cut back to solid pipe. Then new PVC gets tied in.

    Commercial Lavatory Drain Replacement in Utica

    Public restroom drains take constant use. Offset grid drains and heavy traps go in, with insulated covers where accessibility rules apply. We can work after closing. Inspectors get a clean, labeled job.

    Code Updates During Drain Replacement in Utica

    Old drains sometimes break current rules. S-traps and drum traps were common decades ago, and when we replace the drain we bring it up to a vented P-trap with the right trap arm length. That can mean an air admittance valve. Local rules decide.
