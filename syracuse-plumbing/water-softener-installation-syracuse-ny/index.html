    While some homeowners attempt DIY installation, it's generally recommended to hire a professional.     We offer a warranty on our workmanship, and the water softener units themselves come with manufacturer warranties, which usually range from 5 to 10 years for the tank and control valve. We will provide you with all warranty details for your specific system after installation.

    A properly sized and installed water softener should not noticeably affect your water pressure. If you experience a noticeable drop in pressure after installation, it could point to an issue with the system or installation, which our plumbers can diagnose and resolve.
