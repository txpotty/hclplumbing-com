    Ecodyne Water Softener Installation in Syracuse

    Ecodyne softeners are sold at many home stores, and we install them for homeowners who already bought one. Install follows the manufacturer manual for bypass, drain and overflow, then we set hardness and regeneration. Bring your own unit. We set it up for you.

    Water Softener Replacement & Upgrades in Syracuse, NY

    Upgrading an old timer-based softener is one of the easiest ways to cut salt use. Newer valves meter actual water use, some connect to Wi-Fi for salt alerts and higher-capacity tanks regenerate less often for larger households. Salt bills drop. We swap it out in a few hours.

    High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in Syracuse

    High-efficiency softeners use upflow brining and metered valves to cut salt and water use. Compared with an older timer-based unit, a modern metered softener can use roughly half the salt and noticeably less regeneration water each month. That pays back over time. Several models are available.

    Whole-Home Water Softener Installation in Syracuse

    Whole-home softeners treat every gallon that enters the house, right after the main shutoff. Placing it on the main means the water heater, the dishwasher and every fixture get soft water, not just the kitchen. Sizing comes first. Your test result and water use decide the tank size.

    Water Softener Replacement Services in Syracuse, NY

    Worn-out softeners usually show it in one of two ways, hard water at the tap or a brine tank that never drops. Our crew pulls the old system, checks the loop and drain line and installs a model matched to your water today. We haul away the old one. The work is usually done in a few hours.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Syracuse

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. Instead of removing hardness, they convert it into a form that resists sticking, which reduces scale without salt or brine waste. Little upkeep is needed. They work best on moderate water.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Syracuse

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. We add a bypass valve so you can isolate the softener for service, and we install a pressure regulator if your street pressure runs above 80 psi. Soft water reaches every fixture. Outdoor taps can be left off.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Syracuse

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. Our RO installs go under the sink with their own faucet, and we can tie in the fridge or ice maker too. Pairs well with a softener. We can handle filter changes.
