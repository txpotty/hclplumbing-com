    Lining and Bursting in Syracuse, NY

    Sectional Point Repair in Syracuse

    Sometimes only one joint or a few feet of pipe is bad. A spot liner fixes that. We position a short resin-soaked sleeve over the damaged area with a camera, inflate it and let it cure, so you pay for a few feet of liner instead of the whole run. It suits isolated damage in Syracuse sewer lines.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Syracuse

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. The resin cures in place. We pull or invert the tube into the cleaned line, inflate it against the pipe wall and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light, which leaves a jointless pipe inside the old one. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Syracuse yard.

    Lateral and Spot Lining in Syracuse

    Not every pipe needs lining end to end. A few bad joints need a short fix. When damage is limited to a few joints or a short section, a sectional liner costs less than a full-length one and goes in quickly, but we confirm the rest of the line is sound by camera before recommending it.

    Pipe Bursting in Syracuse, NY

    Bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. You'll see an entry and exit pit. The head fractures the old pipe as it travels, with new HDPE trailing behind, fused into one length with no joints.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Syracuse, NY

    Main water lines can also be replaced without a trench. Bursting or boring works. Our crew pulls new HDPE or PEX through the old path with a bursting head, or drills a fresh path with a directional boring tool, so lawns, driveways and walkways stay untouched. The line is pressure tested before service is restored.

    Same-Day Sewer Assessment in Syracuse, NY

    Sewer lines that fail suddenly can often be cleared and scoped the same day. Then we schedule the fix. If a liner is a good fit, we book the repair quickly, keep the line flowing where possible in the meantime and tell you what to avoid flushing until the work is complete.

    Pipe Bursting Services in Syracuse, NY

    Bursting replaces a pipe that is too far gone to line. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe. As a winch drags the head through, the old pipe fractures into the soil and new HDPE is pulled into the same path behind it. HDPE does not rust or leak at joints in Syracuse, NY soil.

    Commercial properties have sewer and storm lines under parking lots, sidewalks and buildings. Tearing it out costs plenty. Trenchless repair avoids that demolition and the disruption to tenants and customers, and we line larger diameter pipe and multiple laterals on a schedule that limits the impact on business.
