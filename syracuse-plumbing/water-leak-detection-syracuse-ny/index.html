    Reliable Water Leak Detection Services in Syracuse, NY

    Smart Leak Detection Device Installation in Syracuse

    Smart shutoff valves like Moen Flo and Phyn monitor water flow and can shut off water on their own when they detect a leak. Small sensors placed near water heaters, sinks and washers send alerts to your phone. We install and set up these systems so you get warnings before small leaks become big ones. Some insurers offer discounts for these devices.

    Irrigation Leak Detection in Syracuse

    Drip and sprinkler systems run underground across yards and often account for hidden water loss. Cracked tubing, broken emitters and stuck valves can waste huge amounts of water. We isolate the irrigation system, test each zone and trace leaking lines. Repairs restore proper watering for your landscape.

    Slab Leak Detection in Syracuse

    Slab leaks are common in homes on shifting soil and moving foundations. These leaks occur in pipes beneath your concrete slab, often indicated by warm spots on the floor, sudden spikes in your water bill or the sound of running water when all fixtures are off. HCL Plumbing uses specialized acoustic listening devices and thermal imaging cameras to precisely locate these hidden leaks without destructive guesswork. Early detection is essential to preventing major foundation damage.

    Pool Water Leak Detection in Syracuse, NY

    Pools lose a lot of water to evaporation in summer heat, so a leak can hide behind normal loss. We use a bucket test to compare evaporation with actual water loss. Then we pressure test pool plumbing and dye test fittings, lights and skimmers. Finding the leak protects the pool and the soil around it.

    Water Heater and Softener Leak Detection in Syracuse

    Water heaters and softeners can leak at fittings, valves and drain lines. A softener stuck in regeneration can send water down the drain nonstop, and a dripping relief valve on a water heater wastes water too. We check these units carefully and repair or replace failed parts. That often explains a mystery bill in homes that have water softeners.

    Pinhole Leak Detection in Syracuse, NY

    Pinhole leaks, often found in copper or PEX piping, are small but can cause serious damage over time, especially behind walls or under floors. We use non-invasive moisture meters to detect elevated humidity levels and thermal cameras to identify cold spots that indicate water. Our plumbers are skilled at tracing these elusive leaks without tearing apart your entire home. Swift action on pinhole leaks prevents extensive drywall damage, mold growth and compromised structural integrity. Humid summers bring condensation on cold pipes and toilet tanks, which can drip onto ceilings and floors below.

    Toilet and Fixture Leak Detection in Syracuse, NY

    Toilets are one of the most common sources of wasted water. A worn flapper or a fill valve that never shuts off can leak silently around the clock. We use dye tests to find leaking toilets and check faucets, showers and supply lines for drips. These fixes are typically quick and save water right away.

    Preventative Leak Detection Audits in Syracuse, NY

    Beyond emergency services, HCL Plumbing also offers preventative leak detection audits for homeowners and businesses in Syracuse, NY. This proactive approach involves a thorough inspection of your plumbing system to identify potential weak points or very small, developing leaks before they become major problems. Regular audits can help extend the life of your plumbing, save on water bills and avoid unexpected expensive repairs, especially in older properties.
