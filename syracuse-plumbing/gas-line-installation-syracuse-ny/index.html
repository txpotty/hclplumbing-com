    New Gas Appliance Hookups in Syracuse

    Adding a new gas range, dryer or water heater needs a dedicated gas line.     Commercial Gas Line Installation in Syracuse

    Restaurants, laundromats and light industrial spaces need gas piping sized for high-demand equipment like fryers, ovens, dryers and rooftop units. We install schedule 40 black iron or approved commercial systems, add regulators where elevated pressure is supplied and coordinate inspections.     Pool and Spa Heater Gas Lines in Syracuse

    Pool heaters are among the largest gas appliances in a home, often rated at 250,000 to 400,000 BTU. They usually sit at the equipment pad far from the meter, so line size and length are critical. We may recommend a larger main line or a meter upgrade from your gas utility. A properly sized line lets the heater reach its rated output and heat the water efficiently.

    Whole-Home Gas System Installation in Syracuse, NY

    For new construction or major renovations, HCL Plumbing provides complete whole-home gas system installation. We design and install the entire network of gas lines to supply all your gas-powered appliances. This includes main line connections, branch lines and shutoff valves at each appliance, for safe, efficient distribution throughout your Syracuse property.

    CSST Gas Line Installation in Syracuse, NY

    Corrugated stainless steel tubing offers flexibility and can be installed with fewer fittings, usually reducing installation time. Our team is certified in CSST installation, including the bonding and grounding code requires. CSST is a common choice for new gas lines or extensions because it routes easily and tolerates building movement, making it a practical option for many Syracuse, NY homes.

    Gas Water Heater and Tankless Line Upgrades in Syracuse, NY

    Tankless water heaters often need up to 199,000 BTU, far more than the 40,000 BTU a typical tank heater uses. Many existing 1/2 inch lines cannot supply that demand, which causes error codes and lukewarm water. We calculate the full house load and upsize the line to 3/4 inch or larger as needed. This upgrade is one of the most important steps in a successful tankless conversion.

    Gas Range and Cooktop Line Installation in Syracuse

    Switching from an electric range to gas is one of the most frequent reasons homeowners call us. We run a dedicated branch from the nearest adequate supply, install a shutoff valve behind the range and connect with a new approved flexible connector. Professional-style ranges can need 60,000 BTU or more, so we confirm the branch and main can handle the load. The appliance is then checked for proper burner flame and ignition.

    Flexible Gas Line (CSST) Installation in Syracuse, NY

    Corrugated stainless steel tubing offers a flexible, efficient option for gas line installation. HCL Plumbing is proficient in installing CSST, which can simplify routing and reduce the number of fittings compared to traditional black iron pipe. We ensure CSST is properly bonded and grounded, as New York code requires, to protect against lightning and electrical surges in your Syracuse home.
