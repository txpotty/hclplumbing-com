    Choosing Drain Material in Syracuse, NY

    PVC is the default choice under most sinks. It does not rust, it stands up to drain cleaners, and it costs less than brass, while polished 17-gauge brass makes sense under a pedestal sink or any trap you can see. ABS works the same way. We match what you have, with a proper transition where they meet.

    Kitchen Drain and Disposal Tie-In in Syracuse

    Disposals change the kitchen drain. The outlet on the disposal has to sit above the trap inlet, the dishwasher hose needs a high loop or an air gap, and the trap has to line up without the slip joints pulling sideways. They all get set together. Then the disposal runs with water while we watch.

    Pop-Up Drain Assembly Replacement in Syracuse, NY

    A stopper that drops on its own has a worn pivot. When the pivot ball wears and the nut stops sealing, the stopper drops or sticks and the nut drips. A new pop-up fixes all of it. Takes about an hour.

    Leaking Drain Assembly Replacement in Syracuse

    Water on the cabinet floor needs tracking, not guessing. We dry the drain, fill the bowl and pull the stopper, then watch the strainer and every slip joint with a dry towel until the drip shows itself. Supply leaks look similar. We check those too.

    P-Trap Replacement in Syracuse

    Thin chrome traps are the ones we replace most.     Utility and Basement Sink Drain Replacement in Syracuse

    Basement sinks often drain to a pump. Sinks below the sewer line drain through a pump, so we check its valve and discharge line during the job. Pump trouble is easy to miss. A gurgling pit is a clue.
