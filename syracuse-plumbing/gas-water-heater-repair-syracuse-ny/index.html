    Flammable Vapor Sensor Repair in Syracuse

    Since about 2003, most residential gas tanks have been built with a flammable vapor ignition resistant combustion chamber. Air enters through a flame arrestor screen at the base that also keeps flame inside. When lint, dust or pet hair coats that screen, the burner starves, the chamber overheats and a thermal switch shuts the whole heater down, sometimes several times a week. We clean the screen and the chamber. A tripped thermal switch gets replaced.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Repair in Syracuse

    Plan on $150 to $700 for most repairs in Syracuse. Pilot and thermocouple work runs at the low end of that. Parts drive the price. Gas control valves and power vent parts sit at the high end, and if the tank is old enough that the repair price starts to approach half of a replacement, we will put both numbers in front of you. We start only after you approve.

    A weak thermocouple is the usual cause. A plugged pilot orifice can also make the flame too small to heat it. On vapor-resistant tanks, a lint-clogged flame arrestor can starve the burner and trip the thermal switch, which looks like a pilot outage from the outside. Garage drafts can snuff out an older pilot.

    Eight to twelve years is typical for a gas tank. Tanks that get flushed and get a new anode can run past that. A heater near the end of that range that needs a gas valve, a flue repair and a relief valve all at once is usually a better candidate for replacement than for another round of parts. Check the serial number for the build date.

    A thermocouple is a small copper-sheathed rod that sits in the pilot flame and makes a tiny electric current. The current keeps a magnet in the gas valve pulled open. No flame, no current. When the pilot goes out, the magnet lets go and the valve shuts off gas to both the pilot and the burner, which is the safety that keeps raw gas from filling a closet. Weak thermocouples cause more pilot outages than anything else.

    Following the label to relight a pilot is fine. Swapping gas valves, thermocouples or vent sections should be left to a licensed plumber. A bad gas joint or a vent that is put back slightly wrong can leak fuel or carbon monoxide into the house, and many manufacturer warranties are void after unlicensed gas work. Codes in Syracuse, NY generally require a licensed plumber for gas work.

    Labor on every repair carries our own warranty, and parts carry the manufacturer's coverage. Gas valves and thermocouples typically come with one year or more from the maker. The terms are written on your invoice. If the same part fails inside the warranty window, we come back, replace it and do not charge for the labor at your Syracuse, NY home.

    Same-day repair is the goal on most calls. Thermocouples, pilot assemblies and thermostats are stocked, so those jobs rarely need a return trip. When a heater needs a brand-specific gas valve or a power vent blower, the supply house may need a day to get it, and we will leave the heater safe in the meantime. We give you a time for the return visit.
