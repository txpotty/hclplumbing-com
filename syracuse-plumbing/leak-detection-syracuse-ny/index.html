    Underground Water Line Leak Detection in Syracuse, NY

    The service line from the meter to the house can leak for months without surfacing, especially in old galvanized pipe. Green grass in a dry spell is a clue. We trace the line with an electronic locator, then use a ground microphone to hear the leak, switching to tracer gas on plastic lines that do not carry sound.

    Pool Leak Detection in Syracuse

    A pool losing more than a quarter inch a day beyond evaporation likely has a leak somewhere. We start with a bucket test. Then we pressure test the suction and return lines, listen for leaks with a hydrophone underwater, and dye test around the skimmer, lights and main drain to find the exact crack or fitting that is losing water.

    Commercial Leak Detection in Syracuse

    In Syracuse, large buildings have long supply runs where a leak can hide behind finished walls for a long time. Zones get tested one at a time. Our techs isolate risers, mains and fire lines with pressure gauges, then locate leaks with acoustic and thermal tools, scheduling around your business so customers never see us.

    Slab Leak Signs in Syracuse, NY

    Warm floor spots, cracked tile and a meter that never stops are the common signs. So is the sound of water with everything off. We isolate hot and cold lines with pressure tests, then trace and listen through the slab, and once it is located we go over spot repair, reroute and repipe options with prices for each.

    Sewer Line Leak Detection in Syracuse, NY

    A cracked drain line leaks wastewater into the soil, which can show up as a sewer smell in the yard, a soggy patch or slow drains. We use a camera for these. Our tech runs a camera from the cleanout to the city tap, recording cracks, offset joints and root entry in PVC, clay and cast iron, so you see the exact distance and depth of the break.

    Gas Line Leak Locating in Syracuse

    Gas leaks are found by test, not by nose. Our plumbers pressure test the system with a gauge, then check each joint with a detector and soap solution. We also walk the yard line, since a dead strip of grass over a buried gas pipe often marks a leak that has been there a while. We coordinate with the gas utility.

    Leak Testing for Multifamily and Offices in Syracuse

    Multifamily buildings and office parks have long supply systems where leaks hide for a long time. We isolate each unit or floor, test it and trace leaks with the same acoustic and thermal tools we use in single-family houses. Every unit gets its own report. Catching leaks early cuts tenant complaints and insurance claims across the building.

    Irrigation Leak Detection in Syracuse, NY

    Sprinkler laterals crack and zone valves stick open, and either one can waste thousands of gallons before the bill shows it. Check the yard first. Our tech tests the irrigation system separately from the house, runs each zone and traces buried lines, since sprinkler leaks are the first thing most water departments suggest checking.
