    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in Syracuse

    A bursting head is a steel cone, slightly wider than the old pipe, pulled through the line by a hydraulic unit or a winch cable. It cracks the old pipe outward into the soil while a string of new HDPE pipe follows right behind it. Fused joints, no gaps. This is the fix for collapsed pipe.

    Commercial Trenchless Sewer Lining in Syracuse, NY

    Office and retail buildings often have a cast iron building drain under the slab that has rotted along the bottom. Lining it avoids cutting the floor. Work gets scheduled around tenant hours, nights included. A typical commercial lining job runs one to three days, depending on footage and how many branch connections need to be reinstated with a cutter.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Prep and Lining in Syracuse

    Prep is most of the job. We jet the line at high pressure to strip roots, grease and loose scale, then camera it again to measure the exact footage before cutting the liner tube to length. Skip that step and the liner can wrinkle or fail to bond. Lining works best on cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their round shape.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Upgrades in Syracuse

    Bursting can upsize the line. A 4-inch clay lateral can be replaced with 6-inch HDPE, which helps on lines that clog because they were undersized from the start or have been added onto with extra bathrooms. Each end only needs a pit around three by four feet. No trench in between.
