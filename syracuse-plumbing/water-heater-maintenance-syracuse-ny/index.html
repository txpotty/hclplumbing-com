    Tankless Water Heater Service in Syracuse, NY

    Tankless units need descaling. Mineral scale coats the inside of the heat exchanger, restricting flow and making the unit work harder, and eventually it starts throwing error codes or cutting out in the middle of a shower. A pump circulates descaler through it. We also check the igniter, the fan and the exhaust vent for safe operation.

    Tank Water Heater Maintenance in Syracuse

    Flushing is the heart of tank maintenance. Water hardness in New York varies a lot, with many surface water supplies running soft and groundwater in some areas running hard. We run a hose from the drain valve, open it up and let the tank drain until the water runs clear, then refill and check the anode rod, the relief valve and every connection for drips or corrosion. Plan on an hour. Less sediment means more years.

    Gas Burner and Venting Checks in Syracuse

    A blue flame is what we want to see. Poor flames mean dirty burners, so we clean them, test the sensor and check the draft. Bad venting is dangerous. The gas line and shutoff get leak tested.

    Gas Water Heater Safety Checks in Syracuse, NY

    Gas units need a clean burner and a good draft. We check for gas leaks at the valve and connector, watch the flame, clean the burner and confirm the flue pulls exhaust up and out instead of spilling it into the room. Carbon monoxide is the concern. The thermocouple or flame sensor gets checked as well.

    Attic, Closet and Garage Units in Syracuse

    Location changes the risks. Attic units need a working drain pan and drain line so a leak does not come through the ceiling, and garage gas units often need to be raised off the floor or protected from vehicles under local code. We check the pan and drain. Access platforms and venting get looked at too.

    Tank Flush and Inspection in Syracuse

    Sediment forms from minerals in the water. We flush the tank, test the relief valve, check the anode and look over each fitting. Efficiency comes back. Recovery speeds up.

    Descaling Tankless Units in Syracuse

    Error codes often mean scale. Scale in the exchanger cuts flow and trips the unit's safety shutoff. A pump kit handles it. Most makers recommend descaling at least yearly with hard water.
