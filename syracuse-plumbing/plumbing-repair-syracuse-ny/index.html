    What We Fix in Syracuse, NY Homes

    Well Pump Service in Syracuse, NY

    Submersible pumps sit well below the water line, so diagnosis happens at the surface first. HCL Plumbing checks the pressure switch, the control box capacitor and the tank precharge, then measures amp draw on the motor. A pump drawing high amps is wearing out. Jet pumps are simpler, and a lost prime or a bad foot valve is the usual cause when one stops moving water.

    Water Leak Repair in Syracuse

    Hidden leaks sit inside walls, under sink cabinets and in the buried yard line that runs between the meter and the house. We narrow it down with a moisture meter and a thermal camera. We open just enough drywall or dirt to make the repair. Fixes include pinholed copper, failed PEX crimps, leaking hose bibs and worn shutoff valves.

    Well Pump Repair in Syracuse, NY

    No water at the tap on a well system narrows the list quickly. It is usually the pressure switch, the capacitor in the control box, the pump motor or a waterlogged pressure tank. Voltage and amp readings at the control box tell us whether the pump needs to come up. A submersible pull takes two techs and a truck.

    Slab Leak Repair for Syracuse, NY Homes

    Much of New York sits on glacial till, rock and clay, which makes excavation for sewer and water lines slower and harder. Lines under a slab take the brunt of that movement, and New York homeowners often find out from a warm floor or a spinning meter. HCL Plumbing locates the leak electronically before any concrete is cut. From there you choose between opening the floor at one spot or rerouting the line overhead. Reroutes skip the jackhammer.

    Drain Repairs in Syracuse

    Slow drains that come back right after a snake usually mean the pipe itself is damaged. Cracked cast iron or a sagging run will hold debris every time. We run a camera to find the spot, then replace that section or hydro-jet the line if buildup is the only problem. Grease is the usual buildup.

    Gas Leak Repair in Syracuse

    Rotten-egg odor near the range or a hiss at the meter means get out of the house first. Phone the gas company from the yard, then call us to fix it. We track the leak with a combustible gas detector and bubble solution, then replace whatever failed and pressure test the line. No shortcuts on gas.

    Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Syracuse, NY

    Pinhole leaks in copper begin inside the pipe, where water chemistry and high velocity wear through the wall. Homeowners around Syracuse with hard water often add a softener to protect water heaters and keep scale out of faucets. One pinhole usually means others are coming in the same run. We solder in a new section or use a press fitting, and we check the house pressure while we are there. Above 80 psi, add a regulator.

    Tank and Tankless Water Heater Repair in Syracuse

    Tank and tankless heaters fail in different ways. HCL Plumbing repairs both, including the major brands, gas and electric. Tank calls usually come down to the thermocouple, the gas valve, an element or the anode. Tankless trouble is usually scale in the heat exchanger or a dirty inlet screen, and a descaling flush fixes more of them than new parts do.
