    Tightening a slip nut or replacing a plastic trap is fair game. Hand snakes are fine too. Chemical cleaners can make things worse.

    We advise against them. Most only open a small hole. Harsh cleaners can soften old glue joints, eat at metal drain parts and sit in the trap if they do not clear the clog, leaving caustic liquid in the pipe for the plumber to deal with. Mechanical cleaning is safer.

    Small fixture drain repairs usually do not. Larger jobs often do. Replacing drain stacks, relocating drains or adding new lines may require one depending on the scope and your local rules. We handle permits when they apply.

    Cleaning removes a blockage from a sound pipe. Repair fixes the pipe. A crack, a sag, a broken fitting or a missing vent will keep causing clogs no matter how often the line gets cleaned, so a drain that clogs repeatedly usually needs repair rather than more snaking. We check on video.

    Unclogging a drain does not need one. Larger work often does. Replacing a stack, relocating a drain or replacing a section of the building drain in Syracuse may require a plumbing permit, depending on local rules and how much of the system is being changed. We pull it when one applies.
