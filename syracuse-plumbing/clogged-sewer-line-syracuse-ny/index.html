    Sewer Camera Inspection in Syracuse

    A sewer camera is a waterproof head on a push rod with a locator transmitter built in. It shows us everything. Cracks, offsets, sags, roots and heavy scale all show up on the screen, and the transmitter lets us mark the exact spot and depth on the ground above it. It saves guessing where to dig. You get a copy of the footage.

    Identifying and Repairing Damaged Pipes in Syracuse

    A clog that keeps coming back usually means broken pipe. Cleaning will not fix that. Our camera finds the crack, offset or collapse, and the locator marks where it is and how deep, so a spot repair or a trenchless liner can be priced accurately. HCL Plumbing gives you options from a single dig to full replacement.

    Hydro-Jetting Services in Syracuse, NY

    Jetting sends water at thousands of psi through a nozzle that sprays backward and drives itself down the pipe. It scrubs the whole wall. A cable punches a hole through a grease plug, and a jetter clears the coating that starts the next clog. We camera the pipe first. Badly cracked pipe gets a gentler approach.

    Residential Clogged Sewer Lines in Syracuse

    Most home sewer clogs come from three things: roots, grease and wipes. Often all three together. Grease coats the pipe, wipes catch on it and roots lock everything into a plug. We clear it with a sewer machine, then check on camera to see the main cause.

    Tree Root Sewer Clogs in Syracuse

    Clay and older cast iron sewer pipe have a joint every few feet. Roots love those joints. Roots from trees and shrubs push into the gaps and fill the pipe, then we cut them out and camera the line for damage. Ongoing root problems may call for a treatment schedule or lining.

    Grease and Wipes Sewer Blockages in Syracuse, NY

    Flushable wipes do not break down the way toilet paper does. They stay intact for weeks. Mixed with grease that hardened in the main, they form a dense plug that a cable can poke a hole through but not fully remove from the pipe. Jetting scours the walls and flushes the debris downstream. Toss wipes in the trash.

    Grease and Sludge Buildup Removal in Syracuse, NY

    Kitchen grease cools in the sewer line and sticks to the walls. Then it grows. Soap scum and food bind to it until the opening shrinks to a fraction of the pipe, and at that point even normal use backs the line up into the lowest fixture in the house. HCL Plumbing jets it out with a grease nozzle in Syracuse, NY. The camera confirms it is gone.
