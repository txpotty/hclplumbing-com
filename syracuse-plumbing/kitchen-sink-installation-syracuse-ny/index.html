    Double Bowl Sink and Continuous Waste in Syracuse

    Same-Day Sink Replacement in Syracuse, NY

    Undermount Sink Installation in Syracuse, NY

    Undermounts hang from clips anchored into the underside of the stone, with silicone run around the rim.
