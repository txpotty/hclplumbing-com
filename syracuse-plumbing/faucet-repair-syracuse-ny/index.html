    Common Faucet Repairs in Syracuse, NY

    Pull-Down Kitchen Faucet Repair in Syracuse

    Pull-down kitchen faucets wear at the hose, the spray head and the docking magnet or counterweight that pulls the hose back into the spout. The hose usually splits near the quick-connect fitting. Brand-matched hoses, heads and weights go in, then check for drips under the sink where the hose connects.

    Faucet Handle Repair and Replacement in Syracuse, NY

    Stripped handle splines, cracked handle bodies, worn adapters and loose set screws make a faucet hard to turn or impossible to shut tight. Handles are often sold separately. We match the handle to the stem or cartridge and the finish, and if the stem itself is stripped we replace it at the same time.

    Ball Faucet Repair in Syracuse, NY

    Ball faucets have a single lever over a domed cap, and the drip usually comes from the two small rubber seats and springs that press against the ball. A seats and springs kit fixes most of them. Loose handles happen too. Tightening the adjusting ring under the cap usually stops a handle leak.

    Low Water Flow Faucet Repair in Syracuse

    Sputtering flow, where the water spits and surges, often means air or debris in the line after a water main repair, or a partly clogged cartridge screen. A flush of the lines usually fixes it. On pull-down faucets, the check valves in the hose can stick too, and we clean or replace those.

    Dripping Faucet Solutions in Syracuse

    Every drip has a source, and on a faucet that has dripped for years there is usually more than one worn part. We replace what failed and check the rest. Each valve type, from compression to ceramic disc, wears in its own way, so the repair is matched to the valve inside yours.

    Cartridge Faucet Repair in Syracuse, NY

    Single-handle faucets from Moen, Delta, Kohler and Pfister mostly use replaceable cartridges, and a worn one shows up as a drip, a stiff handle or water that will not hold a steady temperature. The part number depends on the brand and model. We match it. Swapping a common cartridge like the Moen 1225 takes about half an hour, cleanup included.

    Commercial Faucet Repair in Syracuse, NY

    Restaurant and office faucets cycle hundreds of times a day, so cartridges, handle springs, metering valves and sensor solenoids wear out far faster than they do in a house. Commercial cartridges are on the truck. Metering faucets get timed so they run long enough to rinse without wasting water, and we can work after hours.

    Low Water Pressure Faucet Fix in Syracuse, NY

    Weak flow at one faucet usually points to a clogged aerator or a cartridge packed with scale, not a problem with the house pressure. Homeowners around Syracuse with hard water often add a softener to protect water heaters and keep scale out of faucets. We pull the aerator with a key and clean or replace it, then check the cartridge screens. Low flow everywhere is a different problem.
