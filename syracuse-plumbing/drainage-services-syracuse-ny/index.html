    Most homes never need scheduled drain cleaning. Clean them when they slow down. Homes with root-prone sewers, old cast iron, or a kitchen that sends a lot of grease down the drain do better with a cleaning every 12 to 24 months, timed before the line slows to a crawl.

    Roots grow toward the water and nutrients inside sewer pipe. A hairline crack is enough. Once a root tip gets in through a cracked joint or loose seal, it grows into a mass that catches paper and grease, and clay and older concrete pipe with many joints are the most vulnerable.
