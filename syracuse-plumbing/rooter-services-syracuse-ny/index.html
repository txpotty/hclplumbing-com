    Sewer Root Removal in Syracuse

    Roots cause more main sewer clogs than anything else on older streets. They enter through tiny gaps in clay and cast iron joints and grow into thick mats that catch everything flowing past. We cut them back with a root saw on a heavy cable. Most of these lines need cutting every year or two.

    Video Camera Drain Inspection in Syracuse

    A camera on a push rod shows the inside of the pipe in real time. For a line that keeps plugging, it ends the guesswork fast. We can see roots at a joint, a dropped toy, a cracked hub or a belly where water sits and drops its solids. You can watch the screen alongside the tech.

    Clog Prevention & Maintenance in Syracuse, NY

    Grease belongs in a can, not the sink. Even wipes labeled flushable hold together long enough to snag in a sewer. HCL Plumbing can recommend strainers, a cleanout where one is missing, and a rooting schedule for your Syracuse, NY home based on what we pulled out last time. Small habits make a big difference.

    Floor Drain Rooter in Syracuse

    Basement and laundry floor drains in Syracuse fill with lint, sand and sump discharge grit. A plugged floor drain turns a small leak into standing water. We clear them with a 1/2 inch cable and test the trap primer where one is installed. Most of the time it is washer lint.

    Hydro-Jetting Services in Syracuse, NY

    Jetting puts water through a nozzle at 3,000 to 4,000 psi and scrubs the pipe wall clean. Grease, scale and fine roots that a cable rides over come off with the water. Water hardness in New York varies a lot, with many surface water supplies running soft and groundwater in some areas running mineral-heavy. The pipe gets a camera check first. Pressure that high can damage cracked clay or thin cast iron, so we jet only after we have looked at the pipe.

    Main Sewer Line Rooter in Syracuse

    Water coming up in the shower when the washer drains is a main line problem. The usual approach is a 3/4 inch cable and root saw run from the outside cleanout toward the city tap. A camera follows if the cable hits something hard. That tells us whether it was roots, a sag holding sludge or a broken section, and it settles the question of digging.

    Main Line Rooter Service Through Cleanouts in Syracuse, NY

    A cleanout is a capped pipe that gives us a straight shot into the main. From the cleanout we run cable all the way to the city tap, which clears the full length of the line. Homes without one can have a cleanout added. We flow test with running water when the cable comes out.

    Tree Root Removal in Syracuse

Lining or replacing the bad section is the permanent fix, and we will tell you if your line is a candidate.
