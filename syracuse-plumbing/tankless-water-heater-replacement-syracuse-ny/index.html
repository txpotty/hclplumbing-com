    Tankless-to-Tankless Replacement in Syracuse

    Replacing an existing tankless with a new one is usually the simpler job, since the gas line, vent path and water connections are already there. We confirm they are sized right for the new unit. Corroded service valves get swapped while we are there.

    Gas Line Testing on Tankless Installs in Syracuse, NY

    Every gas connection on a tankless gets a soap test and a manometer reading at full fire before we leave the house. A line that holds pressure at rest can still sag under full load. We test both.

    Leaking Tankless Replacement in Syracuse

    A leaking tankless can drip inside the cabinet for weeks before anyone notices the puddle. Close the cold isolation valve and the gas cock first. Then we look at whether the leak is a fitting or the heat exchanger itself, because one is a $20 fix and the other means a new unit.

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Syracuse, NY

    Pulling a 50 gallon tank out of a garage frees up floor space and ends standby heat loss. The catch is gas, since most tankless units need a 3/4-inch line or bigger plus new venting. Before quoting, we check the meter and pipe run, and afterward we cap the old flue and seal the roof. Plan on a full day.

    Commercial Tankless Racks in Syracuse, NY

    Restaurants and salons run tankless racks hard. Four units together can pull over 600,000 BTU, which means checking gas pressure with all of them running. Installs can happen after closing.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Syracuse, NY

    Commercial tankless setups in restaurants and offices often use two or more units on a manifold, with a controller that splits the load. We replace failed units without shutting down the rest. Health code sets the hot water temperature at the hand sinks and dish areas.

    Point-of-Use Electric Tankless in Syracuse

    Point-of-use electric tankless heaters under a sink are a different animal from whole-house units. A small under-sink unit needs one 20 or 30 amp circuit and suits a distant bath or break room. Most take under two hours.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Syracuse

    A gas tankless unit at full fire can pull 199,000 BTU, roughly five times what a 40 gallon tank burner uses. Most need at least a 3/4-inch line. We check meter capacity and pipe length against the manufacturer's sizing chart, then confirm pressure with the unit running. An undersized line causes ignition faults.
