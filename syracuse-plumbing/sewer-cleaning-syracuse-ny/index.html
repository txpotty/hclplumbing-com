    Jetting a Sewer Line in Syracuse, NY

    Grease and scale are jetting work. A cable only scratches them. The rear jets drive the hose forward while the spray scours the wall, so the line ends up much closer to its original size.

    Sewer Camera Inspections in Syracuse, NY

    On a repeat backup, the camera goes in first. Guessing costs money. A waterproof color camera on a push rod shows the inside of the line live on a screen, so we can see the clog, measure its distance from the cleanout and spot any crack or sag.

    Dealing with Grease Buildup in Syracuse, NY

    Grease hardens as it cools. Then it narrows the pipe. Kitchen grease coats the sewer line the same way plaque coats an artery, and over a few years the opening can close to the size of a finger, which is when drains start to back up, and jetting is the right fix.

    Clogged Main Sewer Line Solutions in Syracuse, NY

    Several drains backing up at once is a main line problem. Every drain shares that one pipe. After the line is open, a camera run tells us if the blockage was debris or something structural that will keep catching waste.

    Grease and Scale Buildup Cleaning in Syracuse, NY

    Grease bonds with mineral scale into a hard crust. Private wells serve a large share of rural homes, and they can carry iron, sulfur or hardness that stains fixtures and clogs filters. Over years the opening shrinks. A cable can punch a hole through the crust, but a jetter cleans the walls back to full size and leaves less for new grease to grab onto, and the method depends on pipe material and condition.

    Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in Syracuse

    Jetting cleans the whole pipe wall. A cable only makes a path. Water at 3,000 to 4,000 PSI strips grease, scale and fine roots off the inside of the line and flushes it all toward the street, which leaves the pipe close to its original diameter.
