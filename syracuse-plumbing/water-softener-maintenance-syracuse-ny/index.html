    Control Valve and Injector Cleaning in Syracuse, NY

    Brine draw depends on a venturi injector, a tiny nozzle and throat that pulls brine out of the salt tank as water rushes past. It does not take much debris to block it.     Resin Bed Checks and Regeneration Settings in Syracuse

    Resin does the actual softening. The beads swap sodium for calcium and magnesium, and once they are loaded up they need brine to recharge. We compare the softened hardness against the gallons used since the last regeneration to see whether the bed still holds its rated capacity.

    Yearly Water Softener Tune-Ups in Syracuse

    A tune-up starts with two hardness readings and ends with a full manual regeneration that we watch from start to finish. Between those, the salt level, brine well and float all get a look. Figure on an hour for a typical unit.

    Salt Bridge Removal and Brine Tank Cleaning in Syracuse, NY

    Humidity and cheap salt cause most salt bridges. The top layer fuses into a crust with an empty pocket below it. The softener keeps regenerating on schedule, but it draws plain water instead of brine, so the resin never recharges and hard water flows to every tap.
