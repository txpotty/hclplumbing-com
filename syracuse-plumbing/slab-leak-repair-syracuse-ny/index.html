    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Syracuse, NY

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat expands and contracts the pipe against concrete and gravel. Mineral scale and constant recirculation can wear the pipe wall faster. We repair the leak or reroute the hot line, and we can adjust recirculation pumps to limit wear. Warm floors and higher gas or electric bills are common signs.

    Slab Leak Repair for PEX and Copper Lines in Syracuse, NY

    Whether your home in Syracuse, NY has modern PEX piping or traditional copper lines, our plumbers are proficient in repairing both. Pinhole leaks are common in older copper due to corrosive soil conditions and water chemistry, while PEX can sometimes suffer from installation issues or rodent damage. We carry a full range of parts and tools for both materials, making for a correct and durable repair no matter your pipe type.

    PEX Repiping for Slab Leaks in Syracuse, NY

    If multiple slab leaks point to a systemic issue with your home's original plumbing, a full PEX repipe might be the most cost-effective and durable solution. PEX tubing is flexible, corrosion-resistant, and less prone to leaks caused by foundation shifts, making it a strong choice for many homes. We can replace all the water supply lines in your Syracuse home, providing a brand-new, leak-free plumbing system that will last for decades.

    Direct Slab Penetration Repair in Syracuse, NY

    When a slab leak is directly accessible and localized, direct slab penetration is often the most efficient repair method. This involves carefully cutting a small section of your concrete slab to access and repair or replace the damaged pipe section. Our team makes sure the repair is fully compliant with New York plumbing codes and the slab is properly restored, preventing future issues. We take real care to contain dust and debris during this process in your Syracuse home.

    Electronic Slab Leak Detection in Syracuse
