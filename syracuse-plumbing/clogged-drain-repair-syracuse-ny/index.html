    Kitchen Sink Clog Repair in Syracuse

    Kitchen sinks clog from grease that cools and hardens on the pipe wall. Then food scraps and soap pile on. We pull the trap, run a cable through the trap arm and down the branch line to the main stack, and check the disposal for a jam or worn impeller. A heavy grease line may need a quick jet afterward.

    Bathroom Sink & Shower Clog Repair in Syracuse, NY

    Shower drains slow down a little each week as hair collects on the crossbars. One day the water just sits. We pull the strainer, use a small drum auger to pull the mass out, then clear the trap behind it. We can fit a hair screen for you.

    Main Sewer Line Clog Clearing in Syracuse, NY

    Several drains backing up at once usually means the clog is in the main. It needs a bigger machine. We find the cleanout, relieve the pressure slowly and then run a large cable with a cutting head. A camera afterward shows what caused it.

    Laundry and Utility Drain Clogs in Syracuse, NY

    A washing machine dumps up to 20 gallons in a couple of minutes. Fibers and lint go with the water. Over months that lint packs the standpipe and the branch line behind it, which is why a clogged laundry drain usually overflows at the standpipe in the middle of a cycle. A lint filter on the hose helps. Some laundry lines share the kitchen branch, so we check both.

    Preventative Drain Maintenance in Syracuse
