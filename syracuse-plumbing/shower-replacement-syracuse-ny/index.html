    Replacing Showers Damaged by Hard Water in Syracuse, NY

    Water hardness in New York varies a lot, with many surface water supplies running soft and groundwater in some areas running hard. Scale shows up first as a weak showerhead and a stiff handle.     Custom Tile Shower Replacement in Syracuse, NY

    Custom tile replacement starts with careful demolition.     Leaking Shower Pan Replacement in Syracuse, NY

    Leaking pans soak the subfloor and the ceiling below. Whether it is a prefab base with a crack or a tiled pan with a failed liner, we tear it out, replace any rotted subfloor and install a new pan that gets flood-tested for a full day. We replace the drain as well. It ends the cycle.

    Walk-In Shower Conversion in Syracuse, NY

    Walk-in conversions are one of the most requested bathroom jobs we do. We relocate the drain to fit the new pan, raise the valve to shower height and set the floor slope so water stays in the shower instead of running out the entry. We add blocking for grab bars. ADA layouts are available.
