    What Our Tankless Techs Fix in Syracuse, NY

    Flow Sensor and Thermistor Repair in Syracuse

    Tankless heaters start when the flow sensor sees about half a gallon a minute, and they hold temperature off thermistor readings. A dirty inlet screen or a failing sensor shows up as no hot water, temperature swings or a unit that drops out mid-shower. Our tech cleans the screen, checks sensor values in the service menu and tests thermistor resistance against the chart. Usually under an hour.

    Condensing Tankless Repair in Syracuse, NY

    Condensing tankless heaters pull extra heat out of the exhaust and make acidic condensate that has to drain somewhere. That condensate runs through a trap and, where code or the manual calls for it, a neutralizer filled with limestone or marble chips before it reaches the drain. A plugged trap will lock the heater out. We clean the trap, refill the neutralizer and check the PVC vent for sag or the wrong slope.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Repair in Syracuse

    Navien condensing units use a secondary stainless exchanger to recover heat from the flue gas. That makes condensate, and a plugged trap or a full neutralizer will shut the heater down with a drain or flue error. On NPE-A models we also test the built-in recirculation pump, after clearing the trap and checking vent pitch. Then we fire it at low and high flow.

    Emergency Tankless Water Heater Repair in Syracuse, NY

    A tankless heater that dies on a cold morning in Syracuse is an emergency for a family of five. We take no-hot-water calls first, and the truck carries the parts that fail most: flame rods, igniters, flow sensors, thermistors and inlet filters. If a heat exchanger is leaking, we shut the water off at the service valves and order the part the same day. Control boards take longer.

    Tankless Flush & Scale Removal in Syracuse

    Lukewarm water at high flow and a unit that runs loud are two early signs of scale. Well-done installs usually include isolation valves with purge ports for this, and if yours does not, we can add them during the visit so future flushing takes less time. White vinegar will do in a pinch. Commercial descaler works faster.

    Rinnai Error Code Repair in Syracuse

    Rinnai owners usually call about a code 10, which points to an air supply or exhaust blockage. Bird nests and dryer lint at the terminal come first, then pull the front cover to inspect the intake screen and the combustion fan. A code 10 can also come from a vent run that is too long for the model. We measure.

    No Hot Water Tankless Repair in Syracuse

    No hot water at all usually points to ignition, gas supply or a heat exchanger choked with scale. We start with the error history in the service menu, then test the igniter, flame rod and gas pressure in that order, because a weak flame signal and low pressure can look identical on the display. Venting gets checked too. Common igniters and flame rods ride on the truck.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Repair in Syracuse

    Restaurants and laundromats in Syracuse often run two to six tankless units on a common manifold. One unit down means the others work harder and scale faster, so we check the cascade controller, the isolation valves on each heater and the water quality coming in. Downtime costs money. We schedule around service hours when we can.

    Tankless Water Heater Repair Near Me in Syracuse, NY

    Tankless units in Syracuse hang on outside walls or get tucked into garages, and every location brings its own problems. Outdoor units deal with wind-blown debris in the intake. A closet install can run short of combustion air when the louvers get blocked. We check for both.

    Water hardness in New York varies a lot, with many surface water supplies running soft and groundwater in some areas running mineral-heavy. We see how that scale treats each brand's heat exchanger, and we set descaling intervals around your water instead of a generic calendar. Softened homes can often stretch to two years.
