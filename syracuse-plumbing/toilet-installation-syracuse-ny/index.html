    Kinds of Toilet Installation in Syracuse, NY

    New Construction Toilet Installation in Syracuse

    On new builds we set toilets after the finish floor is down, never before. A flange belongs flush with or just above finished flooring. Set it too early and the tile setter has to cut around it, which leaves the flange low and forces a thick seal or an extender later on. Each bowl gets a fresh seal.

    Toilet Flange and Wax Ring Replacement in Syracuse, NY

    The closet flange is the ring that ties the toilet to the drain, and it is the weak point on most old installs. On cast iron, the bolt slots rust out first. Plastic flanges crack from overtightened bolts, and a stainless repair ring screwed over the old one usually gives a solid anchor again. Seal goes on last.

    Commercial Restroom Toilets in Syracuse, NY

    Restaurants and offices get heavy-duty fixtures and accessible stalls built to code. Downtime costs a business money. We schedule commercial installs early in the morning or after hours, and we stage the fixtures and valves on site so each stall is down for as short a time as possible. We work with your GC on inspections.

    Smart Toilet Installation in Syracuse, NY

    Smart toilets with heated seats and built-in bidets need two things the old toilet did not: power and a dedicated water connection. Most require a GFCI outlet within reach of the cord. We run the water side and mount the unit, and if a new outlet is needed we bring in a licensed electrician rather than splicing anything ourselves. Then we test every button.

    Smart Toilet and Bidet Seat Installation in Syracuse

    Bidet seats like the TOTO Washlet and Bio Bidet bolt onto most existing elongated bowls. A T-valve splits water off the fill line. Heated seats with a warm-air dryer need a nearby GFCI outlet, and when there is none we bring in an electrician first. Non-electric seats take about thirty minutes.

    ADA-Compliant Toilet Installation in Syracuse

    ADA toilets put the seat 17 to 19 inches off the floor, and the centerline sits 16 to 18 inches from the side wall. It is set that way so a grab bar fits. We measure the existing drain before ordering, since moving a drain two inches to hit the ADA centerline means opening the floor. Elongated bowls are standard here.

    Comfort Height Toilet Installation in Syracuse, NY

    Comfort height bowls stand about two inches taller than standard ones. Consider who uses the bathroom first. Shorter adults and kids sometimes find the taller seat leaves their feet dangling, while anyone with bad knees or a hip replacement usually finds it much easier to sit and stand. Installation is the same as a standard bowl.

    Rough-In Toilet Installation in Syracuse, NY

    Rough-in is the plumbing that goes in before walls and floors close up. For a toilet that means a 3 inch drain with a vent, plus a cold supply stub. We set the drain 12 inches from the framed wall plus the thickness of drywall, so the finished rough-in lands right where the fixture expects it. Then the inspector sees it open.

A permit is usually needed to relocate a toilet or add a bath, but a straight swap in the same spot usually goes without one. We tell you which applies.
