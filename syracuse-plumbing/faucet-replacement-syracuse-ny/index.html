    Kitchen Faucet Replacement in Syracuse

    Kitchen faucets usually fail at the hose first. The pull-down hose wears where it bends over the spout neck, and once it splits, water runs down the inside of the faucet and into the cabinet without anyone seeing it. Sometimes a new hose is enough. When the body or cartridge is worn too, we swap the whole faucet and put new supplies on.

    Corroded Faucet Replacement in Syracuse

    Green crust at the base means the faucet body is corroding. Once the brass under the finish is pitted, cartridges stop sealing against it, and every repair drips again within a few weeks. Replacement is the only fix. We pull it, even with seized nuts, and set a new faucet with a ceramic disc cartridge.

    Bathroom Faucet Replacement in Syracuse, NY

    Old lav faucets rarely come off clean. Lav faucet nuts hide behind the bowl in a tight gap, and after a couple of decades they tend to fuse to the threaded shank. A basin wrench or a cutter gets them off. The pop-up drain gets replaced too, because its old gasket has usually cracked by now.

    Water-Saving Faucet Upgrades in Syracuse

    Federal rules cap new kitchen and lav faucets at 2.2 gallons per minute. WaterSense bathroom faucets drop to 1.5 gallons per minute, and the aerated stream feels about the same in your hands. The savings show on the bill. A WaterSense lav faucet is an easy swap.

    Commercial Kitchen Faucet Replacement in Syracuse
