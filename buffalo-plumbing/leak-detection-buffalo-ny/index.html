    PEX and Copper Leak Detection in Buffalo, NY

    On a ground microphone, copper leaks are easy to hear. PEX is quieter, since plastic pipe does not carry sound well, so we lean more on thermal scans, moisture readings and tracer gas when the house is plumbed in it. We handle both every week. Pinholes in copper and failed crimp fittings in PEX top the list of what we find.

    Gas Line Leak Locating in Buffalo

    You cannot locate a gas leak by smell alone. Our plumbers pressure test the system with a gauge, then check each joint with a detector and soap solution. We also walk the yard line, since a dead strip of grass over a buried gas pipe often marks a leak that has been there a while. We coordinate with the gas utility.

    Underground Water Line Leak Detection in Buffalo, NY

    The buried line from your meter to the house can leak for months without surfacing, especially in old galvanized pipe. Green grass in a dry spell is a clue. We trace the line with an electronic locator, then use a ground microphone to hear the leak, and on plastic pipe we inject tracer gas and sniff for it at the surface.

    Fast Leak Response in Buffalo

    A sudden drop in pressure or water spreading across a floor needs someone fast. Close the main. Our techs arrive with detection gear and repair parts, so we can find the leak and often fix it in a single visit, then test the system to confirm nothing else is losing water in your Buffalo home.

    Sewer Line Leak Detection in Buffalo, NY

    A cracked drain line leaks wastewater into the soil, which can show up as a sewer smell in the yard, a soggy patch or slow drains. We use a camera for these. The camera goes in at the cleanout and runs to the main, recording cracks, offset joints and root entry in PVC, clay and cast iron, with a locator giving the exact depth and distance.

We pressure test the hot and cold sides separately, then use a ground microphone to hear the leak through the concrete and confirm with a thermal camera on hot water lines.
