    Commercial Kitchen Sink Installation in Buffalo, NY

    Three-compartment sinks, prep sinks and hand sinks each have their own health code rules.     Disposal and Dishwasher Hookup with Sink Installation in Buffalo, NY

    Most new kitchen sinks get paired with a disposal and a dishwasher. The disposal gets mounted, the dishwasher drain goes through an air gap or high loop, and the dishwasher gets its own hot line and shutoff. One visit costs less than three. We test it all the way you will use it.

    Kitchen Sink Installation Cost Factors in Buffalo, NY

    Undermount and farmhouse installs cost more than a drop-in swap. Adding a disposal, moving the drain, lowering a wall stub or replacing old galvanized stubs all add labor, and we list each one separately on the quote. The countertop is priced separately.

    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in Buffalo

    An undermount depends on its clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. The bowl is mounted, the rim sealed with silicone and the sink braced while the adhesive sets, normally overnight before it can take a full bowl of water. Strainers and traps go in after.

    Double Bowl Sink and Continuous Waste in Buffalo

    A double bowl drains both sides through one trap, using either a center outlet continuous waste or an end outlet tee. The layout depends on your disposal and dishwasher, because the disposal side changes where the tee sits and how much slope each bowl gets. We use heavy-wall fittings.

    Workstation Sink Installation in Buffalo, NY

    Workstation sinks have a ledge for cutting boards, colanders and drying racks, and most are single deep bowls with an offset drain. We plan the trap around that offset drain so the disposal fits and the cabinet keeps usable space on one side. Faucet reach matters too.

    Apron-Front Sinks in Buffalo, NY

    Apron sinks need a cabinet cut to fit the front panel, and the sink has to be in place before the counter is templated. We set the sink and frame early, so the fabricator measures from the actual bowl instead of a spec sheet. It prevents a crooked reveal. Cast iron versions can top 150 pounds, so we bring two people.

    New Construction Sink Rough-In in Buffalo, NY

    The rough-in decides where the drain and valves end up long before the cabinets arrive. We usually set the kitchen drain stub around 16 to 20 inches off the floor, then adjust for deep bowls or a disposal so the trap does not end up below the wall outlet. Venting happens at this stage. We work from your sink and faucet specs to avoid moving pipe later.
