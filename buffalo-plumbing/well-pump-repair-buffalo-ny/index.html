    Pressure Switch Replacement in Buffalo

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, clogged sensing tubes and insect damage are common causes of failure. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank. This is one of the most affordable well repairs.

    Well Pump Electrical Troubleshooting in Buffalo, NY

    Many well pump issues stem from electrical problems, including tripped circuit breakers, faulty wiring, or a malfunctioning control box. These can prevent the pump from starting or cause it to run erratically. Our licensed plumbers are also skilled in electrical diagnostics, safely testing circuits, relays, and capacitors to identify and repair electrical faults. We ensure all wiring and connections meet New York electrical codes, providing a safe and reliable power supply to your well pump.

    Diagnosing Low Water Pressure in Buffalo

    Low water pressure from a well system can be a frustrating problem, often indicating issues beyond just the pump itself. This could be due to a failing pressure switch, a partially clogged well screen, a leak in the supply line, or even a pump that is no longer meeting demand. Our team conducts careful diagnostics to pinpoint the exact cause of low pressure in your Buffalo home, delivering a targeted and effective repair solution.

    Constant Pressure System Repair in Buffalo, NY

    Variable speed systems like the Grundfos SQE and Franklin SubDrive keep pressure steady by adjusting motor speed. When they fault out, the drive usually shows a code that guides diagnosis. We troubleshoot drives, sensors and wiring and repair or replace components. These systems need a smaller pressure tank but correct setup.

    Pressure Tank Repair in Buffalo, NY

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes the pump short cycle, turning on and off every few seconds. That rapid cycling burns out pumps prematurely. We check the air charge, inspect the bladder and recharge or replace the tank. Correct tank sizing protects the pump and gives steadier pressure at the tap.

    Well Pressure Tank Repair in Buffalo

    The pressure tank works with your well pump to maintain consistent water pressure and reduce pump cycling. Issues like a waterlogged tank, sudden pressure drops, or continuous pump operation usually point to a faulty pressure tank. We inspect the air bladder, pressure switch, and tank connections to identify the problem. In Buffalo, we repair or replace tanks to improve your well system's performance and extend pump life.
