    Commercial Restroom Fixture Repair in Buffalo, NY

    Restroom calls are the most common commercial work we do. A flushometer that runs, a urinal that will not stop, or a sensor faucet that drips are usually fixed with a diaphragm kit, a solenoid or a new battery pack. We stock those. Wall-hung toilets that keep clogging or rocking get a carrier check, since a loose bowl can leak inside the wall. Low pressure on one fixture usually points to a clogged supply stop.

    Backflow Testing and Certification in Buffalo

    Each backflow test takes about 30 minutes per assembly and requires a short water shutoff to that line. Plan for a brief outage. Our tester opens each test cock in order, records the check valve and relief valve readings on a calibrated gauge and compares them against the pass limits printed on the test form. Passing devices get tagged. The provider gets the report from us, and we keep a copy for your next test.

    Commercial Restroom Plumbing in Buffalo

    A broken flushometer in a busy restroom gets noticed by every customer who walks in. Sloan and Zurn flush valves, sensor faucets, wall-hung toilets and carriers are routine work for us. We also handle ADA upgrades. That includes setting fixtures at the right heights, adding blocking for grab bars and wrapping exposed drain lines under lavatories.

    Commercial Leak Detection in Buffalo, NY

    Water bills that jump 30 percent with no change in business usually mean a hidden leak or a stuck fixture. Start with the meter. Zone-by-zone shutoffs and meter readings narrow it down, then acoustic sensors, thermal imaging and tracer gas pinpoint the spot. Running toilets count too. One stuck flush valve can waste several thousand gallons a week.

    Slab Leak Detection for Businesses in Buffalo

    A slab leak under a store or office floor often shows up as a warm spot, a running water meter with everything off, or a crack in floor tile. Much of New York sits on glacial till, rock and clay, which makes excavation for sewer and water lines slower and more involved. We confirm it with a meter test and pressure test, then use acoustic listening gear and thermal imaging to pinpoint the leak within a foot or two. Then we decide. A spot repair through the floor makes sense for some, and an overhead reroute makes more sense for others. We price both so you can compare.

    Commercial Water Heater Service in Buffalo, NY

    Big tank heaters fail in a few common ways: a failed gas valve, a cracked flue baffle, a stuck T and P valve or a tank full of scale. Each sounds different. We check draft and combustion, test water temperature at the far end of the building and flush the tank, then tell you whether repair or replacement makes more sense. Tankless racks get descaled. Recirculation pumps on long runs get tested as part of the visit.

    Sewer Camera Inspection for Businesses in Buffalo

    A camera inspection shows the inside of a drain line on a monitor and records it so you can share it with a landlord or contractor. Guesswork goes away. We push the camera from a cleanout, note the footage at every break, root mass or sag, and use a locator to mark depth and position on the ground above. The video is yours to keep. That footage helps you budget repairs and explain them to owners or a board.
