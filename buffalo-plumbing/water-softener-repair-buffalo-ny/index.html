    Water Softener Problems We Fix in Buffalo, NY

    Emergency Water Softener Repair in Buffalo

    A softener stuck in regeneration can run water to the drain for hours or flood a utility room. We handle these calls quickly. The crew in Buffalo shuts the unit into bypass first, then finds out whether the valve, the timer or the drain line caused it. Most of the time it is a stuck piston or a failed motor that can be replaced on the same visit.

    Resin Testing and Replacement in Buffalo

    Resin beads trap hardness minerals and release them during regeneration. Treated city water slowly breaks resin down into mush. We test resin capacity, then clean it with resin cleaner or replace it, and a solid tank and valve with fresh resin works like new for years. Mushy resin can clog the valve.

    Water Softener Leak Detection & Repair in Buffalo, NY

    Cracked tanks, loose fittings and failed O-rings are behind most softener leaks. Even a slow drip wastes water and soaks the floor. Once we find the source, from the bypass to the drain connection, the failed seal or fitting gets replaced. We test it under pressure.

    Softener Repair vs Replacement Cost in Buffalo, NY

    Most softener repairs cost $150 to $450, while a resin replacement often runs $400 to $900 depending on tank size. A new quality softener installed typically costs $1,500 to $3,500. With a good tank and valve, repair is the better value. An obsolete valve with scarce parts tips the decision toward a new head or a new unit.

    Control Valve Rebuild in Buffalo

    Service, backwash, brine and rinse are the four cycles the control valve directs every time the softener regenerates, usually in the middle of the night. Hard water and chlorine wear out the seals, spacers and pistons. Common signs are leaks at the head or constant drain flow. A matching rebuild kit fixes it.

    Water Softener Leak Repair in Buffalo, NY

    Drips around fittings waste water. Our plumbers find the source, whether seals, O-rings, hoses or internal parts. We replace the failed part with silicone-lubricated O-rings rated for potable water in your Buffalo, NY home and test it under line pressure.

    Brine Tank and Salt Bridge Repair in Buffalo, NY

    A crust of salt across the brine tank stops regeneration, and hard water shows up at every tap within a day or two. We break up and remove the bridge and clean the tank. Older brine tanks can crack, and we repair or replace those too. Fill the tank halfway.

    Resin Bed Problems in Buffalo

    Fouled resin is the usual reason a softener regenerates on schedule but still sends hard water to every faucet in the house. Iron and sediment coat the beads so they cannot grab hardness. Depending on the test, the resin gets cleaned or replaced. Fresh resin brings capacity back.
