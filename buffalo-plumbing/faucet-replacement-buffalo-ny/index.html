    Bathroom Faucet Replacement in Buffalo, NY

    Crusty Bathroom Faucet Replacement in Buffalo, NY

    Crusty lav faucets are the most common swap we do. We pull the old centerset or widespread faucet, scrape the deck down to bare porcelain and install a new faucet with a matching pop-up drain so the finishes line up. It takes about an hour. The handles get set to close at the same angle.

    Pull-Down Kitchen Faucet Replacement in Buffalo

    Pull-down spray heads take a beating. After a few years, the hose wears thin, the spray button sticks and the head will not dock the way it did. We replace the faucet as a unit. We clip the new hose weight at the right height and connect fresh braided supplies to both stops.
