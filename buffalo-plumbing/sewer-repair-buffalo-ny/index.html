    Sewer Pipe Relining in Buffalo, NY

    Sewer pipe relining is a trenchless repair technique where a new, durable pipe is created within the existing damaged one. A flexible, epoxy-impregnated liner is inserted into the old pipe and inflated, curing in place to form a seamless, strong and corrosion-resistant new pipe. This method is well suited for addressing cracks, minor breaks and root intrusion without major digging. It extends the life of your sewer system and is an great option for preserving landscapes in Buffalo.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Buffalo

    When trenchless repair isn't feasible due to severe damage or specific pipe configurations, traditional dig-and-replace methods are necessary. This involves excavating a trench to access and replace the damaged section of the sewer line.     Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Buffalo

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful cleaning method that uses high-pressure water streams to clear stubborn blockages, grease buildup and even tree roots from sewer lines. It's far more effective than snaking for thorough cleaning and stopping recurring clogs. This service is especially helpful for restaurants and commercial properties in Buffalo dealing with heavy grease accumulation, as well as homes with recurring drain issues.

    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Buffalo

    Clay and cast iron sewer pipe crack from soil movement, heavy vehicles and simple age. A cracked section lets sewage leak into the soil and lets dirt fall into the pipe, which leads to recurring clogs. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded couplings rated for the transition. The repair is tested before we backfill so you know it holds.

    Root Intrusion Removal in Buffalo, NY

    Tree roots are a common cause of sewer line blockages and damage, especially with the mature trees found in many Buffalo neighborhoods. Roots can work into tiny cracks, expand and eventually crush or clog pipes. We use specialized augers and hydro-jetting to cut through and remove roots, followed by inspection to assess pipe integrity. For ongoing issues, we might recommend trenchless repair or pipe replacement to prevent future root problems.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Buffalo, NY

We cut the roots with a mechanical cutter or hydro-jetter, then repair or seal the joint so they cannot come right back. For lines with many rooted joints, we will show you when lining or replacement makes more sense than ongoing cleaning.
