    What Sewer Repair Covers in Buffalo, NY

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Buffalo, NY

    Relining builds a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line with a jetter first. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy is pulled or inverted into place, inflated against the old wall and left to cure, which seals cracks and root entry points in a single continuous tube. The finished pipe loses a little diameter. For most houses the flow difference is negligible.

    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Buffalo

    Clay and cast iron crack from settling soil, tree roots and vehicles parked over the line. Dirt falls in through the crack. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded stainless couplings rated for the transition between materials, then test the repair with water and video before closing the hole. Most of these are one-day jobs.

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Buffalo

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. Snaking just opens a path. That makes jetting the better choice for grease, scale and fine root growth, and it is the standard prep step before any pipe lining job. Brittle or broken pipe is a different story. We camera first to make sure the pipe can take the pressure.

    Sewer Repair for Commercial Properties in Buffalo, NY

    Commercial laterals run six inches or larger and carry far more flow than a house. Downtime costs money. For restaurants, strip centers and apartment buildings in Buffalo we schedule repairs after hours where we can, set up temporary bypass pumping when the line has to stay in service, and give managers a clear window to share with tenants. Grease trap outlet lines are one of our most common commercial repairs.

    Emergency Sewer Repair in Buffalo

    Raw sewage on a basement floor is not a next-week problem. Our phone is answered after hours. The first goal on an emergency call is to get the line flowing, usually with a cable or jetter, and then camera it to find out whether you have a clog or a structural failure that will back up again tomorrow. Sometimes the permanent fix waits for daylight. Until then, we tell you which fixtures are safe to use.

    Restaurant and Retail Sewer Repair in Buffalo

    Restaurants, retail centers and multifamily buildings load a lateral far beyond a single house. Grease is the usual culprit. We jet the line, camera it, and repair the weak section with options that limit how long you are closed, such as overnight work or temporary bypass pumps that keep the building running. We also install two-way cleanouts for future maintenance.

    Pipe Burst & Collapse Repair in Buffalo, NY

    A collapsed sewer pipe stops all flow at the point of failure. Every drain backs up. Collapses usually come from corroded cast iron, crushed clay under a driveway or tar-paper pipe that has finally flattened, and the camera tells us exactly where the pipe gave way and how deep it sits. The broken length comes out and new pipe goes in. Lining cannot fix a full collapse.

    Commercial Sewer Repair in Buffalo

We repair larger diameter lines, add two-way cleanouts so future maintenance is easy, and provide inspection video that property managers can hand to owners or insurers.     Frequently Asked Questions About Sewer Repair in Buffalo

    Most jobs fall between $500 for a small spot repair and about $3,000 for a trenchless section. Replacing the whole line is a bigger number.     Trenchless repair fixes the pipe through one or two small access pits. No long trench. Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE pipe through the old one while splitting the old pipe apart, and cured-in-place lining inserts a resin-soaked tube that hardens into a new pipe wall inside the old one, which leaves concrete and landscaping alone. We check on camera whether yours does.

    Flush only toilet paper. Flushable wipes are not.     Multiple drains backing up at once is the big one. The tub often shows it first. Water rising in the shower when the washer drains, a sewage smell near the cleanout, and wet or very green patches over the line are all signs the main lateral is blocked, cracked or separated somewhere between the house and the street.     Clay, cast iron, ABS, PVC and Orangeburg all come up in Buffalo and around New York. We work on all five. Joining new PVC to old clay or cast iron takes a shielded transition coupling sized to the outside diameter of each pipe, and getting that fitting right is what keeps the repair from leaking at the joint later.     A rental auger can open a soft clog. Structural damage stays. Forcing a cable through a broken or offset joint can snag, tear the pipe further or leave a cutter head stuck underground, and then someone has to dig to get the cable out. Repeat clogs deserve a video inspection.

    We use Schedule 40 PVC or ABS that meets local code. Transitions get shielded couplings.
