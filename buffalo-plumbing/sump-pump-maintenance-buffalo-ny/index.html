    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Maintenance in Buffalo

    A standard sump pump inspection and cleaning usually runs $125 to $250. Replacing a float switch or check valve typically adds $75 to $250 depending on parts.

    HCL Plumbing services all major brands of sump pumps common in Buffalo homes, including Zoeller, Wayne, Little Giant, Liberty Pumps and Flotec. Our technicians are trained on the specifics of many models, providing expert care regardless of your pump's manufacturer.

    Routine maintenance typically does not require a permit. However, if a significant repair involves altering the discharge line or replacing the entire unit, especially if electrical work is involved, a local permit might be necessary. We take care of all permit requirements for you.

    Watch for a pump that runs constantly, hums without moving water, cycles very often or makes grinding noises. Rust, a stuck float or a musty smell around the pit are also red flag signs.

    If your primary sump pump fails during a power outage and you don't have a working battery backup or water-powered backup system, your basement is at risk of flooding. This underscores the importance of maintaining backup systems.

    We recommend having your sump pump professionally maintained at least once a year. If your home is in an area prone to heavy rainfall or has a high water table, twice-yearly maintenance might be more worthwhile. Regular checks ensure it's ready for peak seasons.

    If your basement is finished or storms often knock out your power, a backup is a wise choice. We install and maintain battery and water-powered backup systems.

    Most local codes do not allow sump discharge into the sanitary sewer. We route discharge to the yard, a dry well or another code-approved location.
