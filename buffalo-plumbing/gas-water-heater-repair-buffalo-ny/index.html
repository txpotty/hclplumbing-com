    Pilot Light and Ignition System Repair in Buffalo

    A frequent culprit for no hot water in gas heaters is a faulty pilot light or ignition system. Our technicians are skilled at diagnosing issues like a dirty pilot tube, thermocouple failure or a bad igniter. We carry common replacement parts on our service vehicles to ensure a quick fix and restore your hot water flow promptly.

    Pilot Light Repair in Buffalo

    A pilot that will not light or will not stay lit is the most common gas water heater call. Causes include a worn thermocouple, a clogged pilot orifice, a bad piezo igniter or a tripped thermal cutoff. We test the thermocouple output with a meter and clean or replace the pilot assembly. Most pilot repairs are completed in about an hour.

    Emergency Gas Water Heater Repair in Buffalo

    When your gas water heater suddenly stops working, especially on a busy morning, it is an emergency. HCL Plumbing offers prompt emergency repair services for gas water heaters in Buffalo.     Tank Leaks and Pressure Problems in Buffalo

    Even gas water heaters can develop tank leaks, often due to corrosion from hard water or high water pressure. Homeowners around Buffalo with hard water often add a softener to protect water heaters and keep mineral buildup out of faucets. While some leaks might require a replacement, minor leaks or pressure relief valve issues can often be repaired. Our team assesses the extent of the damage, inspects the anode rod and addresses pressure imbalances to prevent further problems in your Buffalo home.

    Gas Control Valve Replacement in Buffalo, NY

    The gas control valve regulates temperature and shuts off gas if the pilot goes out. Newer valves from Honeywell and White-Rodgers use status lights that blink codes when something fails. If the valve itself has failed, we replace it with an exact match or approved universal model. We leak test and verify temperature control afterward.

    Flammable Vapor Sensor Repair in Buffalo

    Most gas water heaters built in recent decades have a flammable vapor ignition resistant design. A sensor or flame arrestor screen shuts the burner down if it senses vapors or overheating. Lint and dust in garages can clog the arrestor and shut down the system. We clean the combustion chamber and replace sensors or thermal switches as needed.
