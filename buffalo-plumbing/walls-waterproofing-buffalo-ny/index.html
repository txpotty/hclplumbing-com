    Frequently Asked Questions About Walls Waterproofing in Buffalo

    The cost of walls waterproofing in Buffalo, NY can vary a great deal based on the method used, the extent of the damage, and the size of your foundation. Basic crack sealing might range from $300 to $800 per crack, while a full exterior foundation waterproofing project could be $8,000 to $20,000 or more. Interior drainage systems with a sump pump typically fall between $4,000 and $12,000. We provide transparent, itemized quotes after a thorough inspection.

    Damp proofing is a less robust solution, typically a spray-on asphalt coating that resists soil moisture but isn't designed to withstand hydrostatic pressure. Waterproofing uses stronger membranes, coatings, or drainage systems specifically engineered to prevent liquid water from entering, even under pressure. For basements or crawl spaces in areas with high water tables or heavy rainfall, waterproofing is the right choice.

    The duration of a walls waterproofing project varies significantly. Smaller jobs like interior crack sealing might take a day or two, while extensive exterior waterproofing with excavation and drainage system installation could take several days to over a week. We'll provide a clear timeline during your consultation.

    For extensive walls waterproofing projects involving excavation, structural changes, or major changes to drainage systems, a permit from the city of Buffalo may be required. Simple crack repairs typically do not need a permit. HCL Plumbing will help you determine if a permit is necessary for your project and handle the application process if needed.

    While minor DIY fixes like patching small cracks are possible, full walls waterproofing is best left to professionals. It involves specialized knowledge of drainage, foundation mechanics, and proper material application. Improper DIY work can lead to poor results, wasted money, and worse water damage over time.

    Look for bubbling paint, soft drywall, stained baseboards and musty odors. White crust on block or stucco walls is another sign of moisture moving through. Loose tiles or cracked grout in a shower also suggest water is getting behind.

    A shower wall waterproofing project typically takes two to four days, including drying time between coats. Exterior block sealing can often be completed in a day. Larger foundation jobs may take a few days.
