    Deciding between repair and full replacement depends on the extent of the damage.     PEX Water Line Replacement in Buffalo

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a popular and budget-friendly choice for water line replacement in Buffalo. It's flexible, resistant to freezing, and less prone to scaling and corrosion than some traditional materials. Its flexibility allows for easier installation, often requiring fewer connections, which reduces the potential for leaks. PEX is also a good option for homes with aggressive soil conditions, offering a reliable alternative to metal pipes.

    Copper Service Line Installation in Buffalo, NY

    Type K copper is a tough option for underground water lines. It resists punctures and lasts for decades. Copper costs more than PEX but is favored by some homeowners. We install copper with proper bedding to protect it. We can also install sleeves where it passes through foundations.

    Polybutylene Water Line Replacement in Buffalo, NY

    Polybutylene pipe was used in some homes built in the 1980s and 1990s. It can become brittle and fail without warning. Many insurers list it as a risk. We replace polybutylene service lines with modern materials. This reduces the chance of sudden leaks.

    Galvanized Main Line Replacement in Buffalo

    Galvanized steel pipe was common in homes built before the 1970s. Over time, it corrodes inside and narrows the pipe. This causes low pressure and rusty water. We replace galvanized lines with modern PEX or copper. The difference in water pressure is often noticeable right away.

    PEX Water Line Installation in Buffalo, NY

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping is a modern, flexible, and very popular choice for water line replacement. It resists corrosion, scaling, and chlorine, making it highly durable for most water conditions. Lake-effect snow and deep cold snaps can keep temperatures below freezing for days, so insulating exposed lines is worth it. PEX can expand and contract without bursting, which is a significant advantage when a cold snap hits Buffalo. Its flexibility allows for fewer fittings and quicker installation.
