    From Tank to Drain Field: Septic Repair in Buffalo, NY

    Drain Field Line Jetting in Buffalo

    Distribution laterals clog from the inside with solids and slime, especially after years without pumping. We jet each lateral from the distribution box or a cleanout at the far end, and the water that comes back tells us how bad it is. Roots get cut. If the soil itself has sealed, jetting will not fix it, and we say so.

    Drain Field Repair in Buffalo, NY

    Green stripes over the trenches and a wet lawn in dry weather are the classic signs of a field in trouble. Most of the time it is biomat, a slimy layer that seals the trench bottom. Compaction and hydraulic overload from a running toilet can do it too. We test before recommending anything big. Resting or jetting can bring a field back.

    Septic Pump and Alarm Repair in Buffalo

    Pumped systems move effluent uphill or to a far field, and the pump sits in its own chamber with a float tree and an alarm. An alarm going off means the water rose past the high-level float. We check the breaker and meter the pump draw before replacing anything. Zoeller and Liberty parts ride on the truck.

    Septic Tee Repair in Buffalo

    Sanitary tees on the inlet and outlet can crack or get knocked off by the hose on a pump truck. Without the outlet tee, the floating scum layer flows straight into the field lines and seals the trenches. We glue in a new Schedule 40 tee. An effluent filter goes on if the outlet has room.

    Septic Pipe Repair in Buffalo, NY

    Old homes may still have clay tile or Orangeburg pipe running to the tank, and both fail badly with age. Basements are standard in most homes, and many of them depend on sump pumps to manage groundwater during wet seasons. Shifting ground and roots crack the joints and flatten the pipe. We replace the bad run with 4-inch PVC, set on sand bedding with a quarter inch per foot of fall. Short sections take an afternoon.

    Inlet and Outlet Baffle Replacement in Buffalo, NY

    Baffles hold back the scum layer on top and the sludge on the bottom so only clear effluent leaves the tank. Concrete ones corrode from the acid that sewer gas forms and eventually fall off into the tank. Once the outlet baffle is gone, solids head straight for the field. We replace them with PVC sanitary tees. Usually a short job.

    Camera Inspection of Septic Baffles in Buffalo, NY

    Some tanks have no riser, so we look at the baffles through the small inspection port with a push camera. That saves digging up the whole lid. If the outlet tee is cracked or missing, we dig at that end only, then cut in a new PVC tee and an effluent filter housing. Concrete lids get a riser while they are open.

    Commercial Septic Repair in Buffalo, NY

    Churches and small offices on septic run more water through the tank than any house, and a commercial kitchen adds grease on top of that. We size repairs to the actual daily flow, not a residential guess. Grease interceptors and lift stations are part of it. Downtime costs you, so we schedule around open hours.
