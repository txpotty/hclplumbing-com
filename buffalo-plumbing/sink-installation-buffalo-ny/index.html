    Commercial Sink Installation in Buffalo, NY

    Commercial kitchens need sinks the health inspector will sign off on. A three-bay sink drains indirectly through an air gap to a floor sink, and hand sinks need tempered water near each prep area. We read the plan review first. Mop basins and grease interceptor ties are part of it.

    Pedestal Sink Installation in Buffalo, NY

    Pedestal sinks hide almost nothing. The supply stops and the trap sit right behind the column, so we center the drain stub, use chrome or polished brass trap parts, and set the stops tight to the wall where the pedestal can hide them. The wall needs solid blocking. The basin hangs from it, not the pedestal.

    Undermount Bowl Setting on Stone in Buffalo

    Stone shops cut the hole, but we set the bowl. Getting an undermount centered under a polished cutout so the reveal is even on all sides takes a helper, a few shims and patience before the clips get tightened. Uneven reveals look bad forever. We check it from both ends of the counter.

    Kitchen Sink Replacement in Buffalo

    Pulling the old kitchen sink is half the job. Strainers are often fused on, the disposal has to come off, and the dishwasher hose needs a fresh high loop. Then the new bowl goes in. We reuse the disposal if it still runs well.

    Kitchen Sink Installation in Buffalo

    Kitchen sinks come in four mounts: drop-in, undermount, farmhouse and flush-set.     Vanity tops with integrated bowls are the easiest bath install. The top sits on the cabinet in a bead of silicone, the faucet and pop-up go on first, and the trap arm usually needs a small adjustment to meet the new tailpiece. Backsplash comes last. We caulk it to the wall.
