    What We Fix Under the Sink in Buffalo, NY

    Bathroom Sink Repair in Buffalo, NY

    Bath sinks have small parts that wear. The pop-up linkage sits in a damp cabinet for years, and the pivot nut where the rod enters the drain is the most common leak we find on a bathroom sink. We check pedestal and wall-hung sinks the same way. Vanity sinks just hide the leak better.

    Sink Parts Replacement in Buffalo, NY

    Most sinks do not need replacing. A new tailpiece or pop-up assembly costs a fraction of a new sink and installs in an hour, and the bowl and faucet you already like stay right where they are. We match finish where we can. Discontinued faucets sometimes force a full faucet swap.

    Garbage Disposal Repair in Buffalo

    A disposal that hums but will not spin is usually jammed. We free the flywheel from underneath with a 1/4 inch hex key, press the red reset button, and check for a bent impeller or a broken grinding plate. Leaks are a different story. A cracked housing means a new disposal, usually an InSinkErator or a Waste King.

    P-Trap Repair and Replacement in Buffalo, NY

    The trap holds a plug of water that keeps sewer gas out of the room. Most codes want 2 to 4 inches of trap seal, and a leaking or cut-down trap can lose it and let sewer odor into the house. Bad traps get a new matched kit. Chrome traps that have rusted thin get swapped for PVC or brass.

    Low Water Pressure at the Sink in Buffalo, NY

    Unscrew the aerator before anything else. A clogged aerator screen causes most low flow at a single faucet, and if cleaning it does not help we check the cartridge and the angle stop for debris. Whole-house low pressure is another call. We test the pressure regulator in that case.

    Overflow and Drain Flange Leak Repair in Buffalo, NY

    Some leaks only show up when the bowl is full. A leak like that is usually the overflow gasket or the flange putty, which only get wet when the bowl fills. We pull the drain and reseal it. Marble and some granite get silicone, because putty can stain them.

    After-Hours Sink Leak Repair in Buffalo, NY

    Leaks do not keep office hours. A burst supply line can dump gallons a minute, and we answer at night and on weekends with a stocked truck. Close the angle stops while you wait. If those will not hold, use the main.

    Slow or Clogged Sink Drain Repair in Buffalo, NY

    Slow drains almost always clog at the trap or the first few feet past the wall. Kitchen clogs are grease and food that cooled and stuck, and bathroom clogs are hair wound around the stopper with soap scum holding it together. A hand auger does most of the work. Hydro-jetting is for main lines, not sink arms.
