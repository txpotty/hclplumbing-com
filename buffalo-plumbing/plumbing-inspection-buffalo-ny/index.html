    What a Plumbing Inspection Covers in Buffalo, NY

    Pre-Purchase Plumbing Inspections in Buffalo

    A house can pass a general inspection and still have a sewer full of roots. Buyers use our inspection to learn the pipe type, the sewer condition and the heater's age before the option period ends. That leaves room to negotiate. Old galvanized, a damaged sewer or an aging heater can all become a repair request or a closing credit.

    Sewer Line Camera Inspection in Buffalo, NY

    Older homes in Buffalo often have cast iron or clay sewer pipe that cracks, scales up or lets roots in at the joints. The camera records every defect along with its distance from the cleanout. Then we locate it. With depth and position marked, repair pricing gets accurate, and choosing between a spot fix, a liner or replacement gets easier.

    Hard Water Damage Assessment in Buffalo

    Water hardness in New York varies a lot, with many surface water supplies running soft and groundwater in some areas running hard. Hard water leaves scale in pipes, fixtures and water heaters. We look for crusty aerators, scaled showerheads, slow-filling toilets and sediment in the water heater, and we can run a simple hardness test on the spot. You get options, not a pitch. A softener, a filter or a scale-reducing system can extend heater and fixture life, and we tell you which one fits the hardness we measured.

    Whole-Home Plumbing Inspections in Buffalo

    Our whole-home inspections focus on the problems we see most in New York houses. Common finds include old pipe, stiff shutoffs, tired heaters, slow drains and rooty or sagging sewers. We test each one directly. Catching a corroded fitting or a partial sewer blockage now keeps it from becoming a ceiling stain or a backup on a holiday weekend.

    Gas Connection Safety Check in Buffalo, NY

    During a full inspection, we look at the visible gas piping and the connectors to each appliance. We check for old uncoated brass connectors, missing shutoffs, missing sediment traps and corrosion, and we test joints with an electronic detector. Any leak is reported on the spot. Older homes are where we find the most issues, usually at appliance connectors that have been there since the house was built.

    Pre-Purchase Inspection With Sewer Video in Buffalo

    Plumbing problems are some of the most expensive surprises after closing. We check supply pipe, record the sewer, date the water heater and test every fixture. We send the report to your agent too. Buyers often use the findings to request repairs or credits, and the sewer video by itself can head off a very costly surprise.

    Pipe Material and Water Quality Review in Buffalo, NY

    Pipe material says a lot about what to expect from a house. Our plumber identifies the supply pipe, whether copper, CPVC, PEX, galvanized, poly or Kitec, and explains how each usually fails. Certain types need a plan. Polybutylene and Kitec are known for sudden failures, and galvanized slowly closes up with rust, so those get flagged with replacement options.

    Residential Plumbing Inspections in Buffalo

    A full home inspection covers the system from the water meter to the sewer connection. Our plumber looks at all exposed pipe, runs every faucet and toilet and tests the heater, the main valve and the fixture stops. Pressure gets measured too. High pressure gets flagged.
