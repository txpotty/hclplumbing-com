    What We Replace in Buffalo, NY Homes

    Sump Pump and Well Pump Replacement in Buffalo, NY

    A dead sump pump during a storm floods a basement fast, and a dead well pump means no water at all. In Buffalo, NY, we replace sump pumps with cast iron or thermoplastic models sized for your inflow, add a check valve, and recommend a battery backup, while well pumps get matched to depth, flow and the pressure switch setting. We run each one through cycles.

    Water Heater Replacement in Buffalo

    Water heaters in Buffalo that leak from the tank, rumble on every heat cycle or run out of hot water halfway through a shower are usually cheaper to replace than to keep repairing. A leak from the tank body itself cannot be fixed. We match the fuel and size, update the venting and add an expansion tank or drain pan if code calls for one. Hot water is back by evening.

    Water Softener Replacement in Buffalo, NY

    Softeners wear out as the resin breaks down and the control valve stops cycling properly. Homeowners around Buffalo with hard water often add a softener to protect water heaters and keep mineral buildup out of faucets. Once scale returns, dishes spot or salt stops dropping, we test hardness, size a new unit and plumb it with a bypass so the house keeps water during service. Salt use often drops too.

    Well Pump & Pressure Tank Replacement in Buffalo

    Rural homes around Buffalo that run on a well have no water at all when the pump quits. The old pump comes up, wire and drop pipe get checked, and the new pump goes in at the right depth, often with a new tank and switch too. Most jobs finish same day.

    Pipe Replacement & Whole-Home Repiping in Buffalo, NY

    Homes in Buffalo, NY with galvanized steel or polybutylene usually show it the same ways: low pressure, rusty water and leaks that keep coming back. A whole-home repipe replaces every supply line from the main shutoff to each fixture with PEX or copper, and in most homes it takes two to four days with water back on each evening whenever possible. Walls get patched after.

    Pipe Replacement and Repiping in Buffalo

    Corroded, leaking or galvanized pipe in Buffalo can stain water and cause costly damage. Failing sections or the whole house get new PEX or copper, routed to keep openings small and pressure tested long enough to catch any slow drip. Patching is included.

    Repair vs Replacement Cost Comparison in Buffalo, NY

    A common rule of thumb is to replace when a repair costs more than half the price of new, or when the part is near the end of its life. A 12-year-old water heater with a bad gas valve is a good example, since the valve might cost a third of a new heater and the tank behind it is already close to failing in most homes. We price both.

    Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Buffalo, NY

    Switching to tankless when the old tank fails is common in Buffalo, NY, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. A whole-home tankless unit can draw four times the gas of a 40-gallon tank, so the gas line from the meter usually needs to be upsized, and the vent changes from metal B-vent to stainless or PVC. Both are on the quote.
