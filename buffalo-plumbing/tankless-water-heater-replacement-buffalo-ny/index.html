    Picking a Tankless for Your Buffalo, NY House

    Commercial Tankless Racks in Buffalo, NY

    Restaurants and salons run tankless racks hard. Four units together can pull over 600,000 BTU, which means checking gas pressure with all of them running. We work after hours when needed.

    Navien Venting and Install in Buffalo

    Navien allows 2-inch or 3-inch PVC venting on its condensing units, with maximum lengths that drop as you add elbows. We stay inside those limits. Go past them and the warranty suffers.

    Gas Line Testing on Tankless Installs in Buffalo, NY

    Every gas connection on a tankless gets a soap test and a manometer reading at full fire before we leave the house. A line that holds pressure at rest can still sag under full load. Both readings get taken.

    Leaking Tankless Replacement in Buffalo

    A leaking tankless can drip inside the cabinet for weeks before anyone notices the puddle. Close the cold isolation valve and the gas cock first. We then find out whether it is a fitting or the heat exchanger, since one is cheap and the other is replacement.

    Like-for-Like Tankless Swaps in Buffalo

    A cracked heat exchanger or a failed control board on an older tankless is usually not worth repairing past the ten-year mark. The replacement gets matched to the existing gas, vent and mount where possible. Most swaps take four to six hours.

    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Buffalo, NY

    Exterior-mount tankless units hang on an outside wall and vent straight out the front, so there is no vent pipe to run. Sun and weather wear on the covers and wiring. Lake-effect snow and deep cold snaps can keep temperatures below freezing for days, so insulating exposed lines pays off. On every outdoor swap, we check freeze protection and the pipe cover. They are usually the easiest swap.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Buffalo, NY

    Electric tankless water heaters skip the gas line entirely, but a whole-house model can need 120 to 160 amps on two to four double-pole breakers. Plenty of panels do not have room. Panel size and wire gauge get checked first.

    Commercial Tankless Replacement in Buffalo

    Cascade controls on a commercial rack decide which unit fires first, and after one unit is replaced they need rebalancing. Otherwise one unit does all the work. Descaling gets scheduled by usage.
