    Water Main Underground Repair in Buffalo

    Your water main is the pipe between the meter and the house. You own that part. When it breaks, we locate the leak without digging, expose the pipe and splice in new PEX or copper with fittings approved for direct burial, then pressure test the repair. Rusted steel lines rarely patch well.

    Pipe Bursting Services in Buffalo, NY

    Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one while breaking the old pipe outward. New pipe follows the old path. A bursting head on a cable splits clay, cast iron or brittle plastic, while a continuous HDPE pipe is pulled in behind at the same diameter or bigger. Collapsed sections are fine. Lining cannot do that.

    Main Water Line Repair in Buffalo

    Your main water line runs from the meter box to the house under constant pressure. Small leaks grow. A spinning meter dial with every fixture off, falling pressure or a wet spot that never dries tell us to locate, dig and splice the line with code-approved fittings. Rusted steel is better replaced.

    Underground Sewer Line Repair in Buffalo

    Buried sewer damage shows up as odor, slow drains or a soggy patch over the line. Video shows the break. We run a camera with a sonde through the lateral, mark the break on the surface and choose between a spot repair, lining or replacement based on the video. Trenchless methods often save Buffalo driveways. Not every line does.

    Foundation Leak Detection and Repair in Buffalo, NY

    A leak under a slab often shows up as a warm spot on the floor. Or a hissing sound. We isolate hot and cold lines with pressure tests and locate the leak with acoustic gear and thermal imaging, then repair it through a small slab cut or reroute the line overhead so the floor stays intact. Both options get a price.
