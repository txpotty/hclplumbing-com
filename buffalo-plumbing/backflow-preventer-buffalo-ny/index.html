    Freeze and Heat Protection for Backflow Preventers in Buffalo, NY

    Above-ground backflow assemblies sit in the open through summer sun and winter cold. Lake-effect snow and deep cold snaps can keep temperatures below freezing for days, so insulating exposed lines pays off. A single hard freeze can crack a brass body or split a bonnet, and years of UV make plastic test cock caps and covers brittle. Protection is cheap. Insulated bags, heated boxes and locking cages are all options.

    Irrigation Backflow Preventer Installation in Buffalo

    Sprinkler and drip lines sit in soil with fertilizer and pesticides, which makes them a common cross-connection.     Watts Backflow Preventer Service in Buffalo, NY

    Watts assemblies show up on more jobs than any other brand we see. Common models include the 007 double check, the 009 and LF009 RPZs and the 909 series on bigger commercial services.     Febco Backflow Preventer Experts in Buffalo

    Febco assemblies are common on irrigation and commercial lines. We work on the 765 pressure vacuum breaker, the 850 double check and the 825Y and 860 reduced pressure assemblies. Each has its own quirks. On a 765, the air inlet poppet and its seat are the usual trouble, and on the 825Y it is often the relief valve diaphragm.

    Reduced Pressure Zone Assembly Service in Buffalo, NY

    RPZ assemblies protect against high-hazard cross-connections at restaurants, medical offices, car washes and buildings with chemical feed. They have two independent check valves with a relief port between them that opens and dumps water if either check starts to fail. That discharge needs a drain. We install, test and rebuild RPZ units from Watts, Febco, Wilkins and Apollo.

    Backflow Preventer Repair and Rebuild in Buffalo, NY

    Worn check rubbers, grit under a seat and a relief valve that drips are the common failures. The assembly gets isolated and opened, each seat gets cleaned, and the worn parts are replaced from the factory kit. Then we retest. A rebuild is almost always cheaper than replacement.
