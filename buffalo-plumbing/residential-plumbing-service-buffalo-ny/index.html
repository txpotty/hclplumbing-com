    Slab Leak Detection and Repair in Buffalo

    Slab leaks hide under concrete until something gives them away. Basements are standard in most homes, and many of them depend on sump pumps to manage groundwater during wet seasons. Our techs find them with ground microphones and thermal imaging, so the only concrete we break is the small patch right over the leak, not half the kitchen floor. After that, you choose the repair. Options include a spot repair through the slab or a reroute through the attic.

    Water Softener and Filtration Service in Buffalo

    Homeowners around Buffalo with hard water often add a softener to protect water heaters and keep mineral buildup out of faucets. Then we set the regeneration schedule.

    Pressure Regulators and Shutoff Valves in Buffalo, NY
