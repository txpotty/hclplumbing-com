    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Buffalo

    Many plumbing codes require the installation of an expansion tank with a new water heater, particularly in closed-loop systems. Permits are commonly needed for water heater swaps, gas lines and sewer work, and HCL Plumbing handles the filing and inspections. An expansion tank protects your water heater and plumbing system from excessive pressure buildup, which can be caused by the heating process. Our installation service includes correctly sizing and installing the expansion tank to ensure proper system operation and prevent premature wear on your new water heater and other plumbing fixtures in Buffalo. This small component offers real long-term protection.
