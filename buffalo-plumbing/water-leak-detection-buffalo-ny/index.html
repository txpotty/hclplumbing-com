    Slab leaks are common in homes on shifting soil and moving foundations. HCL Plumbing uses specialized acoustic listening devices and thermal imaging cameras to precisely locate these hidden leaks without destructive guesswork.     Hidden Wall & Ceiling Leak Detection in Buffalo, NY

    Leaks behind walls or in ceilings can cause significant damage before they become visible, leading to stained drywall, peeling paint or a musty odor.
