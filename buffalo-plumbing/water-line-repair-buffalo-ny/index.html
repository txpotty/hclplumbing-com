    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Buffalo, NY

    Many homes built before the 1970s still have galvanized steel service lines that rust from the inside. Water hardness in New York varies a lot, with many surface water supplies running soft and groundwater in some areas running mineral-heavy. Hard water speeds up scale buildup, which chokes flow and causes rust-colored water. Patching galvanized pipe rarely lasts because the surrounding pipe is just as corroded. We usually recommend replacing the full run from meter to house with copper or PEX, which restores pressure and stops repeat leaks.

    Main Water Line Replacement in Buffalo, NY

    When repairs are no longer cost-effective due to extensive damage or repeat issues, a full main water line replacement may be necessary. This often applies to older homes in Buffalo, NY with original galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes that are prone to corrosion and leaks. We offer full replacement services, upgrading your system with durable, modern materials like PEX or copper for a reliable water supply for decades to come. We handle all necessary permits and inspections.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Buffalo

    If your service line runs under a driveway, pool deck or mature landscaping, trenchless replacement can save a lot of restoration cost. We dig small pits at each end and pull new HDPE or PEX pipe through using a pneumatic boring tool or pipe pulling equipment. The process usually takes one day. You keep your pavers and trees, and the new line carries a lengthy manufacturer warranty.

    Emergency Water Line Repair in Buffalo, NY

    A burst water line is a true emergency, which can cause extensive water damage and make your home unlivable. HCL Plumbing offers rapid-response emergency water line repair services across Buffalo, NY. Our plumbers are equipped to handle sudden breaks, providing immediate shut-off and repair to prevent further damage. Feel free to call us anytime, day or night, if you suspect a major water line failure. Quick action is essential to limit the impact of a severe leak.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Buffalo

    Many water line problems are made worse by high incoming pressure. A failed pressure reducing valve can push well over 80 psi into your plumbing, which stresses the service line and every fixture. We test static pressure with a gauge and replace worn regulators from brands like Watts and Zurn Wilkins. We also swap seized gate valves for full-port ball valves so you can shut the water off quickly in an emergency.

    Trenchless Water Line Repair in Buffalo

    For some water line repairs, traditional trenching can be avoided. Trenchless repair methods, like pipe bursting or pipe lining, allow us to fix or replace underground pipes with little disruption to your landscaping, driveway, or foundation. This method is often faster and less intrusive than traditional digging, making it a popular choice for homeowners in Buffalo looking to preserve their property. We'll assess whether your water line issue is a good candidate for trenchless technology.

    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Buffalo, NY

    A single leak on a newer copper or PEX line is usually a quick spot repair. Repeat leaks, galvanized pipe or a line older than about 40 years usually justify full replacement. Spot repairs often run a few hundred dollars, while a full replacement costs more but resets the clock for decades. We show you the condition of the pipe we expose so you can make the call with real information.

    Water Line Repair at the Meter in Buffalo, NY

    Leaks often start at the compression fittings and couplings right next to the meter box. Shifting soil and heavy traffic over the box can loosen those joints over time. We determine whether the leak is on the utility side or the customer side, since the water provider is responsible for the meter and the pipe before it. When the leak is yours, we replace the fittings and the customer shutoff valve on the same trip.
