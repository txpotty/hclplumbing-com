    Look for bubbling paint, soft drywall, stained baseboards and musty odors. White crust on block or stucco walls is another sign of moisture moving through. Loose tiles or cracked grout in a shower also mean water is getting behind.

    While minor DIY fixes like patching small cracks are possible, full walls waterproofing is best left to professionals.     Walls waterproofing is generally not recommended as a DIY project due to its complexity and the critical importance of a sound foundation.     Interior waterproofing addresses water after it has entered the foundation, usually managing it with sealants and drainage systems inside the basement or crawl space.
