    Flammable Vapor Sensor Repair in Irondequoit

    Since about 2003, most residential gas tanks have been built with a flammable vapor ignition resistant combustion chamber. Air enters through a flame arrestor screen at the base that also keeps flame inside. When lint, dust or pet hair coats that screen, the burner starves, the chamber overheats and a thermal switch shuts the whole heater down, sometimes several times a week. The screen and the chamber both get cleaned. A tripped thermal switch gets replaced.

    Power Vent Water Heater Repair in Irondequoit

    Power vent heaters push exhaust through PVC with a blower on top of the tank. When one quits, it is usually the blower motor, the pressure switch or the hot surface igniter. Condensate pooling in a vent with the wrong slope can also trip the pressure switch, so we check the pipe run all the way to the termination before we sign off on a part. We read the error code first. Then each part gets a meter test.

    Propane Water Heater Repair in Irondequoit

    Propane and natural gas heaters look the same but burn at different pressures. Propane units need a matching orifice and a control set near 11 inches of water column. Natural gas runs closer to 7 inches, so a heater converted badly, or a regulator that drifted, gives you soot, a yellow flame and a pilot that keeps going out for no obvious reason. We check pressure with a manometer. Wrong parts get swapped for the right ones.

    Gas Control Valve Replacement in Irondequoit, NY

    Honeywell and White-Rodgers valves report faults with a blinking LED, and the flash count matches a chart on the label. We read the code, then test the thermopile and sensors before blaming the valve. Valves do fail. When the control itself is the problem, we replace it with an exact-match valve or a listed universal model, set the temperature and leak test every joint on the gas train before relighting. We leave the old valve with you if you ask.
