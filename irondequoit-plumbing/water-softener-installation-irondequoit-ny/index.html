    Softener Loop Installation in Irondequoit

    No loop? A softener loop is a pair of capped stubs on the main line, usually in the garage, that lets a softener be connected without cutting pipe later. We add the loop. Where we can, we keep hose bibs and irrigation on hard water to save salt. Future service gets easier too.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Irondequoit, NY

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. HCL Plumbing sets them up with the right resin and brine tanks. A metered control valve regenerates only when needed, which keeps salt and water use down. They work best on very hard water.

    Fleck Water Softener Installation in Irondequoit, NY

    Fleck valves, especially the 5600SXT and 7000SXT, are on a lot of the softeners we install. Their parts are easy to find and the valves can be rebuilt instead of replaced, so the system lasts. Settings match your test. We confirm the setup before leaving.

    Whole-Home Water Softener Installation in Irondequoit

    Whole-home softeners treat every gallon that enters the house, right after the main shutoff. Placing it on the main means the water heater, the dishwasher and every fixture get soft water, not just the kitchen. We size before we sell. Hardness and household use set the grain capacity.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Irondequoit

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. They change the minerals into crystals that do not stick to pipe walls and heating elements, so scale drops off without adding sodium or using a drain line. Maintenance is light. They work best on moderate water.
