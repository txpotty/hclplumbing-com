    Sump Pit Installation in Irondequoit, NY

    Pit size controls how often the pump starts. Too small a pit means the pump short cycles in heavy flow, so we usually install an 18 by 22 inch or 18 by 30 inch basin with a solid bottom and perforated sides wrapped in filter fabric. Fewer starts mean a longer life.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Irondequoit, NY

    Submersible units sit down in the pit, sealed, with the water around them cooling the motor. You hear far less of them than a pedestal, and in Irondequoit, NY we set them on a couple of bricks so the intake stays above the silt that settles on the bottom of every liner. The cord seal matters.

    Commercial and Multi-Unit Sump Installations in Irondequoit

    Duplex systems with two pumps and an alternating panel are standard for parking garages and multi-unit buildings with below-grade levels in Irondequoit. The panel swaps the lead pump every cycle, so wear stays even. Staff get a high water alarm. We coordinate the wiring with your electrician.

    Same-Day Sump Pump Installation in Irondequoit

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Irondequoit

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the water. The motor stays dry and you can reach it without pulling the pump, which is why they suit narrow pits under 14 inches across, and why they often outlast cheap submersibles in Irondequoit homes. They are louder, though.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Irondequoit

    Outages and heavy rain tend to show up together. Without house power, a battery backup keeps a second pump going, and a fully charged 75 amp-hour battery can run a typical backup pump for several hours of on-and-off cycling, longer on a light night. We load test the battery.
