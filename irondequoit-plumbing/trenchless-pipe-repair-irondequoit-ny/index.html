    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. The resin cures in place. Once inside the cleaned pipe, it gets inflated against the wall and cured with heat or UV, forming a new pipe with no joints. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Irondequoit yard.

    Pipe Bursting Trenchless Repair in Irondequoit, NY

    Bursting works when the pipe has collapsed or is too broken to line. It can go up a size. With an entry and exit pit dug, the winch pulls the head and new HDPE through, replacing the run in a day or two in most homes. HCL Plumbing joins the new pipe by heat fusion.
