    Frequently Asked Questions About Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Irondequoit

    The cost of tankless water heater maintenance in Irondequoit typically ranges from $150 to $300, depending on the unit's condition, age and brand. This usually includes flushing, descaling and a complete inspection. More extensive repairs or part replacements would be quoted separately after diagnosis.

    Common signs include reduced hot water flow, fluctuating water temperatures, odd noises like banging or popping, error codes displayed on the unit, or a noticeable increase in your energy bills. If you notice any of these symptoms, it's time to schedule a professional inspection and maintenance.

    Once a year is standard, and homes with very hard water and no softener may need it every six to nine months. Heavy users and commercial units need it more often.

    Tankless water heater maintenance focuses mainly on descaling the heat exchanger and checking electronic components for efficiency. Hot water tank maintenance usually involves flushing sediment from the bottom of the tank, checking the anode rod for corrosion and inspecting the pressure relief valve. Both aim to prolong life but address different system needs.

    If you have isolation valves, a pump and the right solution, a handy homeowner can do it. A pro visit also checks gas pressure, venting, sensors and condensate, which a DIY flush leaves out.

    The descaling process involves shutting off the unit, isolating the water lines and connecting a circulating pump to flush a mild acidic solution through the heat exchanger. This solution dissolves mineral deposits like calcium and magnesium, which are common in hard water, restoring heat transfer efficiency.

    A cold water sandwich can come from restricted flow, a dirty inlet filter or a flow sensor not reading correctly. Descaling and filter cleaning often improve it.

    Most visits take one to two hours, including the descaling cycle. Installing isolation valves on the same trip adds roughly an hour.
