    What Fails on a Disposal, and How We Fix It in Irondequoit, NY

    Jam Clearing and Motor Repair in Irondequoit

    Bones, fruit pits and bottle caps lodge between the grind ring and the impeller lugs, and the motor stalls. We shut off power, insert a 1/4 inch hex key into the socket on the bottom of the unit and work it back and forth until the flywheel turns freely. Then the object comes out with pliers. A motor that stalled for a long time may have overheated, so we test it under load before calling the job done.

    InSinkErator Disposal Repair in Irondequoit, NY

    The InSinkErator lineup runs from Badger units to the Evolution series, and we repair both. Badgers are simple single-stage units, while Evolution models have multi-stage grinding and, on some models, a separate stage that can jam on its own. Most repairs use parts from the truck. A failed motor means a new unit.

    After-Hours Disposal Service in Irondequoit

    A dead disposal during dinner prep is a real problem, especially if the sink is full of water. Do not try drain chemicals. Chemical openers sitting in a disposal can damage the seals and splash back when the plumber opens the trap, so it is better to leave the water and call us.

    Slow Draining Disposal Repair in Irondequoit, NY

    Fine grinding with slow draining usually means the discharge tube or the trap arm is packed. Rice and coffee grounds are the usual culprits. We pull the trap, clear the tube and the arm with the right size cable, and check that the arm has at least a quarter inch of fall per foot toward the wall.

    Commercial Disposal Repair in Irondequoit

    Restaurant disposals run 1 to 5 horsepower and often have a separate control panel and solenoid water valve. When one stops, the dish line backs up fast. We check the controls first. A failed solenoid or a tripped overload in the control panel stops the unit just as completely as a jam, and it is a much faster fix.

    Disposal Odor and Buildup Cleaning in Irondequoit, NY

    Food buildup and grease on the chamber walls and under the splash guard are behind most disposal odors. Private wells serve a large share of rural homes, and they can carry iron, sulfur or hardness that stains fixtures and clogs filters. Mineral scale traps the food. Cleaning the guard and chamber, checking the drain and swapping a torn guard often solves a smell that lemon peels never fixed.

    Humming Disposal Repair in Irondequoit

    Humming with no spin almost always means a jam. Bones, pits, glass and spoons are the usual suspects. We cut power, work the flywheel free with a hex wrench on the bottom of the unit, remove the object and then test the motor and the reset button with water running.

    Flange and Inlet Leak Repair in Irondequoit, NY

    Leaks show up at the flange, dishwasher inlet, discharge tube or body. We trace the leak, reseal the flange with plumber's putty and replace gaskets or tubes as needed. Water from the bottom usually means a failed internal seal, which makes replacement the smarter move. It is not a repairable part.
