    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Cleaning in New York

    The cost of drain cleaning in New York usually ranges from $150 to $500, depending on the severity and location of the clog. More complex issues like main line blockages requiring hydro jetting or camera inspection might cost between $400 and $800. We always provide a written estimate before starting any work.

    Hydro jetting is best for tough, persistent clogs, especially those caused by heavy grease buildup, mineral deposits, or significant tree root intrusion. It provides a more complete cleaning than traditional snaking, scouring the entire pipe interior.

    Common signs include slow drains, gurgling noises from pipes, foul odors coming from drains, standing water in sinks or showers, and water backing up into other fixtures. If you notice any of these, it is time to call a plumber.

    Most standard drain cleaning jobs, like clearing a kitchen or bathroom sink, can be completed within 1 to 2 hours. More complex main line blockages or jobs requiring hydro jetting might take 2 to 4 hours, depending on accessibility and severity.

    Caustic and acid cleaners can damage older cast iron, soften plastic joints and harm the person using them. They also rarely clear the full clog. Mechanical cleaning is safer and more effective.

    Hydro jetting is usually safe for most modern plumbing systems. However, it may not suit severely corroded or fragile pipes, which are sometimes found in very old New York properties. We always perform a camera inspection first to assess pipe condition and use the safest method.

    To prevent future clogs, avoid pouring grease down drains, use drain screens to trap hair and food particles, flush only toilet paper, and consider periodic professional drain maintenance, particularly if you have older pipes or large trees nearby.

    Most fixture drains are cleared in 30 to 60 minutes. Main line jobs typically take 1 to 2 hours, longer if roots are heavy or there is no cleanout.
