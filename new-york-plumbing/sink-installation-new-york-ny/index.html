    Each Kind of Sink We Install in New York, NY

    Wall-Mount and ADA Sink Installation in New York, NY

    Wall-hung lavatories need a carrier or heavy blocking. ADA layouts call for a rim at or under 34 inches, open knee space, and insulated pipes below. An offset tailpiece frees up knee room. Lever or sensor faucets finish it off.

    Kitchen Sink Installation in New York

    Kitchen sinks come in four mounts: drop-in, undermount, farmhouse and flush-set. A cast iron farmhouse bowl can top a hundred pounds empty, so the base cabinet often gets reinforced before it goes in. The cabinet gets checked before install day. Disposal and dishwasher connections are part of the job.

    Bathroom Vanity Sink Installation in New York, NY

    Vanity tops with integrated bowls are the easiest bath install. The top sits on the cabinet in a bead of silicone, the faucet and pop-up go on first, and the trap arm usually needs a small adjustment to meet the new tailpiece. Backsplash comes last. We caulk it to the wall.

    Commercial Restroom Sink Installation in New York

    Public restroom lavatories take a beating. We install wall-hung and countertop units on carriers, add sensor or metering faucets, and set thermostatic mixing valves where code requires tempered water at public hand sinks. We can fit tamper-proof parts. Spec sheets go to your general contractor.

    Faucet and Disposal Hookup in New York, NY

    New sinks often get a new disposal. The disposal goes on a new flange, the dishwasher knockout gets punched, and the outlet lines up above the trap. An unpunched knockout backs up the dishwasher. We run both before leaving.

    Vessel Sink Installation in New York, NY

    Vessel sinks sit on top of the counter. Since the rim sits higher, the faucet has to be taller or wall-mounted, and the drain must match whether the bowl has an overflow. Bowls without an overflow need an unslotted drain. Getting that wrong leaves water under the bowl.

    Sink Replacement and Upgrades in New York

    Upgrading the sink is a cheap way to refresh a room. A same-size drop-in swap usually takes a morning, and a deeper bowl may only need a new trap. Countertop changes add time. We tell you which one you are looking at.

    Drop-In Sink Installation in New York, NY

    Drop-in sinks are the most forgiving style. The rim covers small flaws in the cutout, so a laminate counter can take a new self-rimming sink with only a check of the hole size and a bead of silicone or putty as the maker specifies. Mounting clips snug it down. Stainless drop-ins weigh very little.
