    Rough-In Plumbing for Urinals in New York, NY

    Rough-in decides whether the finish goes smoothly. We set the carrier, run a 2 inch waste line to the outlet height on the spec sheet, stub the 3/4 inch supply where the flush valve will land, and tie the vent in before anyone hangs drywall. The rough gets inspected before cover. Then the walls close.

    ADA-Compliant Urinal Installation in New York

    At least one urinal in a public restroom with more than one usually has to be accessible. That means a rim no higher than 17 inches above the floor, an elongated bowl projecting at least 13-1/2 inches from the wall, and a 30 by 48 inch clear floor space in front. The flush handle has to be easy to reach. We mark it all during rough-in.

    Sloan Flush Valve Installation in New York, NY

    Sloan Royal valves are on most commercial urinals we see. Sensor versions mount the same way. Battery models need no wiring.

    Flushometer Urinal Installation in New York

    Manual flushometers are still the most common setup. Manual valves go on a 3/4 inch line with the control stop tuned and the vacuum breaker at the correct height. Handle side follows the layout. ADA units need the handle within reach.

    Urinal Permits and Compliance in New York, NY

    New urinals usually need a permit. Adding a fixture means new drain, vent and supply work that most cities inspect at rough-in and again at final, and commercial jobs often need plans that show fixture counts and accessible layouts. We pull the permit. Like-for-like swaps may not need one.

    Home Gym and Garage Urinals in New York, NY
