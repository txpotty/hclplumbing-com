    Thawing and Burst Pipe Repair in New York, NY

    Frozen Pipe Thawing in New York

    When a faucet only trickles or nothing comes out after a cold night, ice is blocking the line. We open the faucet and find the frozen section. Copper and steel can be thawed with an electric machine in minutes, while PEX and CPVC get infrared or warm air, which takes longer but is safe for plastic. Patience pays here.

    Pipe Insulation and Heat Cable Installation in New York, NY

    Insulation slows heat loss, but it does not add heat. For truly exposed runs, we add heat cable. Self-regulating cable on a GFCI outlet, covered with insulation, keeps the pipe above freezing.

    Heat Cable for Exposed Pipes in New York

    Some pipes cannot be rerouted or insulated enough on their own, like a supply line under a manufactured home or a pipe through an unheated porch. Those get heat cable. Old-style constant-wattage heat tape can overheat and is a known fire risk, so we install self-regulating cable listed for the pipe material and replace worn or brittle heat tape we find.

    Frozen Water Line Diagnosis in New York, NY

    Finding the frozen spot takes the most skill. Thermal cameras make it faster. By checking which fixtures work and which do not, then scanning walls and ceilings with the camera, we can usually narrow the ice to a few feet of pipe and thaw it without cutting into the wall.

    Outdoor Faucet and Sprinkler Line Freezing in New York, NY

    Outdoor plumbing is the first to freeze, especially a spigot with a hose still attached. A connected hose holds water in the stem. Irrigation backflow devices sit fully exposed, so we drain or cover them before winter and repair split ones.

    Pool, Outdoor Equipment and Water Heater Line Freeze Damage in New York, NY

    Pool equipment lines, outdoor kitchen sinks and ice maker tubing in garages are easy to forget until they crack. Many pool systems run the pump on cold nights. If power goes out during a freeze, the equipment pad plumbing, the filter housing and any outdoor shower or kitchen line are at risk, and we repair the PVC and fittings once things thaw.
