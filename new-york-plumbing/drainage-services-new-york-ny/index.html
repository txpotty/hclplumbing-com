    Drain cleaning usually means a cable machine with a cutting head that bores through the clog. It is quick and cheap.     Most homes never need scheduled drain cleaning. A slow drain is the signal. Root-prone sewers and grease-heavy kitchens benefit from cleaning every one to two years.

    Simple downspout extensions are a reasonable DIY project. French drains are harder. A French drain or dry well needs correct slope, the right fabric and stone, and an outlet that will not dump water on a neighbor, and digging without an 811 locate risks hitting a gas or power line.
