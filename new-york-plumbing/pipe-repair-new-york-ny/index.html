    PEX and CPVC Pipe Repair in New York, NY

    Plastic supply pipe like PEX and CPVC is common in homes built over the last few decades. Each fails its own way. PEX can fail at crimp rings, older brass fittings or where it was exposed to UV light in an attic, while CPVC turns brittle with age and heat and cracks when bumped, and we use parts and methods made for each pipe type. Brittle CPVC needs a gentle hand.

    Corroded Pipe Replacement in New York

    Galvanized steel and old copper both corrode with age. Brown water is a clue. Low pressure, discolored water and leaks at threaded joints tell us the pipe is closing off from the inside, so we swap the failing pipe for PEX or copper with proper fittings between metals. You will notice the flow.

    Pinhole Leak Repair in New York

    Pinhole leaks form when the copper wall thins from the inside. Water chemistry causes most. High velocity, poor grounding and aggressive water all speed it up, and the leak starts as a tiny drip that stains drywall or a ceiling, so we cut out the affected section and replace it with new copper or a PEX transition. More pinholes often follow.

    Burst Pipe Repair in New York

    A burst supply line can put several gallons a minute on the floor. Close the main valve.
