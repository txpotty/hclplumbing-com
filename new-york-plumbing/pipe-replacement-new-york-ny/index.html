    Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in New York, NY

    Pinhole leaks in copper usually start as a small green stain or a damp spot on drywall. They are caused by a mix of water chemistry, high velocity and thin Type M pipe, and once they begin they tend to keep coming back. Patching each leak costs $300 to $800 per visit plus drywall repair. We can replace the affected branch or repipe the home with PEX, which does not develop pinholes. We help you compare the cost of both paths.

    Whole-Home Repiping in New York

    If your home in New York is older and experiencing frequent leaks, low water pressure throughout, or discolored water from multiple fixtures, a whole-home repiping might be the most cost-effective long-term solution. We can replace all the old galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes with modern, durable PEX or copper piping.     Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in New York

    Gray polybutylene pipe was installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid-1990s. It breaks down from the inside when exposed to chlorinated water, and fittings can crack without warning. Some insurers raise premiums or decline coverage on homes that still have it. We remove polybutylene supply lines and replace them with PEX-A or copper. A completed repipe can also help when listing the home.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in New York

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping has become a popular choice for residential pipe replacement due to its flexibility, affordability, and resistance to freezing. Lake-effect snow and deep cold snaps can keep temperatures below freezing for days, so insulating exposed lines pays off. Unlike rigid pipes, PEX can expand, reducing the risk of burst pipes during sudden cold snaps. HCL Plumbing specializes in PEX installation, which often allows for less invasive replacement methods compared to traditional materials. This modern solution offers excellent performance and water quality for your home.

    Slab Leak Pipe Replacement in New York

    Slab leaks, common in homes built on concrete foundations, can be destructive and difficult to detect. These leaks often stem from corroded or damaged pipes embedded within or under your home's slab. HCL Plumbing uses modern leak detection technology to locate the exact source, then expertly repairs or reroutes the compromised pipe, often using durable PEX to bypass the slab entirely, avoiding future issues in the same spot.

    PEX Pipe Installation in New York

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping is a common choice for modern pipe replacement projects in New York due to its flexibility, durability, and resistance to corrosion and freezing. It's also less expensive to install than copper. Homeowners around New York with hard water often add a softener to protect water heaters and keep mineral buildup out of faucets. HCL Plumbing carefully installs PEX piping for full home repipes or targeted section replacements, providing a reliable and long-lasting plumbing solution that handles local water conditions well.

    Corroded Pipe Replacement in New York, NY

    Many older homes in New York, NY still have galvanized steel pipes, which are prone to internal corrosion over time. This corrosion chokes water flow, reduces water pressure, and can introduce rust particles into your drinking water. Our pipe replacement service targets these corroded sections, replacing them with new, non-corrosive materials like PEX or copper. This restores proper water flow and ensures clean, fresh water throughout your home.
