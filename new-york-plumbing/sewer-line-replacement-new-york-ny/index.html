    How Pipe Bursting Works in New York

    A winch pulls the bursting head. The head is slightly bigger than the old pipe, so it splits clay or cast iron outward into the soil while fused HDPE trails behind it in one continuous piece. Only two pits get dug. With no joints in the new pipe, roots have no way back in.

    Trenchless Sewer Replacement in New York, NY

    Two holes instead of one long trench. Pipe bursting drags a cone-shaped head through the old line, cracking it outward while new fused HDPE follows right behind. The other option is a cured-in-place liner. Lining puts a resin tube inside the old pipe and cures it hard, but only if the host pipe is still mostly round and has no major sags.
