    Whole-House Water Softener Installation in New York

    Homeowners around New York with hard water often add a softener to protect water heaters and keep mineral buildup out of faucets. Softener sizing comes down to hardness times daily gallons, which tells us how many grains the resin has to remove between regenerations.     Sump Pump Installation in New York, NY

    A sump pump that fails during a storm usually failed months earlier: a stuck float, a seized impeller, a dead check valve or a cord unplugged for the shop vac. We dig the pit to the right depth, set a primary pump sized to the inflow and add a battery backup if you want one. Discharge placement matters most. We run the discharge line far enough from the foundation that the water does not cycle straight back into the pit.
