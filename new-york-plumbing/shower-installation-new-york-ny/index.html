    Prefab Shower Unit Installation in New York, NY

    Fiberglass and acrylic shower units are budget-friendly and quick to install. We set the base, connect the drain and install the valve and trim. Prefab units are ideal for guest baths, rentals and guest houses. We make sure the base is fully supported so it does not flex or split.

    Shower Pan Installation in New York

    A correctly installed shower pan is essential for preventing leaks and protecting your bathroom subfloor. HCL Plumbing expertly installs various shower pan types, including pre-fabricated units and custom-tiled pans with proper waterproofing membranes. We make sure the pan is level and correctly sloped toward the drain, a vital step that prevents costly water damage in the long run. We use high-quality sealants for lasting protection.

    Walk-in Shower Installation in New York

    Walk-in showers offer accessibility and a modern look, and HCL Plumbing specializes in their installation. We handle the necessary drain relocation, waterproofing, and tile work to create a beautiful and functional walk-in shower. Proper slope for drainage is essential to prevent standing water, and we make sure this is done right. We also advise on appropriate grab bars and seating options.

    Tub-to-Shower Conversion in New York, NY

    Converting an old tub to a walk-in shower is one of the most requested bathroom upgrades. We remove the tub, relocate or resize the drain and install a new shower valve at the right height. On slab foundations, the drain may need to be moved, which involves cutting concrete. The result is a safer, more usable shower.

    Emergency Shower Replacement in New York

    While installation is often planned, sometimes a damaged or severely leaking shower requires prompt replacement. HCL Plumbing provides emergency shower replacement services to quickly address critical failures. We can often install a new, standard shower unit to restore your bathroom without delay.     Standard Shower Stall Installation in New York

    HCL Plumbing installs all types of standard shower stalls, from fiberglass units to acrylic enclosures. We make sure of proper leveling, sealing, and connection to your existing plumbing system. Our team handles the entire process, including securing the base and connecting the drain line, to prevent future leaks and ensure stability. We also verify that water pressure is right for your new showerhead.

    Shower Valve & Fixture Installation in New York, NY

    Beyond the main shower unit, we also install new shower valves, diverters, and various fixtures. This includes pressure-balancing valves to prevent scalding, thermostatic valves for consistent temperature, and different showerhead types like rain showers or handheld units. Our team ensures all connections are watertight and that the fixtures operate smoothly, improving your showering experience. We can upgrade outdated fixtures to newer, more water-wise models.

    Steam Shower Plumbing in New York

    Steam showers need a steam generator, a dedicated water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit. We install the generator, plumb the supply and drain, and connect the steam head. A licensed electrician handles the circuit. Steam showers also need sealed enclosures and sloped ceilings.
