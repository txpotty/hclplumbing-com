    Tank Water Heater Maintenance in New York

    Flushing is the heart of tank maintenance. Private wells serve a large share of rural homes, and they can carry iron, sulfur or hardness that discolors fixtures and clogs filters. We run a hose from the drain valve, open it up and let the tank drain until the water runs clear, then refill and check the anode rod, the relief valve and every connection for drips or corrosion. Plan on an hour. A clean tank heats faster and lasts longer.
