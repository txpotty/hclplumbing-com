    HOA and Multifamily Underground Repair in New York

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in New York

    Polybutylene was used in a lot of homes built in the 1980s and early 1990s. It fails without warning. The gray plastic turns brittle as chlorine in the water attacks it, with failures most common at fittings and tight bends. Patching it just moves the next leak. We use PEX or copper.

    Trenchless Pipe Repair in New York

    Trenchless repair replaces or relines buried pipe without opening its full length. Two small pits, usually. We can pull new PEX or HDPE through the old pipe path or line a sewer lateral with resin, which leaves driveways, patios and mature landscaping in place. Not every pipe qualifies. The route and condition decide it.

    Irrigation and Yard Line Repair in New York

    Sprinkler supply lines sit shallow and break easily. Landscapers hit them a lot. A broken irrigation main can run up a water bill fast in the growing season, and we fix the main, the backflow connection and the feed to the valves. We can reroute lines away from trees.

    Main Water Line Repair in New York

    Your main water line runs from the meter box to the house under constant pressure. Leaks never fix themselves. A spinning meter dial with every fixture off, falling pressure or a wet spot that never dries all point to a main line leak, and we locate it, expose the pipe and splice in a new section with approved fittings. Rusted steel is better replaced.

    Foundation Leak Detection and Repair in New York, NY

    A leak under a slab often shows up as a warm spot on the floor. Sometimes you hear water running. We isolate hot and cold lines with pressure tests and locate the leak with acoustic gear and thermal imaging, then repair it through a small slab cut or reroute the line overhead so the floor stays intact. Both options get a price.

    Emergency Underground Pipe Repair in New York

    Trenchless Underground Pipe Repair in New York
