    Gas and Electric Water Heaters in New York, NY

    Electric Water Heater Installation in New York, NY

    Electric tanks need a 240-volt circuit, usually a 30-amp double-pole breaker with 10-gauge wire. We confirm the breaker and wire size match the new unit before connecting it, since many older homes have the wrong gauge. They do not need a vent. That makes placement easier.

    Tankless Water Heater Installation in New York, NY

    Going tankless means the heater fires only when a tap opens, and it never runs out. Gas tankless models usually need a bigger gas line than the old tank had, plus a dedicated vent run through the wall or roof. They cost more to install. We confirm your meter and line can handle it first.

    Brand-Specific Water Heater Installation in New York

    Each manufacturer publishes its own install rules, and they are not identical. Rheem, AO Smith, Bradford White and Navien each spell out clearances, vent lengths and relief valve placement, and a warranty claim can be denied if those were ignored. We follow them exactly. That protects your coverage.

    Traditional Tank Water Heater Installation in New York

    Tank heaters still heat most homes' water, holding 40 to 75 gallons at temperature until you need it. Sizing is based on the first-hour rating on the yellow label, which tells you how much hot water the tank can deliver during a busy hour. Either fuel works. We connect the fuel, set the vent and haul the old tank away.

    Emergency Water Heater Replacement in New York, NY

    Water on the garage floor and no hot water usually means the tank has rusted through. Shut the cold supply valve on top of the tank and turn the gas knob to pilot or flip the breaker, then call us. Common tanks ride on our trucks. Most emergency swaps finish the same day.

    Gas Water Heater Installation in New York, NY

    Gas heaters recover faster than electric, often refilling a 50 gallon tank in under an hour. We install the gas line, the sediment trap and the vent connector, then check draft at the hood with a match or a smoke pencil to confirm exhaust is going up and out. Then we check for leaks. Expansion tanks go in where code requires.

    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in New York, NY

    Without gas service, an electric tank is the usual pick. We match voltage and breaker size to the panel, and since there is no vent, an electric tank can sit in spots a gas unit cannot. Closets and attics get a pan. Quiet and simple.

    Water Heater Installation Cost in New York, NY

    Up front, a tank heater costs less than tankless. Gas tankless installs often run higher because of the bigger gas line and new venting, and attic jobs, relocations or code upgrades add labor on top of that. Your quote lists permits. We compare operating costs so you can choose with real numbers.
