    Scheduled cleaning keeps buildup from turning into a repair. Most homes need it yearly. For older homes in New York with cast iron or a history of kitchen clogs, a yearly jetting and camera check catches small defects before they back up. Hard water lines benefit most.

    Drain Vent Repair in New York, NY

    Gurgling and slow draining often mean the vent is blocked. The drain cannot breathe. Without air, draining water pulls the trap seal along with it and lets sewer gas into the room, which is why we inspect the roof opening and the vent piping inside the walls. Clearing it is often quick.

    Emergency Drain Repairs in New York, NY

    A drain that bursts under a sink can soak a cabinet in minutes. Stop using that fixture. We answer after hours, talk you through stopping the flow, and send someone out to cut out the broken pipe and fix it the same visit if possible. We also flag any wet drywall.

    Vent Pipe Repair in New York

    Sewer gas leaking into a room can point to a missing vent. Old renovations often skipped it. Bird nests, debris and roofing work can block existing vents, while fixtures that never had a vent can sometimes get an air admittance valve if code allows. Good venting keeps all trap seals intact.

    Cast Iron Drain Stack Repair in New York, NY

    Old cast iron stacks rot from the inside out. Cracks start along the bottom. Rust scale catches debris and hairline cracks let water seep into walls and floors, so we cut out the failing section and replace it with ABS or PVC using shielded transition couplings. If the whole stack is failing, we price it separately.
