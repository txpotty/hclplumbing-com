    Sinks, Disposals and Drains in New York, NY: How We Handle Them

    InSinkErator Models We Service in New York

    Badger units are the basic line, with a single grind stage and a 1/3 to 3/4 horsepower motor. Evolution models add grind stages and sound insulation, which matters in an open kitchen, and some carry longer in-home warranties than the basic line does. We stock the common sizes. We also service older InSinkErator units when the motor is still sound.

    Disposal Jams and Hums in New York, NY

    A hum without grinding tells us the motor has power and the plate will not turn. Usually a ten minute fix. We free the plate from below with a hex key, clear the hopper with pliers, never a hand, and reset the overload before running water through it. Silence with no hum points to the switch, the outlet or the breaker instead.

    InSinkErator Installation and Service in New York

    InSinkErator units use the Quick Lock mount, so swapping one InSinkErator for another is usually fast. We install the Badger line for basic use and the Evolution series when you want more grinding stages and less noise. Older mounts get replaced. We reset the sink flange in new putty, knock out the dishwasher plug when there is a dishwasher, and match the cord to the outlet.

    Workstation and Farmhouse Sinks in New York

    An apron-front sink needs cabinet support built to hold it, since a 30 inch fireclay bowl can weigh over 100 pounds before water. We check the frame first. Many workstation sinks have a drain set off to the back or side, so we plan the trap and disposal layout around it before the counter crew arrives.

    Disposal Installation and Upgrades in New York

    Most families do fine with a 1/2 horsepower unit, though heavy cooks and big families get more from 3/4 or 1 HP. Each install gets a new mounting assembly, a new discharge tube and a punched dishwasher knockout if you have a dishwasher. We run it with water before we go. A dry towel goes around the flange and discharge tube after.

    Kitchen Drain Clearing in New York, NY

    Water rising in the second bowl when the first drains tells us the clog is past the trap, in the branch line behind the cabinet. We run a 1/4 or 3/8 inch cable from the cleanout or the trap arm, and on grease-packed lines we follow up with a jetter. Drain chemicals do little to grease. What they do is sit in the trap and weaken the glue joints.

    Recurring Kitchen Clogs in New York, NY

    Grease, rice, pasta and coffee grounds stack up in the trap and the drain arm over months, not days. We clear the line with a cable sized for kitchen pipe and can camera it when the same clog returns. Sometimes the pipe itself is the problem. A low spot in the line or a drain arm without enough fall will collect grease however often it is cleared.

    Disposal Replacement in New York, NY

    Disposals last about eight to twelve years. When the housing starts weeping from the bottom, it is done. We drop the old unit, check the mounting ring, install the new one and reconnect the dishwasher hose using a fresh clamp so it does not leak at the inlet. We haul the old one away.
