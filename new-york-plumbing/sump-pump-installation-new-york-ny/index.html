    Sump Pump Installation Cost in New York, NY

    Price depends mostly on whether a pit already exists. Swapping a pump in an existing pit is the cheapest job, while a new pit means saw cutting, digging, gravel and a concrete patch, which roughly doubles the labor. Battery backups and alarms add to the total. Your written quote shows each option and its price.

    Exterior Drainage and Yard Sump Pumps in New York

    Sunken patios, below-grade driveways and deep window wells collect water with nowhere to go. For those areas we set an exterior basin with a submersible pump and run the discharge to a safe point, often a pop-up emitter in the yard. Lots that slope toward the house need this most. We keep the outlet away from your foundation and the neighbor line.

    Sump Pump Replacement in New York, NY

    Floats stick and motors seize when a pump sits idle for months between storms. When we replace a pump we also check the basin for silt, look at the check valve flapper and run water through the discharge line to find any clogs. If the old one could not keep up, we size up. We fill the pit and watch it run.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in New York, NY

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. The charger keeps the battery topped off. Batteries need replacing every few years. Finished basements get the most out of them.

    Combination Sump Pump Systems in New York

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. They fit pits where two separate pumps would crowd each other. Install time is shorter too. We set them in homes that want two layers without a bigger pit.

    New Sump Pit Installation in New York

    No pit yet? We saw-cut the slab at the low corner, break out the concrete and dig down through soil, hardpan or rock to set an 18 by 22 inch basin. Washed gravel goes around the basin so water can reach it. The slab gets patched with new concrete. The pump, lid and discharge go in last.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in New York, NY

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the pit, so the motor never sits in water. That makes service easy. They are louder than submersibles. We set the float rod and the discharge so the pump cycles cleanly.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Installation in New York, NY

    Zoeller has built cast iron sump pumps for decades, and parts are easy to get. The M53 and the M98 are the two we set most, depending on lift and inflow, and both come with mechanical float switches that hold up for years. They make matching backup units. Pit size and lift decide which model we recommend.
