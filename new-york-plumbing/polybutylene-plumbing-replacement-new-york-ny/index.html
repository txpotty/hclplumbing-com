    Polybutylene to PEX Transition in New York, NY

    Transitioning from polybutylene to PEX is a popular and cost-effective solution for many homeowners in New York, NY. We use specialized transition fittings and techniques to cleanly connect your existing main water line to the new PEX system. This provides a watertight and code-compliant connection, providing a complete upgrade to a modern, durable plumbing material throughout your home's interior. This transition offers the benefits of PEX without the need for extensive excavation or disruption to your main service line.

    Yard Service Line Replacement in New York, NY

    Many poly-era homes also have blue or black polybutylene service lines running from the water meter to the house. These buried lines can split and leak under your yard, raising your bill and soaking the soil. We call 811 to mark utilities, trench the route and install new PEX or copper service pipe. The new line connects to a new main shutoff at the home.

    Detecting Polybutylene Leaks in New York

    Even small, hidden leaks from polybutylene pipes can cause significant damage to your New York home's foundation or interior. Our technicians use modern leak detection equipment, including acoustic sensors and thermal imaging, to pinpoint the exact location of leaks without destructive guesswork.
