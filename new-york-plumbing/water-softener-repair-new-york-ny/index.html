    Resin Bed Replacement & Regeneration Issues in New York

    Over time, the resin beads inside your water softener can become fouled, damaged, or depleted, reducing their ability to remove hardness minerals. If your system is regenerating but still delivering hard water, the resin bed may be the cause. We can test the resin, flush the system to remove iron or sediment buildup, or perform a complete resin replacement. This process restores your softener's core function, ensuring effective hard water treatment for years to come.

    Salt Usage and Settings Repair in New York

    A softener using far too much salt or water often has settings that do not match your hardness or household. Older timer units may regenerate on a schedule regardless of use. We retest hardness, reprogram the valve or upgrade the head to a metered control. Proper settings can significantly cut salt and water use.

    Resin Bed Replacement in New York

    Over time, the resin beads in your water softener can become fouled or degrade, losing their ability to exchange ions and soften water. This is a common issue, especially with older systems or those exposed to high levels of chlorine. HCL Plumbing provides professional resin bed replacement services, carefully removing old resin and installing new, high-quality media to restore your softener's efficiency and capacity in New York.

    Salt Bridge & Brine Tank Repair in New York

    A frequent issue with salt-based water softeners is a salt bridge forming in the brine tank, preventing the salt from dissolving properly. This leads to inefficient regeneration and hard water. Our technicians are skilled at breaking up these bridges, cleaning the brine tank, and making sure the float switch and brine well are functioning correctly. We also inspect for any leaks or cracks in the tank itself that could be compromising your system's performance, offering targeted repairs or replacements.

    Water Softener Leak Detection & Repair in New York, NY

    Leaks around your water softener can be caused by cracked tanks, loose fittings, faulty O-rings, or pressure issues. Even a small leak can lead to water damage and wasted water. Our team uses dedicated tools to quickly locate the source of any leak, whether it's a slow drip or a significant pool. We then perform precise repairs, from tightening connections and replacing seals to patching cracks or replacing damaged components, protecting your home from water intrusion.

    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Repair in New York, NY

    A salt bridge is a hard crust that forms in the brine tank and keeps salt from dissolving. The tank looks full, but the softener cannot make brine. We break up bridges, clean out mushy salt, inspect the float and brine well and repair the safety valve. We also suggest salt types that bridge less in warm or humid spaces.
