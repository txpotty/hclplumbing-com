Complex jobs, like extensive root removal or main sewer line blockages, may be on the higher end. We provide a written estimate after an on-site assessment.

    Yes, our drain jetting comes with a written guarantee. If the line clogs again under normal use during the coverage period, we return at no charge.

    While drain jetting focuses on clearing existing pipes, we work with trusted brands for replacement parts if needed, such as Charlotte Pipe for PVC, Cast Iron Soil Pipe Institute (CISPI) standards for cast iron, and several manufacturers for cleanout assemblies. Our focus is on the integrity of your existing drain system.

    We strongly advise against using a home pressure washer for drain jetting. These machines lack the power, specialized nozzles, and safety features of professional equipment. Improper use can damage your pipes, cause serious injury, or push clogs deeper. Always rely on trained professionals for drain jetting.

    Generally, a permit is not required for routine drain jetting because it is maintenance within existing plumbing. However, if the jetting reveals significant pipe damage requiring excavation and repair or replacement, a plumbing permit from New York may be necessary. Our team will let you know if that happens.

    Consumer jetter attachments for pressure washers exist, but they can cause leaks or injuries if misused. A pro has the right equipment and experience.

    Signs you might need drain jetting include often recurring clogs, very slow drains in multiple fixtures, foul odors coming from drains, gurgling sounds, or sewage backups. If snaking hasn't solved your issues, jetting is likely the next step.
