    Fixing Showers in New York, NY

    Emergency Shower Leak Repair in New York

    Water coming through a ceiling below a bathroom needs fast action. Close the main, set a bucket under the drip and stay clear of any drywall that is bulging, because wet ceilings can drop. Then call us. Our plumber locates the leak, stops it and starts drying the area.

    Showerhead and Arm Leak Repair in New York

    Shower arms leak at the threads more than people realize. A leak at the arm threads follows the pipe into the wall, and it shows up as bubbling paint in the next room. We reseal the threads. We can add a handheld or slide bar while we are there.

    Commercial Shower Repair in New York, NY

    Gyms and apartment buildings run showers dozens of times a day. Commercial valves get heavy-duty cartridges, and we schedule around your busy hours so the showers stay open when people need them. We stock tamper-resistant heads. Limit stops are set to code on each valve.

    Weak Shower Spray Repair in New York, NY

    Most weak showers come down to the head. Minerals fill the nozzles and the screen behind them, and the spray fades so gradually you barely notice until it is a mist. Cleaning often works. A clogged cartridge gets replaced, then we test the flow.

    Mixing Valve Repair in New York

    Mixing valves blend hot and cold inside the wall. A sticking cartridge or spool shows up as temperature that drifts or a handle that has to go much farther than it used to. A new cartridge usually fixes it. Valves with no parts left get a replacement plan and an honest look at the tile.

    Showerhead Repair and Replacement in New York

    Showerheads clog from the inside out. Minerals collect behind the face and inside the restrictor, which turns the spray patchy or weak with the valve fully open. Soaking helps sometimes. When it does not, we install a new head with soft rubber nozzles that you can wipe clean with a thumb.

    Shower Diverter Repair in New York, NY

    Diverters send water up to the head instead of out the tub spout. A worn spout gate or a tired diverter stem lets water keep running from the spout, and the head pressure drops off. Usually a new spout fixes it. Spout replacements take about twenty minutes.

    Moen and Delta Shower Repair in New York, NY

    Moen 1222 and Delta RP46074 cartridges sit inside a huge share of the showers we open. The Moen 1222 runs the Posi-Temp line, and the Delta RP46074 runs the Monitor 13 and 14 series, and both wear out on a schedule you can almost set a calendar by. Both ride on our trucks. Original owners can often get the cartridge free under warranty.
