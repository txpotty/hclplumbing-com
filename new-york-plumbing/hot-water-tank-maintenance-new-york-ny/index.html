    Gas Burner and Venting Tune-Up in New York, NY

    On gas tanks we clean the burner, inspect the thermocouple or flame sensor and look for soot, rust flakes or scorching around the combustion chamber. We check the draft hood and vent connector for proper slope and secure joints. Dust and lint from garages can clog the flame arrestor screen on newer FVIR models, which causes the pilot to drop out. A clean burner gives a steady blue flame and lower gas bills.

    Sediment Flush and Descaling in New York

    Hard water leaves calcium carbonate behind every time the burner heats the tank. Over a year that layer can harden into chunks that insulate the water from the flame and cause loud popping. We connect a hose to the drain valve, flush with full line pressure and agitate the bottom until the water runs clear. When scale is severe we use a descaling solution approved for potable systems. A clean tank heats faster and puts less stress on the bottom seam.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Hot Water Tank Maintenance in New York

    A typical hot water tank maintenance service usually takes about 1 to 2 hours. This includes draining, flushing, inspection and any minor adjustments or part replacements like an anode rod.

    The anode rod, also known as a sacrificial rod, is a metal rod inside your hot water tank designed to corrode before the tank itself. It protects the tank lining from rust and corrosion, which matters most when water carries a lot of minerals, and it extends the lifespan of your unit.

    Most manufacturers and plumbing experts recommend yearly hot water tank maintenance. This is especially true where water is hard, since minerals speed up sediment buildup and anode rod depletion.

    Many manufacturers, including Rheem, A.O. Smith and Bradford White, expect regular maintenance and can reject claims for sediment damage. Keeping service records safeguards your warranty.
