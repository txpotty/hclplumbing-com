    Sediment Flush and Descaling in New York

    Calcium carbonate drops out of hard water every time the tank heats, and over a year it can harden into chunks the size of a thumbnail. That is what makes the popping noise. When a plain flush will not move them, we use a descaling solution rated for potable systems, let it sit and then flush it all out. A clean bottom puts less heat stress on the seam.

    Standard Tank Flushing in New York

    Flushing is the one job every tank needs, gas or electric, new or old. The harder the water, the sooner it matters. We drain, partly refill and drain again until the hose runs clear, sometimes four or five rounds. Then we close the valve and check that it reseals without a drip.

    Gas and Electric Tank Checks in New York, NY

    Gas and electric tanks share the same steel shell, but the parts that wear out are different. Gas units get a burner, thermocouple and vent check. On electric units we meter both elements and check each thermostat terminal for scorching. Both get a flush.

    Sediment Flushing in New York, NY

    Sediment is mostly calcium carbonate and sand, and it settles in a layer that can reach several inches on a neglected 50-gallon tank. That layer insulates water from the heat. Our method is short bursts of line pressure that churn up the bottom. The drain hose runs to a floor drain or outside, never onto a lawn where hot water can burn the grass.

    Maintenance Versus Replacement Costs in New York, NY

    A yearly flush costs a small fraction of a new installed tank, which often runs $1,400 to $3,000 or more depending on size and venting. You get a straight answer on which one is worth it. A tank over 10 to 12 years old with rust at the base is a replacement. Younger tanks with a sound liner are worth maintaining.

    Attic and Garage Tank Checkups in New York, NY

    Garage and attic tanks live through bigger temperature swings than closet units, which wears out plastic drain valves and wiring insulation faster. Attic tanks need a working pan. We check that the pan drain line runs to a safe spot and is not cracked or plugged, because one slow leak over a bedroom can bring down a ceiling. Gas tanks in garages need their ignition source at least 18 inches off the floor unless they are FVIR models.

    Yearly Maintenance Plans in New York, NY

    A maintenance plan puts your tank on the calendar so nobody has to remember. We call you when it is due. Plans cover one or two visits a year at a fixed rate, with priority scheduling if the heater fails between visits and a discount on parts like anodes and drain valves. Homes on well water or very hard water usually do better with the twice-a-year option.

    Electric Element Check in New York

    Electric tanks have two elements, usually 4,500 watts at 240 volts, and the lower one does most of the work and takes most of the scale. Each element gets an ohm reading. If the hot water quits halfway through a shower, the lower element is the first suspect. We replace it with the matching wattage and voltage.
