    Rental machines are easy to get, but they are dangerous in untrained hands. Cables can kink, whip and wrap around a wrist in a second. They also get stuck. Getting a rental cable stuck in clay pipe can mean digging it out, which costs far more than a rooter visit.

    Sewer pipes leak a little water vapor at the joints. Roots follow that moisture to the gap and push in. Once in, they grow quickly. In New York, clay with joints every couple of feet and old cast iron with lead and oakum joints get hit the most.

    Keep grease, coffee grounds and wipes out of drains. Put strainers in tub and shower drains to catch hair. Run hot water after using the disposal, and scrape plates into the trash before rinsing so less food goes down the kitchen line. Root-prone lines need cutting every year or two.
