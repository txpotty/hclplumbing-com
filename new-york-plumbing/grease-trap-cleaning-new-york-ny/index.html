    Compliance and Documentation in New York

    Pretreatment rules in New York are usually set and enforced at the city or sewer district level, and nearly all of them want proof of pumping kept on site. Every visit ends with a dated record.     Scheduled Grease Trap Maintenance Plans in New York

    Most pretreatment programs use the 25 percent rule: pump before floating grease and settled solids together take up a quarter of the trap's liquid depth.     Residential Grease Trap Cleaning in New York, NY

    Homes rarely have a grease trap, though a few large custom kitchens and some home-based catering setups in New York, NY put a small unit under the sink. These are usually 10 to 20 gallon units. We pull the lid, scoop out the grease and solids by hand, clean the baffle and reset the gasket so it does not leak or smell. About thirty minutes.

    Commercial Grease Trap Cleaning in New York

    Restaurants and hospital kitchens in New York run the same basic setup, a trap or interceptor sitting between the sinks and the sewer, sized by flow rate or by gallons. We pump it empty and scrape it. Before the lid goes back on, we look over both tees and the baffle wall for cracks or missing pieces. Most kitchens land between every two weeks and every three months.

    Grease Trap Cleaning Cost in New York, NY

    Cost follows trap size and fill level. Small under-sink traps go quickly. A 1,500-gallon interceptor left five months needs a truck, a pressure wand and disposal fees by the gallon, which puts it at the top of the range. Regular pumping keeps the per-visit price down.

    Compliance Documentation for FOG Programs in New York, NY

    Inspectors usually ask for at least a year of pumping records. Missing records can count as a violation even if the trap is clean.     Scheduled Grease Trap Maintenance in New York

    Pumping frequency comes from two numbers: how big the trap is and how fast your kitchen fills it. We measure the second one with a sludge judge on the first few visits.     Large Outdoor Grease Interceptor Cleaning in New York

    Gravity interceptors in the parking lot often hold 750 to 2,000 gallons across two or three compartments. Each compartment gets pumped separately. On big tanks we break the grease cap with a pressure wand first, since a cap that has set up hard will plug the suction hose and leave half the grease behind. No one goes inside without confined-space gear.
