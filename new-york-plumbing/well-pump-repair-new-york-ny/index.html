    Well Pump Pressure Tank Issues in New York

    The pressure tank works in conjunction with your well pump to maintain consistent water pressure and reduce pump cycling. A waterlogged tank, a ruptured diaphragm, or a faulty air valve can cause rapid pump cycling, fluctuating water pressure, or even no water at all. Our team carefully diagnoses pressure tank problems and performs necessary repairs or replacements. We ensure your tank is properly sized and charged for optimal system performance in New York.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in New York

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, clogged sensing tubes and insect damage are frequent causes of failure. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank. This is one of the most affordable well repairs.

    Submersible Well Pump Repair in New York

    Submersible pumps are widely used in residential wells, designed to operate submerged within the well casing. When these pumps fail, symptoms usually include no water, low water pressure, or strange noises from the well. Our technicians are skilled at diagnosing issues like motor burnout, damaged impellers, or faulty check valves that can affect submersible pump performance. We use specialized equipment for safe and efficient pump extraction and reinstallation, protecting the integrity of your well system.

    Control Box and Electrical Repair in New York, NY

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and relay. Coastal areas face storm surge and flooding, which can destroy water heaters and boilers installed at basement level. Lightning and power surges frequently damage these components.
