    Well Pump Problems We Fix in New York, NY

    Submersible Well Pump Repair in New York

    Submersible pumps hang inside the well casing on drop pipe, often 100 feet or more below the wellhead. The usual signs are no water at all, weak pressure or a hum with no flow. Testing starts at the surface. When a pull is needed, a hoist brings the pipe up safely so we can replace the motor, the impellers or the check valve.

    Above-Ground Jet Pump Repair in New York

    Lost prime is the number one jet pump complaint. These pumps can also leak at the shaft seal or wear out an impeller. We reprime the pump, replace the seal and check the foot valve and suction line for leaks, since one tiny air leak will undo the whole repair. Everything is above ground.

    Well Pump Brands We Service in New York, NY

    Goulds, Grundfos, Franklin Electric and Myers make most of the pumps we pull in this area, and HCL Plumbing services all of them. We work on old standard pumps and newer variable speed drives alike. Common parts are stocked. Keeping a pump that still has life left saves you the cost of a new motor and the labor of setting it.

    Pressure Tank Repair in New York, NY

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes the pump short cycle, turning on and off every few seconds. Fast cycling kills pumps young. We check the air charge, inspect the bladder and recharge or replace the tank, then confirm the pump runs at least a minute per cycle. Steadier pressure follows.

    Control Box and Surge Repair in New York, NY

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and a relay. Coastal areas face storm surge and flooding, which can damage water heaters and boilers installed at basement level. Lightning and power surges damage those parts more than anything else. After testing the capacitor, relay and overload, we replace the bad part and suggest a surge arrestor if the box lacks one. Arrestors are cheap.

    No-Water Same-Day Repair in New York

    A dead pump leaves the house without toilets, showers or drinking water. HCL Plumbing answers emergency well pump calls throughout New York and nearby areas ahead of scheduled jobs. The technician checks power and the switch first. A lot of no-water calls end right there, with the pump never leaving the well.

    Well Pump Repair vs Replacement Cost in New York, NY

    Switch and control repairs often cost $150 to $500, while a pressure tank replacement typically runs $600 to $1,500. A submersible pull and replacement runs about $1,800 to $5,000 and up, based on depth and horsepower. An older pump that already needs pulling is often worth replacing on the same trip. We lay out both options.

    Well Pump Electrical Troubleshooting in New York, NY

    Electrical faults cause a big share of well calls, from a tripped breaker to a burned contact in the pressure switch. The control box capacitor and relay are next on the list. We lock out the power before testing anything. All wiring and splices meet New York electrical code before power goes back on.
