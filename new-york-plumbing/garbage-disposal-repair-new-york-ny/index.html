    Disposal Repair in New York, NY: Symptom by Symptom

    Emergency Disposal Repair in New York, NY

    If water is reaching the floor, that comes first. Turn the disposal off, stop the dishwasher if it is running, and close the angle stops under the sink so the faucet cannot add more water to a backed up bowl. Then call us. We can usually stop the leak the same day and either repair the unit or quote a replacement before we leave.

    Noisy Disposal Repair in New York

    Grinding, rattling or a hum that sounds rough usually mean trouble inside the unit. A loose mount will rattle. We check the mount, then look for debris, then run the motor empty, since a whine with an empty chamber means the bearings are going.

    Emergency Disposal and Sink Backup Repair in New York

    When a disposal fails, water can back into the sink and the dishwasher. With water rising or on the floor, turn off the disposal and dishwasher and call, because running them only adds water to the cabinet. We move quickly on these.

    InSinkErator Disposal Repair in New York, NY

    InSinkErator makes the Badger line and the Evolution series, and we repair both. Badgers are simple single-stage units, while Evolution models have multi-stage grinding and, on some models, a separate stage that can jam on its own. Common parts are on board. A failed motor means a new unit.

    Disposal Reset and Electrical Repair in New York

    When a disposal will not turn on at all, the thermal overload may have tripped or the switch may be bad. We check the reset button, breaker, wall switch and wiring, and we also test air switches and batch-feed lids that use a magnet to start the motor. Most are simple fixes.

    Commercial Disposal Repair in New York

    Restaurant disposals run 1 to 5 horsepower and often have a separate control panel and solenoid water valve. When one stops, the dish line backs up fast. Controls get checked first. A failed solenoid or a tripped overload in the control panel stops the unit just as completely as a jam, and it is a much faster fix.

    Preventive Care and Longevity in New York, NY

    Keep cold water running before, during and after use, and let it go about 15 seconds after the motor stops so the food clears the trap. Grease poured in with hot water coats the chamber and the pipe, while cold water keeps it solid so the impellers can break it up. Stringy peels cause most jams. Throw out celery, corn husks and artichoke leaves.

    Flange and Inlet Leak Repair in New York, NY

    Leaks show up at the flange, dishwasher inlet, discharge tube or body. We trace the leak, reseal the flange with plumber's putty and replace gaskets or tubes as needed. Water from the bottom usually means a failed internal seal, which makes replacement the smarter move. That seal cannot be replaced.
