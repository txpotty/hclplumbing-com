    Why Choose HCL Plumbing for PEX Pipe Installation in New York, NY?

    PEX piping offers superior freeze resistance than copper or CPVC. Lake-effect snow and deep cold snaps can keep temperatures below freezing for days, so insulating exposed lines pays off. We make sure your new PEX system is installed correctly to maximize that benefit.

    • Scale Resistant, PEX does not collect mineral scale the way copper and galvanized pipe do.
    • Flexibility, Its flexibility allows for fewer fittings and connections, cutting down potential leak points in your plumbing system.
    • Corrosion Resistant, Unlike metal pipes, PEX does not corrode or develop pinhole leaks from hard water.

    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in New York, NY

    A manifold system sends a dedicated line from a central hub to each fixture, much like a breaker panel for water. Each line gets its own shutoff, which makes repairs easier and keeps pressure steady when several fixtures run. Home-run layouts use more pipe but fewer fittings in walls. We often combine manifold and trunk-and-branch designs to balance cost and performance.

    Uponor PEX System Installation in New York

    HCL Plumbing often installs Uponor PEX systems, a leading brand known for high-quality, durable PEX-A piping. Uponor's ProPEX expansion fittings create strong, reliable and leak-resistant connections, well suited for the demands of a home's plumbing. We recommend Uponor for its proven track record and longevity, giving New York homeowners peace of mind about their plumbing investment.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in New York, NY

    PEX-A, such as Uponor, uses an expansion tool to stretch the pipe and a ring over the fitting, which then shrinks back into a secure seal. The fittings have a larger inner diameter, so flow restriction is lower. PEX-A is also more flexible and can be repaired from kinks with a heat gun. It is our favored choice for many full repipes.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in New York

    PEX-B is stiffer and more affordable, and it is joined with copper crimp rings or stainless steel cinch clamps over brass or poly fittings. It is a solid option for smaller projects and budget-conscious repipes. Brands like Viega, Zurn and SharkBite offer reliable PEX-B systems. We use a go/no-go gauge on every crimp to confirm a proper seal.
