    Preventative Sewer Line Maintenance in New York

    Scheduled cleaning beats emergency calls. Grease builds again. Properties with older plumbing or nearby trees do well with periodic cleaning, often jetting, which clears buildup before it turns into a full blockage and extends the life of the line.

    Maintenance Plans for Sewer Lines in New York, NY

    Maintenance plans cost less than backups. Plan the visits. Periodic jetting removes grease, sludge and early root growth before it becomes a problem, which extends the life of the sewer system and avoids surprise backups.

    Identifying Signs of Sewer Line Clogs in New York, NY

    Several drains backing up at once is the main sign. Listen for gurgling. Water coming up through a drain when you flush, a sewage smell in the yard or basement and slow drains throughout the house all point to a sewer line that needs cleaning.

    Jetting Heavy Buildup in New York

    Some lines need more than a cable. That is jetting work. Water at up to 4,000 PSI scours grease, scale, mineral deposits and roots off the pipe wall, cleaning the full diameter instead of punching a hole through the blockage.

    Clearing Household Laterals in New York

    Most residential sewer clogs have three or four usual causes. Roots lead the list. Flushed wipes, built-up kitchen grease and root intrusion account for most backups, and we locate the problem on camera before choosing between snaking and jetting, so the fix lasts.

    Root-Infested Sewer Line Cleaning in New York, NY

    Roots are the top cause of blockages in established neighborhoods. They grow into a mat. They enter at clay joints and small cracks, so we cut them out with root saws and finish with jetting to remove the fine hairs left behind, then show each entry point on camera.

    Tree Root Removal from Sewer Lines in New York

    Roots enter through small cracks and loose joints. Much of New York sits on glacial till, rock and clay, which makes excavation for sewer and water lines slower and more involved. Then they spread. Root saws on a sectional cable cut the mass loose, and a jetter follows to remove fine root hairs and bring the pipe back to its full diameter, and we explain options to keep them out.

    Big commercial laterals carry more solids. Kitchens add grease. Our larger jetters handle big commercial pipe, focusing on interceptor outlets and long laterals, plus written reports for maintenance logs.
