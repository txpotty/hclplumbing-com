    Common Sink Repairs in Brentwood, NY

    Dripping Faucet Repair in Brentwood

    A faucet that drips once a second wastes thousands of gallons a year. Older compression faucets need a washer and seat, and newer cartridge or disc faucets need the cartridge itself or its seals. Cartridges for Moen, Delta, Pfister and Kohler are on the truck. Odd brands take a day to order.

    Pop-Up Stopper Repair in Brentwood, NY

    Stoppers that will not hold water have a linkage problem. The clevis strap slips on the lift rod, the pivot ball loses its seal, or hair wraps the stopper so tightly it cannot seat on the flange. Half the time, a cleaning does it. The other half get a new pop-up drain in a matching finish.

    Office and Restaurant Sink Repair in Brentwood

    Office breakroom sinks get abused in ways a home sink never sees. Coffee grounds and paper towels go down the drain all day, and the trap behind the cabinet door may not get looked at until it overflows onto the carpet. A strainer basket goes in after we clear it. For restaurants, we keep three-bowl sinks running between the lunch and dinner rush.

    Recurring Sink Clog Repair in Brentwood, NY

    Monthly clogs have a cause past the trap. We run a camera or a longer cable into the wall arm and look for a sagging line or buildup where the sink ties into the tub drain. Now and then it is a plugged vent. A sink that gurgles as it drains is the giveaway.

    Slow or Clogged Sink Drain Repair in Brentwood, NY

    Slow drains almost always clog at the trap or the first few feet past the wall. Kitchen clogs are grease and food that cooled and stuck, and bathroom clogs are hair wound around the stopper with soap scum holding it together. We clear it with a hand auger. Hydro-jetting is for main lines, not sink arms.

    Supply Line and Angle Stop Replacement in Brentwood, NY

    Braided supply lines have a rubber core that ages. After five to ten years the core can split without warning, and old multi-turn stops often seize open. We swap both in one visit. Quarter-turn stops close in one motion.

    Overflow and Drain Flange Leak Repair in Brentwood, NY

    Some leaks only show up when the bowl is full. That points to the overflow gasket or the putty under the drain flange, since neither one sees water during a quick hand wash but both get soaked when you fill the sink. The drain comes out and gets resealed. Marble and some granite get silicone, because putty can stain them.

    Burst Supply Line Repair in Brentwood

    A burst line under a sink can push out several gallons a minute. Close the angle stop, or the main if the stop fails, because every minute adds water to the subfloor. Next, put towels on the floor. We replace the line and the stop and moisture-check the cabinet.
