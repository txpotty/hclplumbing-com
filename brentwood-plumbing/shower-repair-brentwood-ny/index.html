    Weak Shower Spray Repair in Brentwood, NY

    Most weak showers come down to the head. Minerals fill the nozzles and the screen behind them, and the spray fades so gradually you barely notice until it is a mist. Cleaning often works. If the cartridge is clogged too, we replace it and test the flow again.

    Commercial Shower Service in Brentwood

    Hotels and dorms cannot wait days on a shower. We carry commercial cartridges and thermostatic parts, and we can stage repairs room by room so only one unit is down at a time. We can work nights. Each repaired valve gets its limit stop set and tested before we move on.

    Tub and Shower Combo Repair in Brentwood, NY

    Tub and shower combos share one valve, one drain and a diverter between them. Because everything is linked, a weak diverter starves the head and a leaky valve drips from the spout and the head alike. Each part gets tested. The overflow gasket gets a look too, since it is a common hidden leak.

    Emergency Shower Repair in Brentwood, NY

    A shower that will not shut off is an emergency. Close the main valve where the water line enters the house, or the shower's own stops if it has them behind an access panel. Next, call us. HCL Plumbing sends a plumber with cartridges on the truck, and we can often get your water back on within the hour.

    Leaky Shower Valve Repair in Brentwood

    One drip every few seconds adds up to hundreds of gallons a year. In a single-handle shower the cause is almost always the cartridge or its O-rings, and on an old two-handle valve it is the stem washer and the seat. The parts are on the truck. Most valves we open take a Moen, Delta, Kohler or Pfister cartridge, which means a same-visit fix.

    Hot Water Pressure Problems in Brentwood, NY

    Weak hot water with strong cold usually rules out the showerhead. The problem is on the hot side, either sediment in the water heater outlet, a partly closed valve at the heater or a cartridge whose hot port is packed with scale. We test both sides. Good hot flow at the sinks points us straight at the shower cartridge.

    Shower Valve Replacement in Brentwood

    Temperature that swings or a handle that fights you points to the valve. When the body is corroded or the brand stopped making cartridges for it, we replace the valve, usually through a small access panel behind the shower wall so the tile stays put. The new valve will have scald protection. Thermostatic valves are an option if you want exact temperature control.

    Moen and Delta Shower Repair in Brentwood, NY

    Moen 1222 and Delta RP46074 cartridges sit inside a huge share of the showers we open. Moen Posi-Temp showers take the 1222, and Delta Monitor 13 and 14 showers take the RP46074, and both wear out on a fairly regular clock. We stock both. Many of these carry a lifetime warranty to the original owner.
