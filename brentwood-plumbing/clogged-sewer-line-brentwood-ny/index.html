    Cleaning vs. Repairing a Sewer Line in Brentwood, NY

    A sound pipe with a grease or root clog just needs cleaning. Broken pipe needs repair. Offset joints, cracks and collapsed sections keep clogging no matter how often they get cleared, and the camera tells you which path makes sense. We walk you through it.

    Some lines get cabled three times a year and still clog. The cable is not the fix. Those lines are usually coated in grease or scale, and a cable just cuts a small hole that closes back up. Jetting removes the coating down to the pipe wall. It lasts much longer.
