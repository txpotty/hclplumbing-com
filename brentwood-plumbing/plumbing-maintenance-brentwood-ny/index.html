    Water Heater Maintenance in Brentwood

    Regular water heater maintenance is crucial to preventing sediment buildup. Homeowners around Brentwood with hard water often add a softener to protect water heaters and keep mineral buildup out of faucets. Our service includes flushing the tank, inspecting the anode rod for corrosion, checking the temperature and pressure relief valve, and examining all connections and heating elements. This extends the life of your unit, improves efficiency, and helps avoid sudden cold showers or leaks.

    A camera inspection shows the condition of your sewer line and main drains. We look for roots, bellies, cracks and buildup before they cause a backup.     Sump Pump Maintenance in Brentwood, NY

    Spring snowmelt and heavy rain can raise water tables quickly in New York, sending water into basements and testing sump pumps. A working sump pump is essential for homes with basements or crawl spaces when heavy rain overwhelms drainage. Our maintenance includes testing the float switch, checking for proper discharge, cleaning the pit of debris, and inspecting the check valve. That way your sump pump is ready to protect your home from flooding when sudden storms hit Brentwood.

    Water Softener Maintenance in Brentwood

    Water softeners combat hard water, protecting your pipes and appliances from mineral scale. Our maintenance service includes checking salt levels, cleaning the brine tank, confirming the regeneration cycle works correctly, and inspecting valves and resin beds. Proper softener maintenance extends its life and keeps soft water flowing to your home, preventing costly scale damage.

    Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Brentwood

    Tankless water heaters need specialized maintenance, mainly descaling to remove mineral buildup from the heat exchanger. This is especially important where the water is hard. Our technicians use approved descaling solutions to keep your tankless unit running at peak efficiency, preventing reduced hot water flow and possible breakdowns.
