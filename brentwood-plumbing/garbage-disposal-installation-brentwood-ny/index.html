    Dishwasher Connection With Disposal Installation in Brentwood

    Every new disposal has a plug sealing the dishwasher inlet. We knock it out with a screwdriver and hammer, fish the plug out of the chamber and connect the hose with a new clamp. Forgetting the plug floods the dishwasher. An air gap or high loop goes in too, which keeps sink water from running back into the dishwasher.

    Standard Disposal Installation in Brentwood

    Most households in Brentwood do well with a continuous-feed unit between 1/3 and 1/2 horsepower. We remove the old unit if there is one, set a new flange in fresh plumber's putty, hang the disposal on a new mount and connect it to the trap with a new discharge tube and gasket. Wiring and testing come last. A smooth spin and a clean drain tell us it is right.

    Switching From Batch-Feed to Continuous-Feed in Brentwood, NY

    Going from batch-feed to continuous-feed means adding a way to switch it on. That can be a wall switch, an air switch button on the sink deck or a switched outlet under the sink, and the choice depends on how hard it is to run wire to the spot you want. An air button skips the wall wiring. The drain usually stays the same.

    Disposal Installation for Double Sinks in Brentwood, NY

    Double bowl sinks put the disposal on one side and a continuous waste or end outlet tee on the other. The layout keeps both bowls draining and stops the disposal from pushing water into the second bowl, a problem when the tee is too close to the discharge. We use heavy-wall fittings.

    High-Capacity Disposal Installation in Brentwood, NY

    Busy kitchens get the most from a 1 HP unit, like an InSinkErator Evolution or a Waste King Legend model. We confirm the circuit can carry the motor and the drain can handle the flow, because a strong unit pushing into a flat trap arm will still clog. These units weigh more. They get supported during mounting.

    Replacing an Old Disposal in Brentwood, NY

    Old disposals usually come off with a twist of the mounting ring, once the power is off and the dishwasher hose and discharge tube are disconnected. If the new unit is the same brand and mount style, the swap is faster, but we still replace the flange gasket and putty when the old seal has hardened. Seals harden with age. We haul the old unit away.

    Batch-Feed Disposal Installation in Brentwood, NY

    A batch-feed disposal will not start until the lid is in, and the lid holds a magnet that closes a switch inside the unit. No wall switch is needed, which makes them handy in kitchens where running a new switch would mean opening finished walls. You grind one load at a time. Parents of young kids often think that is worth it.
