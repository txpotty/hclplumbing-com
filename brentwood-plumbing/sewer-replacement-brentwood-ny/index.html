    What Goes Into a Sewer Replacement in Brentwood, NY

    Traditional Trench Sewer Replacement in Brentwood

    An open trench gives full access to the pipe from the house to the main. A small excavator does most of it, with hand digging near other lines. The old pipe comes out, the trench bottom gets a bed of gravel or sand, and new 4-inch PVC goes in at a steady quarter inch of fall per foot, checked with a level before backfill. Then the camera.

    Sewer Pipe Material Upgrades in Brentwood, NY

    Original sewer pipe in older homes is usually clay, cast iron or Orangeburg. Clay joints open up and let roots in, cast iron scales and cracks from the inside, and Orangeburg, a tar-paper pipe from the mid 1900s, squashes into an oval over time. Replacement means PVC or HDPE.

    Sewer Line Material Upgrades in Brentwood

    Clay, cast iron and Orangeburg all failed for different reasons. PVC avoids all three of those problems. Schedule 40 or SDR 35 PVC is what most codes call for on a lateral, and we match the grade to the depth and load over the pipe. Glued joints stay sealed.

    Emergency Sewer Line Replacement in Brentwood, NY

    Sewage coming up a floor drain or tub is an emergency. Stop using sinks, toilets and the washer. We open the cleanout to relieve the backup, run a camera to find the break, and if the line has collapsed we dig to it the same day, putting in a section of new pipe to get you flowing before planning the full replacement.

    Advanced Diagnostics for Sewer Issues in Brentwood, NY

    Diagnosis comes before price. Video tells us the problem and the sonde tells us the spot. We sometimes add a hydro-jet cleaning first, because a line full of grease can hide a crack, then camera again so we are looking at the pipe itself. Guesswork costs money.

    Trenchless Sewer Repair Options in Brentwood, NY

    Pipe bursting needs two pits. We dig a launch pit near the house and a receiving pit near the main. A cone-shaped head is pulled through the old line, breaking it outward, while fused HDPE pipe follows right behind it, so the new line takes the same path without a trench. Driveways stay put.

    Sewer Line Repair vs. Full Replacement in Brentwood

    We look at the whole line before recommending either. One offset joint near the house is a repair. Breaks every few feet, roots at every joint, or a belly that holds water for twenty feet mean the rest of the pipe is close behind, and in that case a full replacement saves you from paying to dig the same yard twice.

    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Brentwood

    Cast iron sewer pipe lasts a long time, though not indefinitely. The inside rusts and scales until the bottom of the pipe thins out and cracks. We replace it with PVC, and where cast iron remains under the house we use a shielded no-hub coupling at the transition. Clean seal.

    Local Sewer Replacement Near Me in Brentwood, NY

    Our crews work on sewer lines across Brentwood every week. Neighbors usually have the same pipe material, since streets were built together. If the house next door had clay pipe full of roots, yours probably does too, which gives us a good guess before we scope. Patterns help.

    A local crew can come back for the inspection. That matters with sewer work. The trench usually has to stay open until the inspector signs off, so we plan our schedule in Brentwood around the inspector's visit and keep the hole covered, fenced and marked overnight until it passes.
