    A full replacement takes every poly supply line out of service, not just the sections that have failed. Partial jobs leave risk behind. New pipe goes from the main to every fixture and appliance, each one gets a new quarter-turn stop and the system is tested before any wall is closed.

    Full Home Repiping with PEX in Brentwood

    Most poly replacements we do use PEX-A, a flexible tubing that runs long distances with few fittings. For slab houses, new PEX-A runs through the attic and drops down interior walls, so none of it sits under the floor. It also tolerates freezing better. Expansion fittings keep the bore full size, which helps pressure at the last fixture on the line.

    Polybutylene Identification and Inspection in Brentwood

    Polybutylene is usually gray, though it can be white, blue or black, and is often stamped PB2110. Start at the water heater. It also shows up under sinks, at the main and through garage walls, and we follow it into the attic to see how much of the house it feeds.

    Emergency Polybutylene Burst Repair in Brentwood

    Poly fittings tend to let go during sharp temperature swings, when the pipe expands and contracts more than usual. Lake-effect snow and deep cold snaps can keep temperatures below freezing for days, so insulating exposed lines pays off. Close the main if a line bursts, and call us to stop it, patch it and get water back on. A full replacement comes next. Living with poly until the next failure costs the most in the end.
