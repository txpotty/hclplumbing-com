    Exterior Drain Tile Repair in Brentwood, NY

    Drain tile, sometimes called a French drain, carries groundwater away from the footing. It clogs with silt.     Storm Drain Repair in Brentwood, NY

    Yard and storm drains carry rain away from the foundation. Spring snowmelt and heavy rain can raise water tables quickly in New York, sending water into basements and testing sump pumps. Debris and silt fill them up. We clear the line with a jetter, camera it to look for crushed or separated sections, and replace damaged corrugated or PVC pipe so water flows to the outlet instead of pooling against the house.     Drain Vent Repair in Brentwood, NY

    Gurgling and slow draining often mean the vent is blocked. The drain cannot breathe.     Cast Iron Drain Stack Repair in Brentwood, NY

    Old cast iron stacks rot from the inside out. Cracks start along the bottom. Rust scale catches debris and hairline cracks let water seep into walls and floors, and we replace the bad length with plastic pipe joined to the old iron with shielded couplings. Full stack replacement gets a separate quote.

    Leaking Drain Pipe Repair in Brentwood, NY

    Drain leaks are low pressure, so they drip instead of spray. They hide well. Corrosion, shifting framing and cracked fittings are the usual causes, and we trace the leak by running each fixture and watching the joints, then replace the bad length with pipe that matches what is already there. Repairs meet local plumbing code.
