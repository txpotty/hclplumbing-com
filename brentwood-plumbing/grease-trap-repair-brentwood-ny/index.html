    Grease Trap Fixes We Handle in Brentwood, NY

    Clogged Grease Trap Repair in Brentwood

    Hardened grease in the inlet tee is the usual culprit when a trap backs up. We pump the trap down, then jet the inlet line with a 4,000 PSI unit until water runs free. Food solids packed against the baffle get scraped out by hand. Then we look for the reason it clogged, such as a dishwasher dumping too much hot water at once or a missing sink strainer upstream. Repeat clogs nearly always have a cause.

    Damaged Baffle Repair in Brentwood

    Baffles are the walls inside the trap that slow the water down long enough for grease to float and solids to sink. Once one cracks or falls loose, the trap is just a box that grease passes through. We pump it out and pull the old baffle. On plastic units we order the factory part for that model number. Steel traps with a rusted-out baffle may need a fabricated piece, and sometimes a new trap is the cheaper answer.

    Emergency Grease Trap Repair in Brentwood

    Mid-shift backups usually come from a plugged inlet or a trap that is overdue for pumping. Pump-out and an inlet cleaning often take less than two hours on site. Our trucks carry common lid gaskets, tees, flow control fittings and test plugs, so a broken part does not always mean a second trip and another lost night. Rush visits take priority.

    Grease Line Clog Repair in Brentwood, NY

    Lines downstream of the trap should stay clean if the trap is working. When we find grease past the outlet, the trap is not doing its job. Clearing the pipe without fixing the trap just resets the clock.

    Grease Trap Repair vs Replacement Cost in Brentwood, NY

    Age and material decide most repair versus replace calls. A 25-year-old steel trap with rust holes is done. A plastic or fiberglass trap with a broken baffle is usually worth fixing, and a concrete interceptor with sound walls and a bad riser almost always is, since replacing a buried tank means excavation and several days closed. We put both options in writing with a price on each.

    Hydro-Jetting for Grease Trap Repair in Brentwood

    A cable machine punches a hole through grease. Jetting actually scours the pipe wall clean. We run a commercial jetter at 3,000 to 4,000 PSI through the line feeding the trap and the line leaving it, working from the cleanout toward the main. On old cast iron we lower the pressure and watch the camera so we do not open up a thin spot.
