    Many older homes in Brentwood, NY still have galvanized steel pipes, which are vulnerable to corrosion, rust, and mineral buildup. This upgrade not only resolves existing issues but also significantly improves water quality and flow, boosting your home's value and plumbing reliability for decades to come.

    Repipe Costs Versus Leak Repairs in Brentwood, NY

    A whole-home PEX repipe for an average home often runs $5,000 to $12,000, with copper usually costing more. A single slab leak repair can run $1,000 to $4,000 including access and restoration. After two or three leaks, a repipe often pays for itself. We give you a side-by-side comparison so you can make the call.

Visible pipe at the water heater often tells us which type you have.
