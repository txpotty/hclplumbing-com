    Standard Bathtub Faucet Replacement in Brentwood

    Single-handle and three-handle tub faucets come out differently. A single-handle valve often takes new trim and a cartridge from the front, while a three-handle valve usually has to be cut out of the wall and replaced with a modern pressure-balance body. We check before quoting. Two-handle models fall in between.

    Moen Tub Faucet Replacement in Brentwood

    Moen Posi-Temp valves use the 1222 cartridge. A worn 1222 causes drips or weak hot water, and replacing it takes about an hour with a puller. Older Moentrol valves take another cartridge. We carry both.

    Emergency Tub Faucet Leak Service in Brentwood

    A broken stem can leave water running nonstop. Use the stops behind the access panel or the main shutoff, then open a low faucet to bleed pressure. Running water moves you up the list. Most get fixed on the first visit.

    Brand Tub Faucet Replacement in Brentwood

    Brand matters more on tub valves than anywhere else. Moen, Delta, Kohler, American Standard and Pfister each use their own cartridges and trim threads, and a new trim kit from the same maker as the valve usually bolts right on without touching the plumbing. Mixing brands rarely works. We find the logo or the casting mark first.

    Three-Handle to Single-Handle Conversion in Brentwood

    Three-handle faucets are worth converting. We cut out the old valve, sweat or crimp in a single-handle pressure-balance valve, and cover the old handle holes with a remodel plate so the tile stays intact around it. We usually work from behind the wall. Tile around the plate stays intact.

    Delta Tub Faucet Replacement in Brentwood, NY

    Delta tub valves are some of the easiest to update. Many Delta Monitor valves accept a new cartridge and trim from the front, and the MultiChoice universal rough-in lets you switch trim styles later without touching the pipes in the wall. Popular Delta cartridges are on the truck. Older Delta valves sometimes need full replacement.

    Thermostatic Tub Valve Installation in Brentwood, NY

    Thermostatic valves hold the temperature you set. They sense real water temperature, not just pressure, which helps kids and older adults avoid hot spikes. Volume and temperature often get separate handles. They cost more than pressure-balance.

    Cartridge and Stem Replacement in Brentwood, NY

    Most dripping tub faucets need internals, not a new valve. We replace Moen and Delta cartridges or old compression stems and seats, and we grease the O-rings with silicone plumber's grease so the handle turns smoothly for years. Seat wrenches come out often. About an hour, usually.
