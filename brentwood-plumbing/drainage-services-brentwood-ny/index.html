    Channel and Trench Drains in Brentwood, NY

    Driveways and patios that slope toward the house can send runoff straight into a garage or a door threshold. A trench drain cut into the concrete stops it. We saw-cut the slab, set a polymer or concrete channel with a grate rated for the traffic it will see, and pipe it in solid PVC to a safe discharge point away from the foundation.

    Outdoor & Storm Drain Cleaning in Brentwood

    Area drains, catch basins and driveway trench drains fill with leaves and silt. Once the grate is buried, water sheets across the yard or into the garage instead of going down the drain, and the pipe below can pack solid with mud. Basins get cleaned out and the pipe gets jetted. Fall cleaning helps most.

    Hydro-Jetting Services in Brentwood

    A hydro jetter sends water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full pipe wall. Cables punch through clogs, but jetting removes the grease and scale coating the pipe, which is what keeps causing them. Restaurants need it most. A camera check comes first to confirm the pipe can take it.

    Preventive Drain Maintenance in Brentwood, NY

    Scheduled drain cleaning costs less than one emergency call. Maintenance plans suit root-prone sewers and old cast iron, and every visit includes a camera check. The footage gets saved. Comparing it year to year shows whether roots are growing back faster.

    Sump Pump Services in Brentwood, NY

    A sump pump protects a basement or crawlspace from groundwater. We test the float, check the discharge line and check valve, and replace pumps that hum, run nonstop, or cannot keep up during a hard rain. Discharge must go outside. A frozen or buried discharge line is a common reason a working pump still floods the pit.

    Drainage System Maintenance in Brentwood

    Maintenance on a drain system costs less than one emergency visit. For homes with a history of root intrusion, we cut roots from the sewer every 12 to 18 months and camera the line so we can see if a section needs repair before it collapses. We flush yard drain lines as well.

    Commercial Drainage Services in Brentwood

    Retail centers and office buildings need floor drains, roof drains and parking lot catch basins that keep working. We set up service schedules for property managers. Silted basins can flood a lot and the suites next to it, so we clean and jet them ahead of the wet season.

    French Drain Installation in Brentwood

    A French drain is a gravel-filled trench with perforated pipe that collects groundwater and carries it away. We wrap the gravel in filter fabric so silt does not clog it. The pipe needs at least a 1 percent slope to a daylight outlet, a dry well or a sump, and the outlet end needs a grate so animals do not nest in it.
