    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Niagara Falls, NY

    Going tankless means the heater fires only when a tap opens, and it never runs out. Gas tankless models usually need a bigger gas line than the old tank had, plus a dedicated vent run through the wall or roof. Upfront cost is higher. We check gas capacity before quoting.

    Water Heater Installation Cost in Niagara Falls, NY

    Up front, a tank heater costs less than tankless. Tankless jobs cost more mostly because of gas line and vent work, while attic installs, moves and code updates add labor to any type. Permits are included. You also get an estimate of yearly energy cost for each option.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Niagara Falls

    Swapping a gas water heater comes down to three connections done right, the gas, the vent and the water. We install a new flex connector with a sediment trap, check vent pitch and draft and test every joint with a gas leak detector. Carbon monoxide is the risk. We follow code on all of it.

    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Niagara Falls

    A gas tank is the most common water heater we install, in 40, 50 and 75 gallon sizes. Every job gets a fresh connector, a drip leg, a shutoff and a vent connector with the right rise toward the chimney or flue. We test draft before leaving. Gas suits busy households.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Niagara Falls

    Closed plumbing systems trap the extra volume water gains when it heats. Permits are commonly needed for water heater swaps, gas lines and sewer work, and HCL Plumbing handles the filing and inspections. An expansion tank gives that water somewhere to go, so the relief valve stops dripping and pipe joints stop taking pressure spikes every time the burner runs. We pre-charge it to your house pressure. It mounts on the cold inlet.
