    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Niagara Falls, NY

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. HCL Plumbing installs these with a properly sized resin tank and brine tank.     Every one to two months is typical, depending on water use and hardness. Metered systems use salt more efficiently.

    Adding salt every 4 to 8 weeks is most of the work. Once a year a plumber should check for salt bridges, worn seals and injector clogs. Keep the salt level up.
