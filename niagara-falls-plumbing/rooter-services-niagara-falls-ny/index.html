    Hydro-Jetting Services in Niagara Falls, NY

    Jetting puts water through a nozzle at 3,000 to 4,000 psi and scrubs the pipe wall clean. It strips grease, scale and root hair that a cable slides past. Homeowners around Niagara Falls with hard water often add a softener to protect water heaters and keep mineral buildup out of faucets. The pipe gets a camera check first. Cracked clay or thin cast iron can be hurt by that pressure, so we only jet a line we have seen and trust.

    Floor Drain Rooter in Niagara Falls
