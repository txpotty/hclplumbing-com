    Digital shower systems like U by Moen and Kohler DTV need a little more planning. These valves usually sit in a nearby closet or vanity and need low-voltage wiring to the controller and an outlet close by. The outlet is an electrician's job. We plumb the valve and outlets.

    Shower Stall Installation in Niagara Falls

    A one-piece stall needs a level, fully supported base.     Commercial Shower Installation in Niagara Falls, NY

    Shower pans fail quietly. Pinholes and loose drain clamps let water into the subfloor slowly, which is why tiled pans sit flooded for a full day before tile. We level prefab pans carefully. Either way, the slope runs to the drain.
