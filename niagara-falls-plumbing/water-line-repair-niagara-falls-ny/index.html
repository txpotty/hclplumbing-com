    Main Water Line Repair in Niagara Falls

    Your main line is the pipe from the meter to the house, usually 3/4 inch or 1 inch, buried anywhere from a foot to several feet down depending on frost depth.     Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Niagara Falls

    A slab leak is a supply pipe leaking under the concrete floor of the house. Signs include a warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water and a meter that moves with everything off. Acoustic gear and a thermal camera tell us where it is.
