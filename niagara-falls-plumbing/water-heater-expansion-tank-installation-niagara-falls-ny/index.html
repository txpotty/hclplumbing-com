    Expansion Tank Sizing for Large Water Heaters in Niagara Falls

    A standard 2 gallon tank works for many 40 and 50 gallon heaters at moderate pressure. Larger heaters, higher incoming pressure or high-capacity systems may need a 4.5 gallon tank or larger. We use manufacturer sizing charts that factor in heater volume, supply pressure and temperature setting. Correct sizing keeps the tank from over-flexing and failing early.

    Preventing Water Heater Damage in Niagara Falls

    Thermal expansion is a leading cause of premature water heater failure in Niagara Falls homes. Without an expansion tank, constant pressure swings stress the water heater's tank, connections and internal components, leading to leaks, reduced efficiency and a shorter lifespan. Our installation service solves this issue, providing a buffer that protects your investment. This small addition can save you thousands in future water heater repair or replacement costs.
