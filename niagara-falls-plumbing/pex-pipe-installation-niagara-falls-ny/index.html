    Why Choose HCL Plumbing for PEX Pipe Installation in Niagara Falls, NY?

    Manufacturer rules for PEX are specific: supports about every 32 inches on horizontal runs, a minimum bend radius, and no long sun exposure. We follow them on every run. Skipping them leads to kinks, sagging lines and fittings that strain, and it can void the pipe warranty the manufacturer would otherwise honor.

    • Lower Install Cost, PEX costs less per foot than copper and goes in faster.
    • Freeze Tolerance, PEX stretches as ice forms and often survives a freeze that would split copper.
    • No Scale Buildup, Mineral scale has a hard time sticking on the smooth inner wall.

    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Niagara Falls

    Homeowners ask us which one is better, and it really depends on the house. PEX-A bends tighter, recovers from a kink with a heat gun, and its expansion fittings flow more water, while PEX-B costs less and its crimp joints are just as reliable when they are gauged properly. Either one lasts for decades. For tight attics we lean toward PEX-A.

    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Niagara Falls, NY

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A is the most flexible and joins with expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and joins with crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C sits closer to B in feel. Every one is listed for potable water. We match the pipe to the fittings and tools from one maker so the warranty stays intact.
