    Stainless Steel Sink Installation in Niagara Falls

    Stainless sinks come in gauges from 16 to 22, and the lower the number, the thicker the steel. Thin 22 gauge bowls flex and drum when the disposal runs, so we add sound pads or suggest an 18 gauge or heavier sink if you have not bought one yet. Top-mount and undermount plumb differently. Either way, we hook up the faucet, strainers and disposal.

    Workstation Sink Installation in Niagara Falls, NY

    Workstation sinks have a ledge for cutting boards, colanders and drying racks, and most are single deep bowls with an offset drain. The trap layout follows the offset drain, which lets the disposal fit and leaves storage room on one side. Check the faucet reach as well.

    Kitchen Sink Installation Cost Factors in Niagara Falls, NY

    A drop-in swap costs less than an undermount on stone. Each extra, like a new disposal, a moved drain, a lowered wall stub or replacing galvanized stubs, adds labor and shows up as its own line on the quote. Plumbing is priced apart from the counter.

    Undermount Sink Installation in Niagara Falls, NY

    Undermounts hang from clips anchored into the underside of the stone, plus a bead of silicone along the rim. We support the bowl from below with a brace while the silicone cures, typically for a full day, before loading it with water. Rushing this causes the classic sag. Just an eighth of an inch of sag leaves a gap that catches water and food along the edge.

    Drop-In Sink Replacement in Niagara Falls

    Pulling an old drop-in starts with cutting the caulk line and backing off the clips from below. Old clips are often rusted solid, so we bring a reciprocating saw and cut them off rather than prying on the counter. Laminate edges chip easily. Your new rim has to fully cover the old hole.

    Composite Sink Installation in Niagara Falls

    Granite and quartz composite sinks are tough on top but can chip at the drain opening if a strainer is overtightened. We tighten by hand first. Most composite brands want silicone rather than putty under the strainer because putty can stain them, so we go by the maker's instructions.

    Drop-In Kitchen Sink Installation in Niagara Falls

    Sitting on the counter by their rim, drop-in sinks are a natural fit for laminate, butcher block and tile. It is often the quickest swap. After checking the cutout, we seal the rim and draw the clips down evenly, and in older homes and rentals it is often a two-hour job.

    Granite and Quartz Composite Sinks in Niagara Falls

    Composite sinks are heavier than stainless and usually thicker at the drain, so standard strainers sometimes need a longer threaded body. Strainer fit gets checked first. If the included strainer is too short for the sink wall, we swap in one that fits rather than overtightening and risking a chip around the opening.
