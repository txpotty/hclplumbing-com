    Main Service Line Leak Detection in Albany, NY

    The line from the meter to your house carries full pressure and can leak underground for a long time. Wet soil, soft spots or oddly green patches of yard may be the only clues. We trace the line, listen for the leak and mark it for a targeted repair. Replacing old galvanized or polybutylene service lines is often recommended.

    Slab Leak Detection in Albany

    Slab leaks are common in homes on shifting soil and moving foundations. These leaks occur in pipes beneath your concrete slab, often signaled by warm spots on the floor, sudden spikes in your water bill or the sound of running water when all fixtures are off. HCL Plumbing uses dedicated acoustic listening devices and thermal imaging cameras to precisely locate these hidden leaks without destructive guesswork. Early detection is key to preventing major foundation damage.

    Toilet and Fixture Leak Detection in Albany, NY

    Toilets are one of the most common sources of wasted water. A worn flapper or a fill valve that never shuts off can leak silently around the clock. We use dye tests to find leaking toilets and check faucets, showers and supply lines for drips. These fixes are usually quick and save water right away.

    Irrigation Leak Detection in Albany

    Pool Water Leak Detection in Albany, NY
