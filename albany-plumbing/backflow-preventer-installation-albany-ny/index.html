    Commercial properties in Albany, NY often have complex plumbing systems that pose a higher risk of cross-contamination and require robust backflow prevention.     Backflow Testing and Certification in Albany

    Most water providers require testable backflow assemblies to be certified at installation and then once a year. Our certified testers check each valve's opening point, relief valve operation and differential pressure. If a device fails, we can repair or rebuild it on the spot with genuine parts. We submit passing results so you avoid compliance letters or service interruption.

    Reduced Pressure Zone Assembly Installation in Albany, NY

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of protection and are required for high hazard connections like commercial kitchens, medical facilities, chemical injection and car washes. They include two independent check valves with a relief valve that dumps water if either check fails. Because the relief valve can discharge, we plan for drainage and install the assembly above grade where it can be tested easily. We carry trusted brands like Watts, Febco, Wilkins and Apollo.

    Irrigation Backflow Preventer Installation in Albany

    Landscape irrigation is the most common reason homeowners need a backflow device. Fertilizer, herbicides and stagnant water in sprinkler and drip lines can be siphoned into your drinking water if pressure drops. We install pressure vacuum breakers or double check assemblies based on local requirements and the design of your system. Above-ground installs are set at least 12 inches above the highest sprinkler head when a PVB is used.

    Backflow Enclosures and Freeze Protection in Albany, NY

    Above-ground assemblies sit in direct sun in summer and can freeze on cold nights. Lake-effect snow and deep cold snaps can keep temperatures below freezing for days, so insulating exposed lines pays off. We install insulated enclosures, freeze blankets or heated cabinets based on your location. Enclosures also discourage theft, since brass backflow assemblies are a common target for scrap thieves. We choose enclosures with enough room for testing and repairs.

    Commercial Backflow Preventer Installation in Albany

    Restaurants, salons, dental offices and light industrial buildings often need multiple backflow devices on soda machines, boilers, dishwashers and process water. We survey the building, identify each cross-connection and install containment and isolation assemblies as required. Work is scheduled after hours or on weekends when needed to avoid shutting down your business. You receive documentation for each device for your records and your water provider.

    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Device Installation in Albany, NY

    For high-hazard applications in Albany, NY where toxic substances could contaminate the water supply, a reduced pressure zone, or RPZ, device is usually required. RPZ backflow preventers offer the highest level of protection, using a relief valve to discharge water if the pressure drops. HCL Plumbing specializes in RPZ installation, making sure all components are correctly sized, positioned and set up according to state and local plumbing codes. Proper installation is critical for effective operation.

    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Albany

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules permit. We size them to avoid too much pressure loss on your system.
