    Batch Feed vs Continuous Feed in Albany

    Continuous feed is the type most kitchens have. Flip the switch and it runs until you flip it off. A batch feed unit needs its stopper locked in to run, keeping hands out of the chamber, and it needs no wall switch. Each type gets wired its own way.

    Standard InSinkErator Installation in Albany

    A standard install replaces the unit under an existing sink. The old disposal comes down, the flange gets scraped clean and a new one goes in on fresh putty. Then the mount ring goes on.
