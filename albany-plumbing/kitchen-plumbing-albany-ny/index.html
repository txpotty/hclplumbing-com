    Grease Trap Installation in Albany, NY

    Under-sink grease traps work for small kitchens, while full restaurants often need an in-ground interceptor sized to the local sewer authority's rules. We read the requirement before we quote. Sizing is based on fixture flow, and an undersized trap fills fast and lets grease pass straight into the sewer line.

    Grease Trap Installation and Cleaning in Albany

    Grease traps are sized by flow, and a three-compartment sink typically calls for a trap rated at 20 gallons per minute or more. We set the trap level, install the flow control fitting and vent it so it does not siphon. How often it gets cleaned depends on volume. A kitchen running fryers all day may need service every couple of weeks.

    Kitchen Drain Clearing in Albany, NY

    Water rising in the second bowl when the first drains tells us the clog is past the trap, in the branch line behind the cabinet. We run a 1/4 or 3/8 inch cable from the cleanout or the trap arm, and on grease-packed lines we follow up with a jetter. Chemical cleaners rarely touch grease. What they do is sit in the trap and weaken the glue joints.

    Emergency Kitchen Plumbing in Albany, NY

    Grease Trap Cleaning in Albany

    A grease trap that smells sour is normally overdue for a pump-out.
