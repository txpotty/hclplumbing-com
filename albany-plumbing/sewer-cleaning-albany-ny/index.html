    Preventative Sewer Maintenance in Albany

    Roots come back after cutting. Usually within a year or so. A scheduled cleaning every year or two keeps roots and grease from closing the line and lets you plan around it.

    Shop and Restaurant Sewers in Albany

    Sewer Camera Inspections in Albany, NY

    On a repeat backup, the camera goes in first. Guessing costs money. A waterproof color camera on a push rod shows the inside of the line live on a screen, so we can see the clog, measure its distance from the cleanout and spot any crack or sag.

    Cast Iron Sewer Cleaning in Albany, NY

    Cast iron rots from the inside. Lead service lines remain in some older neighborhoods, and replacing them is a common recommendation during major plumbing work. The bottom of the pipe wears first. Aggressive cutters can break through thin spots, so we use descaling chains and careful jetting made for older iron, which restores flow and gives a clear view of the remaining pipe wall on camera.

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Lines in Albany

    Older properties may still have clay or Orangeburg pipe. Both have weak spots. Roots love clay joints, while Orangeburg fiber pipe tends to squash into an oval and eventually collapse, so we clean these lines carefully and map the joints and soft spots on camera.
