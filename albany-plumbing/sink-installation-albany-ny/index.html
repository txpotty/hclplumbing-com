    Each Kind of Sink We Install in Albany, NY

    Drop-In Sink Installation in Albany, NY

    Drop-in sinks are the most forgiving style. The rim hides small cutout flaws, so laminate tops take a drop-in with just a size check and the right sealant. Clips pull it tight to the counter. Stainless drop-ins weigh very little.

    Wall-Mount and ADA Sink Installation in Albany, NY

    Wall-hung lavatories need a carrier or heavy blocking. ADA layouts call for a rim at or under 34 inches, open knee space, and insulated pipes below. Offset drains help with knee space. Lever or sensor faucets finish it off.

    Kitchen Sink Installation in Albany

    Kitchen sinks come in four mounts: drop-in, undermount, farmhouse and flush-set. Each one needs its own support, and a cast iron farmhouse sink can weigh well over a hundred pounds before it holds any water, so the cabinet often needs reinforcement first. We check the cabinet before the sink arrives. Disposal and dishwasher connections are part of the job.

    Undermount Clips and Cure Time in Albany

    Undermount bowls need overnight to cure. The silicone bead under the rim reaches handling strength in a few hours but needs about a day before it carries the weight of a full basin of water and a pot leaning on the edge. The bowl stays braced until our return. Plumbing goes in on the second visit.

    Emergency Sink Replacement in Albany

    A cracked bowl cannot be patched for long. If your sink split overnight or the drain body rusted through and dumped water into the cabinet, we can pull it, cap the lines so the rest of the house has water, and set a replacement fast. Common sizes can go in the same day. Odd cutouts take longer.

    Restaurant and Office Sink Installation in Albany

    Breakroom and prep sinks get used hard. Stainless bowls with commercial faucets go in strong enough for a full bowl and a stack of dishes. Downtime gets planned around your hours. Night installs are possible.

    Commercial Sink Installation in Albany, NY

    Commercial kitchens need sinks the health inspector will sign off on. Three-compartment sinks usually drain indirectly through an air gap to a floor sink, and hand sinks need tempered water within easy reach of every prep station on the line. We read the plan review first. Mop basins and grease interceptor ties are part of it.

    Leak-Proof Sink Connections in Albany, NY

    Every joint gets tested before we leave. The bowl gets filled and drained while we watch every nut under the sink with a light and a dry towel. A towel shows a weep fast. Pipe dope goes on threads, not compression fittings.
