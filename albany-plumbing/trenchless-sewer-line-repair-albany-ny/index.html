    Sewer Relining and Pipe Bursting in Albany, NY

    Sewer Camera Inspection for Trenchless Repair in Albany

    Every trenchless job starts with a push camera fed in from the cleanout or a pulled toilet. The screen shows the inside of the pipe live, and a sonde in the camera head lets us mark the exact spot on the ground above it. The locator also reads depth, which matters for pit planning. No guessing where to dig.

    Sectional Point Repair in Albany

    A point repair is a short liner, often 3 to 5 feet long, set over one bad spot. It rides in on an inflatable packer, guided by the camera footage, and the packer inflates to press the resin-soaked sleeve against the pipe wall until it cures. It fixes a single crack or root entry, not a whole failing line. Sound lines with one bad joint are the right candidates.

    Cost Comparison of Trenchless vs Traditional in Albany, NY

    Depth changes the math. A line two feet down in open lawn is often cheaper to dig than to line, but a lateral eight feet deep that runs under the driveway and a porch costs far more to excavate than to burst or line from two pits. We quote both ways. Then you pick.

    Emergency Trenchless Sewer Repair in Albany

    Trenchless vs Traditional Sewer Repair Costs in Albany, NY

    Compare total cost, not just the plumbing line on the invoice. Most of those extra costs never show up with a liner or a burst. Sod and sprinkler repair add up too.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Repair in Albany

    Bursting can upsize the line. We can pull in 6-inch HDPE behind a head that bursts an old 4-inch clay pipe, which helps on lines that clog because they were undersized from the start or have been added onto with extra bathrooms. Each end only needs a pit around three by four feet. No trench in between.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Sewer Repair Cost in Albany, NY

    Point repairs often cost $1,500 to $3,500, while full lining of a residential lateral often runs $4,000 to $12,000 depending on length and diameter. Pipe bursting typically ranges from $5,000 to $15,000. Open digging can be cheaper on short, shallow runs. Once you add back concrete and sod, the gap usually closes, and we price both options side by side so you can see the difference.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Albany, NY

    Roots enter at the joints, where clay bells or cast iron hubs have a small gap or a failed gasket. Once inside, they branch into a mat that fills the pipe. We cut them out with a mechanical root cutter or a hydro jet, then line the pipe so those joints are sealed for good and the line stops needing a cleaning every few months. The roots cannot get back in.
