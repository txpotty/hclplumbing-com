    Water-Only Drain Cleaning in Albany, NY

    Hydrojetting uses only water, so nothing caustic goes into your pipes or septic tank. Chemical cleaners can damage pipe. Jetting removes the buildup physically, and the debris flushes to the sewer or septic tank as normal waste, so there is nothing left in the pipe to eat at joints or harm the bacteria a septic system depends on.

    Main Sewer Line Hydrojetting in Albany

    The main sewer carries every drain in the house, so a blockage there backs up the lowest fixture first. We jet the full main to the street tap. Four-inch mains get a heavy nozzle and high flow to push debris downstream.

    Hydrojetting vs. Snaking in Albany, NY

    Snaking pokes a hole through a clog. Jetting cleans the whole pipe. Snaking suits a one-time clog, while jetting fixes a line that clogs repeatedly.

    Grease Trap Line Cleaning in Albany, NY

    Restaurant kitchen lines send warm grease downstream where it cools and sticks. By the time it reaches the interceptor, a lot of it has already coated the pipe. Short jetting passes keep loosened grease from plugging the line downstream. Quarterly works for most.

    Sewer Lateral Jetting in Albany, NY

    The lateral from your house to the street handles every drain in the house, so buildup there causes backups everywhere at once. We jet it from the cleanout to the tap. Settled low spots get extra passes, and we mark them on the video for future reference.

    Hydrojetting vs Snaking Cost Comparison in Albany, NY

    Snaking a main line typically costs $200 to $450, while residential hydrojetting usually runs $400 to $900 with a camera inspection. Jetting costs more up front. Fewer repeat visits often make jetting cheaper over a few years.

    Kitchen Grease Line Jetting in Albany

    Kitchen drains collect grease that cools and hardens along the pipe wall, especially in long horizontal runs under a slab. Cabling only cuts a narrow channel. A spinning nozzle removes the grease all around the pipe so it flushes away cleanly.

    After-Hours Hydrojet Service in Albany

    Backups at night or on weekends are handled with the same gear as any weekday call. On-call trucks are fully equipped. That means we can clear and jet a grease-packed restaurant line or a root-filled home sewer in one visit, without waiting until Monday to bring the jetter back for a second trip.

    Drain Jetting Near Me in Albany, NY

    A local jetting crew has a good idea of the pipe before arriving. Older blocks usually mean clay. PVC takes full pressure and old clay does not, so we bring the right nozzles.

    Working locally helps us plan each job before we arrive. Clay laterals get root heads. Restaurant lines get grease nozzles and short passes, and cast iron in older homes gets lower pressure and a scale nozzle, so the right setup is ready instead of improvised in your driveway.
