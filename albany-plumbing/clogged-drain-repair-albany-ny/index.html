    Tub and Shower Drain Clogs in Albany

    Hair and soap scum are the most common causes of slow tubs and showers. We remove the strainer or overflow plate and pull the clog out with a drum auger designed for small diameter drains. If the drain is still slow, we check the trap under the slab or in the access panel. We take care not to scratch acrylic, fiberglass or porcelain surfaces.

    Laundry and Utility Drain Clogs in Albany, NY

    Washing machines discharge a lot of water quickly, and lint, fabric fibers and detergent residue build up in the standpipe and branch line. A clogged laundry drain often overflows at the standpipe mid-cycle. We clear the line with a cable and recommend a lint trap for the discharge hose. In some homes the laundry line ties into the kitchen line, so we inspect both.

    Bathroom Sink and Vanity Drain Clogs in Albany, NY

    Frequently Asked Questions About Clogged Drain Repair in Albany

    The cost of clogged drain repair in Albany, NY can range from $150 to $500 for a standard clog, depending on severity and location. More complex issues like main line clogs requiring hydro-jetting or camera inspection might range from $400 to $1000 or more. We provide transparent, upfront pricing after a thorough diagnosis.

    Most single fixture clogs are cleared in 30 to 90 minutes. Lines with heavy buildup or limited access can take longer. We tell you if the job will run beyond the usual time.

    Gurgling sounds, water backing up into a different fixture and frequent clogs in several drains signal a deeper issue. Sewage odors or water around a floor drain are also warning signs. These often mean the main line needs attention.

    A plunger or a hand-held hair snake can handle many simple tub and sink clogs. If the clog returns, affects more than one fixture or does not respond to a plunger, call a plumber. Forcing a cable can crack old pipes or scratch fixtures.

    While chemical drain cleaners are widely available, HCL Plumbing generally advises against them for homes in Albany. They often provide only temporary relief, can damage pipes over time, and pose environmental and health risks. Professional tools like augers or hydro-jetting are safer and more effective.

    Drain snaking uses a flexible auger to physically break through or pull out blockages. Hydro-jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the inside of pipes, effectively removing scale, grease, and roots, providing a much more complete cleaning. Hydro-jetting is often used for tough clogs or preventative maintenance.

    We use professional-grade equipment from trusted manufacturers known for durability and effectiveness. This includes drain snakes and augers from brands like RIDGID, high-definition sewer cameras for inspection, and powerful hydro-jetting machines designed for different pipe sizes and clog types.

    Homeowners around Albany with hard water often add a softener to protect water heaters and keep mineral buildup out of faucets. Hard water leaves mineral scale that narrows pipe walls and gives grease and hair something to grab. Older homes may also have cast iron pipes that rust and roughen inside. Both conditions make clogs form faster.
