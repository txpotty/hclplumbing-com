    Frequently Asked Questions About Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Albany

    The cost of hot water tank maintenance in Albany, NY generally ranges from $100 to $250, depending on the tank type, age and specific services required. This normally includes flushing, anode rod inspection and safety valve checks. We provide upfront pricing before any work begins.

    The anode rod, also known as a sacrificial rod, is a metal rod inside your hot water tank designed to corrode before the tank itself. It protects the tank lining from rust and corrosion, which matters most when water carries a lot of minerals, and it lengthens the lifespan of your unit.

    That smell typically comes from bacteria reacting with a magnesium anode. We can switch to an aluminum-zinc or powered anode and sanitize the tank to get rid of it.

    In mineral-heavy water a magnesium rod may last three to five years. We measure it at every visit so you replace it before the tank lining begins to rust.

    While some basic steps like draining a small amount of water can be done by a homeowner, professional hot water tank maintenance involves specialized tools and experience to properly inspect electrical or gas components, anode rods and pressure relief valves. It is best left to a professional to ensure safety and code compliance.
