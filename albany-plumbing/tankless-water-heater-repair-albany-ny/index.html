    Tankless Descaling and Flushing in Albany

    Rinnai shows an LC code and Navien posts a scale warning when the exchanger runs hot from mineral buildup.     Ignition Failure Repair in Albany, NY

    No ignition codes have four usual causes: a dirty flame rod, a bad igniter, low gas pressure or a blocked intake. Undersized gas pipe is the one people miss. A tankless burner can need 150,000 to 199,000 BTU, and the half-inch line that fed a 40,000 BTU tank often cannot carry that, which is why it fires at low flow and quits when two showers are on.

    Same-Day Tankless Repair in Albany

    Igniter and Flame Rod Repair in Albany, NY

    Gas tankless heaters light with a hot surface or spark igniter, then prove flame with a flame rod. A rod coated in oxide reads low on a microamp meter and the board shuts the gas valve after a few seconds. We clean or replace the rod, look for a cracked igniter or a lazy spark, and confirm the gas valve opens on call. Five-minute fix sometimes.

    Navien Condensate and Fan Repair in Albany

    Navien's E016 is a heat exchanger overheat, and scale or a backed-up condensate line is usually behind it. We clean the trap and screen, then retest.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling Service in Albany

    Scale is calcium and magnesium carbonate that drops out of hard water when it heats, and a tankless exchanger heats water fast through narrow passages.     Gas Tankless Water Heater Repair in Albany

    Pressure at full fire matters on a gas tankless burner, not just at the moment it lights.     Electric Tankless Element Repair in Albany, NY

We check each element's resistance, measure voltage under load and check amperage on each leg, then confirm the panel and wire size match the unit's rating.
