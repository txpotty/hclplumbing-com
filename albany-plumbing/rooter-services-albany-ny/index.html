    Main Sewer Line Rooter in Albany

    A clogged main sewer line can cause widespread backups throughout your Albany property. Our main sewer line rooter service addresses blockages caused by tree roots, sludge, or collapsed pipes. We use camera inspections to locate the exact location and nature of the clog, then use powerful rooter machines or hydro-jetting to clear it. This critical service protects your entire plumbing system.

    Sewer Root Removal in Albany

    Tree roots are the leading cause of main sewer clogs in many older neighborhoods. Roots from shade trees and thirsty landscape plants enter through tiny gaps in clay and cast iron joints and grow into thick mats. We run a heavy sewer machine with a root saw head to cut them back to the pipe wall. Regular rooting every 12 to 24 months keeps many lines open.

    Hydro-Jetting Services in Albany, NY

    For the most stubborn clogs and extensive pipe buildup, HCL Plumbing offers hydro-jetting services in Albany, NY. This technique uses high-pressure water to scour the inside of your pipes, removing grease, mineral deposits, and even tree roots. Water hardness in New York varies a lot, with many surface water supplies running soft and groundwater in some areas running mineral-heavy. Hydro-jetting cleans pipes more thoroughly than traditional snaking, restoring them to near-original condition and reducing future clogs.

    Emergency Rooter Service in Albany

    A sudden sewer backup or completely blocked drain can be an emergency. HCL Plumbing offers emergency rooter services in Albany, available to address urgent plumbing crises. Our rapid response team arrives equipped to diagnose and resolve severe blockages quickly, limiting water damage and restoring function to your home or business as fast as possible.

    Rooter Plus Hydro Jetting in Albany

    Rooting cuts through a clog, while jetting scours the inside of the pipe. Combining the two clears heavy roots and grease more thoroughly than either one by itself. We camera the line before jetting to make sure the pipe can handle the pressure. This combination is a common choice for restaurants and homes with recurring root problems.

    Toilet and Bathroom Rooter Service in Albany

    Toilets clog from too much paper, wipes and objects dropped by kids. We use a closet auger designed to protect the porcelain and, when needed, pull the toilet to root the line beneath it. Bathroom sinks and tubs get a smaller cable that removes hair and soap buildup. We reset any fixture we remove with a new wax ring or gasket.

    Kitchen Line Rooter Service in Albany, NY

    Kitchen drains collect grease, soap and food that harden into stubborn plugs. We access the line through the trap or a cleanout and use a medium cable with a spiral cutter. For very heavy grease, a follow-up jetting pass washes the pipe walls clean. This approach keeps the drain flowing well for longer.
