    Underground Pipe Repair in Albany

    Buried pipe takes stress from settling soil, roots and vehicles. Leaks hide well. We locate underground leaks with acoustic equipment and tracer gas, then dig a small pit at the mark or pull new pipe through the old path when the line is too far gone to patch. Every repair gets a pressure test.

    Pinhole Leak Repair in Albany

    Pinhole leaks form when the copper wall thins from the inside. Water chemistry causes most. High velocity, poor grounding and aggressive water all speed it up, and the leak starts as a tiny drip that stains drywall or a ceiling, so we cut out the affected section and replace it with new copper or a PEX transition. One usually means more are coming.

    Pipe Leak Detection in Albany, NY

    A repair starts with finding the leak. Guessing wastes drywall. We use electronic listening devices, thermal cameras and moisture meters, shutting off zones one at a time until the leak has nowhere to hide. One cut, one fix.

    Leaking Pipe Repair in Albany, NY

    Even a slow drip can rot a subfloor in a year. They hide inside walls. We find leaks with moisture meters, thermal imaging and acoustic gear, cutting one access hole sized to the repair rather than a string of guesses. Older Albany homes see this a lot.

    PEX and CPVC Pipe Repair in Albany, NY

    Plastic supply pipe like PEX and CPVC is common in homes built over the last few decades. Both have weak points. PEX can fail at crimp rings, older brass fittings or where it was exposed to UV light in an attic, while CPVC turns brittle with age and heat and cracks when bumped, so we replace failed fittings and damaged sections with parts matched to each material. We cut CPVC carefully.

    Corroded Pipe Replacement in Albany

    Galvanized steel and old copper both corrode with age. Brown water is a clue. Low pressure, discolored water and leaks at threaded joints tell us the pipe is closing off from the inside, and we replace the worst sections or the whole run with PEX or copper and dielectric fittings at every transition. Pressure comes right back.
