    Frequently Asked Questions About Drainage Services in Albany

    Drain clearing in Albany typically runs $125 to $500 depending on the line, and hydro jetting costs $400 to $900. Yard drainage projects such as French drains or dry wells typically range from $1,500 to $6,000. We give you a written price after inspecting the problem.

    We use professional equipment from trusted brands like Spartan Tool, RIDGID, and Gorlitz for our drain cleaning and inspection services. These brands are known for their reliability and effectiveness in clearing even the toughest clogs, ensuring a high-quality repair.

    Most standard residential drain cleaning services, like clearing a sink or toilet clog, can be completed within 1 to 2 hours. More complex jobs, such as main sewer line clearing or hydro-jetting, may take 2 to 4 hours, depending on the severity and access of the blockage.

    A French drain collects water that soaks into soil and is best along foundations and low areas. A channel drain catches water flowing across hard surfaces like driveways and patios. Many properties benefit from both.

    Tree roots are attracted to the water and nutrients inside sewer pipes. Small cracks or loose joints in the pipes, commonly found in older systems, allow roots to enter and grow, eventually creating significant blockages. This is a frequent issue in New York neighborhoods where many homes have mature trees.

    Watch for slow or gurgling drains, sewer odors, water pooling near the house after storms, soil erosion and cracks in walkways. Standing water in the yard for more than a day is another sign. Call us to have it looked at.

    We do not recommend using chemical drain cleaners. They can be corrosive, potentially damaging your pipes, especially older ones, and are often ineffective against tough clogs. They also pose a safety risk to anyone who later works on the drain. Professional tools are much safer and work better.

    Drain snaking uses a flexible auger to mechanically break through or retrieve a blockage.
