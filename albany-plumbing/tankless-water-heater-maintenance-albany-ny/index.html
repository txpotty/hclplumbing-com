    Service Valve Kit Installs in Albany, NY

    Plenty of tankless heaters went in without service valves, and without them there is no clean way to hook up a descaling pump. We add a pair of isolation valves with purge ports on the hot and cold lines. The kit usually has a relief valve port too. After that, every flush takes minutes to set up.

    Inlet Screen and Air Filter Cleaning in Albany

    Grit gets in. A small screen at the cold inlet catches sand and scale flakes, especially after a main break. Units with an air intake filter collect dust and lint, which can push the burner into a combustion code. We clean both on every visit.

    Recirculation and Combi Unit Service in Albany, NY

    Built-in recirculation pumps and buffer tanks get hot water to the tap faster, but they also push more water through the exchanger every day, so those units scale sooner. The recirc loop gets flushed with the exchanger. Then we check whether the system uses a crossover valve or a dedicated return line and set the schedule to match.

    Noritz Tankless Tune-Ups in Albany, NY

    Noritz builds a solid heater, but solid does not mean maintenance-free. We start a Noritz tune-up at the exhaust, checking for soot and any blocked termination. Next comes the combustion air intake and the fan. Last, we check the temperature setting and run a few fixtures at once, which shows whether the unit holds temperature between back-to-back showers.

    Home Tankless Flushing in Albany

    Water hardness in New York varies a lot, with many surface water supplies running soft and groundwater in some areas running hard. Inside a tankless heater, that water runs through a small copper or stainless coil while the burner fires, and every minute of hot water leaves a little calcium behind. A pump on the service valves pushes descaling solution through the coil for roughly an hour. Flow comes back. The temperature holds steady and the high-limit shutdowns stop.

    Flush Now or Replace the Exchanger Later in Albany, NY
