    Frequently Asked Questions About Faucet Installation in Rochester

    Labor for a standard kitchen or bathroom faucet installation typically costs $150 to $350. Touchless faucets, wall-mount faucets and pot fillers range from $300 to $800 or more depending on the work involved. We give you a flat price up front.

    Most standard faucet installations can be completed within 1 to 3 hours. More complex installations, such as those requiring new drilling or significant plumbing changes, can take more time. We aim to complete the job efficiently while ensuring quality workmanship.

    Replacing a faucet does not typically require a permit. Adding new supply lines, like for a pot filler during a remodel, may be part of a larger permitted project.

    Common signs include persistent dripping or leaking, low water pressure specifically from the faucet, visible corrosion or rust, difficulty turning handles, or an outdated appearance. Constant repairs often signal it is time for a replacement.

    Water hardness in New York varies a lot, with many surface water supplies running soft and groundwater in some areas running mineral-heavy. Where water carries a lot of minerals, deposits can build up inside faucet components over time, causing reduced water flow, stubborn stains, and accelerated wear on internal seals and cartridges. Regular cleaning and professional installation can help limit these effects, and new faucets are often more resilient.

    Typically, a permit is not required for a simple like-for-like faucet replacement in Rochester. However, if the installation involves altering the main water lines, relocating a sink, or other major plumbing changes, a permit may be necessary. We can walk you through specific permit requirements.

    If your current faucet has internal blockages or a faulty aerator, a new faucet can improve water pressure at that specific fixture. However, if the low pressure is a whole-house issue, a new faucet alone will not solve the problem.

    We install all major faucet brands, including Moen, Delta, Kohler, American Standard, Pfister, and more. Our plumbers are well versed in the specific installation requirements for a wide range of manufacturers and models.
