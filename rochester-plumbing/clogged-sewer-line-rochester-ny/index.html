    Hydro-Jetting Services in Rochester, NY

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful method for clearing severe sewer line clogs, especially those caused by grease, scale, or heavy sludge buildup. This process uses highly pressurized water jets to scour the inside of your pipes, not only clearing the blockage but also cleaning the pipe walls. It is an effective preventative maintenance tool and is especially effective for older pipes in Rochester that may have collected years of debris. This technique is safe for most pipe types.

    Sewer Belly and Sagging Pipe Clogs in Rochester, NY

    Basements are standard in most homes, and many of them depend on sump pumps to manage groundwater during wet seasons. Soil that swells, shrinks or settles can cause sections of sewer pipe to sink and form a belly. Waste and water collect in the low spot and lead to repeat clogs. Our camera and locator show where the belly is and how deep it sits. We can then quote a spot repair to restore correct slope.

    Preventative Sewer Line Maintenance in Rochester

    Regular maintenance can greatly reduce the risk of future clogged sewer lines, especially for homes in Rochester with older plumbing or heavy tree cover. HCL Plumbing offers scheduled sewer line cleanings and inspections to prevent buildup of grease, sludge, and minor root growth before they become major problems.     Tree Root Sewer Clogs in Rochester

    Roots from thirsty trees and shrubs seek out moisture and push into sewer pipe joints. Clay and older cast iron pipe are especially prone to root intrusion because of their many joints. We cut roots with a sewer machine fitted with root saws and then camera the line to see how much damage remains. For ongoing root problems, we can recommend a root treatment schedule or pipe lining.

    Hydro-Jetting for Stubborn Clogs in Rochester

    HCL Plumbing uses powerful hydro-jetting technology to clear even the most stubborn sewer line blockages. This method uses high-pressure water streams to blast away grease, scale, mineral buildup, and tree roots, restoring your pipes to their original diameter. It's a very effective, non-invasive way to clean your sewer lines thoroughly and prevent future clogs. Unlike snaking, hydro-jetting cleans the entire pipe wall.

    Cleaning vs. Repairing a Sewer Line in Rochester, NY

    If the camera shows a sound pipe with a grease or root clog, cleaning is usually all you need. Cracked, offset or collapsed pipe will keep clogging no matter how often it is cleared. Cleaning typically costs a few hundred dollars, while repairs range from a spot fix to trenchless lining or full replacement. We walk you through the footage and costs so you can decide.
