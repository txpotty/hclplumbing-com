    High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in Rochester

    High-efficiency softeners use upflow brining and metered valves to cut salt and water use. Compared with an older timer-based unit, a modern metered softener can use roughly half the salt and noticeably less regeneration water each month. The savings add up. We install several models.

    Ecodyne Water Softener Installation in Rochester

    Ecodyne softeners are sold at many home stores, and we install them for homeowners who already bought one. Install follows the manufacturer manual for bypass, drain and overflow, then we set hardness and regeneration. Bring your own unit. Programming is part of the job.

    Whole-Home Water Softener Installation in Rochester

    Whole-home softeners treat every gallon that enters the house, right after the main shutoff. That protects every faucet, shower, water heater and appliance downstream, which is where hard water does most of its damage over the years. We size before we sell. Your test result and water use decide the tank size.

    Softener Loop Installation in Rochester

    No loop? A softener loop is a pair of capped stubs on the main line, usually in the garage, that lets a softener be connected without cutting pipe later. We cut it in for you. Outdoor taps can stay on hard water, which saves salt. Future service gets easier too.

    Commercial Water Softener Installation in Rochester, NY

    Restaurants, hotels and laundromats feel hard water in their equipment first. Commercial softeners we install use larger resin tanks or twin-tank alternating systems so soft water is available around the clock, even during regeneration. Equipment lasts longer. We schedule around your hours.

    Residential Water Softener Installation in Rochester

    Hard water costs homeowners money in shorter appliance life and extra cleaning. We test the water, ask about daily use and choose a capacity that lets the system regenerate roughly once a week. Weekly is the sweet spot. Every detail is handled on one visit.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Rochester

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. We add a bypass valve so you can isolate the softener for service, and we install a pressure regulator if your street pressure runs above 80 psi. Every tap gets soft water. We can skip the hose bibs.

    Water Hardness Testing and Programming in Rochester

    Testing is what makes the settings right. We test for hardness, iron and chlorine before installing, then program the regeneration frequency and salt dose so you get soft water without wasting salt. Iron adds to the setting. You get a walkthrough of the settings.
