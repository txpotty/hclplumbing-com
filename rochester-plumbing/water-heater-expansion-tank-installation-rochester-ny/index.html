    Expansion Tanks With Tankless Water Heaters in Rochester, NY

    Tankless water heaters hold very little water, so thermal expansion is minimal in most homes. However, homes with recirculation loops, storage buffer tanks or certain closed system setups may still benefit from an expansion tank. We evaluate your system and only recommend a tank when it serves a real purpose. This keeps you from paying for parts you do not need.

    Brands and Tank Materials in Rochester, NY

    We install potable-rated thermal expansion tanks from brands such as Watts, Amtrol and A.O. Smith. Potable tanks use a butyl diaphragm and a lined interior so water never touches bare steel. Water hardness in New York varies a lot, with many surface water supplies running soft and groundwater in some areas running hard. Stainless steel connections hold up against corrosion from hard or aggressive water. We avoid tanks designed only for hydronic heating, since they are not approved for drinking water.

    Expansion Tank Sizing for Large Water Heaters in Rochester

    A standard 2 gallon tank works for many 40 and 50 gallon heaters at moderate pressure. Larger heaters, higher incoming pressure or high-capacity systems may need a 4.5 gallon tank or larger. We use manufacturer sizing charts that factor in heater volume, supply pressure and temperature setting. Correct sizing keeps the tank from over-flexing and failing early.

    New Expansion Tank Installation in Rochester

    If your home has a pressure reducing valve and no expansion tank, the water heater is likely seeing a pressure spike every heating cycle. We install a new tank on the cold water supply near the heater using a tee and short nipple. The air side is precharged to match your static pressure, usually between 50 and 70 psi. We then run the heater and watch the gauge to confirm pressure stays stable.
