    Commercial Water Heater Replacement in Rochester

    Businesses in Rochester run their water heaters harder than homes do, so commercial replacements need more capacity and faster recovery. Restaurants, salons, laundromats and offices each have a different demand curve, and we size the new unit by peak gallons per hour and the temperature health code requires, which can mean a larger tank, a booster or a rack of tankless units. Downtime costs money.

    Rheem and AO Smith Water Heater Replacement in Rochester, NY

    Rheem and AO Smith make tanks in nearly every size, from 30-gallon short tanks to 80-gallon tall models. Both are solid, and the pick usually depends on what fits the space, the warranty length you want and what the supply house has in stock. We help you compare.

    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Rochester, NY

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters move heat from the surrounding air into the tank, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need room and airflow, about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they do best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. The space runs cooler.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Rochester, NY

    Gas water heaters recover faster than electric, and in Rochester, NY homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. Gas gets shut off and capped, the old tank drained and removed, and the new one set with a fresh connector, sediment trap and properly sloped vent. Every joint gets checked.

    Bradford White Water Heater Services in Rochester

    We use Bradford White a lot on our trucks because the tanks hold up and the parts are easy to find. When we replace one, we match the fuel, size and venting, check whether the old warranty might still cover the tank, and register the new unit so the warranty is on file from day one. Registration matters.

    Attic Water Heater Replacement in Rochester

    Lead service lines remain in some older neighborhoods, and replacing them is a common recommendation during major plumbing work. Attic water heaters take more work to replace than ones in a garage or closet. Getting a 50-gallon tank up a pull-down ladder or through a narrow scuttle takes two people and care, and code calls for a drain pan with a line to the outside or a safe drain, a service platform and a light near the unit. We bring the help.

    Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in Rochester

    Going tankless when the old tank fails is common, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. A whole-home tankless unit can draw four times the gas of a 40-gallon tank, so the gas line from the meter usually has to be upsized, and the old metal vent gets replaced with stainless or PVC run to the maker's specs. You see both numbers.

    Emergency Water Heater Replacement in Rochester, NY

    A leaking tank can empty itself onto the floor faster than most people expect. If water is coming from the heater, shut off the cold supply valve on top, turn the gas valve to off or flip the breaker, and open a hot tap to relieve pressure, then call and we will try to have a plumber and a new tank at your home that day. Do not wait it out.
