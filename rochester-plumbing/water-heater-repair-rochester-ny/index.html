    Pilot Repair in Rochester

    A pilot that lights and then dies when you let go of the knob almost always means a weak thermocouple. Sometimes the cause is a clogged flame arrestor screen starving the burner of air. We test the thermocouple output in millivolts. A good reading points us to the gas valve instead.

    Electric Water Heater Repair in Rochester

    Two elements and two thermostats run a standard electric tank, and any one of the four can quit without warning. If the water is warm but runs out quickly, the lower element is the first suspect. We test resistance with a meter. The replacement parts ride on the truck, which keeps it to one visit.

    Electric Water Heater Element Repair in Rochester

    Lukewarm water or hot water that runs out in five minutes usually means a burned-out element or a stuck thermostat. We check each element with an ohmmeter. A shorted element will trip the breaker, while an open one just sits there cold, and the readings tell us which one is which. Parts for most tanks ride on the truck.

    Gas Water Heater Repair in Rochester, NY

    Gas units add a burner, a pilot or igniter and a gas control valve to the list of parts that can fail. Most no-heat calls trace to the thermocouple or the gas control. We leak test each gas connection after the repair. Draft at the vent gets checked too.

    Sediment Flush and Noise Repair in Rochester

    Popping, rumbling or crackling usually means a thick layer of sediment is trapping water and overheating the steel at the tank bottom. The tank gets drained and refilled several times to break up scale, and the drain valve gets cleared. A flush brings back lost capacity. Homes on hard water benefit from a yearly flush.

    Leaking Water Heater Repair in Rochester, NY

    Not every leak means a dead tank. Relief valve, drain valve and supply line drips are cheap fixes. Water from the tank body or bottom seam usually means the lining has failed, and replacement is the only real fix. We protect your floors either way.

    Tankless Water Heater Repair in Rochester

    Scale is the main enemy of a tankless heater. Mineral buildup on the heat exchanger triggers overheating codes and shutdowns. We descale with a pump and approved solution, clean the inlet filter and check the igniter and flow sensor while the unit is open. All major brands.
