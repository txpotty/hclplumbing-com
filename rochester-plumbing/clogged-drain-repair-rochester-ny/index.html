    While chemical drain cleaners are widely available, HCL Plumbing generally advises against them for homes in Rochester. They often provide only temporary relief, can damage pipes over time, and pose environmental and health risks. Professional tools like augers or hydro-jetting are safer and more effective.

    Most standard clogged drain repairs, such as those in a sink or bathtub, can be completed within 1 to 2 hours. More complex issues, like main sewer line clogs or those requiring camera inspection and hydro-jetting, might take 3 to 4 hours or longer, depending on the difficulty.

    For most standard clogged drain repairs within your home, permits are typically not required in Rochester.     You should call a professional if a plunger or simple home remedies don't work, if you experience multiple clogged drains, sewage smells, or water backing up.
