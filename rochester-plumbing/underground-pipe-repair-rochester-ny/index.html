    Frequently Asked Questions About Underground Pipe Repair in Rochester

    The cost of underground pipe repair in Rochester, NY can vary a lot, typically ranging from $500 for minor spot repairs to $3,000 to $10,000 or more for trenchless full line replacements, or even more for extensive excavation and re-piping. Factors include the pipe material, depth, length of the damaged section, the repair method chosen and the extent of property restoration needed. We provide transparent, upfront pricing after a thorough diagnosis.

    In most cities, the homeowner owns and maintains the water line from the meter to the house. The utility is responsible for the meter and the line on the street side. We can help confirm which side the leak is on.

    HCL Plumbing is equipped to repair a wide range of underground pipe materials, including PVC, ABS, cast iron, copper, PEX and even older materials like clay or polybutylene. Our technicians are trained on the right repair techniques for each material.

    Common signs of a broken underground pipe include unexplained wet spots or lush patches in your yard, a sudden drop in water pressure, unusual odors such as sulfur or sewage, gurgling sounds from drains or toilets or an unexpected increase in your water bill. If you notice any of these, it's wise to call for a professional inspection promptly.

    Absolutely, tree roots are a very common cause of underground pipe damage, particularly in older, porous pipes or those with existing cracks. Roots seek out water sources and can infiltrate pipes, growing inside them and causing blockages or eventually fracturing the pipe. This is a frequent issue in many established Rochester neighborhoods.

    Ignoring an underground pipe leak can lead to serious problems, including costly water damage to your foundation, landscaping erosion, mold growth, higher water bills and potential contamination of your water supply. It's crucial to address these issues promptly.

    The duration of an underground pipe repair depends on the complexity. A minor leak repair might take a few hours, while a full trenchless sewer line replacement could take one to three days. We always aim for efficient service with minimal disruption.

    Whether an underground pipe leak is covered by insurance depends on your homeowner's policy and the cause of the damage.
