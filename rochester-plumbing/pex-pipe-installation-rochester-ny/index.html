    Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Rochester, NY

    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Rochester

    Homeowners ask us which one is better, and it really depends on the house. PEX-A makes tighter bends, can be heat-fixed if it kinks, and its fittings flow better, while PEX-B is cheaper and its crimps hold just as well when each one is gauged. Either one lasts for decades. For tight attics we lean toward PEX-A.

    Copper to PEX Conversions in Rochester

    Pinhole leaks in copper rarely show up alone. Homeowners around Rochester with hard water often add a softener to protect water heaters and keep scale out of faucets. A switch to PEX ends it. Where new PEX meets existing copper stubs or valves, we use listed transition fittings.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Rochester

    PEX-B is stiffer, costs less per foot, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. Since a crimp is only as good as the tool that made it, every ring gets a go/no-go gauge check, and any ring that fails gets cut off and redone. It is a five-second check. Skipping it is how leaks start.
