    Salt Bridge Removal and Brine Tank Cleaning in Rochester, NY

    Humidity and cheap salt cause most salt bridges. The top layer fuses into a crust with an empty pocket below it. The softener keeps regenerating on schedule, but it draws plain water instead of brine, which leaves the resin without a recharge and hard water flows to every tap.

    Full-System Softener Inspections in Rochester

    An inspection covers more than the two tanks. We trace the inlet and outlet plumbing, the bypass, the drain line and the brine overflow. Green crust on copper fittings, a slow leak at the bypass or a drain line jammed down into a standpipe all go on the written report.

    Breaking Up Salt Bridges in Rochester

    To check for a bridge, we push a broom handle straight down through the salt. A hard stop, then a drop, means a bridge. We break the crust, pull out the mushy salt underneath with a wet-dry vac, then check the brine well, safety float, brine line and grid plate before refilling to about half full.

    Resin Cleaning and Regeneration Tuning in Rochester, NY

    Iron is the most common resin fouler we see. The beads turn rusty brown and stop grabbing hardness. We feed a resin cleaner through the brine well, run a regeneration and retest, and if the capacity comes back we set the valve to regenerate more often so iron cannot load the bed again.

    Hardness Testing at the Tap in Rochester

    We test with a titration kit, counting drops until the sample changes color, because test strips are too rough for setting a valve. We take the raw sample from an unsoftened hose bib or with the softener on bypass. Softened water comes from the kitchen tap.

    Commercial Softener Service in Rochester

    Commercial units work harder. Restaurants and laundromats can burn through a softener's capacity in a single day, so twin-tank systems swap tanks, keeping one in service while the other regenerates. We set visits around your business hours so the water never goes hard during a rush.

    Brine Tank Cleanout and Inspection in Rochester, NY

    Brine tanks collect a gray sludge of salt fines and insoluble grit, and it builds up faster with cheap rock salt. We empty, scrub and rinse the tank. Then we look for cracks around the brine well and overflow fitting and refill with fresh salt.

    Softener Brands We Work On in Rochester

    Most softeners sold in the last 20 years run on a handful of valve platforms, mainly Fleck, Clack, Autotrol and a few proprietary heads. The valve matters more than the brand. Kinetico and Culligan build their own valves, so we check part availability before quoting.
