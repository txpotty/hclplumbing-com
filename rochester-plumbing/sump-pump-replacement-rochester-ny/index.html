    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Rochester, NY

    Sump Basin and Pit Replacement in Rochester, NY

    Old clay tile or 18-inch plastic basins crack and fill with silt, and a pump in a basin that small cycles every few minutes during a heavy rain. The fix is to cut the slab, drop in a bigger poly basin with a bolted lid, and patch the concrete. More volume in the pit means fewer starts per hour. Fewer starts, longer motor life.

    High-Capacity Sump Pump Upgrades in Rochester

    If your pump runs every 30 seconds during a storm and still loses ground, it is undersized for the water coming in. First we time the pump cycles and measure the pit refill rate. From there it is math. Often a 1/2 or 3/4 HP pump with more GPM at your lift, plus a 2-inch discharge line, solves it.

    Pedestal Pump Service in Rochester, NY

    Louder, yes, but a pedestal motor sits high and dry, so in a narrow pit it often outlasts a submersible by several years. Narrow pits favor pedestals. We replace worn units and set the float rod stops for a proper cycle. A submersible is worth a look when the pit is wide enough.

    Pedestal Pumps for Shallow Pits in Rochester, NY

    Older basements sometimes have a narrow, shallow pit poured right into the floor, and a pedestal pump is often the only thing that fits without breaking concrete. We replace the pump and float rod and rerun the PVC up to the check valve. They usually cost less than a submersible.

    Light Commercial Sump Pump Replacement in Rochester, NY

    Light commercial work, think retail basements and small office buildings, usually means a 1/2 HP or larger cast iron pump with a 2-inch discharge and a panel alarm. We match the old pump or step it up. Sewage ejectors and grinder pumps we quote separately.

    Combination Sump Pump System Replacement in Rochester, NY

    Combination units put a primary AC pump and a DC backup on one discharge manifold, so you get two pumps and two floats in a single assembly. They are handy when the basin is too small for two pumps side by side. We replace the whole assembly or just the failed side.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Replacement in Rochester, NY

    Zoeller cast iron pumps, the M53 and M63 in particular, are what we pull off the truck most often because they take heat and hard cycling better than plastic housings. An existing Zoeller can usually be swapped for a current model with the same 1.5-inch outlet and float style. Need more lift? We can move you up to a half-horse model like the M98.
