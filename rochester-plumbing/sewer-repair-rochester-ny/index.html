    Sewer Line Leak Detection & Repair in Rochester, NY

    Undetected sewer line leaks can lead to unpleasant odors, wet spots in your yard and even structural damage to your home's foundation. HCL Plumbing uses precise leak detection methods to pinpoint the source of the leak, whether it's a minor crack or a major break.     Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Rochester

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg pipe, a tar-paper material that flattens and blisters over time. Cast iron from the same era corrodes from the inside and scales up until little flow remains. When a camera shows widespread failure, patching is a waste of money. We replace the line in sections or end to end with PVC, and we can usually use trenchless methods to avoid tearing up the whole yard.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Rochester, NY

    Tree and shrub roots go straight for the moisture and nutrients leaking from old sewer joints. Once inside, they grow into dense mats that catch paper and waste. We cut the roots with a mechanical cutter or hydro-jetter, then repair or seal the joint so they cannot come right back. For lines with many rooted joints, we will show you when lining or replacement makes more sense than repeated cleaning.

    Sewer Line Backups and Blockages in Rochester

    Sewer backups are often caused by severe blockages from grease buildup, foreign objects or extensive tree root intrusion. Our team uses advanced sewer camera inspections to identify the exact cause and location of the blockage. We then use hydro-jetting or mechanical snaking to clear the obstruction, restoring proper flow to your system and preventing further damage. This approach is very effective for the stubborn clogs often found in older New York homes.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Rochester, NY

    For many sewer line issues, especially those caused by tree roots or pipe corrosion, trenchless repair offers a less invasive solution. This method, which includes pipe bursting or pipe lining, requires minimal excavation, saving your yard and landscaping from extensive disruption. We use specialized equipment to either pull a new pipe through the old one or insert a resin-coated liner that cures in place, creating a new, durable pipe inside the old one. It's an efficient and popular choice for protecting property appearance in Rochester.

    Pipe Burst and Collapse Repair in Rochester, NY

    A burst or collapsed sewer pipe can lead to significant damage and unsanitary conditions. These issues can result from shifting soil, corrosion of old cast iron or clay pipes, or heavy vehicle traffic above the line. HCL Plumbing offers emergency repair services to promptly address these critical failures. We precisely locate the damaged section and provide durable repair or replacement options, often using trenchless methods where appropriate to limit disruption.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Rochester, NY

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. Bellies often form where the original bedding washed out or where the soil settled after heavy rain. Much of New York sits on glacial till, rock and clay, which makes excavation for sewer and water lines slower and more involved. We confirm the depth of standing water on camera, then re-bed and re-grade that section to restore proper fall. Correct slope is typically a quarter inch per foot for a typical residential line.
