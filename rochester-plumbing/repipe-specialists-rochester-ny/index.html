    PEX Versus Copper Repipe Pricing in Rochester, NY

    PEX repipes usually cost less than copper because the material is cheaper and installation goes faster. Copper costs more. For a typical house, copper often runs 20 to 40 percent above PEX, though some buyers and homeowners like the look and feel of metal pipe and are willing to pay for it. Both prices include permits, fittings, patching and cleanup.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Rochester, NY

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside, and over decades the inside diameter closes down to a fraction of what it was. Pressure drops slowly. Rust-colored water in the morning, poor pressure upstairs and dripping threaded joints all point to failing galvanized. New PEX or copper takes its place. Most homeowners notice the pressure difference the first time they turn on the shower.

    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Rochester

    Kitec, an orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe with brass fittings, and gray polybutylene are both known for failures. Many homes built from the 1980s through the early 2000s have one or the other. The pipe at the water heater usually tells us which one you have, and we replace the full system so the home is easier to insure and to sell.

    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Rochester

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water and long attic runs. We use Uponor or similar PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that open the pipe end and let it shrink back over the fitting for a full-bore joint. Fewer joints, fewer leaks. Two or three days covers most houses.

    Slab Leak Repiping in Rochester

    A slab leak shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a running meter or a wet patch of carpet with no visible cause. Basements are standard in most homes, and many of them depend on sump pumps to manage groundwater during wet seasons. Instead of breaking concrete to patch one pipe, we reroute new supply lines overhead through the attic and down interior walls, then abandon the old lines in the slab. No jackhammer needed. It ends this leak and takes the rest of the slab plumbing out of the picture.

    Emergency Pipe Failures and Repipes in Rochester

    Sometimes a house reaches the repipe point all at once, with a burst line and two more leaks right behind it. We stop the active leak first, make temporary repairs that get water back on and then plan the full repipe so you are not rushed into it. That gives you time. Insurance may cover the water damage from the burst even if it does not cover the repipe itself, so we document what we find with photos.

    Repipe Costs Versus Leak Repairs in Rochester, NY

    A whole-home PEX repipe for an average house often runs $5,000 to $12,000, with copper costing more. A single slab leak repair can run $1,000 to $4,000 once access and restoration are included. Do the math. By the second or third leak, a repipe usually pays for itself, and we put both options side by side so you can decide.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Rochester, NY

    PEX tubing resists freezing damage, corrosion and scale better than most metal pipe. It is quieter too. Because PEX flexes, it absorbs some of the shock that causes water hammer, and long runs with few fittings mean fewer places for a leak to start inside a wall. Attic PEX gets kept away from flues and out of UV.
