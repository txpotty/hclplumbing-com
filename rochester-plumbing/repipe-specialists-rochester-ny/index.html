    Homeowners around Rochester with hard water often add a softener to protect water heaters and keep scale out of faucets. Water in many areas contains minerals that cause scale buildup inside pipes over time. Our repipe specialists consider local water conditions, recommending materials like PEX or properly installed copper that are more resistant to mineral buildup, ensuring a longer-lasting plumbing system.

    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Rochester

    Slab leaks are a serious and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Basements are standard in most homes, and many of them depend on sump pumps to handle groundwater during wet seasons. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the damaged pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use advanced detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.

    Efficient PEX Repiping in Rochester, NY

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and very popular choice for repiping projects in Rochester, NY. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points and making installation quicker and less intrusive than traditional rigid pipes. PEX is also highly resistant to freezing, scaling, and chlorine, common issues in many water systems. We use high-quality PEX-A or PEX-B tubing, installed with durable crimp or expansion fittings, to create a reliable and efficient plumbing network throughout your home.
