    PEX Water Line Installation in Rochester, NY

    PEX bends around corners, so a typical service run needs no fittings between the meter and the house. Lake-effect snow and deep cold snaps can keep temperatures below freezing for days, so insulating exposed lines is worth it. PEX can swell a little in a freeze and go back to shape, where copper splits. We still bury it below the frost depth the local code sets, and we keep it out of direct sun at the meter box, since UV light breaks it down. Short exposure only.

    PEX Service Lines for Long Runs in Rochester

    We buy PEX in long coils. That makes it the easy pick when the meter sits out at the road and the house is set far back, since a single coil reaches all the way without a splice. Fewer joints underground means fewer places a leak can start, and it is the joints that usually fail first.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Upgrades in Rochester, NY

    Street pressure varies a lot from one neighborhood to the next. Plumbing code caps house pressure at 80 psi.     Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Rochester, NY

    Bursting fits old cast iron or galvanized lines that will shatter under the head. Copper and poly are better pulled than burst. The failures tend to be sudden splits rather than slow drips, and some home insurers refuse to cover homes that still have it. Worth replacing.
