    Copper Service Line Installation in Rochester, NY

    Type K copper is a durable option for underground water lines. It resists punctures and lasts for decades. Copper costs more than PEX but is favored by some homeowners. We install copper with proper bedding to protect it. We can also install sleeves where it passes through foundations.

    Polybutylene Water Line Replacement in Rochester, NY

    Polybutylene pipe was used in some homes built in the 1980s and 1990s. It can become brittle and fail with no warning. Many insurers flag it as a risk. We replace polybutylene service lines with durable materials. This reduces the chance of sudden leaks.

    PEX Water Line Installation in Rochester, NY

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping is a modern, flexible, and increasingly popular choice for water line replacement. It resists corrosion, scaling, and chlorine, making it very durable for most water conditions. Shutting off and draining outdoor spigots before winter is a must for homes around Rochester, along with keeping the heat set when traveling. PEX can expand and contract without bursting, which is a significant advantage when a cold snap hits Rochester. Its flexibility allows for fewer fittings and quicker installation.
