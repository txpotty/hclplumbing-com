    Roots in Clay and Cast Iron in Rochester

    Older clay and cast iron invite roots. Joints are the weak spot. We clear root growth with cutters and jetting and talk about lining or repair to stop the next round.

    Residential Sewer Line Cleaning in Rochester

    Household laterals collect grease and hair. Both build up slowly. Kitchen grease and bathroom hair stick to rough spots and joints until the 4 inch line narrows enough to back up, and older cast iron makes it worse by adding scale for debris to grab, and we clear it with a cable or a jetter sized for the pipe.

    Sewer Line Camera Inspection in Rochester

    A camera shows the line in real time. Bellies and cracks show up. Our inspection checks for sags, separated joints, roots and corrosion, and we mark each defect on the ground above, with a copy of the video for you.

    Tree Root Removal from Sewer Lines in Rochester

    Roots enter through small cracks and loose joints. Frost reaches deep into the ground here, so water service lines must be buried well below the surface to avoid freeze damage. Then they spread. Root saws on a sectional cable cut the mass loose, and a jetter follows to remove fine root hairs and bring the pipe back to its full diameter, and we explain options to keep them out.

    Sewer Line Cleaning Cost Compared to Replacement in Rochester, NY

    Cleaning costs a few hundred dollars. Replacement costs thousands. If the pipe is structurally sound, regular cleaning is often the better value, but if the camera shows a collapse or a long belly, cleaning only buys time, and you see both prices.

    Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in Rochester, NY

    Jetting cleans the pipe wall. A snake punches a hole. A jetter scours the full circumference of the pipe at pressures we set for the material, from PVC to clay.

    Identifying Signs of Sewer Line Clogs in Rochester, NY

    Several drains backing up at once is the main sign. Gurgling is another. Water coming up through a drain when you flush, a sewage smell in the yard or basement and slow drains throughout the house all point to a sewer line that needs cleaning.

    Routine Sewer Line Cleaning in Rochester

    Routine cleaning clears slow buildup. It narrows the line over time. Our tech runs a cable from the cleanout with a blade sized for the line, then jets the walls if needed, and most older lines do well with cleaning every one to two years.
