    Water Softener Problems We Fix in Rochester, NY

    Resin Bed Replacement in Rochester

    Chlorine eats resin slowly, and after enough years the beads swell, break and stop exchanging the calcium and magnesium they are supposed to hold. Older systems on chlorinated city water see it most. HCL Plumbing pulls the old resin and fills the tank with new media. Capacity comes back right away.

    Water Softener Leak Detection and Repair in Rochester, NY

    Cracked tanks, loose fittings and failed O-rings are behind most softener leaks. A small drip still wastes water and damages flooring. Once we find the source, from the bypass to the drain connection, the failed seal or fitting gets replaced. Then it gets a pressure test.

    Softener Repair vs Replacement Cost in Rochester, NY

    Most softener repairs cost $150 to $450, while a resin replacement often runs $400 to $900 depending on tank size. A new quality softener installed typically costs $1,500 to $3,500. With a good tank and valve, repair is the better value. If the valve is obsolete and parts are scarce, replacing the head or the whole unit may make more sense than chasing used parts online.

    Resin Testing and Replacement in Rochester

    Resin beads trap hardness minerals and release them during regeneration. Chlorine and chloramine in city water slowly break resin down until it turns to mush. After a capacity test, the resin gets cleaned or replaced, and a good tank and valve with new resin performs like new. Mushy resin can clog the valve.

    Control Valve Rebuild in Rochester

    Service, backwash, brine and rinse are the four cycles the control valve directs every time the softener regenerates, usually in the middle of the night. Over the years, seals, spacers and pistons wear down. Leaks at the valve or water running to the drain are common signs. The valve gets rebuilt with the kit made for it.

    Softener Not Regenerating in Rochester

    Hard water coming back usually means the softener has stopped regenerating. A failed timer motor, a bad circuit board or a stuck meter turbine are the usual causes, along with settings lost in a power outage. Each part gets tested. After the repair, the valve is programmed for your household size and hardness so you have soft water by the next cycle.

    Salt Usage and Settings Repair in Rochester

    Too much salt or water use usually means settings that do not match your hardness or household. An old timer head regenerates by the calendar, used or not. We retest hardness and reprogram the valve, or upgrade the head to a metered control that only regenerates when the resin is actually close to exhausted. Salt bills drop.

    Control Valve Troubleshooting and Repair in Rochester, NY

    Stuck pistons, worn seals and failed drive motors cause most control valve trouble, and the symptoms are hard water, constant regeneration or a leak at the head. We diagnose the valve on site. Our plumbers carry seal kits and pistons for Fleck, Clack and Autotrol heads, which covers a big share of the softeners installed in homes around here.
