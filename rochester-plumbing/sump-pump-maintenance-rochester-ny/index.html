    Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Rochester, NY

    The float switch is the most frequent reason a sump pump fails to start. Tethered floats can tangle on the pump body, and vertical floats can stick from mineral crust or debris. We clean or replace the switch and set the on and off levels so the pump does not short cycle. Electronic sensor switches are an option for tight pits where floats get stuck.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Maintenance in Rochester

    HCL Plumbing is proficient in servicing Zoeller sump pumps, a widely trusted brand known for its durability. Our maintenance includes specific checks for Zoeller models, such as inspecting the cast iron housing for cracks, confirming the motor is running smoothly and testing the float switch assembly for correct activation. We stock common replacement parts for quick repairs, keeping your Zoeller pump in peak condition.

    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Rochester

    Spring snowmelt and heavy rain can raise water tables quickly in New York, sending water into basements and testing sump pumps. We test your pump ahead of the rainy season by filling the pit until the float trips. We watch the pump empty the basin, listen for grinding bearings and measure how fast it cycles. If the pump struggles, you still have time to fix it before the first big storm.

    Emergency Sump Pump Maintenance in Rochester

    If your sump pump is acting up or has stopped working, HCL Plumbing provides emergency maintenance services in Rochester. We focus on rapid response to diagnose and address urgent issues, whether it's a jammed impeller, a faulty float switch or a complete pump failure. Our goal is to quickly restore your sump pump's function to prevent immediate flooding and protect your property from water damage.

    Battery Backup Pump Maintenance in Rochester

    Severe storms often bring power outages along with the rain. A battery backup pump keeps water moving when the main pump loses power or fails. We load test the battery, check the charger and alarm and run the backup pump on its own. Most backup batteries need replacement every three to five years.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Check-Ups in Rochester, NY

    For homes equipped with pedestal sump pumps, HCL Plumbing provides specialized maintenance services in Rochester, NY. We focus on inspecting the external motor, confirming proper lubrication and checking the impeller for any obstructions. This type of pump, with its motor above the water, requires specific attention to its shaft and bearings to prevent premature wear and support reliable performance during heavy rains.

    Check Valve and Discharge Line Service in Rochester, NY

    The check valve stops pumped water from falling back into the pit when the pump shuts off. A failed valve makes the pump run twice as often and wears out the motor. We test and replace check valves and inspect the discharge pipe for cracks and loose joints. We also make sure water exits away from the house so it does not soak into the soil around the slab or foundation.
