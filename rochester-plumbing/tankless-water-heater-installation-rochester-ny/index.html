    Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Rochester, NY

    Electric tankless heaters make sense in Rochester, NY homes without gas, or for a single sink or a guest bath far from the main heater. Whole-house electric units pull a lot of current.     Gas Tankless Water Heater Installation in Rochester

    Gas tankless units in Rochester are the common pick for whole-house hot water because a single 199,000 BTU burner can feed two showers and a sink at the same time without losing temperature. Gas is the catch. In most houses, the line from the meter has to be upsized, often from half-inch to three-quarter inch or bigger. We test the new gas piping before first fire.

    Recirculation Systems for Tankless Heaters in Rochester

    Tankless heaters can take a few extra seconds to get hot water to a far bathroom, since the burner fires only after it senses flow. A recirculation pump on a timer or a motion sensor keeps the hot line primed, so the wait at the farthest sink drops from a minute to a few seconds. Less water goes down the drain.

    Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Rochester, NY

    Tankless vs Tank Cost Comparison in Rochester, NY
