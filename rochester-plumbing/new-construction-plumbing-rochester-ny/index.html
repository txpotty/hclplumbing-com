    Plumbing Rough-In Services in Rochester

    Rough-in covers every pipe that ends up inside a wall or floor. Holes through bearing studs stay within the framing code limit, usually 40 percent of the stud width. Vents go up through the roof. Each fixture gets a trap arm sized and sloped to its vent distance.

    Water Heater Setup for New Builds in Rochester

    Tank or tankless, the venting decides the install location. Atmospheric tanks need a metal flue straight up, and power-vent or condensing units can go out a sidewall in PVC. We set the heater on a stand in a garage where code requires it. Every one gets a T&P line piped to an approved spot.

    Residential Plumbing for New Builds in Rochester

    A new single-family house usually needs a 3/4-inch or 1-inch water service, a 4-inch building drain and somewhere between 20 and 40 fixture connections. Private wells serve a large share of rural homes, and they can carry iron, sulfur or hardness that stains fixtures and clogs filters. We plan supply materials and any softener or filter loop around that water before rough-in starts. Pipe size comes from the fixture count. Most weak-shower complaints in new homes trace back to a supply line one size too small.

    Commercial Plumbing for New Construction in Rochester, NY

    Retail suites and offices tend to need accessible restrooms, a mop sink and testable backflow on the water service. Carrier fittings for wall-hung fixtures and accessible heights get set during rough-in. Backflow testers certify the assembly. The test report is in hand before your occupancy inspection.

    Gas Line Installation for New Construction in Rochester, NY

    Gas piping is sized from the total BTU load and the longest run, using the tables in the fuel gas code. Undersize it and the furnace short-cycles or the tankless heater throws an error on a cold morning. Black iron or CSST goes in per the plans. Any CSST run is bonded to the grounding electrode system per the manufacturer.

    Final Trim and Fixture Setting in Rochester

    At trim we install toilets, faucets, garbage disposals and dishwashers, plus ice maker boxes and laundry connections. We let every fixture run long enough to show a slow drip. We also check that hot and cold are on the correct sides, set the water heater to 120 degrees, and clean aerators clogged by construction grit. Then the walkthrough.

    Rough-In and Top-Out Plumbing in Rochester, NY

    Once framing is up, our crew runs supply and vent lines through the walls and attic. Every stub-out for sinks, showers and appliances is set to the cabinet and tile drawings. Nail plates protect pipe near stud faces. Shower valves get set to the finished wall depth the tile setter gives us, which saves cutting tile around a trim plate that sits too deep.
