    What Our Tankless Plumbers Fix in Rochester, NY

    Commercial Tankless Repair in Rochester, NY

    Hotels and gyms draw hot water from open to close, so their tankless racks scale up two or three times faster than a home unit. We service rack systems with manifold kits, checking each heater's flow sensor and fault log separately, and set a descaling schedule by run hours. Quarterly is common.

    Condensing Tankless Repair in Rochester, NY

    Condensing tankless heaters recover heat from the flue gas and make acidic condensate that has to drain somewhere. That condensate runs through a trap and, where code or the manual calls for it, a neutralizer filled with limestone or marble chips before it reaches the drain. A plugged trap will lock the heater out. We clean the trap, refill the neutralizer and check the PVC vent for sag or the wrong slope.

    Tankless Flush and Scale Removal in Rochester

    Lukewarm water at high flow and a unit that runs loud are two early signs of scale. Most tankless installs include isolation valves with purge ports for exactly this job, and if yours does not, we can add them during the visit so future flushing takes less time. Vinegar works in a pinch. Commercial descaler works faster.

    Flow Sensor and Thermistor Repair in Rochester

    Tankless heaters start when the flow sensor sees about half a gallon a minute, then trim the burner using thermistor readings. A dirty inlet screen or a failing sensor shows up as no hot water, temperature swings or a unit that drops out mid-shower. Our tech cleans the screen, checks sensor values in the service menu and tests thermistor resistance against the chart. Usually under an hour.

    No Hot Water Tankless Repair in Rochester

    No hot water at all usually points to ignition, gas supply or a heat exchanger choked with scale. The service menu's error history is where we start, then test the igniter, flame rod and gas pressure in that order, because a weak flame signal and low pressure can look identical on the display. We look at the vent run as well. Common igniters and flame rods ride on the truck.

    Same-Day Tankless Repair in Rochester

    Water dripping from inside the cabinet, or a fault you cannot reset, needs a look the same day. We bring sensors, igniters, flame rods and filters, so a good share of visits end with hot water. Heat exchangers are different. We check the warranty, order the part, and shut the water at the service valves so the cabinet stays dry until it arrives.

    Electric Tankless Element Repair in Rochester, NY

    Electric units fail in their own way. Stiebel Eltron and EcoSmart models use two to four heating elements in separate chambers, so a single burned element leaves the heater behind at high flow, while a failed flow switch means no heat at all. We check each element's resistance, measure voltage under load and check amperage on each leg, then confirm the panel and wire size match the unit's rating.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Repair in Rochester

    Restaurants and laundromats in Rochester often run two to six tankless units on a common manifold. One unit down means the others work harder and scale faster, so we check the cascade controller, the isolation valves on each heater and the water quality coming in. A down kitchen loses money. We schedule around service hours when we can.
