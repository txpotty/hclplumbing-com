    Gas Control Valve Replacement in Rochester, NY

    Honeywell and White-Rodgers valves report faults with a blinking LED, and the flash count matches a chart on the label. The tech reads the code and tests the thermopile and sensors before condemning the valve. Sometimes the valve really is bad. When the control itself is the problem, we replace it with an exact-match valve or a listed universal model, set the temperature and leak test every joint on the gas train before relighting. You get the old valve back if you want it.

    Power Vent Water Heater Repair in Rochester

    Power vent heaters push exhaust through PVC with a blower on top of the tank. The usual failures are the blower motor, the pressure switch and the hot surface igniter. Condensate pooling in a vent with the wrong slope can also trip the pressure switch, so we check the pipe run all the way to the termination before we sign off on a part. The control's error code is our starting point. Then each part gets a meter test.

    Flammable Vapor Sensor Repair in Rochester

    Since about 2003, most residential gas tanks have been built with a flammable vapor ignition resistant combustion chamber. Air enters through a flame arrestor screen at the base that also keeps flame inside. When lint, dust or pet hair coats that screen, the burner starves, the chamber overheats and a thermal switch shuts the whole heater down, sometimes several times a week. The screen and the chamber both get cleaned. We replace any tripped thermal switch.

    Repairing Older Gas Water Heaters in Rochester

    Older tanks with standing pilots and no vapor sensor are still out there in Rochester, mostly in basements and utility closets. Their parts are simple, and a universal thermocouple fits nearly all of them. We can keep a pre-2003 heater running when the tank is sound, but we also check the draft hood, the flue connector and the gas cock, since those fittings on an old install are often original and corroded. Some old setups lack a sediment trap. One goes in while we are there.

    Bradford White & Rheem Gas Water Heater Repair in Rochester, NY

    Bradford White and Rheem tanks make up a big share of what we repair in Rochester, NY. Most Rheem gas units run a White-Rodgers or Honeywell valve with a blinking status LED. Bradford White uses its own sealed combustion design with a lint screen at the base and a thermal switch, and it needs a specific kit when the pilot assembly goes bad. We keep both on the truck. Using factory parts keeps your tank warranty valid.

    Thermostat and Gas Valve Repair in Rochester, NY

    Scalding water at the kitchen tap is a valve problem until proven otherwise. Some controls drift high as the sensor ages, and some dials get turned past hot. Our plumbers in Rochester, NY measure the outlet temperature at the nearest fixture, compare it to the dial and replace the gas control if it is overshooting, because water at 140 degrees can burn a child's skin in a few seconds. A setting near 120 degrees works for most households.

    Pilot Light & Burner Issues in Rochester

    A pilot that lights, holds for ten seconds and then goes out is almost always a thermocouple or a weak magnet in the gas valve. A pilot that will not light at all is more often a clogged orifice or a dead igniter. Main burner problems show up another way. The pilot stays lit but the main flame never comes on, which points to the thermostat side of the valve, a tripped thermal cutoff or a flame arrestor so clogged with lint that the burner starves for air. We test each piece in that order.
