    Preventive Hydrojet Maintenance in Rochester

    Preventive jetting pays off for lines that clog more than once a year. Camera findings set the interval. A line with heavy grease might need jetting every six months, while a sewer with a few root entry points might only need it every 18 to 24 months, and the footage tells us which.

    Chemical-Free Drain Cleaning in Rochester, NY

    Hydrojetting uses only water, so nothing caustic goes into your pipes or septic tank. Caustic cleaners can harm old pipe. Jetting removes the buildup physically, and the debris flushes to the sewer or septic tank as normal waste, so there is nothing left in the pipe to eat at joints or harm the bacteria a septic system depends on.

    Commercial and Restaurant Hydrojetting in Rochester

    Hotels, apartment complexes and shopping centers rely on drains that handle heavy daily use. A backup closes rooms or stores. We set up jetting plans for each building's needs, schedule work during slow hours, and keep the camera footage on file so property managers can show tenants or owners the condition of the lines.

    Kitchen Grease Line Jetting in Rochester

    Kitchen drains collect grease that cools and hardens along the pipe wall, especially in long horizontal runs under a slab. A cable cuts a channel through it. Jetting with a spinning nozzle strips the grease from the full circumference so it flushes downstream instead of re-forming a plug at the next fitting.

    Hydrojetting vs. Snaking in Rochester, NY

    Snaking pokes a hole through a clog. Jetting cleans the whole pipe. For a single clog in an otherwise clean line, snaking is cheaper and works fine, but for a line that clogs every few months, jetting removes the buildup that keeps catching debris.

    Hydrojetting for Cast Iron Pipes in Rochester, NY

    Older cast iron drains rust and scale from the inside, leaving a rough surface that grabs debris. A jet clears that crust. Thin or cracked cast iron gets gentler settings or a lining recommendation instead.
