    Sewer Pipe Material Upgrades in Rochester, NY

    Many older homes in Rochester, NY still have original sewer lines made of clay, cast iron, or Orangeburg pipe, which are prone to degradation, cracking, and root intrusion over time. When we perform a sewer replacement, we often suggest upgrading to modern, durable materials like PVC (polyvinyl chloride) or ABS (acrylonitrile butadiene styrene). These materials are more flexible, resistant to corrosion and roots, and have a much longer lifespan, providing a significant upgrade to your home's plumbing infrastructure.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Rochester

    For significant damage or older, failing systems, traditional sewer line replacement often involves excavating a trench to access and replace the entire pipe. This method is reliable for replacing severely collapsed or extensively rooted lines, especially common in older Rochester homes with cast iron or clay pipes. We thoughtfully plan the excavation to minimize disruption to your yard and landscaping, using heavy-duty equipment for efficient digging and backfilling.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Rochester, NY

    Some lines have severe sagging, wrong slope or are too shallow for trenchless methods. In these cases, open-trench replacement is the right option. We excavate, remove the old pipe and install new PVC with proper bedding. We restore the trench once it passes inspection. This method allows full correction of slope issues.

    All sewer replacement work performed by HCL Plumbing in Rochester closely adheres to local plumbing codes and regulations. This includes proper pipe sizing, depth, slope, and connection methods.
