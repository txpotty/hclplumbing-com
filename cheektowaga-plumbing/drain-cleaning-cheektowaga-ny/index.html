    Jetting Versus Cabling in Cheektowaga, NY

    Cabling and jetting solve different problems. A cable cuts through a blockage. A jetter scrubs the pipe wall clean and flushes the debris downstream to the city main or septic tank, so we cable first on a hard root mass and jet afterward when the line has years of buildup on it.

    Hydro Jetting for Drains in Cheektowaga

    Jetting does what a cable cannot. A jetter sprays the full circumference of the pipe at high pressure. Cabling opens a path, but jetting strips the buildup off the walls. It works best on a kitchen or main line that keeps clogging every few months no matter how often it gets cabled.
