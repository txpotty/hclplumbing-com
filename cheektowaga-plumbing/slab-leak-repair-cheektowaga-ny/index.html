    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Cheektowaga, NY

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat expands and contracts the pipe against concrete and gravel.     Tunneling Slab Leak Repair in Cheektowaga, NY

    In some cases, especially when the leak is under a load-bearing wall or in a hard-to-reach area, tunneling beneath the foundation is the preferred method.     Cold Water Slab Leak Repair in Cheektowaga

    Cold water slab leaks are harder to feel but still waste water and wet the soil. They often show up as a high water bill or a meter that moves with all fixtures off. We isolate the cold line, locate the leak and repair or reroute it. Fixing cold leaks quickly helps protect your foundation.

    Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in Cheektowaga

    For more extensive damage, multiple leaks, or pipes that are too old and corroded to repair reliably, pipe rerouting is a long-term solution. This involves abandoning the leaking line under the slab and installing a new pipe through the walls or attic. This method bypasses the problem area entirely, providing a fresh, durable plumbing line. It is a common solution for aging homes in Cheektowaga with original copper or galvanized plumbing.
