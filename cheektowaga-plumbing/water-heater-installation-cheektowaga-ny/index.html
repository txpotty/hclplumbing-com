    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Cheektowaga

    A standard 40 to 50 gallon tank usually costs $1,600 to $3,500 installed. Tankless installs often run $3,000 to $6,500, depending on how much gas line and venting work the house needs.

    A standard tank replacement usually takes two to four hours. Tankless or relocation work takes longer.

    For two to four people, a 40 or 50 gallon tank usually does the job. Larger families may need a 75 gallon tank or a tankless unit sized by flow rate.

    It looks simple but involves gas, electrical and plumbing connections, plus a vent that has to carry exhaust out of the house every time the burner fires. Wrong installs cause leaks, carbon monoxide or electrical hazards. A licensed plumber is the safe call.

    Start with correct sizing and a thermostat set at 120 degrees. Insulating the first few feet of hot pipe and flushing the tank once a year both help, and a tankless model cuts standby loss.

    A like-for-like tank swap usually takes three to four hours. Converting from tank to tankless, or any job with major gas, vent or electrical changes, can take a full day or more. We give you a timeline first.

    Doing it yourself means handling gas, 240-volt power and pressurized water at once. A bad vent joint or a missing relief line can lead to carbon monoxide, scalding or a tank failure, and many local codes require a licensed plumber anyway. Hire a pro.

    An expansion tank absorbs the extra pressure created as water heats. Homes with a closed plumbing system are often required by code to have one, since it protects the heater and every pipe in the house.
