    Our plumbers are skilled at repairing all major faucet brands commonly found in New York homes, including Delta, Moen, Kohler, American Standard, Pfister, and many others. We keep a stock of universal and common brand-specific parts to speed up repairs.

    Water hardness in New York varies a lot, with many surface water supplies running soft and groundwater in some areas running hard. Hard water contains high levels of minerals like calcium and magnesium. Over time, these minerals can build up inside your faucets, corroding internal parts, clogging aerators, and stiffening moving components. This often leads to leaks, reduced water pressure, and a shorter faucet lifespan, making regular maintenance or timely repair important.

    While some minor leaks, like a simple washer replacement on a compression faucet, can be a DIY project, most modern faucets involve specific tools and intricate parts like cartridges or ceramic discs.
