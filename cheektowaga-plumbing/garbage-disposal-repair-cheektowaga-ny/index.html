    What Goes Wrong on a Disposal, and How We Fix It in Cheektowaga, NY

    Humming Disposal Repair in Cheektowaga

    Humming with no spin usually means something is stuck. Spoons, glass, bones and pits cause most of them. With the power off, we free the flywheel using the hex socket underneath, pull the object, and test the motor and reset with water running.

    Slow Draining Disposal Repair in Cheektowaga, NY

    Good grinding but slow draining usually means the discharge tube or the trap arm is packed. Rice and coffee grounds are the usual culprits. We pull the trap, clear the tube and the arm with the right size cable, and check that the arm has at least a quarter inch of fall per foot toward the wall.

    Disposal Reset and Electrical Repair in Cheektowaga

    When a disposal will not turn on at all, the thermal overload may have tripped or the switch may be bad. The reset, breaker, wall switch and wiring all get tested, along with air switches and the magnetic lids on batch-feed units. Most electrical faults are easy once found.

    Jam Clearing and Motor Repair in Cheektowaga

    Bones, fruit pits and bottle caps lodge between the grind ring and the impeller lugs, and the motor stalls. We shut off power, insert a 1/4 inch hex key into the socket on the bottom of the unit and work it back and forth until the flywheel turns freely. Next we pull the object with pliers. A motor that stalled for a long time may have overheated, so we test it under load before calling the job done.

    Noise and Odor Diagnosis in Cheektowaga

    A rattle that stops after a few seconds is usually a spoon or a pit bouncing in the chamber. A constant high whine means something else. That whine often comes from worn motor bearings, and once bearings start to fail the noise only gets worse until the motor seizes. Odors usually trace to the splash guard. The guard comes out, the chamber gets cleaned and a torn guard gets replaced.

    Grinding Noise Repair in Cheektowaga

    Loud metal-on-metal sounds may mean a loose mount, a foreign object or worn impellers. We inspect the chamber, tighten the mounting ring and remove any debris, then run the unit empty to hear whether the motor itself is making the noise. Failing bearings are not worth fixing.

    Noisy Disposal Repair in Cheektowaga

    Grinding, rattling or a hum that sounds rough all point to something inside. Loose mounting rings rattle too. We check the mount first, then the chamber for debris, then listen to the motor with the chamber empty, because a motor that whines with nothing in it has bad bearings.

    InSinkErator Disposal Repair in Cheektowaga, NY

    InSinkErator makes the Badger line and the Evolution series, and we repair both. Badgers are simple single-stage units, while Evolution models have multi-stage grinding and, on some models, a separate stage that can jam on its own. Common parts are on board. A failed motor means a new unit.
