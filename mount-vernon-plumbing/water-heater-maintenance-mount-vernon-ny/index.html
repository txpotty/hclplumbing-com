    Electric Water Heater Tune-Up in Mount Vernon

    Electric tanks fail at the lower element. Sediment piles up around the lower element, it runs hotter than it should, and eventually it burns out, which is why you suddenly get only a few minutes of hot water. We test both elements. With power off, we check thermostats and connections.

    Water Heater Leak Checks in Mount Vernon, NY

    Small drips become floods. We inspect the supply connections, the relief valve outlet, the drain valve and the tank seams for moisture or rust trails, and we check the drain pan and the floor around the unit for signs of a slow leak. Rust trails mean trouble. Early repairs protect floors and drywall.

    Tank Flush and Inspection in Mount Vernon

    Sediment forms from minerals in the water. We flush the tank, test the relief valve, check the anode and look over each fitting. Efficiency comes back. Recovery speeds up.

    Gas Burner and Venting Checks in Mount Vernon

    A blue flame is what we want to see. Poor flames mean dirty burners, so we clean them, test the sensor and check the draft. Bad venting is dangerous. The gas line and shutoff get leak tested.

    Electric Element Service in Mount Vernon, NY

    Two elements, two thermostats. Sediment coats the lower element first, it runs hot and burns out, and that is the most common reason an electric tank suddenly gives you only half a tank of hot water. Power gets shut off first. We only replace what failed.

    Flushing a Tank Water Heater in Mount Vernon

    Popping and rumbling mean sediment. Scale on the tank bottom traps heat and causes water to pop and rumble as it heats. We flush until it runs clear. Heavy buildup may take several cycles.

    Relief Valve and Safety Testing in Mount Vernon, NY

    The relief valve is the tank's last line of defense. We lift the lever, confirm water discharges and the valve reseats without dripping, and check that the discharge pipe runs full size to an approved spot within a few inches of the floor or outside. Leaky valves get replaced. Expansion tanks get an air charge check.

    Thermostat, Element and Burner Checks in Mount Vernon

    Wrong temperature means a part is off. Electric units get thermostat and element tests, and gas units get burner, pilot and control checks. Humid summers bring condensation on cold pipes and toilet tanks, which can stain ceilings and floors below. Bad parts cost money. You run out sooner.
