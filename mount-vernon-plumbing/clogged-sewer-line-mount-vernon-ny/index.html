    Main Line vs. Branch Line Clogs in Mount Vernon, NY

    Understanding the difference between a main sewer line clog and a branch line clog is key for proper diagnosis and repair in Mount Vernon, NY. A main line clog affects all drains in your home, often causing backups in lower-level fixtures. Branch line clogs are typically isolated to a single fixture or area. Our technicians are skilled at pinpointing the exact location of the blockage, whether it is in the main line or a specific branch, for a targeted and efficient repair.

    Hydro-Jetting for Stubborn Clogs in Mount Vernon

    HCL Plumbing uses powerful hydro-jetting technology to clear even the most stubborn sewer line blockages. This method uses high-pressure water streams to blast away grease, scale, mineral buildup, and tree roots, restoring your pipes to their original diameter. It's a very effective, non-invasive way to clean your sewer lines thoroughly and prevent future clogs. Unlike snaking, hydro-jetting cleans the entire pipe wall.

    Main Line Hydro Jetting in Mount Vernon

    Hydro jetting uses water at thousands of psi through a specialized nozzle to cut roots, grease and scale. It cleans the full diameter of the pipe rather than just boring a hole through the clog. We check the pipe condition with a camera first so jetting is only used on lines that can handle it. Many homeowners find that jetting buys them years before another backup.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Mount Vernon

    Before any work begins, a sewer camera inspection is crucial for accurately diagnosing the cause and location of a clog. Our high-resolution cameras provide a clear view inside your sewer lines, allowing us to identify issues like cracks, breaks, root intrusions, or excessive grease. This non-invasive method saves time and money by avoiding unnecessary digging, giving you a precise repair plan for your Mount Vernon property. It is essential for older homes with cast iron or clay pipes.

    Sewer Cleanout Installation in Mount Vernon

    Some older homes have no accessible cleanout, which forces plumbers to pull a toilet or go through a roof vent to clear the main line. We install a two-way cleanout near the foundation so future clearing is faster and cheaper. The cleanout also gives the city or a future plumber easy access for inspections.     Tree Root Sewer Clogs in Mount Vernon

    Roots from thirsty trees and shrubs seek out moisture and push into sewer pipe joints. Clay and older cast iron pipe are especially prone to root intrusion because of their many joints. We cut roots with a sewer machine fitted with root saws and then camera the line to see how much damage remains. For ongoing root problems, we can recommend a root treatment schedule or pipe lining.
