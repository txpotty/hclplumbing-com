    Preventative measures include regular plumbing inspections, being mindful of tree roots near your water lines, and protecting outdoor pipes from extreme temperatures.     For water line replacements, we mainly use durable and code-compliant materials such as PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) and copper.     Trenchless water line repair is a method of fixing or replacing underground pipes without extensive digging.
