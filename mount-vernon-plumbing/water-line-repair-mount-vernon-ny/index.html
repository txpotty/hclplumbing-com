    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Mount Vernon, NY

    One leak on a newer copper or PEX line is a spot repair. Two or more leaks on galvanized is a replacement. Age matters too, and a line past about 40 years is usually near the end no matter the material. Spot repairs often run a few hundred dollars while a full replacement costs more and resets the clock for decades. You see the cut-out section yourself.

    Underground Leak Detection in Mount Vernon, NY

    A ground microphone picks up the hiss of water escaping a pressurized pipe, even through a couple of feet of dirt. Quiet leaks are harder. For those we push a hydrogen and nitrogen tracer gas into the drained line and sniff for it at the surface, which finds leaks the microphone cannot hear. Basements are standard in most homes, and many of them depend on sump pumps to manage groundwater during wet seasons. You get a written price as soon as the leak is marked on the ground.

    Main Water Line Replacement in Mount Vernon, NY

    Replacement beats repair once a line has leaked two or three times, or when it is galvanized or polybutylene. Patches on those materials rarely hold. We run new Type K copper or PEX from the meter to the house, typically finishing in one day whether we trench or pull the pipe. We pull the permit, set up the inspection and pressure test the new line before it is covered.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Mount Vernon

    A trench through a paver driveway or a mature garden can cost more to restore than the pipe itself. Trenchless avoids most of that. We dig a pit at each end and pull HDPE or PEX through with a boring tool or pipe puller, either along the old route or through fresh soil next to it. Most replacements finish in a day. The new pipe carries its own manufacturer warranty.

    Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Mount Vernon

    A slab leak is a supply pipe leaking under the concrete floor of the house. New York has some of the oldest housing in the country, and many homes still have galvanized steel supply pipes and cast iron drains. Signs include a warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water and a meter that moves with everything off. We pinpoint the leak with acoustic gear and thermal imaging. From there you have three choices: break the slab at the spot, reroute the line through the walls or attic, or skip the old under-slab pipe and repipe overhead with PEX. We price each one for you.

    Trenchless Water Line Repair in Mount Vernon

    Trenchless work makes sense when the line runs under a driveway, patio or a row of mature shrubs. We dig a pit at the meter and another at the house. The new line travels between them without a trench. A pneumatic boring tool or a cable puller drags HDPE or PEX through the soil, or through the old pipe if it is a straight run, usually in one working day from start to backfill.

    Water Line Repair at the Meter in Mount Vernon, NY

    The meter box is where a lot of leaks start. Compression fittings there work loose over time. Before we touch anything, we figure out whether the leak is before or after the meter, because the water provider owns the meter and everything on the street side. When it is on your side, we repair it. That often means new fittings and a new customer shutoff valve on the same visit.

    PEX and Copper Water Line Repair in Mount Vernon

    Copper and PEX fail in different places. Copper tends to pinhole where a rock rubs it or where the water chemistry eats the wall from inside. PEX usually fails at a fitting. We cut out the bad section of copper and sweat or press in a new length, and for PEX we use the matching crimp or expansion tool so the joint is made the way the manufacturer intends.
