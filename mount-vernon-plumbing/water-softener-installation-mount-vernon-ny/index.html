    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Mount Vernon

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. Every whole-house install gets a bypass valve, and we add a pressure reducing valve if incoming pressure is too high. Every tap gets soft water. We can skip the hose bibs.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Mount Vernon

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. Instead of removing hardness, they convert it into a form that resists sticking, which reduces scale without salt or brine waste. Little upkeep is needed. They suit moderate hardness.

    Water Softener Replacement & Upgrades in Mount Vernon, NY

    Upgrading an old timer-based softener is one of the easiest ways to cut salt use. Modern units meter water use, send salt reminders by app and come in larger capacities for growing families. The savings add up. We swap it out in a few hours.

    Whole-Home Water Softener Installation in Mount Vernon

    Whole-home softeners treat every gallon that enters the house, right after the main shutoff. Placing it on the main means the water heater, the dishwasher and every fixture get soft water, not just the kitchen. Sizing comes first. Hardness and household use set the grain capacity.

    Residential Water Softener Installation in Mount Vernon

    Hard water costs homeowners money in shorter appliance life and extra cleaning. We test the water, ask about daily use and choose a capacity that lets the system regenerate roughly once a week. Weekly is the sweet spot. Every detail is handled on one visit.

    Water Softener Replacement in Mount Vernon, NY

    Older softeners lose ground as resin wears out, valve seals leak or injectors clog. While the old system is out, we inspect the loop and the drain, then install a newer metered unit. Salt use drops. Performance comes back right away.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Mount Vernon

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. We install RO units under the kitchen sink with a dedicated faucet, and many can also feed a refrigerator water line or an ice maker. Pairs well with a softener. Filters get changed on schedule.
