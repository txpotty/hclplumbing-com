    Sewer Line Camera Inspections in Troy, NY

    A sewer camera shows the inside of the main drain without digging. The camera goes in at a cleanout and travels to the main or the tank, recording roots, cracks, offsets and bellies along the way. We mark problem spots from above.     Pre-Purchase Inspection With Sewer Video in Troy

    Plumbing problems are some of the most expensive surprises after closing. We check supply pipe, record the sewer, date the water heater and test every fixture. Your agent gets the report. Buyers often use the findings to request repairs or credits, and the sewer video by itself can head off a very costly surprise.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Troy, NY

    Many older homes have clay, cast iron or Orangeburg sewer lines that can crack, sag or fill with roots. We run a high-resolution camera from a cleanout to the city connection or septic tank. The video shows offsets, bellies, roots and breaks along with their depth and location, and you keep a copy of the recording for your records or for a contractor.

    Water Pressure and Supply Pipe Review in Troy, NY

    We measure static water pressure and check the pressure reducing valve if one is installed. High pressure ages fixtures fast. Pressure above 80 psi shortens the life of fixtures, supply hoses and water heaters, and we also identify the supply pipe material, from copper and CPVC to polybutylene, Kitec and galvanized.

    Residential Plumbing Inspections in Troy

    A full home inspection covers the system from the water meter to the sewer connection. We check every visible pipe for corrosion and leaks, run each fixture for flow and drainage and test the water heater, the main shutoff and the angle stops. Pressure gets measured too. High pressure gets flagged.

    Gas Connection Safety Check in Troy, NY

    During a full inspection, we look at the visible gas piping and the connectors to each appliance. Old brass connectors, missing shutoffs or drip legs and rust all get noted, and we sweep joints with a gas detector. Any leak is reported on the spot. Older homes are where we find the most issues, usually at appliance connectors that have been there since the house was built.

    Preventive Maintenance Checks in Troy, NY

    A maintenance check is a lighter version of a full inspection for owners who want to stay ahead of problems. The visit covers flushing the heater, working the shutoffs, reading pressure and checking for slow drains and small leaks. It takes about an hour. Doing this every year or two adds years to a water heater and keeps the shutoffs working for the day you really need them.

    Leak Detection and Slab Screening in Troy

    A spinning water meter with all fixtures off is a clear sign of a hidden leak. We also check for warm floor spots. We test the meter, watch for pressure loss and look for damp baseboards and mildew, and on slab homes we follow up with acoustic and thermal equipment to pinpoint the leak. Early slab leak finds save floors and foundations.
