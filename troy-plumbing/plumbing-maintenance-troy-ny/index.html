    Hot Water Tank Service in Troy, NY

    Gas and electric tanks need different checks. Gas tanks get a burner, flue and leak check, and electric tanks get element and thermostat tests. Sediment comes out either way. Mineral buildup on the bottom wastes fuel and can lead to early tank failure.

    Sump Pump Testing in Troy, NY

    Basements flood when the pump fails during a storm. Nor'easters and tropical storm remnants can overwhelm municipal sewers, so backwater valves are a sensible addition for basement homes. We fill the pit to test the float, check the discharge line and confirm the check valve holds. Backup pumps get tested too.

    Backflow Preventer Testing in Troy

    Backflow devices protect the drinking water. Many water providers require yearly testing on irrigation and commercial backflow preventers, and our certified testers check each check valve and relief valve and file the results with your utility. Failed devices get rebuilt. We also check that devices are protected from heat and freezing where needed. Late tests can mean fines.

    Softener Cleaning and Testing in Troy

    Hard water scale builds in pipes and appliances fast. Brine, injector, resin and regeneration all get checked, and we test the water at a tap. Fouled resin can be cleaned. Worn resin gets swapped.

    Commercial and Priority Maintenance in Troy, NY

    Restaurants, offices and apartments need plumbing that works every day. Scheduled service covers drains, heaters, backflow and restrooms, with priority emergency response for clients. You get records each time. They help with budgeting and inspections.

    Tank and Tankless Heater Tune-Ups in Troy

    Mineral-heavy water shortens water heater life. Sediment drain, anode check, burner or element inspection and a relief valve test cover both tank and tankless. Most failures give warning. We spot the signs early.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Troy

    Tankless units need descaling. Scale builds up inside the heat exchanger, especially with hard water, and the unit starts throwing error codes, running lukewarm or cutting out mid-shower long before it actually wears out. We pump descaler through the service valves. We also clean the inlet screen and check venting and gas supply.

    Softener Upkeep in Troy, NY

    Softeners fail quietly. A softener that quit regenerating still sends water, just hard water, and you only notice when scale appears. We check the bypass and drain line. Then we test the treated water.
