    Constant Pressure Well Pump Installation in Troy, NY

    Constant pressure systems hold the house at one pressure, say 50 psi, whether one faucet is open or the shower and the irrigation are running together. A variable frequency drive speeds the pump up and slows it down to match demand. Showers stop going weak.

    Jet Pump Replacement in Troy, NY

    Jet pumps sit above ground, in a well house or basement, and pull water up by suction from wells where the water level is within about 25 feet. Deep-well jets with a two-pipe setup can reach further, but they work harder. Lost prime is the classic complaint. Most of the time a bad foot valve or a suction leak is letting water drain back, and we check those before condemning the pump.

    Solar Well Pump Systems in Troy, NY

    Solar well pumps make sense for livestock tanks and cabins where running power from the meter would cost more than the pump itself. A few panels feed a DC pump through a controller while the sun is up. The storage tank does the job a battery would. Panel count comes from how many gallons you need a day.

    Drop Pipe and Wire Replacement in Troy

    Galvanized drop pipe from the 1970s and 1980s rusts from the inside, and a pinhole at 150 feet will bleed pressure every time the pump shuts off. Depending on depth and pump weight, it gets replaced with schedule 120 PVC or poly. Wire gets the same look. Nicked jackets and old tape splices go.

    Franklin Electric Well Pump Replacement in Troy

    Franklin Electric builds a large share of the submersible motors sold in the US, including ones sitting under other brands' pump ends. Parts and control boxes are easy to find. We match the motor and the wire gauge to the depth of your well.

    Well Pump Sizing in Troy

    Sizing starts with the well log. We get depth and static level from the well log or a sounder, then a draw-down test shows what the well can really produce in gallons per minute. Pump more than the well yields and it runs dry.

    Control Box and Pressure Switch Replacement in Troy, NY

    Not every dry faucet means a dead pump. A pressure switch with pitted contacts or a plugged sensing tube can stop the pump even though nothing is wrong down the well. A new switch runs about $30 in parts. You get to see the old part.
