    Slab leaks, where water lines running beneath your home's concrete foundation leak, are a particularly challenging issue. New York has some of the oldest housing in the country, and many homes still have galvanized steel supply pipes and cast iron drains. These leaks can undermine your foundation, leading to major structural damage. We use specialized listening devices and camera inspections to pinpoint slab leaks precisely and offer repair options including direct slab penetration, re-routing, or pipe lining. Addressing a slab leak promptly in Troy is crucial to prevent extensive, costly foundation damage.

    Water Line Repair at the Meter in Troy, NY

    Leaks often start at the compression fittings and couplings right next to the meter box. Shifting soil and heavy traffic over the box can loosen those joints over time. We determine whether the leak is on the utility side or the customer side, since the water provider is in charge of the meter and the pipe before it. When the leak is yours, we replace the fittings and the customer shutoff valve on the same trip.

    Underground Water Line Leak Repair in Troy

    Underground water line leaks are often hard to detect until serious damage has occurred. Common signs include sudden spikes in your water bill, damp spots in your yard, or a noticeable drop in water pressure. Our team uses acoustic leak detection and ground-penetrating radar to precisely locate these hidden leaks in Troy, reducing the need for extensive excavation. We repair breaks caused by shifting soil, tree root intrusion, or aging pipes, restoring your water service efficiently.

    Emergency Water Line Break Repair in Troy, NY

    A full break can flood a yard, wash out soil under walkways and leave your home without water. Our emergency crews shut off the supply, find the break and set up a temporary bypass when the repair will take more than a few hours. Most emergency breaks are repaired on the same day. We also document the damage with photos for your insurance or HOA.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Troy

    If your service line runs under a driveway, pool deck or mature landscaping, trenchless replacement can save a lot of restoration cost. We dig small pits at each end and pull new HDPE or PEX pipe through using a pneumatic boring tool or pipe pulling equipment. The process usually takes one day. You keep your pavers and trees, and the new line carries a long manufacturer warranty.

    Main Water Line Replacement in Troy, NY

    When repairs are no longer cost-effective due to extensive damage or persistent issues, a full main water line replacement may be necessary. This often applies to older homes in Troy, NY with original galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes that are prone to corrosion and leaks. We offer complete replacement services, upgrading your system with durable, modern materials like PEX or copper for a reliable water supply for decades to come. We handle all required permits and inspections.

    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Troy, NY

    Many homes built before the 1970s still have galvanized steel service lines that rust from the inside. Private wells serve a large share of rural homes, and they can carry iron, sulfur or hardness that discolors fixtures and clogs filters. Hard water speeds up scale buildup, which restricts flow and causes rust-colored water. Patching galvanized pipe seldom holds up because the surrounding pipe is just as corroded. We usually recommend replacing the full run from meter to house with copper or PEX, which restores pressure and stops repeat leaks.
