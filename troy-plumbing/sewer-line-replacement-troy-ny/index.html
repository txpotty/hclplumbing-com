    What Goes Into a Sewer Line Replacement in Troy, NY

    How Pipe Bursting Works in Troy

    A winch pulls the bursting head. The head is slightly bigger than the old pipe, so it splits clay or cast iron outward into the soil while fused HDPE trails behind it in one continuous piece. You get a pit at each end. With no joints in the new pipe, roots have no way back in.

    Trenchless Sewer Replacement in Troy, NY

    Two holes instead of one long trench. Pipe bursting drags a cone-shaped head through the old line, cracking it outward while new fused HDPE follows right behind. The other option is a cured-in-place liner. Lining puts a resin tube inside the old pipe and cures it hard, but only if the host pipe is still mostly round and has no major sags.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Troy

    A collapsed run or a long belly usually means we dig. We open a trench along the old line, pull the broken pipe and lay new PVC on compacted bedding at a steady fall of an eighth to a quarter inch per foot. Where we can, the sod is cut and set aside before the digging starts. The inspector sees open pipe. Backfill goes in compacted lifts so the trench line does not sink into a ditch a year later.

    Cured-in-Place Sewer Lining in Troy

    Lining builds a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line first, often with a cutter and a jetter, then pull a resin-saturated felt tube into place and inflate it so it cures hard against the host pipe. Collapsed pipe will not work. Lining shines under finished basements and slabs. We say plainly whether your line qualifies.

    Sewer Permits and Code in Troy, NY

    Sewer replacement nearly always needs a permit. Our office files the permit and books the inspection, and our crew meets the inspector so you do not have to. Code covers pipe size, fall, bedding and cleanout placement. Skipping the permit can come back to bite you at resale.

    Fixing a Bellied Sewer by Trench in Troy, NY

    A belly is a low spot that holds water. Bursting and lining follow the old path, so they keep the belly, and the only way to correct the fall is to dig, regrade the bedding and lay new pipe at a steady slope. Rocky ground slows the dig. The estimate accounts for depth and soil once we have located the line. Joints are inspected by us before the city does.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Troy

    No replacement quote without video. We run a self-leveling color camera from the cleanout to the main and record every joint, crack, root mass and low spot along the way, with footage you keep. A transmitter in the head lets us paint the spot and depth above ground. That one step decides between a spot repair and a full run.

    Sewer Codes and Inspections in Troy, NY

    Inspectors check what gets buried. Most of New York follows a statewide building code based on the International Plumbing Code, though a few large cities enforce their own rules. They look at sizing, fall, bedding, cleanouts and how the new pipe ties into the city side. We book the inspection and meet the inspector. Unpermitted pipe sometimes has to be uncovered again for inspection, at the owner's expense.
