    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Troy

    Submersible sump pumps are popular in Troy for their quiet operation and ability to sit fully submerged in the sump pit. When replacing a submersible pump, we carefully remove the old unit, clean the pit and install a new, appropriately sized pump. We make sure the check valve and discharge piping are correctly connected to prevent backflow and improve efficiency. That is key for handling heavy rainfall.

    Commercial Pit Pump Replacement in Troy

    Commercial buildings often have elevator pits, parking garage drains and mechanical room sumps. These require pumps rated for higher flow and sometimes oil-sensing controls. We replace commercial pumps and alarms to meet code and insurance requirements. Duplex systems with alternating pumps provide a backup. We schedule work to avoid disrupting operations.

    Sump Pump Basin and Pit Replacement in Troy, NY

    Sometimes it is not just the pump that needs replacing, since the sump basin or pit itself may be cracked, damaged or too small. HCL Plumbing offers sump pump basin and pit replacement in Troy, NY so your entire system works properly. A properly sized and intact pit is essential for the efficient operation and longevity of your new sump pump.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Troy

    Power outages during severe storms can leave a primary sump pump useless, leading to basement flooding. A battery backup sump pump offers important protection during these times. We install and replace complete battery backup systems, including dedicated marine-grade batteries and smart charging units. Our team helps you select a system with sufficient run-time to protect your Troy home through extended power interruptions. You get peace of mind when the lights go out.

    Wayne Sump Pump Replacement in Troy

    Wayne sump pumps offer a range of efficient models, from entry-level to professional-grade. If your Wayne sump pump is failing, HCL Plumbing provides fast Wayne sump pump replacement in Troy. We make sure the new pump is correctly installed with proper discharge and check valve components, restoring your home's flood protection quickly and efficiently.

    High-Capacity Sump Pump Upgrades in Troy

    If your current sump pump struggles to keep up with heavy water flow, especially during intense thunderstorms, it may be undersized. We offer high-capacity sump pump replacements and upgrades designed to handle large water volumes. Our plumbers will assess your water intrusion patterns and recommend a pump with greater horsepower and a higher GPM (gallons per minute) rating. This prevents the pump from cycling too frequently or failing under stress, common issues in some Troy properties.

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Troy

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves without binding. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats suit larger pits.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in Troy

    Basement bathrooms and laundry rooms below the sewer line need sewage ejector pumps instead of sump pumps. These pumps handle solids and must be installed in sealed, vented basins. We replace ejector pumps with models rated for two inch solids. We also check the basin seal, vent and discharge check valve. Proper installation prevents sewer gas odors.
