    Sump Pump Types We Install in Troy, NY

    Elevator and Garage Pit Pumps in Troy

    Elevator pits and parking garage sumps often require oil-sensing controls that stop the pump from sending hydraulic fluid or motor oil into the storm drain. We replace those pumps and controls to meet code. Duplex setups with alternating pumps give you a spare if one quits. Some insurance carriers ask for test records.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Troy

    Nor'easters and tropical storm remnants can overwhelm municipal sewers, so backwater valves are a sensible addition for basement homes. A standard pump just sits there once the power drops. We add a 12-volt backup pump on its own float, mounted a few inches above the primary, with a deep-cycle battery and a charger that tests itself. It also covers a stuck primary switch. A high-water alarm lets you know the backup has taken over.

    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Troy

    A submersible pump sits at the bottom of the pit, sealed in an oil-filled housing, and it runs quieter than anything else you can put down there. Out comes the old pump, out comes the muck at the bottom of the basin, and the new one goes in on a brick to stay clear of silt. The check valve gets replaced at the same time. A worn valve lets water fall back and makes the pump run twice as often.

    Sump Basin and Pit Replacement in Troy, NY

    Old clay tile or 18-inch plastic basins crack and fill with silt, and a pump in a basin that small cycles every few minutes during a heavy rain. We break out the concrete, set a larger poly basin with a sealed lid, and pour the floor back. More volume in the pit means fewer starts per hour. Fewer starts, longer motor life.

    Zoeller Switch and Pump Repair in Troy

    Zoeller units with a piggyback float plug the pump cord into the switch cord, and if that piggyback plug fails the pump never gets power. That is the first thing we test. Sometimes a new switch is all you need, and sometimes the motor windings are shot. We tell you which before we sell you anything.

    Pedestal Pumps for Shallow Pits in Troy, NY

    Older basements sometimes have a narrow, shallow pit poured right into the floor, and a pedestal pump is often the only thing that fits without breaking concrete. We replace the pump and float rod and rerun the PVC up to the check valve. Cheaper than a submersible, too.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Troy, NY

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the pit, out of the water, which is why a good one can run fifteen years in a dry basement. You will hear one kick on from upstairs. We set the new column plumb, secure it so vibration does not walk it across the basin, and adjust the float rod stops for the cycle you want. They fit pits as narrow as 10 inches.

    Cast Iron Submersible Pumps in Troy

    Cast iron sheds motor heat into the water around it far better than a plastic housing does, which is a big part of why a Zoeller M53 or Liberty 250 outlasts a hardware-store pump. Most swaps take one to two hours. We also check that the float has room to travel without touching the basin wall.
