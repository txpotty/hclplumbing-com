    One-Piece Toilet Replacement in Troy, NY

    One-piece toilets have no tank-to-bowl joint, so there are fewer leak points and fewer crevices to clean. They are heavier and pricier than two-piece models.     Comfort Height Toilet Installation in Troy

    Chair height toilets put the seat about 17 to 19 inches off the floor, roughly two inches higher than a standard bowl, and that makes a real difference for knees and hips.     Rental and Multi-Unit Toilet Replacement in Troy, NY

    Commercial Flush Valve Upgrades in Troy

    Manual flush valves on older commercial toilets leak at the handle and stick open, and swapping to a sensor valve during a replacement improves hygiene without changing the rough-in. Some carriers need new bolts. We match the new bowl to the existing carrier whenever possible, which avoids opening the wall.
