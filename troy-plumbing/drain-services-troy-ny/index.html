    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Services in Troy

    The cost of drain services in Troy, NY can vary a great deal, typically ranging from $150 for a simple clog removal to over $5,000 for a major sewer line replacement. Factors like the type of blockage, its location, and the method required (for example, snaking versus hydro jetting) all influence the final price. We provide transparent, upfront pricing after an on-site assessment.

    Hydro jetting is a thorough drain cleaning method that uses high-pressure water to clear blockages and scour the inside of pipes. It's often necessary for severe clogs, tree root intrusions, grease buildup, or when traditional snaking isn't effective, particularly for main sewer lines.

    Common signs include slow draining sinks or tubs, gurgling noises from drains, foul odors coming from pipes, water backing up in sinks or toilets, and multiple fixtures draining slowly. If you notice any of these, it's smart to call a professional before the problem escalates to a full blockage or a sewage backup.

    Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to clean the interior surfaces of your pipes, effectively removing grease, scale, and even tree roots. It is safe for most modern plumbing systems. Our technicians will perform a camera inspection first to ensure your pipes are structurally sound enough to handle the process.

    Many homes benefit from main line cleaning every one to two years, especially with mature trees nearby. Restaurants typically need jetting every three to six months.

    Minor drain cleaning usually does not require a permit. However, major work like sewer line repair or replacement, especially if it involves excavation or connecting to the city's main sewer, typically requires permits from the local building department. We will advise you and handle the necessary permitting.

    Avoid pouring grease down drains, use drain screens in sinks and showers to catch hair and food particles, and be mindful of what gets flushed down toilets. Regular preventative maintenance, like an annual professional drain cleaning, can also be very effective.

    Hydro jetting is safe for pipe in sound condition, including PVC and most cast iron. We camera the line first so we do not jet a pipe that is already cracked or collapsing.
