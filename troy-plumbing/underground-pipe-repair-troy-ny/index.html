    Long-Lasting Underground Pipe Repair Services in Troy, NY

    Galvanized and Polybutylene Line Replacement in Troy

    Galvanized steel pipe rusts from the inside, and polybutylene pipe becomes brittle and splits without warning. If your underground service is either material, repairs are often temporary. We replace the line with PEX or Type K copper, often by pulling the new pipe through the old path to limit digging. A new line restores pressure and removes the risk of repeated leaks.

    Underground Pipe Repair Cost Comparison in Troy, NY

    A single point repair on a shallow water line is the most economical option. Pipe bursting or pulling a new line through the old path costs more but avoids open trenching and long-term risk. Full trench replacement is sometimes needed when the old route is blocked or the pipe is too damaged. We explain each method and its cost so you can choose the best value.

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Troy

    Many homes built in the 1980s and 1990s may have polybutylene pipes, which are prone to sudden failure due to their material composition. If you have polybutylene in your underground water lines, proactive replacement is often recommended. We specialize in safely removing and replacing these problem pipes with modern, durable alternatives, preventing future leaks in Troy.

    Pipe Bursting Services in Troy, NY

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless technique where a new pipe is pulled through the path of the old, broken pipe, fracturing the old pipe outward at the same time. This method is very effective for complete pipe replacement, creating a new, durable pipe of the same or larger diameter. It's a robust solution for severely damaged or collapsed sewer and water lines, offering a long-term fix in Troy, NY.

    Underground Sewer Line Repair in Troy, NY

    Buried sewer laterals crack from root intrusion, soil settling and age. We camera the line to find the exact spot, then excavate and replace the damaged section with PVC. Shielded couplings make a watertight connection to the existing pipe. We confirm proper slope and flow before backfilling and tamping the trench.

    Clogged or damaged underground storm drains can lead to serious flooding and foundation issues, especially during heavy rain.
