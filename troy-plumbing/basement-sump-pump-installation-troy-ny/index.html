    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Troy

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the water. That keeps it dry and easy to reach, which is why they suit narrow pits under 14 inches across, and why they often outlast cheap submersibles in Troy homes. Expect more noise.

    Sump Basin and Discharge Line Installation in Troy

    The basin sets the ceiling on what the pump can do. It goes at the lowest point in the slab, bed it in washed stone, and run the discharge line with a slight pitch to the exit point so nothing sits in the pipe in Troy. Water should never come back.

    Some lots sit in a high water table and fill a standard pit every few minutes during spring. Those basements need a 1/2 or 3/4 horsepower pump in a larger basin, all sized after we time how fast your pit refills. We use a stopwatch.

    Submersibles sit in the pit, sealed, and run quiet. Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the water, which makes them cheaper and easier to service but louder, so they work best in an unfinished utility room rather than a finished family room. Both move water fine.
