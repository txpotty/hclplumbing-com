    Preventative Sewer Maintenance in Troy

    Roots come back after cutting. Plan on regrowth in 12 to 18 months. A scheduled cleaning every year or two keeps roots and grease from closing the line and lets you plan around it.

    Commercial Sewer Cleaning in Troy, NY

    Commercial lines take a beating. Restaurants fight grease every day. Offices and retail buildings deal more with paper towels and foreign objects flushed in public restrooms, which snag on joints and catch everything after them, and our heavy jetters handle 6 inch commercial pipe without much trouble.

    Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in Troy

    Jetting cleans the whole pipe wall. A cable only makes a path. A jetter sends high-pressure water around the full circumference of the pipe, removing buildup that a cable would leave behind.

    Root Masses in Troy Sewer Laterals

    Mature trees and old clay laterals do not mix. Roots find the joints. We cut roots in several passes, then look on camera to judge the damage and talk about lining or a spot repair.

    Apartment and Strip Center Sewers in Troy, NY

    Shared laterals carry heavy loads. Many tenants use a single line. We set up jetting on a schedule that fits your pump-out cycle and do the work when the building is quiet, with a written report and a video file on request.

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Lines in Troy

    Older properties may still have clay or Orangeburg pipe. Each fails in its own way. Roots love clay joints, while Orangeburg fiber pipe tends to squash into an oval and eventually collapse, so we clean these lines carefully and map the joints and soft spots on camera.

    Dealing with Grease Buildup in Troy, NY

    Grease hardens as it cools. Then it narrows the pipe. Kitchen grease coats the sewer line the same way plaque coats an artery, and over a few years the opening can close to the size of a finger, which is when drains start to back up, and jetting is the right fix.

    Sewer Camera Inspections in Troy, NY

    On a repeat backup, the camera goes in first. We would rather look than guess. The camera feed shows roots, grease, breaks and bellies in real time, and the locator marks the spot on the ground above.
