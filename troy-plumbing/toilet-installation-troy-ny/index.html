    Frequently Asked Questions About Toilet Installation in Troy

    Labor to install a customer-supplied standard toilet typically runs $200 to $400. Including a mid-range toilet, most installs total $450 to $900, while wall-hung systems with carriers range from $1,500 to $3,500 or more. We give you a flat price up front.

    We are experienced in installing all major toilet brands, including Kohler, American Standard, Toto, Delta, Gerber, and many others. Our plumbers are familiar with the specific requirements for each manufacturer to deliver a proper and efficient installation.

    Common signs that you might need a new toilet include frequent clogs, constant running, visible cracks in the bowl or tank, excessive wobbling, or a toilet that is very old and inefficient. If you're constantly repairing it, replacement is often more cost-effective.

    Installing a new toilet can offer several benefits, including improved water efficiency, lower water bills, better flushing power, a better look, and increased comfort. Modern toilets are also designed to be more durable and easier to clean.

    While it's possible for a homeowner to attempt a DIY toilet installation, it's often best left to professionals. Improper installation can lead to leaks, wobbling, inefficient flushing, and potential water damage. Professional plumbers ensure proper sealing, secure mounting, and adherence to codes.

    The rough-in is the distance from the finished wall to the center of the drain, typically 12 inches but sometimes 10 or 14. Buying a toilet with the wrong rough-in means it will not fit or will sit too far from the wall.

    A standard toilet installation, assuming no major plumbing modifications are needed, typically takes about 1 to 3 hours. More complex installations, such as those requiring new rough-ins or dealing with damaged subflooring, may take longer.
