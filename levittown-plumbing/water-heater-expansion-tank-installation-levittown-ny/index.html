    Expansion Tank Sizing for Large Water Heaters in Levittown

    A standard 2 gallon tank works for many 40 and 50 gallon heaters at moderate pressure. Larger heaters, higher incoming pressure or high-capacity systems may need a 4.5 gallon tank or larger. We use manufacturer sizing charts that factor in heater volume, supply pressure and temperature setting. Correct sizing keeps the tank from over-flexing and wearing out early.

    New Expansion Tank Installation in Levittown

    If your home has a pressure reducing valve and no expansion tank, the water heater is probably seeing a pressure spike every heating cycle. We install a new tank on the cold water supply near the heater using a tee and short nipple. The air side is precharged to match your static pressure, usually between 50 and 70 psi. We then run the heater and watch the gauge to confirm pressure stays steady.

    Pressure Reducing Valve and Expansion Tank Pairing in Levittown

    Many homes see high incoming pressure, so pressure reducing valves are common. The PRV protects your fixtures but also turns your plumbing into a closed system, which makes an expansion tank necessary. We test the PRV output and replace it if it is creeping or failing. Pairing a healthy PRV with a correctly charged expansion tank gives you stable pressure throughout the house.

    Brands and Tank Materials in Levittown, NY

    We install potable-rated thermal expansion tanks from brands such as Watts, Amtrol and A.O. Smith. Potable tanks use a butyl diaphragm and a lined interior so water never touches bare steel.
