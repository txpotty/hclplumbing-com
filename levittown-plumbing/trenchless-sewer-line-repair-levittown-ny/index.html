    Cast Iron Sewer Line Lining in Levittown, NY

    Cast iron rusts from the inside out, and the bottom of the pipe goes first. Plumbers call that channel rot, a groove that eats through the bottom. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or carbide cutters spinning on a flexible shaft, which brings the wall back to bare metal so a liner can bond to it. Under a slab, lining beats breaking up the floor in two rooms.

    Sectional Point Repair in Levittown

    A point repair is a short liner, often 3 to 5 feet long, set over one bad spot. It rides in on an inflatable packer, guided by the camera footage, and the packer inflates to press the resin-soaked sleeve against the pipe wall until it cures. It fixes a single crack or root entry, not a whole failing line. Sound lines with one bad joint are the right candidates.

    Sewer Line Video Inspection in Levittown

    We record the full camera run on every job, from the cleanout out to the city main or the septic tank. You get the file. On the footage you can see what we see, roots at a joint at 22 feet and an offset at 38 feet where the pipe dropped. That map drives the repair plan.

    Pipe Bursting in Levittown, NY

    Two pits are standard for a burst, one at the house end near the foundation and one out near the street or the city tap. The new HDPE pipe is butt-fused into one length first. The winch or hydraulic puller then drags the bursting head and the new pipe through. It goes quickly.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Repair in Levittown

    Bursting can upsize the line. A 4-inch clay lateral can be replaced with 6-inch HDPE, which helps on lines that clog because they were undersized from the start or have been added onto with extra bathrooms. Each end only needs a pit around three by four feet. No trench in between.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacements in Levittown

    A bursting head is a steel cone, slightly wider than the old pipe, pulled through the line by a hydraulic unit or a winch cable. It cracks the old pipe outward into the soil while a string of new HDPE pipe follows right behind it. Fused joints, no gaps. We use it when the old line is too broken or flattened to hold a liner.

    Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Repair in Levittown

    Orangeburg is bituminous fiber pipe that was laid in houses mostly from the late 1940s into the early 1970s. Over time it softens, goes oval and blisters on the inside, so a liner has nothing round to bond to and bursting is usually the better fix. Clay is different. Clay that still holds its round shape is a good candidate for a liner.

    Sewer Camera Inspections for Trenchless Repair in Levittown

    Every trenchless job starts with a push camera fed in from the cleanout or a pulled toilet. The screen shows the inside of the pipe live, and a sonde in the camera head lets us mark the exact spot on the ground above it. That tells us the depth. No guessing where to dig.
