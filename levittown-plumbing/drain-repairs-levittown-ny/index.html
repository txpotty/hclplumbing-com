    Storm Drain Repair in Levittown, NY

    Yard and storm drains carry rain away from the foundation. Coastal areas face storm surge and flooding, which can destroy water heaters and boilers installed at basement level.     Kitchen Sink Drain Repair in Levittown, NY

    Kitchen drains take grease, food and soap scum. They clog the most. We clear the branch, replace corroded or cracked tailpieces, fix leaking slip nuts and reset traps that were installed too low or too high for the trap arm to drain properly. The disposal and dishwasher hookups get a look too.

    Broken Drain Pipe Repair in Levittown, NY

    Broken drain pipe usually comes from settling, roots or age. Sometimes it is a bad glue joint. We find the break with a camera and a locator, then replace that section with new pipe joined by shielded couplings that fit the old and new outside diameters. We test it before closing up.

    Clogged Drain Repair in Levittown

    Grease, hair, food scraps and mineral scale cause most clogs. Repeat clogs are a warning. We clear the line with a drum auger or a jetter, then camera the pipe when the blockage is deep or keeps coming back, which shows whether you have buildup, a sag or a broken fitting. That decides the repair.

    Recurring Clog Repair in Levittown

    Monthly clogs mean there is a physical cause. Cleaning will not stop it. The usual suspects are a trap arm with too little slope, a branch line with a belly, scale built up inside old metal pipe or a fitting with a rough edge that snags paper, and most of them are obvious once the camera or a pulled trap shows the inside. We repair the part that catches debris.

    Tub and Shower Drain Repair in Levittown, NY

    Hair, soap scum and minerals narrow bath drains over time. Gaskets fail too. We replace worn pop-up assemblies, broken tub shoes and leaking shower drain bodies, often working from inside the tub so the ceiling underneath stays intact. When access is needed, we keep the opening small.
