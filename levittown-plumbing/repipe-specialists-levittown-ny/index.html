    Common signs you need repiping include persistent low water pressure throughout your home, discolored or rusty-looking water, frequent pipe leaks, visible corrosion on exposed pipes, and a metallic taste or odor in your water.     While fixing individual leaks might seem cheaper in the short term, it's often a temporary solution for an aging plumbing system.
