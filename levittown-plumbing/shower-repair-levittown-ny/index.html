    Faulty Shower Valve Repair in Levittown

    Problems with your shower's water temperature or pressure often point to a malfunctioning mixing valve or cartridge. These components control the hot and cold water flow. Our technicians are experienced at diagnosing valve issues and can repair or replace them, ensuring you get consistent water temperature and pressure every time you shower. We work with all major valve makers.

    Shower Diverter Repair in Levittown, NY

    A diverter sends water from the tub spout to the showerhead. When it wears out, water keeps running from the spout and shower pressure drops. We replace diverter spouts, three-handle diverter stems and multi-function diverter valves. This restores full flow to the showerhead.

    Leaky Shower Head Repair in Levittown

    A dripping shower head wastes water and can be very annoying. Our plumbers can quickly identify the cause, whether it is a worn-out washer, O-ring, or a faulty cartridge. We carry common replacement parts on our trucks to ensure a fast fix. We will stop the leak, saving you money on your water bill and preventing potential water damage in your bathroom.

    Tub and Shower Combination Repair in Levittown, NY

    Showerhead Repair and Replacement in Levittown

    A malfunctioning showerhead can spray erratically, have reduced pressure, or even leak. Often, mineral deposits from the water supply can clog the nozzles, or internal components can wear out. We can clean, repair, or replace your showerhead with a new, efficient model. We offer options from standard to rain showerheads, providing proper installation for strong performance and water conservation.

    Showerhead and Arm Repair in Levittown

    Dripping showerheads, leaking arm connections and broken handheld hoses are common problems. We clean or replace showerheads, reseal shower arms and install new handheld hoses and holders. We can also add a slide bar for a handheld shower. Hard water clogs nozzles, so we recommend heads with flexible rubber nozzles that are simple to clean.

    Shower Faucet Leak Fixes in Levittown

    Beyond the shower head, leaks can occur at the faucet handles or behind the wall. These leaks can be harder to spot but can cause serious damage if left unaddressed.     Shower Pan and Wall Leak Repair in Levittown, NY
