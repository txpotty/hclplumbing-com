    Proven Walls Waterproofing Solutions in Levittown, NY

    Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Levittown

    Even minor cracks in foundation walls can allow significant water intrusion. Our services include crack repair using epoxy or polyurethane injection to seal these entry points permanently. This method fills the entire depth of the crack, restoring strength and preventing water penetration. We address both vertical and horizontal cracks, knowing that horizontal cracks can signal structural issues that need a thorough assessment before sealing. Addressing these early prevents further water damage and costly future repairs.

    Waterproofing for Older Homes in Levittown, NY

    Older homes, especially those built before modern waterproofing standards, are especially prone to water intrusion. New York has some of the oldest housing in the country, and many homes still have galvanized steel supply pipes and cast iron drains. Many older foundations may lack adequate exterior drainage or proper sealing. We specialize in assessing and upgrading waterproofing systems for these properties, often combining exterior excavation with interior drainage solutions. We take care to respect the existing structure while installing modern, effective waterproofing, with attention to the challenges of older brick and stone foundations.

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Levittown, NY

    Interior walls waterproofing focuses on managing water that has already passed through the foundation walls. This usually involves installing an interior drainage system, often called a French drain, along the perimeter of the basement floor. Water is collected and channeled to a sump pump, which then sends it safely away from the foundation. While not preventing water from entering the wall, it stops it from pooling inside your living space. This solution is less disruptive than exterior excavation and is suitable where exterior access is limited.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Levittown

    Exterior foundation waterproofing involves excavating around the foundation to apply a waterproof membrane or coating directly to the wall. This is often the most effective method for preventing water from entering the basement or crawl space. We use high-quality asphalt-modified polyurethane membranes or polymer-based coatings that resist hydrostatic pressure and soil movement. Much of New York sits on glacial till, rock and clay, which makes excavation for sewer and water lines slower and harder. Proper drainage solutions, such as French drains, are also added to divert water away.

    Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in Levittown

    New York has some of the oldest housing in the country, and many homes still have galvanized steel supply pipes and cast iron drains. Wherever a home has a basement or below-grade walls, heavy rain and snowmelt can push water through foundation walls and cracks. We seal cracks with injection products, apply interior or exterior waterproofing and install drainage where needed. A sump pump may be added if groundwater builds up.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Levittown

    Tile and grout are not waterproof on their own, so water reaches the backer board in every shower. If the walls were built without a proper membrane, moisture soaks into studs and drywall. We remove failing tile, install cement board or foam board and apply a membrane such as Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban. Seams, corners and niches get extra reinforcement before new tile is set.

    Drainage System Integration in Levittown, NY

    Lasting walls waterproofing often requires a solid drainage system to divert water away from your foundation. This can include interior perimeter drains that channel water to a sump pump, or exterior French drains that collect surface water. Our team designs and installs custom drainage solutions for Levittown, NY properties, so water is efficiently managed and moved away from your home's vulnerable areas.

    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Levittown, NY

    A cracked or poorly sloped shower pan often causes wet baseboards and stained ceilings below. We test the pan by plugging the drain and flooding it to confirm the leak. Then we rebuild the pan with correct pre-slope, a new liner or bonded membrane and a properly clamped drain. A second flood test proves the pan is watertight before tile.
