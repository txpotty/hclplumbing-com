    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Levittown, NY

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. HCL Plumbing sets them up with the right resin and brine tanks. We program regeneration by metered water use so the system only recharges when the resin is actually spent, which saves a lot of salt. Most effective option.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Levittown

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. They change the minerals into crystals that do not stick to pipe walls and heating elements, so scale drops off without adding sodium or using a drain line. Little upkeep is needed. They work best on moderate water.

    Culligan Water Softener Installation in Levittown

    Culligan systems are common in homes around here, and we install and connect them. Each install follows the brand's specs for drain, bypass and power, then we program it to your water test. We handle all of it. Existing loops make it faster.

    Water Softener Replacement & Upgrades in Levittown, NY

    Upgrading an old timer-based softener is one of the easiest ways to cut salt use. Newer valves meter actual water use, some connect to Wi-Fi for salt alerts and higher-capacity tanks regenerate less often for larger households. Salt bills drop. We swap it out in a few hours.

    Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Levittown, NY

    City water often carries chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell. Putting a carbon tank in front of the softener takes out chlorine and keeps it from wearing down the resin. You will taste it. Setups can be two tanks or one dual-stage tank.
