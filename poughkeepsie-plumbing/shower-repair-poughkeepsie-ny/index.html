    Fixing Showers in Poughkeepsie, NY

    Shower Handle Leak Repair in Poughkeepsie

    Leaks at the handle or behind the trim plate are easy to miss. Water running down the inside of the trim gets behind the tile at the valve opening, and over a few months it soaks the drywall around the valve without ever showing on the shower side. We pull the trim and look. It is usually a loose bonnet nut or a worn O-ring.

    Hot Water Pressure Problems in Poughkeepsie, NY

    Weak hot water with strong cold usually rules out the showerhead. The problem is on the hot side, either sediment in the water heater outlet, a partly closed valve at the heater or a cartridge whose hot port is packed with scale. Each side gets tested. Good hot flow at the sinks points us straight at the shower cartridge.

    Emergency Shower Leak Repair in Poughkeepsie

    Water coming through a ceiling below a bathroom needs fast action. Close the main, set a bucket under the drip and stay clear of any drywall that is bulging, because wet ceilings can drop. Then call us. Our plumber locates the leak, stops it and starts drying the area.

    Shower Temperature Swing Repair in Poughkeepsie

    Cold blasts when a toilet flushes mean the pressure-balance spool is stuck. That spool is supposed to slide back and forth to keep the hot and cold mix even when pressure drops on one side, and scale can freeze it in place. Replacing the cartridge solves it. Water that never gets hot enough may only need the limit stop turned.

    Leaky Shower Valve Repair in Poughkeepsie

    One drip every few seconds adds up to hundreds of gallons a year. Single-handle showers drip from a worn cartridge or O-rings, while old two-handle valves drip from a washer and a pitted seat. We carry the common parts. Moen, Delta, Kohler and Pfister cartridges cover most of the valves we open, so the repair is usually done that same visit.

    Emergency Shower Repair in Poughkeepsie, NY

    A shower that will not shut off is an emergency. Close the main valve where the water line enters the house, or the shower's own stops if it has them behind an access panel. Then call us.     Shower Diverter Repair in Poughkeepsie, NY

    Diverters send water up to the head instead of out the tub spout. When the gate in a lift-style spout wears or a three-handle diverter stem loses its washer, water keeps pouring from the spout and the shower gets weak. Usually a new spout fixes it. Spout replacements take about twenty minutes.

    Showerhead Repair and Replacement in Poughkeepsie

    Showerheads clog from the inside out. Minerals collect behind the face and inside the restrictor, which turns the spray patchy or weak with the valve fully open. A vinegar soak can work. When it does not, we install a new head with soft rubber nozzles that you can wipe clean with a thumb.
