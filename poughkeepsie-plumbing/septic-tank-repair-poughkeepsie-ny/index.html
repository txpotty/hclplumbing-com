    Septic Alarm Troubleshooting in Poughkeepsie

    A septic alarm sounding is the system's way of saying the water is too high in the pump chamber or the aerobic unit. Hit the mute switch and go easy on the water. We check whether the pump runs and whether a float is hung up on its cord. A tripped breaker is the cheapest answer.

    Septic Tank Leak Repair in Poughkeepsie

    A tank that never fills to the outlet, or one that fills slowly with groundwater after rain, has a leak. We pump it down and look at the walls and seams with a light, and dye flushed down a fixture shows us where the water moves. Hairline cracks get a hydraulic cement patch. Split tanks get replaced.

    Septic Tee Repair in Poughkeepsie

    Sanitary tees on the inlet and outlet crack or pop loose, sometimes from a careless pump-out hose. Without the outlet tee, the floating scum layer flows straight into the field lines and seals the trenches. We glue in a new Schedule 40 tee. An effluent filter goes on if the outlet has room.

    Effluent Pump Replacement in Poughkeepsie, NY

    Effluent pumps in a dosing chamber last roughly 8 to 15 years, and they usually quit without much warning at all. A pump that hums but moves no water has a stuck impeller or a bad capacitor. Our tech pulls the old unit and checks the discharge before the new one goes in. Floats get replaced at the same time.

    Inlet and Outlet Baffle Replacement in Poughkeepsie, NY

    Baffles hold back the scum layer on top and the sludge on the bottom so only clear effluent leaves the tank. Concrete ones get eaten by sulfuric acid from sewer gas and eventually fall off into the tank. Once the outlet baffle is gone, solids head straight for the field. We replace them with PVC sanitary tees. Usually a short job.

    Aerobic and Alternative System Repair in Poughkeepsie, NY

    Aerobic treatment units use an air pump to feed oxygen to the bacteria, and when that pump dies the unit starts to smell within days. Our techs rebuild linear aerators and swap rotary ones, plus clean out diffusers clogged with solids. Timers and panels get tested too. We also keep the service log your permit may require.

    Septic Lid and Riser Repair in Poughkeepsie

    Old concrete lids crack, and a cracked lid over a 1,000-gallon tank is a real danger to kids and pets. We replace failing lids and add risers that bring access up to the lawn. Future pumping gets cheaper. Each lid we install gets screws or a lock so a child cannot lift it.

    Emergency Septic Overflow in Poughkeepsie

    Raw sewage coming up through a tub drain carries bacteria you do not want on your skin or your floors. Keep kids and pets away from it and stop all water use. Our tech clears the line or calls in a pump truck, depending on what we find. Cleanup guidance comes with it.
