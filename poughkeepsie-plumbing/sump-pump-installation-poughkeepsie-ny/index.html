    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Poughkeepsie, NY

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the pit, so the motor never sits in water. You can reach the motor and the switch without fishing anything out of the pit, which helps in a basement where the pit is shallow or tight. Expect more motor noise than a submersible. We set the float rod and the discharge so the pump cycles cleanly.

    Commercial Sump Pump Solutions in Poughkeepsie

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and loading dock drains need pumps rated for more volume. We install duplex systems with alternating pumps. A flooded elevator pit shuts down a building. We plan the install around your business hours.

    Exterior Drainage and Yard Sump Pumps in Poughkeepsie

    Sunken patios, below-grade driveways and deep window wells collect water with nowhere to go. We install exterior basins with pumps for those spots. Slopes toward the house are the usual cause. We keep the outlet away from your foundation and the neighbor line.

    New Sump Pit Installation in Poughkeepsie

    No pit yet? We cut one at the low corner. We backfill around the basin with washed gravel so groundwater has a path in. Then we patch the slab. Last come the pump, the lid and the discharge line.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Poughkeepsie

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. They run quieter and move more water. Lids close flat over them. We seal the cord grommet and the lid on every install.

    Combination Sump Pump System Installation in Poughkeepsie, NY

    Two pumps on one riser cover both kinds of failure, a dead motor and a dead outlet. The primary handles daily water. People with carpet downstairs buy these. We install them in homes where a flood would ruin flooring and drywall.

    Sump Pump Replacement in Poughkeepsie, NY

    Floats stick and motors seize when a pump sits idle for months between storms. When we replace a pump we also check the basin for silt, look at the check valve flapper and run water through the discharge line to find any clogs. Undersized pumps get upsized. We fill the pit and watch it run.

    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Poughkeepsie

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. We size it from pit diameter and vertical lift. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. We usually pair that with a 1-1/2 inch discharge line.
