    Electric Water Heater Installation in Poughkeepsie, NY

    Electric tanks need a 240-volt circuit, usually a 30-amp double-pole breaker with 10-gauge wire. Before hooking up the new tank we check that the breaker and wire size match the elements, since a mismatch is a common cause of tripped breakers and scorched terminals. They do not need a vent. That makes placement easier.

    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Poughkeepsie, NY

    Without gas service, an electric tank is the usual pick. We match voltage and breaker size to the panel, and since there is no vent, an electric tank can sit in spots a gas unit cannot. A drain pan goes under closet installs. Quiet and simple.

    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Poughkeepsie, NY

    Going tankless means the heater fires only when a tap opens, and it never runs out. Gas tankless models usually need a bigger gas line than the old tank had, plus a dedicated vent run through the wall or roof. Upfront cost is higher. We confirm your meter and line can handle it first.

    Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in Poughkeepsie, NY

    Heat pump water heaters pull warmth from the surrounding air and can use about a third of the power of a standard electric tank. A hybrid needs open air space, often around 700 cubic feet, plus a condensate line to a drain, which rules out most small closets. Rebates may apply. We help you decide if one fits.

    Expansion Tank Installation in Poughkeepsie, NY

    A pressure reducing valve or check valve on the main turns your plumbing into a closed system. When the water heats and expands with nowhere to go, pressure climbs and stresses the relief valve, the toilet fill valves and every supply line in the house. The tank soaks up that extra volume. We pre-charge it to match your water pressure.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Poughkeepsie
