    Float Switch Inspection and Adjustment in Glens Falls, NY

    The float switch is a critical component that tells your sump pump when to turn on and off. During maintenance, we inspect the float switch for freedom of movement and proper calibration, making adjustments as needed to ensure reliable operation for your home in Glens Falls, NY.
