    Gas Tankless Water Heater Repair in Glens Falls

    Pressure at full fire matters on a gas tankless burner, not just when it first fires. We put a manometer on the inlet test port and run every hot tap in the house, since pressure that sags under load leads to flame loss and slugs of cold water. Then we check the exhaust. Codes come off when the numbers are right.

    Tankless Error Code Diagnostics in Glens Falls, NY

    Every tankless brand flashes its own codes, and the same number can mean different things on two units. A Rinnai 11 is no ignition, a Navien E003 is ignition failure, and a Noritz 11 is also an ignition fault, but the tests behind them differ. We read the code, pull the history and test the part tied to it. Guessing gets expensive.

    Flow Sensor and Thermistor Repair in Glens Falls

    Tankless heaters start when the flow sensor sees about half a gallon a minute, and they hold temperature off thermistor readings. A dirty inlet screen or a failing sensor shows up as no hot water, temperature swings or a unit that drops out mid-shower. Our tech cleans the screen, checks sensor values in the service menu and tests thermistor resistance against the chart. Usually under an hour.

    Condensing Tankless Repair in Glens Falls, NY

    Condensing tankless heaters pull extra heat out of the exhaust and make acidic condensate that has to drain somewhere. That condensate runs through a trap and, where code or the manual calls for it, a neutralizer filled with limestone or marble chips before it reaches the drain. Clogged traps shut the unit down. We clean the trap, refill the neutralizer and check the PVC vent for sag or the wrong slope.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Repair in Glens Falls, NY

    Whole-house electric tankless units pull a lot of current, often 100 to 150 amps on two or three dedicated breakers. A breaker that trips under load can mean a shorted element or a loose lug that has been arcing, and both get tested with a meter before anything is replaced. Element resistance and supply voltage get measured. Scorched wire gets cut back.

    No Hot Water Tankless Repair in Glens Falls

    No hot water at all usually points to ignition, gas supply or a heat exchanger choked with scale. We start with the error history in the service menu, then test the igniter, flame rod and gas pressure in that order, because a weak flame signal and low pressure can look identical on the display. We look at the vent run as well. Common igniters and flame rods ride on the truck.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling Service in Glens Falls
