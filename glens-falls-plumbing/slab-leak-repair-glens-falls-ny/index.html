    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Glens Falls, NY

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat expands and contracts the pipe against concrete and gravel.     Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in Glens Falls

    For more extensive damage, multiple leaks, or pipes that are too old and corroded to repair reliably, pipe rerouting is a long-term solution.
