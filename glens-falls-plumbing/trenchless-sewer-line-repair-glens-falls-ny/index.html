    Sectional Point Repair in Glens Falls

    When only one section of a line is damaged, a point repair can fix it without lining the whole length. We position a short resin liner at the damaged spot using the camera and inflate it until it cures. Point repairs are a cost-effective fix for a single crack, hole or root entry. They are common on otherwise healthy lines.

    Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Repair in Glens Falls

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg fiber pipe or vitrified clay sewer laterals. Orangeburg flattens and blisters over time, while clay cracks and separates at joints. Pipe bursting is often the best option for Orangeburg, and lining can work well for clay that still holds its shape. We identify the material on camera before recommending a method.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Repair in Glens Falls

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method where a new pipe is pulled through the old one while the old pipe is fractured outward. This creates a new, seamless and often larger diameter sewer line without the need for long trenching. It is best for severely damaged or collapsed pipes in Glens Falls that require full replacement rather than just relining.

    Cast Iron Sewer Line Repair in Glens Falls, NY

    Cast iron drains and sewer lines corrode from the inside, leaving rough scale, cracks and channel rot along the bottom of the pipe. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or cutters, then line it to restore a smooth, sealed interior. This is particularly useful for cast iron running under slabs where excavation is costly. Lining can add many years of service.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Glens Falls

    The cost of trenchless sewer line repair in Glens Falls, NY can vary widely, typically ranging from $4,000 to $15,000 or more, depending on the length of the pipe, the method used and the complexity of the job. Factors like pipe diameter and accessibility also play a role, so a professional assessment is key for an accurate estimate.

    HCL Plumbing offers solid warranties on our trenchless sewer line repairs, typically covering materials and workmanship for several years. Full warranty details will be provided with your estimate, giving you peace of mind in our durable solutions.

    For pipe bursting, we typically use high-density polyethylene pipe, known for its strength, flexibility and resistance to corrosion and roots. For CIPP relining, a felt liner saturated with epoxy resin, such as products from Perma-Liner or NuFlow, is used to create a new, seamless pipe within the old one.

    While trenchless methods are very versatile, they are not suitable for all situations. For instance, if a section of the pipe has completely collapsed or if there is severe misalignment that prevents the insertion of new pipe or liner, traditional excavation might be necessary. A camera inspection will determine feasibility.

    Most trenchless sewer line repairs can be completed in one to three days, depending on the scope and method. This is much faster than traditional excavation methods, which can often take a week or more plus additional time for property restoration.

    Trenchless sewer line repairs are designed for the long haul. Cured-in-place pipes and new pipes installed via pipe bursting are very durable, often lasting 50 years or even longer. Many manufacturers offer warranties of more than 25 years on their materials.

    Many cities require a permit for sewer replacement or lining, especially if work extends into the public right of way. We take care of permits when they are needed.

    Lines that are cracked, root-infested or corroded but still hold their shape are typically good candidates for lining. Collapsed or badly offset lines may need pipe bursting or a spot excavation.
