    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Glens Falls, NY

    Going tankless means the heater fires only when a tap opens, and it never runs out. Gas tankless models usually need a bigger gas line than the old tank had, plus a dedicated vent run through the wall or roof. They cost more to install. We confirm your meter and line can handle it first.

    Expansion Tank Installation in Glens Falls, NY

    A pressure reducing valve or check valve on the main turns your plumbing into a closed system. When the water heats and expands with nowhere to go, pressure climbs and stresses the relief valve, the toilet fill valves and every supply line in the house. An expansion tank absorbs it. We pre-charge it to match your water pressure.

    Traditional Tank Water Heater Installation in Glens Falls

    Tank heaters still heat most homes' water, holding 40 to 75 gallons at temperature until you need it. We size the tank by first-hour rating, not just gallons, because that number tells you how much hot water it delivers in a busy morning. Either fuel works. The fuel hookup, the venting and haul-away are all part of the job.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Glens Falls

    Closed plumbing systems trap the extra volume water gains when it heats. Plumber licensing is handled locally rather than statewide, so requirements differ from one city or county to the next. An expansion tank gives that water somewhere to go, so the relief valve stops dripping and pipe joints stop taking pressure spikes every time the burner runs. We set the air charge to match your water pressure. It mounts on the cold inlet.

    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in Glens Falls

    Attic and closet installs take more planning than a garage swap. Every attic heater needs a drain pan with a line to the outside or a drain, a relief line routed properly and a clear path for the next tank to come in and out. Ceilings are at stake. Relocation to a safer spot is an option.

    Gas Water Heater Installation in Glens Falls, NY

    Gas heaters recover faster than electric, often refilling a 50 gallon tank in under an hour. We install the gas line, the sediment trap and the vent connector, then check draft at the hood with a match or a smoke pencil to confirm exhaust is going up and out. Then we check for leaks. We add an expansion tank if code calls for it.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Glens Falls

    Swapping a gas water heater comes down to three connections done right, the gas, the vent and the water. Each gas swap gets a new connector, a drip leg, a draft test at the hood and a leak check with a detector at every fitting. Carbon monoxide is the risk. We follow code on all of it.
