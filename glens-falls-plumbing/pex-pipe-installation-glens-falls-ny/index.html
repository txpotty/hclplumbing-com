    Frequently Asked Questions About PEX Pipe Installation in Glens Falls

    A whole-home PEX repipe typically costs $4,500 to $12,000 depending on home size, bathroom count and access. Smaller jobs like a single bathroom remodel rough-in usually run $800 to $2,500.

    We install Uponor PEX-A along with Viega, Zurn and SharkBite PEX-B systems. We recommend the system that fits your project and budget.

    PEX is a good fit for a wide range of climates because of its flexibility and resistance to freezing. Humid summers bring condensation on cold pipes and toilet tanks, which can drip onto ceilings and floors below. While rigid pipes can burst when water freezes, PEX can expand and contract, greatly lowering the risk of burst pipes during cold snaps. It also performs well in high temperatures, making it a reliable choice year-round in Glens Falls.

    We install quality PEX piping from reputable manufacturers such as Uponor (PEX-A), Viega (PEX-B) and Zurn (PEX-B). We also work with other proven brands like SharkBite for fittings, so the best materials are used for your installation.

    PEX can be used for outdoor applications like irrigation lines or outdoor spigots, but it needs to be protected from direct UV light, which can break down the material over time. When used outdoors, PEX should always be buried or otherwise shielded.

    Most whole-home PEX repipes take two to four days. Larger homes or tight attic access can take a bit longer.

    Small repairs are possible for handy homeowners, but a full repipe requires proper sizing, support spacing and code compliance. Mistakes behind walls can cause hidden leaks and rejected inspections.

    PEX piping is designed to be highly durable and has an estimated lifespan of 50 years or more. Its resistance to corrosion, scaling and freeze damage adds to its long-term reliability, making it a smart investment for your Glens Falls home.
