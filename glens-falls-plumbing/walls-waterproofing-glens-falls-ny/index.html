    Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Glens Falls

    Plumbing Leak Behind Walls in Glens Falls

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Glens Falls

    Bare dirt in a crawl space releases gallons of water vapor a day into the air under your floor. A 10 mil or heavier polyethylene barrier, overlapped a foot at every seam and taped, cuts that off and keeps the joists and subfloor dry. We run it up the foundation walls and hold it in place with termination strip. It is a cheap fix that makes a big difference.

    Foundation Crack Repair in Glens Falls

    Crack injection takes about two hours per crack. We set injection ports every 8 to 12 inches along the crack, seal the surface with epoxy paste, and pump in resin from the bottom port up until it pushes out the next port. Polyurethane stays flexible if the wall moves a little, and epoxy bonds the two sides back together. The surface paste gets ground off once it cures.

    Basement Wall Sealing in Glens Falls, NY

    Concrete block is porous, and its hollow cores fill with water like a sponge. Wide swings between summer heat and winter cold cause pipes and fittings to expand and contract, which gradually loosens joints. We drill weep holes at the bottom course to drain the cores, then coat the face with a masonry waterproofing product that lets vapor out but stops liquid water. Most homeowners notice the musty smell is gone within a few weeks. The weep holes empty into interior drain tile when there is one.

    Waterproofing for Older Homes in Glens Falls, NY

    Houses built before modern drainage rules often have no footing drain at all, or a clay tile drain that collapsed decades ago.
