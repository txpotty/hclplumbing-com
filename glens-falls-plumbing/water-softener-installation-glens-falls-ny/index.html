    Whole-Home Water Softener Installation in Glens Falls

    Whole-home softeners treat every gallon that enters the house, right after the main shutoff. Placing it on the main means the water heater, the dishwasher and every fixture get soft water, not just the kitchen. We size before we sell. Hardness and household use set the grain capacity.

    Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Glens Falls, NY

    City water often carries chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell. A carbon filter placed ahead of the softener removes those chemicals and protects the resin, since chlorine slowly breaks resin beads down over the years. You will taste it. Setups can be two tanks or one dual-stage tank.

    Water Hardness Testing and Programming in Glens Falls

    Testing is what makes the settings right. We test for hardness, iron and chlorine before installing, then program the regeneration frequency and salt dose so you get soft water without wasting salt. Iron adds to the setting. We review the settings with you before we leave.

    Water Softener Replacement Services in Glens Falls, NY

    Worn-out softeners usually show it in one of two ways, hard water at the tap or a brine tank that never drops.
