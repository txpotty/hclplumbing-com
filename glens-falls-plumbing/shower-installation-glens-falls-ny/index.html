    How We Install Showers in Glens Falls, NY

    Curbless Shower Installation in Glens Falls

    Curbless showers need the floor lowered in the shower area. On a wood floor, we drop the joists or use a recessed pan system, and on a slab, we cut and lower the concrete so the finished floor runs flat into the shower. Linear drains help a lot. Slope and waterproofing get planned before demo.

    Custom Shower System Installation in Glens Falls, NY

    Multi-head showers depend on valve capacity. A standard pressure-balance valve can feed one head, but rain heads, body jets and handhelds need a thermostatic valve with separate volume controls and supply lines sized up to 3/4 inch. Layout comes first. The water heater gets checked for capacity too.

    Prefab Shower Unit Installation in Glens Falls, NY

    Prefab units are the fastest route to a new shower. The base goes on mortar, the drain and valve get connected and the walls are fastened so they stay rigid. They suit guest baths well. Usually a one-day job.

    Tub and Shower Combo Installation in Glens Falls

    Setting a tub is mostly about level and support. Acrylic and steel tubs get mortar or foam underneath, while cast iron rests on a level ledger, which keeps the caulk line intact. The valve goes in next. We set the spout and head heights to your layout and test the overflow gasket with water.

    Tub-to-Shower Conversion in Glens Falls, NY

    Tub-to-shower conversions are one of our most common jobs. We pull the tub, move the drain from the end of the tub to where the shower pan needs it and raise the valve to shower height, usually around 48 inches off the floor. On a slab, we cut concrete. The new shower is safer to step into.

    Shower Upgrades Without a Full Remodel in Glens Falls

    Not every upgrade needs demolition. Replacing an old pan, converting a tub to a shower or swapping in a new valve and trim can make a big difference without tearing the bathroom down to the studs. We price the options side by side. Most of these finish in a few days.

    Walk-In Shower Installation in Glens Falls

    Walk-in showers depend on floor slope. With no curb, the floor needs a steady quarter-inch-per-foot pitch to the drain and waterproofing that extends past the opening. We set that slope. We add blocking for grab bars while the studs are bare.

    Shower Valve and Trim Installation in Glens Falls, NY

    The valve sits behind the tile for decades. We install pressure-balancing, thermostatic and diverter valves, set to the depth your trim kit needs and plumbed with integral stops when the valve has them. Trim goes on after tile. That timing keeps the trim from getting scratched or caked with thinset while the tile is set.
