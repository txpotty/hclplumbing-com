    Full Repipes for Older Homes in Glens Falls

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Glens Falls

    PEX bends around corners and comes in long rolls, so a typical repipe needs fewer fittings than copper would on the same layout. Lake-effect snow and deep cold snaps can keep temperatures below freezing for days, so insulating exposed lines pays off. It often survives freezing where copper would crack. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings where possible, which give a full-bore connection and hold up well over time. Faster install.

    Galvanized Pipe Removal in Glens Falls, NY

    Removing every piece is rarely needed. It is disconnected and capped, while new lines run beside it, which keeps drywall damage low. Only accessible pipe comes out.

    Copper Pipe Replacement in Glens Falls, NY

    Copper still has fans. It is durable and heat tolerant. We install Type L copper for most interior supply lines, sweat or press the joints, while strapping each line properly, so the new pipe does not rattle or sag. Costs more than PEX.

    Copper Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Glens Falls, NY

    Older homes sometimes have copper that is thin from decades of use. Pinholes start showing up. We replace it with new Type L copper or PEX, matching the layout or improving it where the old routing was awkward. Better flow.

    PEX Pipe Installation in Glens Falls

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster. Scale and rust do not affect it.
