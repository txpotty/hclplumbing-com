    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Glens Falls

    PEX-A tubing is flexible, resists scale buildup and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water. We use Uponor or similar PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that open up the full pipe diameter. Long continuous runs mean fewer joints in walls and attics. Most homes are repiped with PEX in two to four days.

    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Glens Falls

    Kitec, with its orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe and brass fittings, and gray polybutylene pipe are both known for failures. Many homes built in the 1980s through early 2000s contain one or the other. We replace these systems completely so your home is easier to insure and sell. Visible pipe at the water heater often tells us which type you have.

    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Glens Falls

    Slab leaks are a serious and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Basements are standard in most homes, and many of them depend on sump pumps to handle groundwater during wet seasons. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the compromised pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use advanced detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.
