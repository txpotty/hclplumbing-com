    Freestanding and Deck-Mount Tub Filler Installation in Glens Falls

    Tub Spout Replacement in Glens Falls

    Spouts mount two ways. Slip-on spouts slide over a 1/2 inch copper stub and lock with a set screw underneath, while threaded spouts screw onto a 1/2 inch pipe nipple, and putting the wrong type on causes leaks behind the wall. The connection gets checked first. Diverter spouts must match too.

    Hard Water Tub Faucet Replacement in Glens Falls

    Water hardness in New York varies a lot, with many surface water supplies running soft and groundwater in some areas running hard. Scale packs into cartridges and spout diverters until handles stiffen and the diverter will not lift. Soaking and cleaning buys time on a lightly scaled valve, but once the brass is pitted or the cartridge has fused to the body, replacing the faucet is the only fix that lasts.     Standard Bathtub Faucet Replacement in Glens Falls

    Single-handle and three-handle tub faucets come out differently. The valve gets identified before we price it. Two-handle models fall in between.
