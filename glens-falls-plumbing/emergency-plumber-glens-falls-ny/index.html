    Burst and Leaking Pipe Repair in Glens Falls

    Pipes burst from freezing, high water pressure, corrosion or a failed fitting. Hard or aggressive water can cause pinhole leaks in copper that suddenly widen into a spray. We shut off water, find the damaged section and replace it with copper or PEX. We also check your water pressure, since pressure above 80 PSI strains the whole system.

    Slab Leak Detection & Repair in Glens Falls, NY

    Slab leaks can cause significant structural damage and high water bills. Basements are standard in most homes, and many of them depend on sump pumps to handle groundwater during wet seasons. Our emergency team uses acoustic leak detection and thermal imaging to pinpoint the exact spot of the leak under your Glens Falls home's foundation. We then provide options for repair, which may include direct slab penetration, re-routing or epoxy pipe lining, always adhering to local building codes for a lasting solution.

    Emergency Slab Leak Response in Glens Falls, NY

    In homes built on concrete slabs, leaks under the slab can show up as warm spots on the floor, a running meter or the sound of water with everything off. We isolate the hot or cold side so you can keep partial water service. Then we locate the leak with acoustic and thermal equipment. Repair options include direct access through the slab, rerouting the line through walls or attic, or a partial repiping.

    Emergency Sump Pump Service in Glens Falls

    Homes with basements or crawl spaces in low-lying parts of Glens Falls rely on sump pumps. If your sump pump fails during heavy rain, it can lead to immediate flooding.
