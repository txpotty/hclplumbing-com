    Frequently Asked Questions About Backflow Preventer in Glens Falls

    Testing in Glens Falls, NY usually costs between $75 and $150 per assembly. That covers the visit, the test with a calibrated gauge and filing the report with your water provider. Multiple assemblies at one property cost less each, since the tester is already on site with the gauge hooked up. Large lines run higher.

    You will usually get a notice from the Glens Falls water department with a repair deadline. We can diagnose the failure, rebuild the checks or relief valve and retest on the same visit in most cases, then file the passing result. Deadlines are firm.

    Water dripping or running from the relief valve on an RPZ is the clearest sign. A pressure drop, a split bonnet after cold weather or water with an odd taste can also mean trouble. Get it tested soon.

    Backflow preventers stop contaminated water from flowing backward into the public supply during pressure drops. Sprinkler systems, fire lines and commercial equipment are the usual sources, and providers require protection on all of them. Most providers require it.

    An RPZ has two checks with a relief valve between them, and it is rated for high-hazard lines. A DCVA has two checks and no relief valve, so it is used for lower hazards like many fire lines and some irrigation systems. Code decides which one.

    Main breaks, hydrant use and fire flow can drop city water pressure in seconds. When that happens, any cross-connection, a sprinkler line, a boiler or a hose in a bucket, can pull water back toward the main. That is the job the device does.

    Repairs and installs from HCL Plumbing carry a workmanship warranty. We use factory rebuild kits and listed parts, and every job is retested before we leave so you start the warranty with a passing assembly. Details come in writing.

    Testing must be done by a certified backflow tester using calibrated equipment. Providers reject anything else. The gauge also has to be calibrated within the past year, and that calibration date goes right on the report form.
