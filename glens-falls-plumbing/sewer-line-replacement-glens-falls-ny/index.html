    Sewer Pipe Lining as an Alternative in Glens Falls

    Cured-in-place pipe lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. After cleaning, a resin-soaked liner is pulled in and cured in place. This works when the host pipe still holds its shape without major sags or collapse. Lining can be a smart option under slabs or finished areas. We tell you plainly if your line is a candidate.

    Sewer Line Material Upgrades in Glens Falls

    Many older homes in Glens Falls still have sewer lines made from outdated materials like clay or cast iron, which are prone to cracking, corrosion, and root invasion. We offer upgrades to modern, more durable materials such as PVC or HDPE (high-density polyethylene). These materials stand up to corrosion, roots, and shifting soil, giving your sewer system a much longer lifespan.

    Traditional Trench Sewer Line Replacement in Glens Falls

    For significant damage or certain pipe layouts, traditional trenching may be the most effective method. This involves digging a trench to access and replace the damaged sewer line directly. Our team carefully plans the excavation to minimize disruption to your landscape, using the right equipment for clean, precise digging. We make sure of proper grading and backfilling to prevent future issues and restore your property.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Glens Falls, NY

    Traditional excavation is sometimes the best option, especially when the old line has a severe belly or the slope must be corrected. We dig along the line, remove the old pipe and set new PVC on compacted bedding at the proper grade. Rocky or hard-packed soil can slow digging, and we account for that in the estimate. Each joint is glued and checked before backfill. The inspector sees the open trench before we cover it.
