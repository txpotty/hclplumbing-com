    Pull-Down Kitchen Faucet Repair in Glens Falls

    Pull-down kitchen faucets wear at the hose, the spray head and the docking magnet or counterweight that pulls the hose back into the spout. Hoses split near the quick-connect. Brand-matched hoses, heads and weights go in, then check for drips under the sink where the hose connects.

    Cracked Faucet Body Repair in Glens Falls

    Cracked faucet bodies, usually from freezing or an old brass casting that finally gave out, cannot be repaired and need a replacement faucet. Turn off the stops under the sink. A new faucet can usually go in on the same visit, and we will check the supply lines and stops while the water is off.

    Specialty Faucet Repair in Glens Falls, NY

    Pot fillers, laundry tub faucets and wall-mount utility faucets each have their own valve styles, and many use compression stems or quarter-turn cartridges that hardware stores do not stock. The common stems ride on the truck. Filtered water faucets at the kitchen sink get new cartridges or check valves as needed.

    Compression Faucet Repair in Glens Falls

    Two-handle faucets with separate hot and cold handles are often compression faucets, which seal by pressing a rubber washer down onto a brass seat inside the valve. Over time the washer hardens and the seat wears rough. We replace the washer and dress or replace the seat with a seat wrench, and the packing gets redone if the stem leaks.

    Ball Faucet Repair in Glens Falls, NY

    Ball faucets have a single lever over a domed cap, and the drip usually comes from the two small rubber seats and springs that press against the ball. Most of them just need new seats and springs. Loose handles happen too. Tightening the adjusting ring under the cap usually stops a handle leak.

    Delta and Moen Faucet Repair in Glens Falls, NY

    Delta and Moen are the faucets we repair most, and both back their cartridges with lifetime warranties for the original owner on many models. We can check whether yours qualifies. Kohler, Pfister and American Standard use their own cartridges, and we stock the common ones for each brand.

    Outdoor Hose Bib Repair in Glens Falls

    Outdoor spigots take a beating from sun and weather. Frozen pipes are a real winter risk across New York, especially in exterior walls, crawlspaces, unheated garages and vacant homes. Packing and seat washers get replaced on standard hose bibs, and we install frost-free sillcocks with the valve seat inside the warm wall when an old one has split. Every new spigot gets a vacuum breaker for backflow protection.

    Kitchen Sprayer and Diverter Repair in Glens Falls, NY

    Spray heads on kitchen faucets get clogged with scale, and side sprayers lose pressure when the diverter inside the faucet body wears out. Diverters are cheap parts. Touch and touchless faucets add a solenoid and batteries to the list, and we test those before replacing anything.
